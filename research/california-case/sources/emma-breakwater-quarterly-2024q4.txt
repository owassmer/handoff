I. INTRODUCTION

ending December 31, 2024 (the “Continuing Disclosure Quarterly Report”) in connection with the Agency’s Senior
Essential Housing Revenue Bonds, Series 2021A-1 and Junior Essential Housing Revenue Bonds, Series 2021A-1
(The Breakwater Apartments) (collectively, the “Bonds”).
