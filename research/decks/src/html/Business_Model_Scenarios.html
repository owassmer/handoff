SCENARIO ANALYSIS

Business model scenarios

Nine ways a company can build in the move-out market, and the modelled odds of revenue, funding and exit for each, 2026 to 2032.

Handoff · September 2026

SUMMARY

Selling a completed move-out gives the best odds of revenue and funding.

Source: Handoff analysis, a monthly company model with 30,000 runs per path and 300 parameter draws.

Sell the outcome first. It gets a paying customer soonest and leads on revenue and funding.

Grow deeper, not wider. The move-in record, the turn and the settlement beat owner projects and maintenance.

Plan for a modest exit. The likely end is a sale to a larger platform, at $4–10M in the median case.
