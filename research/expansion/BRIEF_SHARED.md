The product (working name: Handoff)
- For residential rental property managers and operators. It takes responsibility for a move-out and the
  turnover of the unit.
- When a tenancy is ending, it gathers the lease, condition records, owner instructions and vendor
  information. It asks vendors for quotes and proposes a work plan and budget. The operator accepts or changes
  the plan. Handoff then orders the work and follows visits, reports, invoices and vendor payments until the
  unit is ready for the next resident. It corresponds by email and works within the owner's funds and
  instructions.
- The part being designed next settles the departing tenant's financial relationship: deposit, charges and
  credits, disputes, and recovery or write-off.
- An AI agent does the coordination and reasoning. Deterministic code checks money, identity and
  permissions. People make the accept-or-change decisions.
- Built on Palantir Foundry for the demo. The production platform is undecided.
