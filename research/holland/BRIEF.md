## Opportunities supported by this work

The strongest current hypothesis is reducing repeated handling while reaching a correct, explainable account and an accepted disposition. Four connected functions deserve examination:

- **Condition to responsibility:** preserve the move-in baseline and inspection/work evidence; separate repair cost from tenant liability.
- **Complete account:** reconcile rent, utilities, damages, deposits, credits and valid offsets under the applicable dated rules.
- **Correction to closure:** propagate changes to accounting, payment and collection records, then retain confirmation.
- **Timely communication:** produce understandable statements with evidence of delivery and track unresolved questions through disposition.

This remains consistent with Handoff's two independent outcomes: physical readiness and financial resolution. It does not narrow the product to a deposit calculator. Operator acceptance of an account and actual bank settlement remain distinct. No labor saving, recovery improvement or willingness to pay has yet been measured at Holland.

The next decision should use Holland's actual work: who owns the outcome and budget; where handling repeats; which records can be supplied; and whether a bounded paid engagement can prove value. That is the next proposed step, not a pilot commitment or outreach completed here. Company shape, long-term segment, pricing and acceptance of a legal layer remain unmade decisions.
