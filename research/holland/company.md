## Key facts at a glance

- Holland Partner Group (HPG) is a privately held, vertically integrated multifamily developer, builder, investor and manager, headquartered at 808 Washington St, #500, Vancouver, WA, with about 700 staff.[34][40]
- Since June 2023 its parent is Holland Holdings, LLC, which has a nine-member board with outside directors from LACERA, Capital One, JLL, Pacific Life, PNC/Freddie Mac and Stanford Management Company.[78]
- Founder Clyde Holland is Chairman and CEO; he previously ran Trammell Crow Residential's West Coast group.[35][78]
- HPG has five operating businesses: Investments (acquisitions and asset management), Development, Construction, Redevelopment, and Residential (property management).[38]
- Holland Residential’s feed lists 61 communities (including Sloane’s future 2027 delivery) in AZ, CA, CO, OR and WA; its own site counter shows 18,120 units managed and 750 employees in 27 cities.[43][34][150]
- The residential platform changed leaders in the past year: David Greenberg became President of Residential in November 2025, and Benjamin Meeker became CFO in May 2026.[74][76]
- The property system is Yardi: job postings ask for Yardi, and 59 of 61 community sites link to Yardi RentCafe portals.[112][150]
- Move-out and collections are partly centralised: there are Central Support, Community Operations, Residential Accounting, AR and "Central Billing" functions, and a third-party collections agency. BBB complaints from 2024–2026 dispute move-out statements.[112][2]
- Regulators have looked at the money-handling side. California's Attorney General settled with Holland in 2024 over tenant screening. In October 2025 the California DRE filed an accusation about trust-fund recordkeeping; these are allegations, not findings.[20][18]
- Holland's stated principles commit it to "continuously promote technology and process innovations". It has a Business Technology team running residential-technology pilots, vendor evaluations and a tech-stack inventory.[30][112]

## 1. Ownership and structure

### 1.1 Corporate form, founders, principals

- In June 2023 Holland Holdings, LLC announced its board of directors and said it "now serves as the new holding company to Holland Partner Group".[78]
- The 2023 board had nine members: Clyde Holland, Curtis Feeny (chair), Trina Bigby-Sanders, Jamie Henderson, Jonathan G. Geanakos, Steve LeBlanc, Tony Premer, Diana Reid and Judy Schneider.[78]
- The current leadership page lists the same board apart from Steve LeBlanc, and adds Joel Kaplan as outside legal counsel.[35]
- Board backgrounds, from the company's own bios:
  - Feeny: senior advisor at Peterson Partners; formerly EVP of Stanford Management Company and a partner at Trammell Crow Company.[7]

  - Henderson: head of Capital One's commercial real estate business.[78]
  - Geanakos: formerly of JLL Capital Markets.[78]

  - Reid: CEO of Freddie Mac from September 2024 to March 2025, and formerly of PNC Real Estate.[13]

- Before founding Holland, Clyde Holland was West Coast group managing partner of Trammell Crow Residential.[78][68]

### 1.2 The five operating companies (HPG's own description)

- **Holland Investments** (Acquisition and Asset Management): "over $3 billion in total acquisition transaction experience and over $4 billion of asset value under management". It targets assets that are "undervalued or inefficiently managed or financed".[38]
- **Holland Development**: "$10 billion in total completed development projects", focused on urban infill and mixed-use in high-barrier markets.[38]
- **Holland Construction**: in-house general contractor, with "nearly 80 projects representing nearly 20,000 units".[38]
- **Holland Redevelopment**: "nearly 50 assets spanning over 14,000 units", over $150M of capital improvements. With Holland Residential it has renovated 10,000 unit interiors using "two primary packages based on customer feedback and ROI analysis".[38]
- **Holland Residential**: "manages over 60 communities in Arizona, California, Colorado, Oregon, and Washington". Each regional office is led by a Vice President supported by Regional Property Managers and "dedicated property-specific accountants" at the corporate office.[38]

## 3. Operating model

### 3.1 Structure

- Regional structure: "Each region is led by a Vice President and supported by experienced Regional Property Managers." Teams connect to corporate "working alongside dedicated property-specific accountants and support divisions … policy-driven processes".[43]
- Two regional operations SVPs split the portfolio into NorCal/OR/WA and SoCal/CO/AZ. A third SVP covers "Residential Corporate".[43]
- Property accounting is centralised in Vancouver, WA. Holland markets a "detailed Residential Accounting team" alongside on-site teams, and posts corporate accounting jobs in Vancouver.[138][112]

Public profiles (snippets) show what central roles do in practice:

  - SODA is a statement of deposit accounting.
- Central Support associates include remote and Northern California seats created in August–October 2025.[120][121]

Reconstruction, not a verified SOP: official job descriptions establish site money handling, regional supervision, property accounting, turns and a Community Operations interface. Historical profile snippets suggest site statement approval and central lease-end support; complaint excerpts refer to Central Billing and an external collections agency. The exact sequence, responsibility split, approval rights and regional variation remain undisclosed. A title catalogue establishes named functions, not current headcount or reporting lines.

### 3.4 On-site staffing and duties

- The retrieved leasing and APM postings assign money handling on site:
  - "Collect security deposits, rents, and all other funds associated with leasing"
  - "Perform daily deposit of funds collected into assigned property's bank account"
  - "Report and record all delinquent rents and post late notices to residents as required. Remain up-to-date on all state laws regarding non-payment and delinquent accounts"[112]
- Sites run on "Holland Residential Standard Operating Procedures" and weekly reports "sent each Sunday", plus a "Point of Sale Renewal program".[112]
  - The point-of-sale focus goes back at least to Tony D'Alto's appointment as Holland Residential president (undated article).[49]
- Clustering: current postings pair two nearby communities under one Assistant Property Manager (Westlake Steps + One Lakefront, Seattle) or one Senior Property Manager (The Flats at Inverness + The District at Hampden South, Denver area). Pay is $90–110K for the SPM.[112]
- Housing discounts are offered for on-site roles, "dependent on the property, ownership approval".[112]

### 3.5 Maintenance, turns and renovation

- Maintenance Managers must "Ensure that unit turns are completed timely, to company standards and inspected upon completion". Technicians "Complete unit turns of assigned units generated on weekly turn schedule". A housekeeper role handles a "weekly cleaning turn schedule".[112]
- Regional floating maintenance managers and technicians cover several communities, for example Seattle at $38–43/hr.[143][112]
- Interior renovation is standardised into two packages. There is a Director of Asset Preservation, and capital project manager roles.[38][43][112]

## 9. Open questions to ask at first contact

1. Walk us through a move-out today, from notice to zero balance. Which steps are on site, which are Community Operations/Central Support, which are Residential Accounting, and when does an account go to the collections agency?
2. Who signs off on the final deposit statement (SODA): the property manager, a central reviewer, or both? Does that differ by state?
3. How many move-outs a month across the portfolio? What share end in a balance owed, a dispute, or a collections referral?
4. How do you track days-to-statement against each state's deadline (using the current law map and the account’s actual trigger dates)? Has a statement ever gone late?
5. How are move-in condition reports captured and stored? Is it the same tool and format at all 61 communities?
6. Is Yardi Voyager still self-hosted with an internal support team? Which Yardi modules (RentCafe, Resident Screening, Procure to Pay, Maintenance, Forms) do you use for move-out?
7. Who is the collections vendor? How do balance corrections flow to them?
8. What changed in deposit and trust-account handling after the California DRE audit and accusation? Is anything still open?
9. Is Bilt the standard payment channel for former residents? Can former residents pay partially or dispute inside it?
10. Do you use, or have you tested, deposit alternatives or deposit-free programs? Why or why not?
11. For assets you manage for third-party owners (for example after selling Savanna or Beardslee), do owners set their own move-out policies or chargeback schedules?
12. What are David Greenberg's first-year priorities for the residential platform? Where does move-out rank against leasing, renewals and expense?
13. How does a pilot get approved (sponsor, KPIs, length, number of communities, security review), and who in Business Technology would run it?
14. Which states or cities give you the most trouble on move-out rules today (Seattle move-in checklist, California itemisation and photos, Colorado, Oregon, Arizona)?
