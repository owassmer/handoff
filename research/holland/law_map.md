## Geographic reach and property gates

Census incorporated-place assignment controls local-layer screening, rather than marketing city names. Summerwalk is screened in Sammamish; Flats at Inverness in unincorporated Arapahoe County; 550 Harborfront in Los Angeles. County names are retained on every community. A county ordinance applying only in unincorporated areas does not extend into incorporated cities merely because they share that county.

**Every reach list is a screening list.** Portfolio building/opening year is not proof of the first certificate-of-occupancy date. Affordable status, ownership, lease dates, tenancy duration and program documents remain unit-level facts. New construction can be exempt from a rent cap but subject to other local protections: San José TPO 17.23.1230 includes multiple dwellings even when its ARO age gate is not met.
