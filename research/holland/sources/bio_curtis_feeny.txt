CURTIS FEENY
BOARD CHAIR
Curtis Feeny is a senior advisor at Peterson Partners. Prior to this, he was a general partner in four venture capital funds at Voyager Capital and Silicon Valley Data Capital. He had previously served as executive vice president of Stanford Management Company, helping oversee the university’s endowment from 1992 to 2000, when assets under management grew from $1.5 billion to $9 billion, and as a partner at Trammell Crow Company, with $700 million of assets under management. Curtis has served on numerous boards, public & private, including Trammell Crow Co. CBRE, Staples, Khan Academy, and Stanford Federal Credit Union.
