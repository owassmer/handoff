JUDY SCHNEIDER
CHIEF OPERATING OFFICER - HOLLAND FAMILY OFFICE
During her over 20 years with Holland, Judy Schneider played a pivotal role in building the foundation and leadership of the company as Chief Operations Officer. She focused on supporting and growing teams, establishing regional office facilities, and leading the expansion of HR/Talent Development and Technology, among other initiatives. Her efforts have also included overseeing external audits that have helped to strengthen the company's capabilities. Judy has also been instrumental in creating important employee benefit programs which have contributed to the exceptional tenure of Holland team members. These programs have been crucial in retaining top talent and fostering a positive company culture. Today, Judy serves as the Chief Operating Officer in Holland's Family Office, and as the Families Representative on Holland’s Board of Directors. Her contributions have been invaluable in shaping the company's growth and success.
