      This matter is before the Court on the Recommendation of United States

Magistrate Judge Michael E. Hegarty filed on December 1, 2021 [Docket No. 18]. The

Recommendation states that objections to the Recommendation must be filed within

fourteen days after its service on the parties. Docket No. 18 at 4-5; see also 28 U.S.C.

§ 636(b)(1)(C). The Recommendation was served on December 1 and December 2,

2021. 1 No party has objected to the Recommendation.

      In the absence of an objection, the district court may review a magistrate judge’s

recommendation under any standard it deems appropriate. See Summers v. Utah, 927

F.2d 1165, 1167 (10th Cir. 1991); see also Thomas v. Arn, 474 U.S. 140, 150 (1985) (“It
