Holland Residential unifies 70+ websites with Oshyn to boost rental applications

Holland Residential, a fully-integrated real estate investment company, faced the challenge of managing over 70 disparate websites hosted by different vendors. They partnered with Oshyn to create a more cohesive digital brand and streamline their web presence to drive increased business and revenue.
Oshyn recreated all of Holland's websites on a single Optimizely platform. This solution provided responsive design, centralized content management, and powerful online marketing tools. As a result, Holland saw a marked increase in rental applications, rental prices, and portfolio net worth, alongside dramatic improvements in SEO. The Oshyn team delivered this efficient and effective infrastructure with a lower investment.
