Clyde Holland

Chairman & Chief Executive Officer

Recognized by the industry for excellence in urban planning and design, Clyde’s work has received numerous awards from the Urban Land Institute, the National Multi-Family Housing Council, and Multi-Family Executive. He has directed over 50,000 units of housing and $10 billion (USD) in transaction value.

Ben Meeker

CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER

Ben Meeker is Senior Vice President, Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer. He is responsible for Taubman’s financial functions and reports to Taubman Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Robert Taubman.
Prior to his promotion in January 2021, Ben most recently served as Vice President, Capital Markets and Treasurer. In this role, he was responsible for capital markets (transactions, including financings, acquisitions and dispositions), as well as treasury, risk management, partner relations and financial planning & analysis.
Before joining Taubman in 2012, Ben was Vice President, Fund & Development for The John Buck Company, and previously worked as a Senior Acquisitions Analyst for Miller Capital Advisory, Inc.
Ben is an active member of the community, serving as a member of the Finance and Budget Committee for the Cranbrook Educational Community, a trustee of the Michigan Opera Theatre and a volunteer for Life Remodeled and Gleaners Community Food Bank. He is a graduate of the ULI Larson Center for Leadership and is a member of the Urban Land Institute and the International Council of Shopping Centers.
Ben earned an MBA from the University of Michigan Ross School of Business, where he graduated with distinction and was inducted into the Beta Gamma Sigma honor society. Ben received a BA magna cum laude from Amherst College and studied abroad at Georgetown University in Italy. He resides in Bloomfield Hills with his wife, a physician, and their two children.

David Greenberg

President - Residential

Judy Schneider

Chief Operating Officer - Holland Family Office

During her over 20 years with Holland, Judy Schneider played a pivotal role in building the foundation and leadership of the company as Chief Operations Officer. She focused on supporting and growing teams, establishing regional office facilities, and leading the expansion of HR/Talent Development and Technology, among other initiatives. Her efforts have also included overseeing external audits that have helped to strengthen the company's capabilities. Judy has also been instrumental in creating important employee benefit programs which have contributed to the exceptional tenure of Holland team members. These programs have been crucial in retaining top talent and fostering a positive company culture. Today, Judy serves as the Chief Operating Officer in Holland's Family Office, and as the Families Representative on Holland’s Board of Directors. Her contributions have been invaluable in shaping the company's growth and success.
