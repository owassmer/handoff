David Greenberg

David Greenberg

Managing Director

Asset Management

Mr. Greenberg serves as Managing Director and Head of Asset Management for Griffin Capital Company, LLC. In this capacity, he is responsible for a nationally diversified multifamily portfolio, including implementing value-creation strategies, monitoring operating and financial performance, and overseeing budgeting and business planning.

Prior to leading the Asset Management team at Griffin Capital Company, LLC, Mr. Greenberg worked in the multifamily sector with other nationally recognized real estate investment management firms, including Brookfield Asset Management, Related, and The Irvine Company. His experience includes oversight of property operations, collaborating on over $10B of multifamily development, acquisition, and capital reinvestment, and expertise in both asset and portfolio management. Throughout his career, Mr. Greenberg has also played a key role in managing Investor and Joint Venture relationships.

Mr. Greenberg graduated from the University of Pennsylvania with a degree in International Relations and is a member of the Board of Trustees of The Irvine Public School Foundation. Mr. Greenberg also sits on Board of the Building Improvement District (BID) for The Arts District, Downtown Los Angeles. [personal detail removed by researcher]
