CULTURE
We are committed to creating a culture of belonging and continue to strive for a workforce that elevates the many voices of our company, prioritizes empathy and understanding, and supports career advancement opportunities for all.
Our dedication to this journey makes us a stronger company that delivers better results.
LEADERSHIP
___
Holland Partner Group is committed to equal employment opportunities, so that we benefit from the unique perspectives of all our team members.  Our Senior Leadership Team is committed to building connections with employees across all regions, business lines, and roles.
VOLUNTEERISM
___
Through our Holland Cares initiative, employees from all business lines can spend time together collaborating in their communities. Our regularly scheduled volunteer events offer opportunities to meet new people and embrace the responsibility of giving back to the regions where we operate.
ENGAGEMENT
___
We rely on the feedback of our employees to create an environment where everyone can reach for their full potential. We actively involve our employees in shaping the future of our company, fostering a culture of collaboration and innovation.
LEARNING
___
Holland Partner Group remains steadfast in its commitment to equal employment opportunities for everyone. We uphold our commitment through ongoing training on fair employment practices as well as discrimination and harassment prevention to create a workplace where everyone can succeed.
"Holland recognizes that building a dynamic workplace involves having a collaborative culture, where all perspectives contribute to our excellent work."
Clyde Holland, Chairman & CEO
