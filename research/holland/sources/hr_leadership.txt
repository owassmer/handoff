LEADERSHIP
Holland Residential’s success is driven by a strong leadership team that manages over 70 communities across Arizona, California, Colorado, Oregon, and Washington. Each region is led by a Vice President and supported by experienced Regional Property Managers, ensuring operational excellence and strategic oversight. Our teams are closely connected to Holland’s corporate office, working alongside dedicated property-specific accountants and support divisions. This collaborative approach, backed by policy-driven processes, ensures accountability and sustained performance across all levels of our organization.
