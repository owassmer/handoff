Civil Code - CIV
DIVISION 3. OBLIGATIONS [1427 - 3273.91]
( Heading of Division 3 amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 160, Sec. 14. )

PART 2. CONTRACTS [1549 - 1701]
( Part 2 enacted 1872. )

TITLE 2.5. ELECTRONIC TRANSACTIONS [1633.1 - 1633.17]
( Title 2.5 added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 428, Sec. 1. )

1633.7.
(a) A record or signature may not be denied legal effect or enforceability solely because it is in electronic form.
(b) A contract may not be denied legal effect or enforceability solely because an electronic record was used in its formation.
(c) If a law requires a record to be in writing, an electronic record satisfies the law.
(d) If a law
requires a signature, an electronic signature satisfies the law.
(Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 428, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
