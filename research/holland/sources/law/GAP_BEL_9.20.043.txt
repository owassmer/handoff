###### 9.20.043 Reasonable accommodation.1.

Requests for reasonable accommodation by any person claiming to have a handicap, or someone acting on their behalf, shall be processed at no cost under Part 20.30T of Title 21 (Land Use Code) of the Bellevue City Code, as now exists or is hereafter amended. (Ord. 6195 § 4, 2014.)

The Bellevue City Code is current through Ordinance 6929, passed July 14, 2026.
