L11: ###### 9.21.100 Public hearing – Unlawful detainer.
L12: 
L13: The owner or tenant may request a public hearing before the hearing examiner to resolve disputes between displaced tenants and the owner relating to unlawful detainer actions during relocation. The public hearing shall be governed by the provisions for appeal of a Process II decision, LUC cite6†20.35.250 . (Ord. 4978 § 29, 1997; Ord. 4354 § 1, 1992.)
