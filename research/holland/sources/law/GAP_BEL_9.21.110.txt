###### 9.21.110 Appeal of hearing examiner decision.

A decision of the hearing examiner under BCC 9.21.070(D) or 9.21.100 is the final city decision. The decision of the hearing examiner may be appealed to superior court by filing a petition within 10 calendar days following the date of the decision. Appeals to superior court are by application for a writ of certiorari, writ of prohibition or writ of mandamus. (Ord. 4354 § 1, 1992.)
The Bellevue City Code is current through Ordinance 6920, passed June 3, 2026.
