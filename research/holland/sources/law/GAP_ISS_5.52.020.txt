###### 5.52.020 Applicability.

IMC 5.52.030 through 5.52.050 apply to tenancies governed by Chapter 59.18 RCW (RLTA) and Chapter 59.20 RCW (Manufactured/Mobile Home Landlord-Tenant Act) and are in addition to the provisions provided in said chapters. (Ord. 2986 § 2, 2022).

5.52.030 
The Issaquah Municipal Code is current through Ordinance 3148, passed August 3, 2026.
