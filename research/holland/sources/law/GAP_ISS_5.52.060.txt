###### 5.52.060 Effect on existing lease provisions.

Any notice of rent increases lawfully given to an existing tenant prior to the effective date of the ordinance codified in this chapter shall be deemed valid. Otherwise, the notice provisions of IMC 5.52.030 apply to all rental agreements as of the effective date of the ordinance enacting these code provisions. (Ord. 2986 § 2, 2022).
