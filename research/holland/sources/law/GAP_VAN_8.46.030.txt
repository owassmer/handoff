###### 8.46.030 Provisions in violation of restrictions null and void.

Any provisions in violation of Section 8.46.020 of this Chapter in a rental agreement are null and void and of no lawful force and effect. (Ord. M-4135 §4, 2015)

The Vancouver Municipal Code is current through Ordinance M-4525, passed December 8, 2025.
