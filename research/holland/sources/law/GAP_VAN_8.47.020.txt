###### 8.47.020 Applicability.

Section 8.47.030(A)(1) applies only to owners of real property, made available for rent, owning a total of five (5) or more dwelling units, all of which are made available for rent, regardless of whether the rental dwelling units are all in the same location/complex or not (Ord. M-4134 §3, 2015)

The Vancouver Municipal Code is current through Ordinance M-4525, passed December 8, 2025.
