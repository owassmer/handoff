or at the beginning of the tenancy, and if the landlord withholds part of a tenant’s security deposit for repairs or cleaning. February 24, 2025 Changes to Security Deposit laws - Photograph Requirements California Assembly Bill 2801 now requires landlords to provide photographic evidence if withholding part of a tenant’s security deposit for repairs or cleaning. Beginning April 1, 2025 , landlords must take photographs of the unit: Within a reasonable time after possession of the rental unit was returned to the landlord, but prior to any repairs or cleanings for which a deduction is made; and, Within a reasonable time after repairs or cleanings for which a deduction is made are completed. In addition, for tenancies that begin on or after July 1, 2025 , a landlord must take photographs of the unit immediately before, or at the beginning of the tenancy.
