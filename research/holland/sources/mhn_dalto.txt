# Holland Residential Announces New President
Holland Partner Group announced that it has named Tony D'Alto president of Holland Residential.
Chief among D'Alto's duties will be developing with Holland a point of sale experience for prospects and consistently delivering an industry changing level of customer service to residents.
Executives at Holland say they were particularly impressed with D'Alto's operational and team development experience, which includes 25 years in senior leadership positions creating and implementing successful operational and sales processes at Wood Residential Services and at AIMCO, one of the largest owners of apartment communities in the United States. Prior to joining Holland, D'Alto was senior vice president of property operations at Wood Residential Services and executive vice president with AIMCO where he had operational management responsibility for more than 1,000 properties. This portfolio totaled approximately 150,000 units across a full spectrum of asset classes nationwide, including $1 billion dollars in properties involved in redevelopment projects.
Holland Partner Group, located in Vancouver, Wash., is the Western U.S.'s premier multifamily developer and operator. As a fully-integrated real estate investment company, Holland has existing holdings which represent over 20,000 apartment homes located in primary Western U.S. markets, including California, Washington, Oregon, Colorado and Arizona, and has a significant portfolio of new development currently underway. Holland Residential is the property management arm of Holland Partner Group.
