# Holland Partner Group Secures $84M Denver Loan
Newmark arranged an $83.8 million loan for The Deveraux, a 374-unit trophy property in Denver. The brokerage firm facilitated the three-year note through BMO Bank, according to public records. Holland Partner Group owns the community, Yardi Matrix data shows.
Previous debt consisted of an $82.5 million construction loan issued by Capital One in 2021, the data provider reveals.
The seven-story community is at 2950 Arkins Court, in Denver's River North neighborhood ... The Deveraux debuted in 2024 ...
