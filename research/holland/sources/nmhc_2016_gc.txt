General contractor rankings are produced using the number of units started by the company for a third-party account in 2015, as reported by the company’s responsible party completing the survey.

Who is a General Contractor/Builder?

An Apartment General Contractor/Builder is a company that is responsible for the physical construction of a new multifamily (defined as five or more units) rental development, (including student housing, independent living and age-restricted housing).
