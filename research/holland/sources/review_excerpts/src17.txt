April 24, 2025|Opinion or OrderFiling 29MINUTE ORDER re CORPORATE DISCLOSURE STATEMENTS: In an action in which jurisdiction is based on diversity under 28 U.S.C. 1332(a), a party must, unless the court orders otherwise, file a disclosure statement upon its first appearance, pleading, petition, motion, response, or other request addressed to the court. Fed. R. Civ. P. 7.1. The statement must name and identity the citizenship of every individual or entity whose citizenship is attributed to the party. Id. Where a defendant is an unincorporated LLC, all members of the LLC must be identified because the citizenship of every member of an LLC is attributed to the LLC for purposes of diversity jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. 1332(a). Siloam Springs Hotel, L.L.C. v. Century Sur. Co., 781 F.3d 1233, 1238 (10th Cir. 2015). Defendants therefore must file compliant corporate disclosure statements identifying the citizenship of all of its members on or before May 1, 2025. SO ORDERED, by Magistrate Judge Scott T.
