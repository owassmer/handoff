Chunga isn't just someone who handles maintenance requests although he does that extremely well and often all on his own he's someone who truly keeps the building running smoothly. It's clear that he's stretched thin, especially when it seems like he's the only one regularly visible and handling work that probably should be shared by a larger team.
Still, he takes everything in stride and finds a way to be kind and helpful no matter how busy he is.
There was a day when the mail was mistakenly delivered to the wrong place, and instead of leaving it at that, Chunga personally called all of us to let us know our packages were downstairs because a neighbor had brought everything over.
Tyra is still here, and she works incredibly hard to keep everything running smoothly behind the scenes, although we don't get to interact with her as much because she's in the office managing things. Staff like Molly, Paul, Heidi and Amber always brought warmth and took the time to build real relationships with residents.
Amber has moved on to manage larger buildings now, and while I'm happy for her, she's definitely missed here. Diego was another familiar face always helpful, incredibly fashionable, and easy to talk to without ever seeming fake.
Mo, who used to be the maintenance supervisor, and Jose were also both hardworking and kind, and they helped create a really welcoming environment.
But now, it feels like most of those great people have moved on, and Chunga is the one who's left holding everything together.
