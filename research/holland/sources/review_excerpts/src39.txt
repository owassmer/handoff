One of the many mid-rise modern apartment buildings in South Lake Union. Lived here for a couple years.
Nice location, close to the lake, Queen Anne, Seattle Center, downtown, public transportation, and offices of the various companies in SLU.
They are in the middle in terms of the more modern apartment complexes in the area. Not as nice as the more expensive high rises, but better finishes for places nearby that are cheaper.
Has electronic access via key fobs, and the apartment door keys are electronic. Facilities like lounge and fitness center are on the smaller side, but functional. Noise insulation seems good, but maybe it's because I lived on the top floor sharing only one wall with another unit.
Maintenance has been prompt and the common areas are cleaned regularly. The office staff has changed a few times during my stay, but I found them to be pleasant. There are resident events, but as with most apartments they tend to decrease in frequency as the building gets older.
There are also the general noise and homeless issues with areas surrounding downtown Seattle. Also there is no AC in the apartments, and it can get pretty hot in the summers especially with heat waves in the last few years.
I stayed a couple of years because it was a convenient location, close to work. Moved out when my workplace moved.
Move out was smooth. They charged the cleaning fee I was expecting and that was that.
