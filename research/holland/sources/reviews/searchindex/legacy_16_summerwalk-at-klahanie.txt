About this Rental
Our leasing office is open for in-person tours by appointment or walk-in. Schedule yours today!
Summerwalk at Klahanie is located on the Issaquah Plateau in the exclusive Klahanie neighborhood. As a resident of Summerwalk, you are able to enjoy an outdoor pool, indoor basketball court, 24 hour fitness center and on site daycare facility.
In addition, Summerwalk residents have access to local private parks, playgrounds, and an indoor pool. Our well appointed one, two and three bedroom floor plans are unique and newly remodeled. Our homes feature full size washers and dryers, microwaves, 9' ceilings, stylish white cabinetry, wood burning fireplaces and modern fixtures throughout.
Many of our homes come with attached garages. Located just minutes from I-90, you will have access to Downtown Seattle, Bellevue, Redmond and live in the Award Winning Issaquah School District. Come home to Summerwalk!
