REVIEW [11/8/18] Phenomenal boutique apartments!
When looking for a new apartment, we stumbled upon 1880 even though it wasn't on our list of places to look into. Once we met Lee and Kaley and completed the tour, we knew we didn't need to look any further! This place is AMAZING! Beautiful interiors, unique floor plans, and a phenomenal leasing team are just some of the great things about living at 1880. The amenities are second to none, with a gorgeous business center, working remotely is convenient and there are individual rooms for privacy and conferencing. You've gotta see the rooftop lounge for yourself! With mountain views and overlooking the park, the cabanas, fire pit, grills, pool and hot tub, there really is no reason not to love this place! I couldn't wait to move in to 1880 and now that I live here, I can't imagine living anywhere else!
