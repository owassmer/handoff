REVIEW [] 
The location of this place can't be beat. You can really walk to anything. The mgmt in the office have always been really nice & helpful as well as maintenance. My only real complaint is lack of parking for residents & guests. The garage gets full around 6 pm so if you come home any time after that you have to drive to the roof. Sucks when you pay for parking. All the parking near the complex is not free. So any guests that visit have to search for parking then pay $10. Just very inconvenient. Other than that I recommend it. They have nice updates interiors & really nice pool. Just prepare to not park close to your unit!

REVIEW [] 
I've lived in CPW for over a year after relocating from the East Coast. The location truly is amazing and my views of the Denver skyline and Riverfront Park are the best in the city. However... The common areas are always dirty and this building attracts a lot of very young tenants who treat the apartment building as though it is their college dorm. The units themselves are spacious but have not been updated. You are paying higher rents at this building than many of the brand new buildings that have recently delivered, and CPW makes it painful. I'm currently paying over $1,900 for a 1 bedroom after you add in all of the extra charges; parking, trash valet, trash rebill, water rebill.. The only way I will stay at CPW is if I win www.apartments.com Rent for Life contest!
