REVIEW [] 
Life at Dimension Apartments in Belltown Seattle satisfies my desires of travel! I feel like I'm on top of the world in this new apartment with the 11th floor balcony (1 of only 10 total balconies offered in the building). And, the rooftop views are absolutely stunning during the day times 10 at night!!! I used to have an aversion to sunsets because they remind me of not affording to travel & enjoy romance since I'm so busy with my career but I've grown to love them and never want to leave here....I can't imagine what would ever take me away! My two dogs are loving the balcony and roof also, just hanging out in the sun privately with no street noise to bother them! Plenty of TV's & seating all throughout the apartment building keep things interested and fullfilling to help rebalance myself after a busy work day/week. I love Dimension Belltown!
