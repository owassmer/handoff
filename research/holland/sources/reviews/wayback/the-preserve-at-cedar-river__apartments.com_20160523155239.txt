REVIEW [] 
Being in the property management industry, I had high standards set. The office staff was exceptionally great to work with and very helpful. Our apartment was spotless and the layout of the apartment was even better! I have a small dog so being able to take him out easily was important and there's doggie stations everywhere! Overall, a fantastic place to live and can't wait to not move for a while.
