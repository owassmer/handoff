Residents enjoy luxury amenities including a swimming pool, spa, rooftop terrace, fitness center, outdoor gathering spaces, barbecue areas, and controlled-access entry. The community is designed to support both relaxation and active lifestyles while taking advantage of its waterfront setting. Does the property have a fitness center? Yes, 550 Harborfront offers an onsite fitness center that allows residents to maintain their workout routines without leaving the community. The fitness space complements the property's wellness-focused amenities. Are the apartments pet-friendly? Yes, 550 Harborfront is a pet-friendly community. Residents benefit from nearby waterfront walking paths, parks, and outdoor spaces that make daily walks and recreation convenient for pet owners. What apartment features are included? Apartment homes feature modern finishes, in-home washers and dryers, efficient appliances, air conditioning, spacious layouts, and select waterfront or harbor views. Many homes also include private patios or balconies for enjoying the coastal atmosphere. Is parking available at 550 Harborfront? Yes, resident parking is available. The community also offers controlled-access entry to help provide convenience for residents. Location & Neighborhood Where is 550 Harborfront located? 550 Harborfront is located at 550 S. Palos Verdes Street in San Pedro, California, along the Los Angeles Waterfront. The community sits in the heart of San Pedro's evolving harbor district, offering residents convenient access to waterfront attractions, dining, and recreation. What neighborhood is the property in? 550 Harborfront is located in the Central San Pedro neighborhood, a coastal community known for its working waterfront, historic attractions, and growing entertainment district. Residents enjoy living steps from the harbor while maintaining convenient access to downtown San Pedro and surrounding coastal communities. What shopping and dining destinations are nearby? The San Pedro Public Market development is located just steps from the community, while Downtown San Pedro retail and dining districts are approximately 0.6 miles away. Residents can also enjoy Ports O' Call Village waterfront attractions (0.4 miles), West Harbor waterfront development (0.5 miles), and additional shopping at Peninsula Center (6.0 miles). What parks and outdoor recreation areas are nearby? Residents enjoy proximity to Los Angeles Waterfront Park (0.4 miles), Cabrillo Beach (2.0 miles), Point Fermin Park (2.5 miles), and Angels Gate Park (2.8 miles). These destinations offer walking paths, beach access, scenic ocean viewpoints, picnic areas, and outdoor recreation opportunities. What major employers are located near 550 Harborfront? Major employers include the Port of Los Angeles (0.5 miles), AltaSea at the Port of Los Angeles (1.2 miles), Kaiser Permanente San Pedro Medical Offices (1.5 miles), and numerous maritime, shipping, logistics, and government employers throughout the harbor district. The Port of Los Angeles remains one of the largest employment centers in Southern California.
