Contour 39 is located at 9005 S. Yosemite Street in Lone Tree, Colorado. The community offers convenient access to the Denver Tech Center, Park Meadows, light rail, dining, trails, and major transportation routes. What neighborhood is Contour 39 in? Contour 39 is located in the Highlands Ranch–Lone Tree area, a community known for parks, trails, shopping, and a comfortable suburban atmosphere. Residents can enjoy outdoor recreation and local conveniences while remaining connected to Central Denver and the Denver Tech Center. What grocery stores are near Contour 39? Safeway at Lincoln Commons is approximately 1.0 mile away, King Soopers on Park Meadows Drive is about 1.8 miles away, and Target Grocery at Park Meadows is approximately 2.2 miles away. Whole Foods Market in Highlands Ranch is about 4.5 miles away. These locations offer convenient options for groceries, household supplies, pharmacy items, and specialty foods. What shopping and dining destinations are nearby? Park Meadows is approximately 2.0 miles away and features shopping, restaurants, and entertainment. Lone Tree Town Center is about 1.0 mile away, while restaurants such as The White Chocolate Grill, Sierra, and California Pizza Kitchen are within approximately 2.0 miles at Park Meadows. The light rail station and surrounding business district also provide additional dining options. What parks and outdoor recreation areas are nearby? Sweetwater Park is approximately 0.5 miles away and provides direct access to Willow Creek biking and jogging trails. Bluffs Regional Park is about 2.5 miles away, and Prairie Sky Park is approximately 3.0 miles away. These destinations support walking, hiking, cycling, outdoor fitness, and scenic views. How is public transportation and commuting from Contour 39? Contour 39 is steps from the Lone Tree City Center light rail station, providing access to the Denver Tech Center, downtown Denver, and other regional destinations. Interstate 25 is approximately 1.5 miles away, while E-470 is about 2.0 miles away. Denver International Airport is approximately 35 miles from the community. What major employers are near Contour 39? Major employers near Contour 39 include Sky Ridge Medical Center, approximately 1.5 miles away; Charles Schwab’s Lone Tree campus, approximately 1.5 miles away; and the Denver Tech Center, approximately 5.0 miles away. Contour 39 offers one- and two-bedroom apartment homes, including select townhome-style residences. Residents enjoy a seasonal resort-style pool, fitness center, clubhouse, coffee café, business center, and wireless internet access.
