The Miles at South Cooper Mountain is located in the desirable South Cooper Mountain area of Beaverton, Oregon, offering a peaceful suburban setting with convenient access to urban amenities. The community is near Progress Ridge Town Square (2.2 miles), where residents can find shopping, dining, and entertainment. Downtown Beaverton is also easily accessible at approximately 4.5 miles away. What grocery stores are nearby? Residents have multiple grocery options nearby, including Safeway (1.5 miles), New Seasons Market (2.8 miles), and Fred Meyer (3.7 miles). Costco Wholesale in Hillsboro is also just 4.5 miles away, offering bulk shopping convenience. These options make every day errands quick and convenient. What shopping and dining options are close to the community? The community is conveniently located near Progress Ridge Town Square (2.2 miles), which features AMC Movie Theater, Big Al’s, and several restaurants. Washington Square Mall (6.8 miles) provides a wide range of retail stores including Nordstrom and Apple. Additional dining and shopping can be found at Murray Scholls Town Center (3.1 miles). Are there parks or outdoor recreation areas nearby? Yes, residents can enjoy nearby outdoor spaces such as Cooper Mountain Nature Park (1.2 miles), which offers trails and scenic views. Jenkins Estate (3.6 miles) and Sexton Mountain Park (2.4 miles) are also popular for walking, picnicking, and recreation. These parks provide excellent opportunities to enjoy the outdoors year-round. What major employers are near The Miles at South Cooper Mountain? The Miles at South Cooper Mountain is conveniently located near a diverse mix of major employers in Beaverton and Hillsboro. Nearby opportunities include healthcare providers like Kaiser Permanente Westside Medical Center and Providence, as well as large public employers such as the Beaverton School District and Portland Community College. Residents also benefit from proximity to Progress Ridge TownSquare for retail and dining jobs, along with a range of technology, manufacturing, and professional services companies throughout the surrounding business corridors. Apartment Features & Amenities What types of apartments are available? The Miles at South Cooper Mountain offers a variety of, one-bedroom, two-bedroom, three-bedroom, and four-bedroom apartment homes. \What interior features can residents expect? Apartment homes feature modern kitchens with stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, and in-unit washer and dryer. Spacious floor plans, large windows, and private patios or balconies are also included at The Miles at South Cooper Mountain. What community amenities are offered? The community is designed to support both relaxation and productivity.
