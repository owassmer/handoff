Lydian is located in downtown Oakland, California, at 401 27th Street. The property is situated in the vibrant Uptown district, offering easy access to local businesses, parks, and transportation. What is near Lydian? Lydian is within 0.5 miles of Lake Merritt, providing scenic outdoor space. Nearby, you’ll find Whole Foods Market (0.3 miles), Target (0.8 miles), and the Uptown Oakland shopping district (0.4 miles). In addition, the Fox Theater is just 0.6 miles away for entertainment options. Is Lydian near bus lines? Yes, Lydian is located near several AC Transit bus lines. The closest bus stop is right outside our doors, offering connections throughout Oakland and the greater Bay Area. Residents also have convenient access to BART stations within 1 mile. Yes, Lydian offers on-site maintenance services to ensure that residents’ needs are promptly addressed and common areas are kept in excellent condition. Is there an elevator at Lydian? Yes, Lydian is equipped with elevators for easy access to all floors, making the property accessible and convenient for residents and guests. Amenities What amenities are available at Lydian? Yes, Lydian is a pet-friendly community, welcoming both cats and dogs. Breed and size restrictions may apply. What are the pet fees, rules, and restrictions?
