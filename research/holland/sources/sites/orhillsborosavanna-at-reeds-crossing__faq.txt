FAQ | Savanna at Reeds Crossing Floor Plans Amenities Gallery Neighborhood RETAIL Contact Residents Schedule A Tour Apply now Savanna at Reeds Crossing FAQ Location and Neighborhood Where is Savanna at Reeds Crossing located? Savanna at Reeds Crossing is located in the award-winning Reeds Crossing master-planned community in Hillsboro, Oregon. The community offers convenient access to Tualatin Valley Highway, OR-8, and major employment centers throughout Hillsboro, Beaverton, and the greater Portland metro area. What grocery stores are nearby? Grocery shopping is exceptionally convenient, with Market of Choice located at Reed's Crossing Town Center less than 1.0 mile from the community. Additional nearby options include Safeway (2.3 miles), Fred Meyer (2.8 miles), WinCo Foods (4.0 miles), and Costco Wholesale (4.5 miles), giving residents access to everything from everyday essentials to specialty foods. What shopping and dining options are close to the property? Reed's Crossing Town Center, located less than 1.0 mile from Savanna, features Market of Choice, Jersey Mike's Subs, QDOBA Mexican Eats, Spitz Mediterranean Street Food, Nikki Sushi & Grill, Khao Yai Thai Kitchen, and Insomnia Coffee Company. Additional shopping and dining can be found at Streets of Tanasbourne (6.8 miles), Tanasbourne Town Center (6.5 miles), and downtown Hillsboro (4.2 miles). Are there parks or outdoor recreation areas nearby? Yes, residents enjoy direct access to the extensive network of parks, walking paths, and green spaces throughout Reed's Crossing. Nearby recreation options include Tamarack Park (0.5 miles), Noble Woods Park (2.5 miles), and Rood Bridge Park (3.8 miles). The neighborhood's pedestrian-friendly design makes it easy to enjoy outdoor activities close to home. What major employers are near Savanna at Reeds Crossing? Savanna is well positioned near the Silicon Forest employment corridor. Major employers include Intel Ronler Acres Campus (4.5 miles), Intel Jones Farm Campus (5.8 miles), Genentech (4.2 miles), Applied Materials (5.5 miles), and Nike World Headquarters (10.5 miles). This location is especially attractive for technology, healthcare, and manufacturing professionals. Property & Community What amenities does the community offer? Residents enjoy amenities such as a resort-style pool, fitness center, resident lounge, co-working spaces, and outdoor gathering areas. The community is designed to encourage both relaxation and connection while providing modern conveniences for everyday living. Is the community pet-friendly? Yes, Savanna at Reeds Crossing is a pet-friendly community that welcomes cats and dogs. Nearby parks, sidewalks, trails, and green spaces provide excellent opportunities for daily walks and outdoor exercise. We do have breed restrictions, so please reach out to our team with any questions. Is parking available? The community offers dedicated resident parking. Parking availability and fees may vary. We recommend reaching out to our leasing team to learn more. Apartment Features What types of floor plans are available? Savanna at Reeds Crossing offers a variety of studio, one-bedroom, two-bedroom, three-bedroom, and townhome style apartment homes. Multiple layouts allow residents to choose a home that best fits their lifestyle and space requirements. What features are included in the apartments? Apartment homes feature modern kitchens with stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, in-home washers and dryers, and spacious closets. Open-concept floor plans create comfortable spaces for both entertaining and everyday living. Do the apartments include air conditioning? Yes, apartment homes include air conditioning to help residents stay comfortable throughout the year. Additional energy-efficient features contribute to a convenient and enjoyable living experience. Are there private outdoor spaces available? Many apartment homes include private patios or balconies that provide additional living space and opportunities to enjoy the surrounding neighborhood atmosphere. Availability varies by floor plan. Leasing & Policies What lease terms are available? Our community typically offers a variety of lease term options based on current availability. Prospective residents can contact the leasing office for the most current lease term and pricing information. Are utilities included in the rent? Utilities are generally billed separately from monthly rent, although some services may be allocated through the community. Residents should review their lease agreement for specific utility responsibilities. How do I apply for an apartment? Prospective residents can submit an application online through our property website or work directly with the leasing office. The application process generally includes credit, income, identity, and rental history verification.
