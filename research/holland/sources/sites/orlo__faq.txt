Orlo & Alba is located at 1200 Rickabaugh Way in Santa Clara, at the crossroads of Santa Clara and San Jose. The community offers convenient access to Silicon Valley employers, shopping, dining and entertainment. What is the neighborhood like? Gateway Crossing is a newer, urban-style community with contemporary residences and a mix of convenient services nearby. Residents benefit from access to employment centers, retail destinations and recreational facilities, while also enjoying the community’s resort-inspired amenities. The property’s surrounding area also provides cafés, fast-casual restaurants and full-service dining. Apartments & Features What apartment floor plans are available? Orlo & Alba offer studio, one-, two- and three-bedroom homes with one or two bathrooms. Current floor plans & square footages vary based on availability. What features are included in the apartments? Homes feature a choice of warm or cool color schemes, solid-surface countertops, stainless-steel appliances, tile backsplashes and vinyl wood-look plank flooring. Residences also include efficient heating and cooling units and in-home washers and dryers. Do apartments have in-home laundry? Yes. In-home washers and dryers are included, providing private laundry convenience. Exact appliance placement and configuration vary by floor plan, so confirm details for the selected home. Do apartments have balconies or patios? Patios and balconies are available in select homes. Availability depends on the specific floor plan and apartment location, so confirm this feature before applying. Community Amenities What amenities are available at Orlo & Alba? Residents have access to resort-style pools, fitness studios, grilling areas, fire pits, game lounges and spaces designed for remote work. The communities also offer resident lounges, coworking areas, package services, gated entry and bicycle-storage options. Are there swimming pools? Yes. The property offers resort-style pool areas, including cabanas, lawn areas, outdoor grilling and fire pits. Does the property have a fitness center? Yes. The fitness facilities include Freemotion equipment and private training studios. The spaces are designed to support both everyday workouts and more focused strength or conditioning routines. Parking assignments, vehicle restrictions and monthly fees should be confirmed with the leasing team. Is electric vehicle charging available? Cats and dogs are welcome, subject to current breed restrictions, pet limits, deposits and monthly charges.
