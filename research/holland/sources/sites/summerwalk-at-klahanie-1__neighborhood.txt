Neighborhood | Summerwalk at Klahanie Floor Plans Site Map Residences Gallery & Virtual Tours Amenities Faq Contact Us Schedule a tour Apply Now NEIGHBORHOOD In the historic town of Issaquah there is so much more than what meets the eye. With quaint historic landmarks such as the Fish Hatchery in the middle of town, Salmon Days Conservation Center, and the Pickering Barn, now a very popular wedding venue. Shop at the local farmers market, visit the huge indoor aquatic center, hop in the Issaquah River, and float downstream or go skiing at the Snoqualmie Pass. Along with several small mountains that can be explored through the Issaquah Alps Trails Club, Issaquah is near restaurants, shopping centers and many other various conveniences. Whether your goal is a historic educational adventure, a traditional Pacific Northwest outdoor experience, or simply a charming lifestyle, you can find it all here at Summerwalk at Klahanie .
