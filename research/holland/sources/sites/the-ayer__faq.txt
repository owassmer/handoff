FAQ | The Ayer Home Floor Plans Penthouse Amenities Gallery Neighborhood Residents Contact Us Schedule A Tour Apply Now The Ayer FAQ Location & Neighborhood Where is The Ayer located? The Ayer is located at 2010 Terry Avenue in Seattle's Denny Triangle neighborhood, adjacent to South Lake Union and Downtown Seattle. Residents enjoy a highly walkable location surrounded by major employers, dining destinations, entertainment venues, and some of the city's most iconic landmarks. What grocery stores are nearby? Residents have several convenient grocery options nearby, including Whole Foods Market (0.3 miles), Amazon Fresh (0.4 miles), Pike & Western Market (0.8 miles), and Trader Joe's (1.0 mile). QFC (0.7 miles) and Metropolitan Market (1.3 miles) provide additional options for everyday groceries and specialty products. What shopping and dining options are close to the property? The Ayer is surrounded by some of Seattle's most popular dining and retail destinations. Residents can explore Pacific Place (0.6 miles), Westlake Center (0.7 miles), Pike Place Market (1.0 mile), and the South Lake Union neighborhood (0.5 miles), which features a wide variety of restaurants, coffee shops, boutiques, and entertainment options. Are there parks or outdoor recreation areas nearby? Yes, residents enjoy easy access to Denny Park (0.3 miles), Olympic Sculpture Park (1.0 mile), Lake Union Park (0.8 miles), and Waterfront Park (1.4 miles). These destinations offer walking paths, green space, waterfront views, and opportunities for outdoor recreation throughout the year. What major employers are near The Ayer? The Ayer is ideally located near many of Seattle's largest employers, including Amazon's headquarters campus (0.4 miles), Google Seattle (0.8 miles), Meta Seattle (0.8 miles), and Fred Hutch Cancer Center (1.2 miles). Residents also enjoy convenient access to employment centers throughout Downtown Seattle and South Lake Union. Property & Community What amenities does the community offer? Residents enjoy rooftop lounges, private work pods, conference rooms, fitness facilities, entertainment spaces, and beautifully designed resident gathering areas. The community was designed to support both productivity and relaxation Is the community pet-friendly? Yes, The Ayer is a pet-friendly community that welcomes cats and dogs. Residents have access to pet-friendly amenities, including a pet play area and pet washing station, as well as nearby parks for daily walks and exercise. Is parking available? Yes, resident parking is available at The Ayer. The community also offers EV charging stations, providing additional convenience for electric vehicle owners. What views can residents expect? Many homes offer sweeping views of the Seattle skyline, Puget Sound, Mount Rainier, Lake Union, and the Space Needle. All of our residences feature floor-to-ceiling windows designed to maximize these iconic views, while our premium and penthouse homes feature extended ceiling heights to further enhance their views. Apartment Features What floor plans are available at The Ayer? The Ayer offers studio, urban one-bedroom, one-bedroom, two-bedroom, and three-bedroom apartment homes, along with an exclusive penthouse collection. A variety of layouts are available to accommodate different lifestyles and space needs. What features are included in the apartments? Apartment homes feature quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, gas cooktops, keyless entry, large closets, in-home Whirlpool washers and dryers, soft-close cabinetry with undercabinet lighting, and designer finishes inspired by the Pacific Northwest. Many homes also include kitchen islands and expansive windows. Our premium and penthouse homes also include integrated wine fridges. Penthouse homes feature upgraded Bosch and Thermador appliances. Do the apartments include smart-home features? Yes, our homes feature smart-home technology, including Nest thermostats, keyless entry systems, motorized blinds, and modern lighting controls. These features are designed to enhance comfort, convenience, and energy efficiency. Leasing & Policies What lease terms are available? The Ayer offers a variety of lease term options depending on availability and market conditions. Prospective residents should contact the leasing office for the most current lease offerings and pricing. Are utilities included in the rent? Utilities are generally billed separately from rent, although specific utility responsibilities may vary by lease agreement. Residents should review leasing documents for complete details. How do I apply for an apartment? Prospective residents can apply online through The Ayer's website or work directly with our leasing team. Applications generally include income verification, credit screening, identity verification, and rental history review.
