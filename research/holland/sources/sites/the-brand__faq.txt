FAQ | The Brand The Brand FAQ Location & Neighborhood Where is The Brand located? The Brand is located in the heart of Glendale, California, within the vibrant Brand Boulevard district. The community is steps from The Americana at Brand (0.2 miles), Glendale Galleria (0.3 miles), and Whole Foods Market (0.4 miles). Residents also benefit from easy access to Griffith Park (3.5 miles), one of the largest urban parks in the country. What grocery stores are nearby? Residents have convenient access to several grocery stores, including Whole Foods Market (0.4 miles), Ralphs (0.5 miles), and Trader Joe’s (0.8 miles). Vons is also located approximately 1.6 miles away, providing additional grocery options. These locations make everyday shopping quick and easily accessible. What shopping and dining options are close to the community? The Brand is ideally located next to premier shopping and dining destinations like The Americana at Brand (0.2 miles) and Glendale Galleria (0.3 miles), both featuring retailers such as Apple, Nordstrom, and Target. Additional options are available along Brand Boulevard (within 0.5 miles), offering a wide range of restaurants, cafes, and entertainment. This location provides a highly walkable lifestyle with abundant options. Are there parks or outdoor recreation areas nearby? Yes, residents can enjoy nearby outdoor spaces such as Verdugo Park (1.2 miles), Griffith Park (3.5 miles), and Deukmejian Wilderness Park (5.8 miles). Fremont Park is also just 0.6 miles away and features open green space and recreational amenities. These parks provide a variety of options for outdoor activities and relaxation. How is public transportation in the area? Public transit is easily accessible with several Metro bus lines within 0.2 miles along Brand Boulevard. The Glendale Transportation Center is about 1.5 miles away, offering Metrolink and Amtrak service. For regional travel, the Hollywood Burbank Airport is approximately 7.5 miles away, making commuting and travel convenient. What major employers are near The Brand? The community is located near several major employers, including Disney Grand Central Creative Campus (2.5 miles), DreamWorks Animation (2.0 miles), and Glendale Memorial Hospital (1.3 miles). Warner Bros. Studios in Burbank is approximately 4.8 miles away, and Providence Saint Joseph Medical Center is about 5.5 miles away. This makes the location attractive for professionals in entertainment, healthcare, and corporate sectors. Apartment Features & Amenities What types of apartments are available? The Brand offers a variety of studio, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom apartment homes, as well as townhome homes. These layouts are thoughtfully designed to meet the needs of different lifestyles, from individuals to households. What interior features can residents expect? Apartment homes typically feature modern kitchens with stainless steel appliances, granite or quartz countertops, and custom cabinetry. Residents can also enjoy in-unit washer and dryer, spacious closets, and private patios or balconies in select homes. Many units offer large windows with city or courtyard views. What community amenities are offered? Residents have access to premium amenities such as a resort-style pool, fully equipped fitness center, and resident clubhouse. Additional features may include outdoor lounges, barbecue areas, and controlled-access entry. These amenities are designed to support comfort, convenience, and an active lifestyle. Leasing & Policies Is The Brand pet-friendly? Yes, The Brand is a pet-friendly community that welcomes both cats and dogs. Breed restrictions and additional deposits may apply; prospective residents should contact the leasing office for specific details. Nearby parks like Fremont Park (0.6 miles) provide convenient outdoor space for pets. What lease terms are available? The community typically offers flexible lease terms ranging from 12 to 15 months. Availability may vary, allowing residents to choose an option that best fits their needs. How do I apply for an apartment? Prospective residents can apply online through The Brand’s website or visit the leasing office for assistance. The application process includes standard credit and background checks. Leasing professionals are available to guide applicants through each step. Are utilities included in the rent? Utilities are generally not included in the base rent and are billed separately. Residents are responsible for electricity, water, and internet services, though some bundled service arrangements may be offered. Resident Services Is parking available? Yes, residents have access to controlled-access parking within the community. Assigned spaces and garage parking options are typically available for added convenience. Is there on-site maintenance? Yes, The Brand provides professional on-site maintenance services. Emergency maintenance is available 24/7 to address urgent issues and ensure resident comfort. Is there on-site management? The community features a dedicated on-site management team available during business hours. They assist with leasing, resident services, and overall community support.
