The Daphne is located at 355 S Ogden Drive, Los Angeles, CA 90036 in the heart of the Fairfax District. Situated directly across from The Grove and The Original Farmers Market, the building places you steps from some of Los Angeles's most celebrated shopping, dining, and cultural destinations. Cedars-Sinai Medical Center, Television City, West Hollywood, Beverly Hills, and Museum Row are all just minutes away. What is the neighborhood like around The Daphne? The Fairfax District is one of Los Angeles's most walkable neighborhoods, and The Daphne's walk score of 98 reflects it. Shopping, dining, wellness, green space, and cultural destinations are all within reach without ever getting into your car. Directly across the street sits The Grove and The Original Farmers Market. Steps away 3rd Street's restaurant row is home to neighborhood favorites like Oste (0.5 miles) and Joan's on Third (0.8 miles). Heated Room's infrared Pilates studio is around the corner (0.6 miles), Pan Pacific Park (0.4 miles), LACMA (0.8 miles), and both West Hollywood and Beverly Hills are approximately 2 miles away. Whole Foods Market is located at The Daphne, Erewhon Market is only 0.5 miles away, Trader Joe's is 0.2 miles away, and Target is approximately 1.7 miles away. These options make everyday shopping simple and convenient. What shopping and dining destinations are near The Daphne? Some of Los Angeles' most popular shopping and dining destinations are located nearby. Beverly Center is about 1 mile away, Melrose Place shopping is roughly 1.8 miles away, and Rodeo Drive and West Hollywood shopping districts are within 3.0 miles. What parks and outdoor recreation areas are close to The Daphne? Outdoor recreation is easily accessible from The Daphne. Pan Pacific Park is approximately 0.4 miles away, Runyon Canyon is roughly 3.2 miles away, La Brea Tar Pits Park is approximately 0.9 miles away, La Cienega Tennis Center is 1.8 miles away, and Beverly Gardens Park is within 3 miles. Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) is 11.5 miles away. Community, Amenities & Lifestyle What type of apartments are available at The Daphne? The Daphne offers studio, one-bedroom, two-bedroom, and three-bedroom luxury apartment homes. Residents can choose from thoughtfully designed layouts featuring light and dark designer finishes that accommodate a variety of lifestyles. Studio apartments are 505 square feet.
