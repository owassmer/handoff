Little Italy Steps from The Torrey, Little Italy is one of San Diego’s most walkable and beloved neighborhoods. Just 0.3 miles away, the Little Italy Mercato, San Diego’s largest certified farmers market, fills six city blocks every Wednesday and Saturday with Californian farmers, artisan food makers, and local purveyors. Acclaimed dining destinations including Herb & Wood and Kettner Exchange are just minutes from your door. Waterfront & Beyond The Embarcadero waterfront is 0.7 miles away, offering scenic harbor walks, outdoor recreation, and waterfront dining along San Diego Bay. The Gaslamp Quarter, just 1.2 miles from The Torrey, brings nightlife, entertainment, and acclaimed dining destinations including Lionfish, Lumi, and Nobu within easy reach. Arts, Entertainment and Sports The Rady Shell at Jacobs Park, San Diego’s premier outdoor concert venue, sits along the waterfront just minutes away. Petco Park, home of the San Diego Padres, is less than a mile from The Torrey, putting Major League Baseball and some of downtown’s best live event energy right at your doorstep. Getting Around Commuting from The Torrey is effortless. I-5 and I-163 are immediately accessible, Santa Fe Depot is less than 0.5 miles away, and San Diego International Airport is just 2 miles from your front door. Major Employers The Torrey is ideally positioned near San Diego’ s leading employers, including UC San Diego Health, Scripps Health, Sharp HealthCare, Qualcomm, and Navy Region Southwest, making it a natural home for professionals seeking a connected, walkable lifestyle in the heart of the city.
