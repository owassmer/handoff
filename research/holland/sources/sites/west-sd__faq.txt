FAQ | West SD West SD FAQ Location & Neighborhood Where is West SD located? West SD is located at 1011 Union Street in Downtown San Diego, placing residents in the center of one of the city's most vibrant and walkable neighborhoods. The community is just 0.4 miles from Little Italy, 0.7 miles from the Embarcadero, 0.8 miles from Waterfront Park, and 0.4 miles from the Gaslamp Quarter, providing convenient access to dining, entertainment, and waterfront destinations What grocery stores are nearby? A: Residents have access to several grocery options, including Ralphs (0.3 miles), Grocery Outlet (1.0 mile), Smart & Final (1.3 miles), and Albertsons (1.0 miles). A Whole Foods Market is also under construction (0.1 miles) and slated to open in 2026. What shopping and dining options are close to the property? West SD is surrounded by some of San Diego's most popular restaurants, cafes, and retail destinations. Little Italy (0.4 miles) offers dozens of local dining options, while Seaport Village (1.2 miles), and the Gaslamp Quarter (0.4 miles), provide additional shopping, dining, and entertainment opportunities. Are there parks or outdoor recreation areas nearby? Yes, residents can enjoy Waterfront Park (0.8 miles), Ruocco Park (0.5 miles), Embarcadero Marina Park North (0.8 miles), and Balboa Park (1.7 miles). These destinations provide access to green space, walking paths, bay views, museums, and outdoor recreation throughout the year. What major employers are near West SD? The community is conveniently located near many of Downtown San Diego's leading employers, including San Diego County administrative offices (0.5 miles), Navy Region Southwest (1.5 miles), UC San Diego Health Hillcrest Medical Center (3.5 miles), and Qualcomm's headquarters (14.0 miles). Residents also benefit from proximity to numerous finance, legal, technology, and healthcare employers throughout Downtown San Diego. Property & Community What amenities does the community offer? Residents enjoy a rooftop pool and spa with sweeping bay and skyline views, a state-of-the-art fitness center, outdoor fitness terrace, resident lounges, co-working spaces, and multiple social gathering areas. The community is designed to support wellness, productivity, and entertainment. Is the community pet-friendly? Yes, West SD is a pet-friendly community that welcomes cats and dogs. Residents have access to a dog park, pet washing facilities, and nearby waterfront walking areas that make pet ownership convenient and enjoyable. Is parking available? Yes, the community offers resident parking and EV charging capabilities. Additional details regarding parking availability, assigned spaces, and associated fees can be obtained from the leasing office. What views can residents expect? Many homes offer impressive views of the San Diego skyline, San Diego Bay, and the Pacific Ocean. Select penthouse residences feature elevated views and premium luxury finishes. Apartment Features What floor plans are available at West SD? West SD offers studio, one-bedroom, two-bedroom, and three-bedroom apartment homes, along with select penthouse residences. A variety of layouts are available to accommodate different lifestyle needs. What features are included in the apartments? Homes feature designer finishes, stainless steel appliances, smart thermostats, in-home Samsung washers and dryers, keyless entry, vinyl plank flooring, and contemporary kitchens. Select homes also offer balconies, deep soaking tubs, and enhanced city or waterfront views. Do the apartments include smart-home features? Yes, residences include modern conveniences such as Ecobee smart thermostats and keyless entry systems. These features provide comfort, efficiency, and ease of use for residents. Leasing & Policies What lease terms are available? West SD typically offers a variety of lease term options based on availability and market conditions. Prospective residents should contact the leasing office for current leasing promotions, pricing, and term options. Are utilities included in the rent? Utilities are generally billed separately from monthly rent, although specific utility responsibilities may vary by lease agreement. Residents should review lease documents for complete details. How do I apply for an apartment? Prospective residents can apply online through our website or work directly with the leasing team. The application process generally includes credit, income, identity, and rental history verification.
