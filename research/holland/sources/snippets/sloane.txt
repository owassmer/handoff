The Elephant Car Wash, which had operated at the site since 1951, closed and was demolished years prior in late 2020. The business's two pink, elephant-shaped neon signs were salvaged and rehoused. Weber Thompson is both the architect and landscape architect for the new high-rise. Cary Kopczynski & Company is the structural engineer. The Sloane site was home to the Elephant Car Wash from the early 1950s until 2020. Sloane is sheathed in a hybrid facade system (called Talon Wall) that combines features of both curtain wall and window wall systems. According to the architect, this system creates “a smoother, more streamlined facade” than a traditional curtain wall. The tower's structural design features a performance-based seismic system with a high strength concrete shear wall core and steel fiber reinforced coupling beams, paired with 8-inch-thick post-tensioned slabs.
