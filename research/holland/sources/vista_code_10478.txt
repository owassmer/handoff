    		Section 16.12.010     Adoption
      			A.       Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, the Uniform Housing Code, 1994
    		Edition, is hereby adopted by reference and incorporated herein as if fully set forth, except for the
    		additions and amendments of this chapter.  In the event of any conflict or ambiguity between
    		any provision contained in the Uniform Housing Code and any amendment or addition thereto
    		contained in this chapter, the amendment or addition set forth in this chapter shall control.
    		(Prior Code 6-2; Ord. 87-39, Amended 12/14/87; Ord. No. 91-1, Amended, 02/12/91; Ord. No. 92-13,
    		Amended, 07/28/92; Ord. No. 95-15, Amended, 11/28/95)
    		Section 16.12.020     Definitions
      			Whenever any of the following names or terms are used in the Uniform Housing Code,
    		such name or term shall be deemed and construed to have the meaning ascribed to it in this
    		section as follows:
       			"Building  Department"  means the  Community  Development  Department,  Building
    		Division, of the City.
       			"Building Official" means the Building Official of the City.
    		(Prior Code 6-2; Ord. 87-39,Amended 12/14/87; Ord. No. 91-1, Amended, 02/12/91)
    		Section 16.12.030     Section 203 Amended
       			Section 203 of the Uniform Housing Code is amended to read as follows:
       			Section 203   General. In order to hear and decide appeals of orders, decisions and
    		determinations made by the Building Official relative to the application and interpretations of the
    		technical code, there shall be and is hereby established a housing advisory and appeals board.
    		The housing advisory and appeals board shall be the Board of Appeals established under the
    		Section 204 of the Uniform Administrative Code.  Appeals to the board shall be processed in
    		accordance with the provisions contained in Section 1201 of this code.
    		(Prior Code 6-2.1; Ord. 87-39, Amended 12/14/87; Ord. No. 91-1, Amended, 02/12/91; Ord. No. 95-15,
    		Amended, 11/28/95)
