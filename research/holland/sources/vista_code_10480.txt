    		Section 16.16.010     Adoption
      			A.       Except  as  otherwise  provided  in  this  chapter,  the  Uniform  Code  for  the
    		Abatement of Dangerous Buildings,  1994 Edition, is adopted by reference and incorporated
    		herein as if fully set forth, except for the additions and amendments of this chapter.
       			B.       In the event of any conflict or ambiguity between any provision contained in the
    		Uniform  Code for the Abatement of Dangerous  Buildings and any amendment or addition
    		thereto contained in this chapter, the amendment or addition in this chapter shall control.
    		(Prior Code 6-3; Ord. No. 87-39, Amended 12/14/87; Ord. No. 91-1, Amended, 02/12/91; Ord. No. 92-13,
    		Amended, 07/28/92; Ord. No. 95-15, Amended, 11/28/95)
    		Section 16.16.020     Definitions
      			Whenever any of the following names or terms are used in the Uniform Code for the
    		Abatement of Dangerous Buildings, such name or term shall be deemed and construed to have
    		the meaning ascribed to it in this section as follows:
       			"Building  Department"  means the  Community  Development  Department,  Building
    		Division, of the City.
    		(Prior Code 6-3; Ord. No. 87-39, Amended 12/14/87; Ord. No. 91-1, Amended, 02/12/91)
    		Section 16.16.030     Section 205 Amended
       			Section 205 of the Uniform Code for the Abatement of Dangerous Buildings is amended
    		to read as follows:
       			Section 205   In order to provide for reasonable interpretation of the provisions of this
    		code and to hear appeals provided  for hereunder,  there is  hereby established a  Board  of
    		Appeals.  The Board of Appeals shall be the Board of Appeals established under Section 204 of
    		the Uniform Building Code.   Appeals to the board shall be processed in accordance with the
    		provisions contained in Section 501 of this code.
    		(Prior Code 6-3.1; Ord. No. 87-39, Amended 12/14/87; Ord. No. 91-1, Amended, 02/12/91; Ord. No. 95-
    		15, Amended, 11/28/95)

       			Section 206   It shall be unlawful for any person, firm, or corporation to erect, construct,
    		enlarge, equip, use, occupy or maintain any building or structure which was unlawful at the time
    		it was constructed and which would be unlawful under the building code if constructed after the
    		effective date of such code, and shall constitute a continuing violation of such code.   Any
    		person, firm or corporation violating any provision of the building code shall be deemed guilty of
    		a misdemeanor and such persons shall be deemed guilty of a separate offense for each and
    		every day or portion thereof during which any violation of any of the provisions of the building
    		code is committed, continued or permitted, and upon conviction of any such violations, such
    		person shall be punished by a fine of not more than $1,000 or by imprisonment for not more than
    		six months, or both such fine and imprisonment.
    		(Ord. No. 91-1, Enacted, 02/12/91)
