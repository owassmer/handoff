 						County Regulations for Condemnation of Buildings
    		Sections:
    		16.44.010       Adoption
    		Section 16.44.010     Adoption
      			There is adopted by the City Council for the purpose of regulating condemnation of
    		buildings, that certain code known as the San Diego County Code of Regulatory Ordinances,
    		Chapter 1 of Division 9 of Title 6 relating to condemnation of buildings.  One copy of the County
    		Code is on file in the Office of the City Clerk and is adopted and incorporated as though fully set
    		out at length in this section, and from the date on which the ordinance codified in this section
    		takes effect the provisions thereof are controlling within the limits of the city.
    		(Prior code 14-3; Ord. No. 90-16 Section 9)
    										16.44 (l)       				(Vista 4/11/11)
