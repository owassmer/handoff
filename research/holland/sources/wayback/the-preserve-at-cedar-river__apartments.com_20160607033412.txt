Suburban Cascade recently became part of the city of Renton, while Fairwood, a mix of residential subdivisions and rural areas, remains part of unincorporated King County. For families and established workers of the industrial and commercial heavy south end of Lake Washington, the neighborhood presents a pleasant combination of convenient access to the urban areas to the west and the rural areas to the east. Cascade-Fairview covers an area about 6 miles southeast of central Renton, 8 miles northeast of central Kent and 18 miles southeast of downtown Seattle. Downtown Bellevue lies 15 miles north.
