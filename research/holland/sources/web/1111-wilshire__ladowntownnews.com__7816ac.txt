Updated: September 30, 2026 @ 9:58 am Full Forecast Home News Arts & Culture Sports Business Opinion Obituaries Los Angeles Obituaries Recommended Classifieds Downtown Development 1111 Wilshire - Residential by Richard Guzmán, Jon Regardie and Ryan Vaillancourt May 21, 2012 May 21, 2012 Facebook Twitter WhatsApp SMS Email Facebook Twitter WhatsApp SMS Email Print Copy article link Save DOWNTOWN LOS ANGELES - Construction is on schedule for a 2013 opening for a 210-unit City West apartment complex from Vancouver, Wash.-based Holland Partner Group, according to a project representative. A groundbreaking took place last summer for the development at 1111 Wilshire Blvd. on the site of a former parking lot. The $60 million project will create a seven-story building with more than 7,750 square feet of retail space with studio to three-bedroom units. There will also be an underground parking garage that will hold 302 cars. The project is near Glo, Holland Partner Group’s first apartment complex in the area. ©Los Angeles Downtown News.
