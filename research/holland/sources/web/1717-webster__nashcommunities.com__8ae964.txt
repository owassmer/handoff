1717 Webster Home About Projects Office 1717 Webster Project description 1717 Webster is a 27-story apartment tower consisting of 247 units, 5,000 square feet of office space, and 1,500 square feet of retail space in Oakland’s Uptown district. The building is surrounded by Uptown’s trendy shops and restaurants. In addition to Uptown’s employment base, which includes Kaiser Permanente and Clorox, among others, the nearby BART (Bay Area Rapid Transit) station provides quick access to downtown San Francisco. The project opened for residents in March of 2021.
