Old Website (2000-2009) Previous Next View Larger Image Speer Roundup JUN 2020 The two major developments in the Speer neighborhood, along the Speer Boulevard corridor, are coming along nicely. Both projects are projected to be complete this year and will provide an additional 413 homes to this corner of the neighborhood. Modera West Wash Park . This eight-story building is shaping up with its two sharp points and a curved facade along Speer Boulevard. We are now able to see some of the facade elements come into play. Along Speer, a light brick facade is starting to get installed with wood paneling starting to go up on the Grant side of the building. Bromwell Apartments . Previously reported as 355 Logan, the Bromwell Apartments are nearing completion. Most of the facade elements are now complete with work now underway for the streetscape. This 171-home project is expected to begin leasing this summer. There are no other development plans filed with the city for the Speer neighborhood at this time but keep in mind, there are only just a few more vacant lots in this neighborhood.
