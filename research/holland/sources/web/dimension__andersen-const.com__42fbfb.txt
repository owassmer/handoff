Dimension by Alta Dimension by Alta Located on a quarter block in Seattle's popular Belltown Neighborhood, this LEED Silver 27-story tower adds 298 market rate apartment units to the growing area. The 343,000 SF mixed use project is supported by 200 parking stalls within six levels of underground parking and 3,800 SF of ground floor retail. Apartments are made up of studios, 1-bedrooms and 2-bedrooms. Resident amenities include a rooftop observation deck and cabana offering panoramic views from Elliot Bay to Mt. Rainier, a dog run, pea patch, green house and fitness room.
