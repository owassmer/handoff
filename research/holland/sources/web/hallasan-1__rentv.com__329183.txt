Password Forgot Password? --> --> MULTIFAMILY NEWS Printer-friendly Version Email an Associate New Res Tower in L.A.s Koreatown Opens to the Public 9/07/23 Hallasan, a new high-rise apartment tower in the heart of Koreatown, has opened to the public. The project, located at 696 S. New Hampshire Ave, was developed by Holland Partner Group. Hallasan stands as a stunning architectural masterpiece that has transformed a former surface parking lot into a 36-story vertical oasis. With its contemporary design, thoughtful amenities, and prime location, Hallasan offers a remarkable living experience to its residents. The tower features 375 thoughtfully designed residences, ranging from studio to three-bedroom layouts, catering to a diverse array of lifestyles. Hallasan's residences have been meticulously crafted to provide comfort and luxury, boasting exceptional views and abundant natural light. Monthly rents range from $2,491 for a stylish 468 sf studio unit to $9,884 for a lavish three-bedroom penthouse. MVE + Partners, renowned for their innovative architectural designs, have brought Hallasan to life. The tower's upper levels are adorned with sleek glass facades, while a transparent screen envelops the parking podium, seamlessly blending aesthetics and functionality. The tower also boasts amenity decks on both its podium and rooftop levels, offering residents the ultimate spaces for relaxation and socializing.
