Residents can enjoy a multitude of amenities, both indoor and out. On the first floor, a brew-cade includes a bar, table games, and ample seating, with an adjacent ‘session and sound’ room for music enthusiasts. The Lydian is clad in a variety of materials such as metal panels, fireclay wall tile, brick veneer, and stucco.
