Lydian Home About Projects Office Lydian Project description Lydian is a seven-story mixed-use building with 261 apartments and 15,500 square feet of street-front retail space just south of Oakland’s Uptown district. The site is two blocks west of Lake Merritt and within walking distance to Uptown’s shops, museums, restaurants, and bars.
