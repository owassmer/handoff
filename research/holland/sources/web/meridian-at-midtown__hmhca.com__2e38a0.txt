Composed of 218 luxury apartment units and 15,000 square feet of retail space, the project will feature studios, one and two-bedroom flats and amenities such as a linear park, pool, cardio and yoga room, coffee bar and more. Meridian at Midtown boasts an industrial chic interior with rustic finishes, clean modern lines and metallic accents that translates into an eye-catching space that is both sleek and functional. Bursts of bright color and geometric patterns breathe life into the sophisticated design, while nature makes itself at home, with environmentally friendly elements at every turn.
