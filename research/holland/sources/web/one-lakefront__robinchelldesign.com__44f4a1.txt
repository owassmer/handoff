One Lakefront | Robin Chell Design Skip to main content Toggle navigation Main navigation Home About Company Team Media Portfolio Multi-family High-rise Commercial Residential Contact One Lakefront --> ABOUT THE PROJECT --> One Lakefront was designed as the ultimate in modern lakeside luxury. The 326-unit apartment complex, developed by Holland Partners includes two mid-rise buildings that feature stunning views of Lake Union. The ground floor of the building features a multitude of amenities, including a concierge, conference room, fireside pavilion lounge, fitness and wellness center, and a bike and kayak storage center. The building also features two expansive outdoor terraces, complete with private cabanas, hammocks and outdoor kitchens. The heart of the project is the two-level Skylounge, which includes a nautically inspired modern dining room and exhibition kitchen. Adjacent to the Skylounge is "The Boardroom" -the ultimate sports lounge featuring a pool table and a spectacular bar with backlit onyx countertop and liquor lockers. Robin Chell Design provided full interior design services, including space planning, finishes, FF&E design, procurement and installation.
