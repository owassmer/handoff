These fluid light installations draw inspiration from the waves of the adjacent lake, evoking a sense of “modern lake-house” style. Featured in the pavilion lobby and in long strips above the elevator lobby, they are a great example of how Kaynemaile ® mesh can be used to incorporate texture, play with light, and bring hints of the great outdoors to your indoor spaces. Kaynemaile ® Architectural Mesh is seamless and lightweight, making it easy to install and perfect for designs like these.
