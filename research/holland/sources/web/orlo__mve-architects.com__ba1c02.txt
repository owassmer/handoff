Orlo Alba at Gateway Crossing MVE Partners MVE+Partners Open main menu About Portfolio Connect Open main menu About Portfolio Connect Search by Tags Affordable Commercial Hospitality Residential/Mixed Use Retail Student Search by Text Enter Search Orlo & Alba At Gateway Crossing Santa Clara, CA Gallery Product Type Multi-Family Residential Program 725 units 15,000 sf retail Client Holland Partner Group Services Architecture Typology Podium Orlo and Alba are the residential heart of Gateway Crossings, a master-planned community anchoring San Jose’s emerging Hi-Line Transit Village. Just a quarter mile from the Santa Clara Transit Center and future BART extension, these two distinct buildings bridge the city’s industrial past with its tech-driven future. Framing a central park, they foster community and walk-ability through lush courtyards, elevated decks, and pedestrian-oriented ground floors with retail and amenities. Orlo features brick cladding, wrought iron, and structural rhythm inspired by historic warehouses. Alba offers a modern contrast with clean lines, glass railings, and contemporary materials that reflect the neighboring Coleman High-line campus. With pools, co-working lounges, and fitness centers, both buildings promote vibrant urban living. Orlo and Alba aren’t just residences—they celebrate heritage while embracing forward-thinking design.
