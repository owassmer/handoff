Gateway Crossing Home About Projects Office Gateway Crossing Project description Gateway Crossing comprises the residential portion of the Coleman Highline mixed-use master-planned community in Santa Clara, CA. North America Sekisui House and Holland Partner Group have partnered to develop Phase 1 of Gateway Crossing, which includes two mid-rise apartment buildings -- Orlo & Alba -- with a combined 724 units and 8,100 square feet of retail space. The site sits adjacent to the Santa Clara Caltrain station and future Santa Clara BART station with connections to all major employment centers in Silicon Valley and is a five-minute drive from the San Jose International Airport.
