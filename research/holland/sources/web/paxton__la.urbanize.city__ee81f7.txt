The project, located at 18750 Delaware Street, stands six stories in height and includes 346 studio, one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments surrounding a parking garage. Aerial view of pool deck Steven J. Magner Photography According to a project website , available units range from 489 to 1,915 square feet in size, with rents ranging from $2,500 to $8,808 per month. Per city records , the approval of the project requires the provision of 480 parking stalls and 20 percent of the apartments as low-income affordable units. Paxton interior Steven J. Magner Photography In addition to housing, the four-acre complex incorporates amenities including a swimming pool, a fitness center, outdoor courtyards, a clubhouse, and co-working spaces. Holland Partner Group is also attached to upcoming projects at several sites in Los Angeles County.
