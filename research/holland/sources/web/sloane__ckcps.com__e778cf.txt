Sloane | Cary Kopczynski & Company, Inc. ☰ MENU Home Projects People Buildable Design Expertise Careers Awards+Press Contact Sloane Seattle, WA Multifamily 45-STORy 5 - below grade PARKING LEVELS concrete Sloane Project Size: 580,000 square feet Architect: Weber Thompson, Seattle, WA Developer: Holland Partner Group, Seattle, WA Contractor: Holland Construction, Seattle, WA Located in the Denny Triangle neighborhood of Seattle, Sloane, formerly known as 616 Battery, is a 45-story residential tower with five levels of subterranean parking. The project includes 442 living units, approximately 2,500 square feet of retail space, 262 parking stalls, and tenant amenities that offer commanding views of the Space Needle, Elliott Bay, and South Lake Union. The structure incorporates a performance based lateral-force-resisting system, combining shear walls with steel fiber-reinforced concrete (SFRC) coupling beams to resist seismic loads. Floor slabs are 8-inch-thick post-tensioned concrete, optimizing both efficiency and structural performance. The tower’s foundation consists of a mat that covers the site’s footprint and is designed to handle gravity and lateral loads while meeting settlement criteria.
