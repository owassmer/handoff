Curious Deal | S2E23 - by Moshe Crane - Curious Deal Curious Deal Subscribe Sign in Curious Deal | S2E23 Notable Buyers Series: Mesirow Financial Moshe Crane Mar 02, 2026 1 Share Curious Deal S2E23 | Notable Buyers Series: Mesirow Financial ACQUISITIONS 9 deals. 3,285 units. $906M in disclosed volume (8 of 9 deals disclosed). Average year built: 2012. Mesirow Financial is a Chicago-based financial services firm whose real estate arm has been quietly one of the more active acquirers in the multifamily space over the past year. Their acquisition activity in this batch spans Denver to Boston, Gilbert to Bothell — a geographically diverse set unified by a consistent thesis: institutional-grade, mid-2000s to mid-2010s vintage product in markets with strong demand fundamentals. Two Blackstone purchases, two acquisitions in the same Gilbert, AZ submarket, and a Boston-area deal at $520K/unit round out a set that signals conviction across both Sun Belt and coastal markets. The District at Hampden South | Denver, CO January 2025 276 units | Built 2007 Sale Price: $91,100,000 | $330,072/unit Seller: GID | Buyer: Mesirow Financial, Holland Partner Group Prior Sale: $60,850,000 (January 2008) Cap Rate: 5.15% Broker: JLL Notes: Up 50% from the 2008 purchase price over a 17-year hold. GID exits a long-hold Denver asset at a 5.15% cap; Mesirow and Holland Partner Group acquire jointly. A co-investment structure with an operator suggests Mesirow is bringing capital while Holland brings platform. Redstone at SanTan Village | Gilbert, AZ June 2025 382 units | Built 2013 Sale Price: $118,500,000 | $310,209/unit Seller: Blackstone & Olympus Property | Buyer: Mesirow Financial Prior Sale: $71,500,000 (August 2017) Notes: Up 66% from the 2017 purchase price. First of two Blackstone/Olympus exits to Mesirow in the Gilbert submarket. Blackstone acquired through the Preferred Apartment Communities portfolio; Mesirow steps in at a meaningful premium to the 2017 basis but a price that reflects today’s Phoenix-area fundamentals. Gramercy Square at Ayrsley | Charlotte, NC July 2025 358 units | Built 2009 Sale Price: $83,700,000 | $233,799/unit Seller: Simpson Property Group | Buyer: Mesirow Financial Prior Sale: $45,500,000 (May 2010) Notes: Up 84% from the 2010 purchase price. Simpson Property Group held for 15 years and exits at a number that validates Charlotte’s long-term rent growth story. Mesirow acquires 2009-vintage product in one of the Southeast’s most active multifamily markets. Vistara at SanTan Village | Gilbert, AZ August 2025 366 units | Built 2017 Sale Price: $115,250,000 | $314,891/unit Seller: Blackstone, Olympus Property | Buyer: Mesirow Financial Prior Sale: $73,200,000 (September 2017) Notes: Up 57% from the 2017 purchase price. Second Blackstone/Olympus exit to Mesirow in Gilbert, closed just two months after Redstone. Mesirow now owns two assets in the same submarket — a deliberate concentration bet on Gilbert’s continued growth as one of the Phoenix metro’s strongest performing submarkets. Villas at Beardslee | Bothell, WA September 2025 451 units | Built 2015 Sale Price: $160,281,470 | $355,391/unit Seller: Holland Partner Group | Buyer: Mesirow Financial Prior Sale: $120,700,000 (June 2017) Cap Rate: 4.6% Broker: CBRE Notes: Up 33% from the 2017 purchase price. The asset includes 34,000 SF of retail space. At a 4.6% cap Mesirow is paying for Bothell’s strong Seattle-area fundamentals. Brookhaven Apartments | Atlanta, GA October 2025 299 units | Built 2014 Sale Price: $87,000,000 Seller: Wafra, Inc | Buyer: Mesirow Financial Prior Sale: $85,700,000 (September 2015) Broker: CBRE Riachi at ONE21 | Plano, TX November 2025 450 units | Built 2006 Sale Price: Not Disclosed Seller: Banner Real Estate Group | Buyer: Mesirow Financial Prior Sale: $50,500,000 (September 2011) Watertown Mews | Watertown, MA December 2025 206 units | Built 2014 Sale Price: $107,300,000 | $520,874/unit Seller: PGIM | Buyer: Mesirow Financial Broker: CBRE Notes: No prior sale on record. The highest price per unit in this batch by a significant margin — $520K/unit for a 2014-vintage Watertown asset reflects the Boston metro’s exceptional supply constraints and rent fundamentals. PGIM exits; Mesirow makes a significant coastal bet at a price that requires strong conviction in Boston’s long-term story. The Aster | Cary, NC December 2025 497 units | Built 2017 Sale Price: $143,000,000 Seller: Kettler, Pacific Life Insurance Company | Buyer: Mesirow Financial Prior Sale: $119,000,000 (July 2019) Broker: Northmarq Notes: At $143M this is the largest single-asset acquisition in the batch. Mesirow closes the year with a significant North Carolina bet. DISPOSITIONS 2 deals. 700 units. $247M in total volume. Average year built: 2008. Mesirow’s disposition activity is lean but deliberate. Two clean exits in markets where they’ve captured meaningful appreciation, recycling capital back into the acquisition pipeline above. The Majestic Luxury Apartments | Vernon Hills, IL August 2025 294 units | Built 2004 Sale Price: $100,000,000 Seller: Mesirow Financial | Buyer: TLC Management Prior Sale: $87,100,000 (September 2018) Broker: CBRE Notes: Up 15% from the 2018 purchase price. Mesirow exits a suburban Chicago asset at a clean gain, recycling capital out of the Midwest into its active acquisition pipeline. TLC Management, a Chicago-area operator, steps in as buyer. The Enclave at Potomac Club Apartments | Woodbridge, VA November 2025 406 units | Built 2013 Sale Price: $147,000,000 Seller: Mesirow Financial | Buyer: UDR Prior Sale: $90,350,000 (January 2019) Broker: CBRE Notes: Up 63% from the 2019 purchase price. The standout exit in this batch — Mesirow nearly doubles its money on a Northern Virginia asset in roughly six years. UDR, one of the country’s largest apartment REITs, steps in as buyer. Selling to a REIT at that premium is a strong validation of both the asset quality and the Northern Virginia market’s long-term fundamentals. Takeaway Zoom out and Mesirow’s activity over the past year tells a clear story: they are deploying capital aggressively across a geographically diverse portfolio while selectively exiting older holds at strong gains. Nine acquisitions totaling over $900M in roughly twelve months is a significant pace for a firm that doesn’t always make the headline lists alongside Blackstone or Greystar. The Gilbert, AZ double-down is the most interesting pattern in the batch two assets in the same submarket acquired two months apart from the same seller. That’s not opportunistic deal flow, that’s a thesis.
