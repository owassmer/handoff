 Best-in-Class Trophy High-Rise

CBRE is pleased to present The Ivey on Boren (the “Property”), a best-in-class high-rise asset with top of the line amenities in the
heart of Seattle’s thriving South Lake Union neighborhood. Located just blocks from major offices of Google, Amazon, Meta,
Apple, Redfin, Zillow and Fred Hutch’s expansive Lake Union campus, the Property offers immediate access to the city’s largest
employers and is surrounded by the city’s best lifestyle amenities, top restaurants, and energetic nightlife.
Built in 2022, The Ivey on Boren includes an abundant array of amenities and oversized units averaging 848 SF and a 0.77
stalls/unit parking ratio. Interior unit finishes are elevated beyond the standard, including two spa-like modern finish schemes, air
conditioning, floor to ceiling windows, floating vanities in the bathroom with tile showers and flooring, built-in closet organization,
motorized shades and Z-wave smart light switches and USB power outlets. The 44-story Property offers 406 units which include
studio, urban-one, large one-bedroom, two-bedroom, three-bedroom, and penthouse apartment living. Residents have access
to numerous luxury amenities including a sun deck with lounging pool, hot tub, fire pits, grilling stations, dry sauna and plunge shower,
as well as, fitness, yoga studio and hiit studio with fitness instructor and wellness lounge. Additionally, the Property includes a game
lounge with billiards and expansive workstations.
South Lake Union is ideally located between outdoor recreation on Lake Union and the highest paying employers in Seattle allowing
for access to the rest of core Seattle while maintaining a relaxed urban neighborhood feeling. The combination of The Ivey on Boren’s
prominent location, high quality, and submarket leading amenities, establishes the Property as the market leader in South Lake Union
and capitalizes on exceptional demographic trends and submarket fundamentals. The location is unparalleled to any other asset in
the market with direct connectivity to transit, easy access to I-5 on ramp eliminating congested areas of SLU, opening of the new
convention center, entertainment, best restaurant options while maintaining the most desired and concentrated employment center
for high paying jobs.
