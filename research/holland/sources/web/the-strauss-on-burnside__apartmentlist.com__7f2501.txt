Property Description In the City of Bridges, we encourage you to be inspired by the man who not only gave Portland our beloved Burnside Bridge, but also the determination to dream. Joseph Strauss, the brilliant designer who contributed to several of Portland's iconic bridges, is the motivation behind our community name, and a figure in Portland's history that we have great pride in celebrating. Located in the heart of Portland's thriving Central Eastside, The Strauss on Burnside is literally surrounded by everything you could ask for - Micro-distilleries and incredible restaurants, proximity to the Portland Streetcar and a 10 minute walk to Downtown PDX, not to mention access to the miles of waterfront trails along the Willamette River. Everything Eastside is at your doorstep, at the foot of our celebrated Burnside Bridge. Come let us welcome your to dream apartment home, here at The Strauss on Burnside.
