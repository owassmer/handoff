Orchids & Onions The Torrey Orchid Private Architecture O&O Home 2026 Nominations Orchids & Onions Jury Evaluation Criteria 2025 Awardees All nominations Address 1200 Front St, San Diego, CA 92101 Owner / Developer Holland Partner Group Architect Carrier Johnson + Culture Contractor Holland Partner Group Civil Engineer Project Design Consultants Landscape Architect RELM Studio Structural Engineer Glotman Simpson Mechanical & Plumbing Engineer Ocean Park Mechanical Electrical Engineer Michael Wall Engineering Interiors Sixteen Fifty Creative Intelligence Photo credit: Steven Magner The Torrey demonstrates how thoughtful architecture can create a memorable urban experience through restraint rather than monumentality. As the northern anchor of the three-block Courthouse Commons master plan, the 34-story mixed-use development brings approximately 450 new homes and more than 40,000 square feet of neighborhood-serving retail together within a carefully composed architectural vision. Rather than competing for attention, the building establishes a refined civic presence that allows landscape, neighborhood-serving retail, public art, and everyday activity to define its identity at the street while contributing a timeless addition to the downtown skyline. Anchored by the first full-service grocer north of Broadway, a transparent Whole Foods Market activates the ground plane and fills a long-standing gap in downtown San Diego’s neighborhood infrastructure. Above, an elevated landscape inspired by the Southern California Torrey Pine transforms the podium into a park-like residential retreat overlooking San Diego Bay, seamlessly connected to thoughtfully crafted interior amenity spaces. Building-scale public art and carefully detailed streetscapes complete a cohesive architectural composition where architecture, interiors, landscape, and public art reinforce one another to create a distinctive place rooted in both its neighborhood and skyline.
