Vespr Home About Projects Office Vespr Project description Vespr is Oakland’s premiere high-rise mixed-use tower consisting of 419 premium apartments and 47,500 square feet of retail and restaurant space across the street from Whole Foods Market in the Uptown district. Located one block north of Lake Merritt, the building offers sweeping views of the lake and surrounding Oakland skyline. In addition to the trendy Uptown shops and restaurants and rapidly growing employment opportunities nearby, residents of the building have quick access to downtown San Francisco with a BART (Bay Area Rapid Transit) station less than a half mile away. The project opened for residents in July of 2022.
