Password Forgot Password? --> --> PACIFIC NORTHWEST NEWS Printer-friendly Version Email an Associate Washington Multifamily Community Lands $67 Mil Recapitalization 7/13/22 Bella Terra Apartments, a 235-unit multifamily complex in Mukilteo, WA, was recapitalized with new financing of $67 mil. The property is by a joint venture between Holland Partners Group and Principal Real Estate Investors, which recently purchased the property. Located at 12101 Greenhaven, the community was built in 2002, with only 9% of units having received light renovations. The loan recapitalization will enable ownership to implement a complete premium renovation program, including modernizing common-area amenity space, refreshing the pool area and renovating all units with premium-level finishes. The complex is comprised of one-, two- and three-bedroom apartments including live-work townhomes. The units feature large floor plans averaging 1.1k sf with vaulted ceilings, in-home washers and dryers, fireplaces and open-concept kitchens with islands. Select units have wood-style flooring, private garages, stainless steel appliances and private decks and balconies. The complex features various on-site amenities, including a poolside outdoor grilling area, heated outdoor swimming pool and year-round hot tub and sauna. Other amenities include a clubhouse, 24-hour fitness center and business center. The property also includes an on-site retail space. Situated in a sought-after suburban waterfront community with a rapidly growing population, the property is within the Mukilteo School district and provides residents with easy access to multiple local transportation hubs.
