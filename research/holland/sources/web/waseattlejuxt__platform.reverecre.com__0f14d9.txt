 The Opportunity
 Jones Lang LaSalle Americas (“JLL”) has been exclusively engaged by Blackstone’s open-ended fund vehicle, Blackstone Property Partners, L.P., and Holland Residential (collectively,
 the “Sponsor” or “Borrower”) to procure financing for Juxt, a 361-unit, Class A multi-family Property located in the highly sought-after South Lake Union neighborhood in Seattle,
 Washington (the “Property” or “Asset”).
 The request represents a unique opportunity for a Lender to provide financing for a strategically located Asset owned and operated by a joint venture between Holland Residential, a
 West coast-leading developer / operator and originally developed the Asset, and Blackstone, the world’s largest alternative asset manager who bought into the deal in 2016. The
 Sponsor demonstrated a successful lease-up strategy and achieved stabilized operations from 2017 to 2019. However, the COVID-19 pandemic had a significant impact on the Seattle
 apartment market resulting in increased concessions and declining rental rates causing the Asset's performance to decline. Operations rebounded strongly since hitting a trough in
 2021 with NOI recovering back to pre-COVID levels. The Property is currently 93.6% occupied and boasts top-of-the-line interior finishes with an ability to push rents +$300 per unit to
 market. In addition, the Borrower is planning a common area investment to renovate shared outdoor and indoor amenities including the courtyard, clubroom, speakeasy, fitness
 center, and more. These investments will further position the Asset to compete against its competitive set and some of the best properties in South Lake Union while driving growth in
 rents.
 Juxt further benefits from its irreplaceable location in South Lake Union, a dynamic neighborhood featuring over 170 acres of parks, lakefront access, pedestrian-oriented
 infrastructure, and unmatched community amenities. The submarkets boasts many of the world’s top technology firms, including Amazon’s global HQ (less than one mile from the
 Property), Meta (less than 700 feet from the Property), Google (less than one mile from the Property), and Apple (less than half a mile from the Property), which combined employ over
 80k area residents.
 In order to facilitate the refinancing of the Property, the Sponsor is seeking proposals for a five-year, fixed or floating rate loan (the "Loan") at 65% LTV ($105 million or $290,859/unit)
 by Wednesday, July 12th. The proceeds will be used to retire existing debt of $101.5 million ($281,163/unit) and cover estimated closing costs totaling $3.5 million ($9,695/unit). The
 new financing represents a going-in and stabilized debt yield of 6.7% / 8.3% (detailed further in the following pages).

 South Lake Union Submarket Overview
 The South Lake Union submarket remains one Seattle’s most desirable work / live / play districts. Per Costar data, for Class A multifamily properties delivered from 2015 – 2021, the
 current occupancy rate is 94.1%, reflecting recent improvements. The region’s rapid expansion in the past decade was primarily driven by the growth of tech jobs, initially sparked by
 Amazon's relocation to the neighborhood over a decade ago. Notably, other tech giants such as Apple, Facebook, and Google have also established a strong presence in the area. In
 addition to tech, Despite a period of record new construction (over 3,000 units delivered in the past five years) that briefly raised vacancy levels, the area has experienced positive
 growth and more normalized occupancy rates. Furthermore, current construction levels (as a percentage of inventory) are at their lowest levels since 2011, which will significantly
 relieve supply-side pressures on a go-forward basis. Given the slowdown in projects under construction, as of 2024, CoStar projects occupancy to remain at or above current levels for
 the foreseeable future.
 Furthermore, the concentration of health and biotech firms in Lake Union is among the highest in the country. The neighborhood boasts renowned research centers, including The
 Allen Institute for Brain Science, UW Medicine, and the Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research Center.
 The combination of high-paying jobs and access to urban amenities contributes to a robust rental market in the area with significant growth in recent years. Additionally, many
 companies (most notably Amazon), have recently announced return to office policies that will be a continued tailwind for apartment demand. As office-based companies navigate
 post-pandemic utilization patterns and are recalling many workers to the office, residents continue to recognize the value of an urban lifestyle as South Lake Union remains one of
 the most thriving submarkets in the region, with strong demand from renters.
