The Ballard Independent Category Podium Style Multifamily Development of the Year Description The Ballard Independent is a 238-unit mixed-use development that has transformed a prominent Ballard corner into a vibrant community anchor. Featuring approximately 10,000 SF of thoughtfully curated retail, the project blends the neighborhood’s historic industrial roots with contemporary design. Local artists were engaged to enrich the public experience through integrated art and placemaking. Delivered on time and within budget, the LEED Silver-certified building offers sustainable, amenity-driven living and has seen strong leasing demand.
