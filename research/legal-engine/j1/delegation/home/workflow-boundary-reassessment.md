# HOME workflow-boundary reassessment — saved bodies only

Recommendation: **narrow and retain the local TBRA program-edition question; defer general compilation of the two providers’ administrative agreements.** This conclusion rests on actual landlord-facing terms, not Huntington Beach geography or unsuccessful retrieval. No new acquisition occurred for this reassessment.

The four source classes are distinct:

- **Federal HOME law:** 24 CFR Part 92 and other independently applicable federal protections supply the general assisted-tenancy layer. A City-provider contract’s citation does not establish the current federal text, and missing local exhibits do not prevent compiling the independently selected federal sources.
- **City program rules and mandated standard forms:** The July 2024 guideline expressly directs the provider to enter a landlord agreement with each participating owner and requires a tenant-protection addendum. These are conditional operating-program requirements reaching landlord conduct and account rights. They are not rules for every Huntington Beach rental.
- **City-provider administrative agreements:** Annual allocations, provider invoices, staff services, insurance, City-provider indemnity, reporting and grant recapture are not automatically Handoff work. Preserve a contract as an acquisition/incorporation/status carrier where needed; do not select its entire administrative content simply because it funds rent. The 2025 §1.1/§7.10 incorporation is evidence that there is an operating guideline counterpart, not evidence that every contract obligation binds the landlord.
- **Executed landlord/provider and landlord/tenant agreements:** These determine the actual unit, parties, dates, amounts, selected deposit recipient and agreed modifications. They are case evidence, not a requirement to collect all executed tenant contracts in J1. Standard forms mandated by the local program belong in source selection; completed forms belong in the tenancy file.

**J1 boundary:** retain the question “What operative local program guideline and required landlord/tenant form edition governs the relevant assisted tenancies?” The saved incorporation provisions make that an identified source question. Narrow the current phrasing so completion does not require exhaustive reconstruction of each provider’s entire annual grant arrangement. An authoritative citywide program edition with established applicability could answer the common question without both physical Exhibit A scans. If the providers actually use different landlord-facing editions, preserve those conditional branches. Do not assume that difference, or unchanged 2024 carryforward.

A narrower product scope expressly excluding these assisted tenancies could defer the program branch. Retrieval difficulty cannot supply that exclusion, and the supported outgoing-account/readiness workflow itself does not justify excluding it. Conversely, Handoff need not undertake household eligibility intake, employment services, provider reimbursement administration or the City’s grant compliance solely to support this branch.
