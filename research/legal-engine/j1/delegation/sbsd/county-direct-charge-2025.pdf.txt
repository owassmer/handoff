                                                  April 22, 2025

TO:                      Cities and Special Districts
ATTENTION:               Finance Directors/Managers
SUBJECT:                 2025-2026 Direct Charges


This is our annual request for information about adding direct charges to the Secured Property
Tax Roll for 2025-2026.
Submission instructions are as follows:
      -   All required files are to be uploaded to https://epts.ocgov.com/direct directly (via
          Chrome) including the signed board resolution in PDF.
      -   Each city, special district, and your consultant will utilize their individual account to upload
          file(s). The account login information remains the same as the previous fiscal year.
      -   Parcel listing will be posted mid-June in a shared folder via https://epts.ocgov.com/direct.

The attached instructions outline the procedures, due dates, and required data format of the
direct charge. Please ensure that your contractor/preparer receives a copy of this package.
Pursuant to Government Code 55340 Section B, any extension for submission after the August
10th deadline must be approved by the Orange County Auditor-Controller Property Tax unit prior
to your original deadline.
If you are a city/special district placing a direct charge on the property tax roll for the first time,
please refer to the attached instructions, “Requirements for New Assessments Only” section.
Direct charge documents, including payment status for the 1915 Bond Act/Mello-Roos
Assessments, are available on the Auditor-Controller’s Property Tax Accounting website at Direct
Charge-Special Assessment Documents – Orange County Auditor-Controller.

For questions, please contact Roeth Bun at (714) 834-2609 or roeth.bun@ac.ocgov.com.
