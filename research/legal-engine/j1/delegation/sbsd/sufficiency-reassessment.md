## Scope and operating consequence

Location alone is insufficient. These annual owner charges are not a move-out fee, do not automatically become a departing tenant's debt, and do not themselves demonstrate that ordinary annual assessment delinquency authorizes disconnection. Retain the published fee framework only for a covered property's actual service/account question: for example, whether vacancy or arranging a temporary construction dumpster removes an existing assessment, or what owner service charge underlies an otherwise legally supported account allocation. Whether a tenant owes any allocation depends on the tenancy agreement and applicable landlord–tenant law; no SBSD notice supplies that entitlement.
