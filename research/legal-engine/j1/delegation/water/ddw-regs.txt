       Drinking Water Regulations 
       The Division of Drinking Water’s Regulatory Development Unit (RDU) leads California’s rulemaking for drinking water standards, including maximum contaminant levels (MCLs), and develops associated public notification materials such as Consumer Confidence Report templates. It also recommends and establishes  notification and response levels for unregulated contaminants , prioritizes and oversees development and validation of new or improved drinking water analytical methods, and supports program implementation.
