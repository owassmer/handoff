## Outcome

Build a reusable agent-led jurisdiction pipeline that supports Handoff's full physical-work and outgoing-account workflows. The engine must help avoid unnecessary work and expense, preserve justified recovery, and reduce disputes and litigation. The agent investigates and synthesizes; Jev supplies rapid atomic semantic judgments; code handles structured I/O, exact computation, and mechanical validation.

## Overall plan

Follow J0 intake → J1 instruments → J2 complete texts → J3 existing-rule matching → J4 semantic decisions → J5 controlling authorities → J6 adjudication/application → J7 operating walk → J8 substantive review → J9 owner acceptance. Work in small reviewable steps. Reasoned adequacy determines completion; scripts support clerical and computational work.

Jev's implementation is revisited within the legal research method when needed. Case facts exercise the resulting system; they never define the source universe. Delivery must connect the resulting legal branches to both physical-work and outgoing-account execution.
