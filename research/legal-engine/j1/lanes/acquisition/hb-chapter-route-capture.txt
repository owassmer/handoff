L104: The procedure for the contracting of services is as follows:

L106: cite26†A.  L107: The Director of the department primarily responsible for the particular project shall prepare a written statement defining the necessity and scope of the project, and submit it to the Director of Finance. L108: 
L109: cite27†B.  L110: Except as identified in Section cite28†3.03.080 , the Director, or his or her designee, shall request written proposals from not less than three available qualified consultants.
