CITY TREASURER'S REPORT

17. 26-506 MyMeter Presentation

City Treasurer Jason Schmitt stated this is a report and file item and presented a PowerPoint
communication titled My Meter Overview with slides entitled: My Meter Program, Intuitive Sign-Up
Options, My Meter Charts and Data Views, My Meter Markers and Alerts, and video presentation.
Councilmembers and City Treasurer Schmitt discussed what the City expects to see in cost savings
when 90% of customers are signed up; how information about My Meter will be distributed; confirmed
that historical information will remain for each account; use of the app will make it easier for customers to
identify water leaks; and confirmed the app is available through the Apple store.
