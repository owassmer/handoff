19. 26-441 Adopted Resolution 2026-13 to Acknowledge Receipt of a Fire Department Report
Regarding the Annual Inspection of Certain Occupancies

A motion was made by McKeon, second Twining to adopt Resolution No. 2026-13, "A Resolution of the
City Council of the City of Huntington Beach, California Acknowledging Receipt of a Report Made by the
Fire Chief of the Huntington Beach Fire Department Regarding the Inspection of Certain Occupancies
Required to Perform Annual Inspections in Such Occupancies Pursuant to Sections 13146.2 and
13146.3 of the California Health and Safety Code."

The motion carried by the following roll call vote:

AYES: Gruel, Kennedy, Twining, McKeon, Burns, and Williams
NOES: None
ABSENT: Van Der Mark

Public Works

20. 26-500 Accepted Lowest Responsive and Responsible Bid and Authorized Execution of a
Construction Contract with Sanz Construction, Inc. in the amount of $155,748 for
City Gym and Pool Flooring Replacement (CC-1850), and authorized a 4.5%
contingency

A motion was made by McKeon, second Twining to accept the lowest responsive and responsible bid
submitted by Sanz Construction, Inc. in the amount of $155,748; and, authorize the Director of Public
Works to approve change orders for potential construction-related changes up to a 4.5% contingency;
and, authorize the Mayor and City Clerk to execute a construction contract with Sanz Construction, Inc.
in a form approved by the City Attorney.

The motion carried by the following roll call vote:

AYES: Gruel, Kennedy, Twining, McKeon, Burns, and Williams
NOES: None
ABSENT: Van Der Mark

21. 26-407 Accepted Lowest Responsive and Responsible Bid and Authorized Execution of a
Construction Contract with Performance Pipeline Technologies, Inc., in the Amount

AYES: Gruel, Kennedy, Twining, McKeon, Burns, and Williams

NOES: None
