RESOLUTION NO. 10-04-03

AMENDMENT TO ASSESSMENT POLICY DATED APRIL 9, 1992

The following language shall be added to the District’s Assessment Policy and become effective
upon passage by the Board of Directors to include language that refunds of User Fees, resulting
from an erroneous assessment, will not exceed what the County will pay based on the County
Statute of Limitations.

Refund Policy: |n the event of an erroneous over-assessment, the District shall direct the
County to refund assessed amounts to the taxpayer to the extent that the County’s statute of

limitations applies. The District shall not be liable to any taxpayer for an amount in excess of

the preceding.

MOTION:

To amend the District’s Assessment Policy as stated above.

Nays: C2
