Republic Services

The Sunset Beach Sanitary District contracts with Republic Services for trash pick up and disposal. Residential trash is picked up regularly every Friday and Monday (delayed one day for holidays).

https://www.republicservices.com/municipality/huntington-beach-ca

The current contract is here: http://www.sunsetbeachsd.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/2017-Rainbow-Franchise-Agreement-1.pdf

The contract is set to expire in May 2030.

The 2025-2026 Residential Rates that the District pays to Republic Services increased less than 5% per the District’s contract:

2024-25 Residential Rates as increased per Franchise Agreement are here: http://www.sunsetbeachsd.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/2024-25-Residential-Rates.pdf
