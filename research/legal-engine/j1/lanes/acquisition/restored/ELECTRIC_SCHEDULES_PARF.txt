                                       POLE ATTACHMENT RELATED FEES



 APPLICABILITY

Applicable to any California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC) eligible entity (ROW entity) that
executes a pole license agreement to place an attachment on Southern California Edison
Company’s (SCE) distribution pole pursuant to the CPUC’s Rights-Of-Way (ROW) rules in Decision
98-10-058. These agreements include, but are not limited to, the Pole License Agreement form
agreement submitted to CPUC via Advice 1395-E and Advice 1742-E.

 TERRITORY

 Within the entire territory served.

 RATES

 Pursuant to the Pole License Agreement and the ROW rules, a pole owner may recover the cost
 association with inspecting an attachment by a ROW entity. The Final Inspection Fee applies at the
 time SCE inspects an attachment made by a ROW entity to SCE’s pole.

 The following applicable service fee will be charged to the ROW entity unless otherwise specified in
 this Schedule.
