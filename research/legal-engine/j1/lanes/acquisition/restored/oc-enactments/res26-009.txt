RESOLUTION NO. 	 RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF SUPERVISORS OF
ORANGE COUNTY, CALIFORNIA

February 24, 2026

On Motion of Supervisor 	, duly seconded and carried, the following Resolution was adopted:

WHEREAS, in 1986, the Board of Supervisors (Board) adopted an abatement ordinance, codified in the Orange County Codified Ordinances (OCCO) Section 3-14-2 to provide remedies for the abatement of public nuisances; and

WHEREAS, the proposed amendments would establish updated abatement practices and procedures to facilitate enforcement under Title 3 – Public Morals, Safety and Welfare and update OCCO Sections 3-14-2 through 3-14-16, land use regulations and site development standards, consistent with the County’s powers pursuant to Government Code Title 7 – Planning and Land Use (sections 65000-66499.58); and

WHEREAS, on December 7, 2010, the Board approved and adopted OCCO Sections 5-1- 31 to 5-1-33 which prohibit the sale or distribution of marijuana or any other controlled substances, as defined herein under local, state or federal law or which is illegal to sell or distribute under local, state or federal law, and set penalties for violations; and
WHEREAS, on December 5, 2017, the Board adopted Ordinance 17-010 to prohibit commercial cannabis activity, including retail sale and distribution, commercial cultivation, outdoor personal cultivation, delivery, manufacturing and testing; and

WHEREAS, on July 28, 2020, the Board adopted OCCO Section 7-9-103, which prohibits commercial cannabis activities and outdoor personal cultivation in all zoning districts; and

WHEREAS, the proposed amendment also clarifies OCCO Sections 3-14-2 through 3-14-16, 5-1- 31, 5-1-33 and 7-9-103 to comply with State of California law AB 1684 fines for commercial cannabis activities and incorporate current planning and development trends.

WHEREAS, the proposed amendments are not a project within the meaning of the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) Guidelines Section 15378 and is therefore not subject to

CEQA, since it does not have the potential for resulting in either a direct physical change in the environment, or a reasonably foreseeable indirect physical change in the environment; and

WHEREAS, on December 10, 2025, the Planning Commission approved authorization to initiate, conducted a public hearing on the amendments and recommended approval of the proposed Zoning Code Amendment CA 25-02; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission reviewed and fully considered proposed Zoning Code Amendment CA 25-02 to Orange County Comprehensive Zoning Code section 7-9-103, County of Orange commercial cannabis activities and outdoor personal cultivation prohibition ordinance, and heard, and considered the public comments that were presented at the public hearing held on this project, and determined after review and consideration to recommend adoption by the Board of Supervisors of proposed Zoning Code Amendment CA 24-02.

WHEREAS, on February 10, 2026, at a duly noticed public hearing, the Board considered the recommendation of the Planning Commission as well as evidence presented by the OC Development Services and other interested parties with respect to this item.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT THE BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:

	•	Find that the amendments are not a project within the meaning of CEQA Guidelines Section 15378 and is therefore not subject to review under CEQA.

	•	Adopt Zoning Code Amendment CA 25-02 to amend Section 7-9-103, County of Orange commercial cannabis activities and outdoor personal cultivation prohibition ordinance.

	•	Amend OCCO Sections 3-14-2 through 3-14-16, and land use regulations and site development standards, consistent with the County’s powers pursuant to Government Code sections 65000-66499.5.

	•	Amend OCCO Sections 5-1-31, 5-1-33 and 7-9-103 to comply with State of California law AB 1684.
