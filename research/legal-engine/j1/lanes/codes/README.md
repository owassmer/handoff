# California statutory source proposal

October 1, 2026. This is the code lane's proposed replacement scope for the 29 California codes and Constitution. It does not declare J1 complete or compile legal rules. The coordinator owns integration, cross-lane dependencies and the completion judgment.

`replacement_instruments.json` contains replacement entries by instrument id. `python3 j1/lanes/codes/propose.py`, run from `research/legal-engine`, reproduces it without changing canonical registers. `baseline.json` preserves the input draft; `preliminary_units.json` incorporates the acquisition lane's concrete replacements for invented preliminary placeholders. The only unresolved acquisition targets at this checkpoint are the Constitution preamble and the second Civil Code Chapter 2.5 (Water Service), described below.

## Substantive decisions

The selected sources serve complete physical work and outgoing-account resolution, including conditional public, assisted, institutional, mobilehome and other residential regimes. Mixed units remain intact where their shared authority, definitions, exceptions and remedies make section review appropriate. Named separate industries and standalone programs are excluded on their actual subject. No current demo fact or assumed market-rate tenancy supplied a boundary.

## Evidence and acquisition boundaries

Full expanded official TOCs are saved for BPC, GOV, HSC, PEN, RTC and EDC. The original complete Civil expanded TOC remains the basis for that code. Selected PRC, PUC, FIN, WAT and INS branches are captured directly; other unchanged root decisions use the existing saved root census. Every replacement entry records its selected/excluded unit and reason. The narrowed entries include concrete hierarchy URLs rather than code-root indexes. Section lists are used where numbered paths are ambiguous.

Key text captures are `sources/constitutional-dependencies.txt`, `edc-equity.txt`, `edc-housing.txt`, `civ-warranty-retry.txt`, `civ-counseling.txt`, `hsc-abatement.txt`, `hsc-bodies.txt`, `prob-health-decision.txt` and `prc-discovery.txt`. Failed direct page requests remain identifiable as errors in their captures; they were not treated as substantive absence. Headings suffice for plainly separate subject exclusions; text was followed when scope or a dependency changed the decision.

The enactments lane owns session/status and delayed-effect completeness. These selections retain whole current code units, including their alternative versions and effective/operative annotations. No snapshot filter silently removes adopted future text.

Two official navigation defects need the acquisition lane's specific treatment:

- Constitution preamble: the real text is an unnumbered TOC caption; its link cycles through expanded-branch pages rather than a normal numbered section. Do not reinstate a fake `preliminary` unit pointing to the entire Constitution.
- CIV Division 3 / Part 4 / Title 5 / Chapter 2.5: Transitional Housing Participant Misconduct (1954.10–1954.18) and Water Service (1954.201–1954.219) share the same path. `sources/civ-chapter25.txt` proves the bare path returns only the first chapter. The water selection requires explicit section enumeration or another verified selector; parent/child deduplication must not delete water coverage.

HSC Division 1 / Part 3 likewise appears twice. The Children's Environmental Health Center is explicitly selected as sections 900 and 901; border-health office section 475 is excluded. HSC Public Health Division 112 is selected through its unique Part 1; the other Division 112's prescription-discount chapters are out.

Final materialization check: regeneration is byte-identical; all 30 instrument IDs are unique; selected instruments have function tags; selected/excluded units have reasons; no invented `preliminary` unit remains. Hierarchy-selector comparison finds only the known Water Service collision against the three Transitional Housing articles. Acquisition owns its replacement and the preamble target; integrate those after this code proposal. These checks establish the proposal's internal consistency, not substantive legal completeness or successful J2 acquisition.
