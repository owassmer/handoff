L29: 
L30: Background
L31: 
L32: The HOME Investment Partnerships Program (HOME) was established under the Federal HOME Investment Partnerships Act Statutes at Title II of the Cranston-Gonzalez National Affordable Housing Act, as amended, 42 U.S.C. 12701 et seq.
L33: 
L34: Eligible Program Activities
L35: 
L36: The State of California HOME Program supports the following housing activities:
L37:   * First-Time Homebuyer (FTHB) Program, including scattered-site infill new construction — Helping eligible households achieve sustainable homeownership.
L38:   * Owner-Occupied Rehabilitation (OOR) Program — Supporting low-income homeowners in making critical repairs and improvements to maintain safe, decent housing.
L39:   * Tenant-Based Rental Assistance (TBRA) Program — Providing rental support to income-qualified households facing housing instability.
L40: 
L41: Eligible Applicants
L42: Eligible applicants include cities, counties, and federally recognized tribal entities seeking to operate one or more HOME-funded programs within their jurisdictional areas provided they do not receive HUD HOME funding through another source. Additionally, prospective HCD-certified Community Housing Development Organizations (CHDOs) may apply exclusively for the HOME FTHB Infill New Construction Program, within an eligible jurisdictional area.

L17: Two Programs. One Purpose.
L18: Streamlines two of HCD's homeownership programs to align eligibility criteria, scoring, and release of funds allowing for a coordinated single application and award process. Learn more about the individual programs with funding available under this Super NOFA.

L33: 
L34: Eligible Applicants
L35: 
L36: To apply for CalHome funding, the applicant shall be a Local Public Entity, Local Public Agency, Tribal Entity, or Nonprofit Corporation.
L37: 
L38: To apply for the Joe Serna, Jr. Farmworker Housing Grant Homeownership funding, the applicant shall be a Local Public Entity, Local Public Agency, Tribal Entity, Nonprofit Corporation, Limited Liability Company, or Limited Partnership.
L39: 
L40: Eligible Activities
L41:   * Homeownership Development Project Loans
L42:   * First-Time Homebuyer Mortgage Assistance
L43:   * Owner-Occupied Rehabilitation Assistance
L44:   * Technical Assistance for Self-Help Housing Projects
L45:   * Technical Assistance for Shared Housing Programs
L46:   * ADU/JADU Programs
L47:   * Acquisition Of Manufactured Housing
L48: 
L49: Assistance Type and Terms
L50: This Homeownership Super NOFA is issued to distribute funds for the CalHome Program and funds for the homeownership activities within FWHG only. Please note that new construction or rehabilitation (with or without acquisition) of a multifamily rental housing development, including under the FWHG program, is not an eligible activity under this Super NOFA.
L51:   * CalHome program, which supports homeownership programs aimed at lower and very low income households, and in the case of a disaster, households at or below moderate income, and operated by private nonprofit and local government agencies, to increase homeownership, encourage neighborhood revitalization and sustainable development, and maximize use of existing homes.
L52:   * Joe Serna, Jr. Farmworker Housing Grant (FWHG or Serna) program, which supports development projects involving multiple home ownership units, including single-family subdivisions, for lower-income agricultural employees and their families, and programs that assist lower-income agricultural employees and their families to become or remain homeowners.
