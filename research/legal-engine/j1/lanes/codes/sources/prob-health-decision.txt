L44: #### Probate Code - PROB
L45: #### DIVISION 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS [4600 - 4806]
L46: 
L47:   ( Division 4.7 added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. )
L48: 
L49: 
L50: 
L51: #### PART 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL [4600 - 4665]
L52: 
L53:   ( Part 1 added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. )
L54: 
L55: 
L56: 
L57: ##### CHAPTER 1. Short Title and Definitions [4600 - 4643]
L58: 
L59:   ( Chapter 1 added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. )
L60: ###### 4617.
L61: 
L62: (a) “Health care decision” means a decision made by a patient or the patient’s agent, conservator, or surrogate, regarding the patient’s health care, including the following:
L63: 
L64: (1) Selection and discharge of health care providers and institutions.
L65: 
L66: (2) Approval or disapproval of diagnostic tests, surgical procedures, and programs of medication, including mental health conditions.
L67: (3) Directions to provide, withhold, or withdraw artificial nutrition and hydration and all other forms of health care, including cardiopulmonary resuscitation.
L68: 
L69: (b) “Health care decision” does not include a decision made by a patient’s agent, conservator, or surrogate to consent to treatments identified in Section 4652.
L70: 
L71: (Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 171, Sec. 1. (AB 1029) Effective January 1, 2024.)
