## Acquisition handoff

Every target is source_unit_kind=document. These are acquisition documents, not one legal section per PDF. J2 must subdivide headings/numbered requirements, OCR image-only Fish/Gabriel/Hesseltine/Lee/Recio documents and inspect footer/caption conflicts. Whole document acquisition is appropriate because general policies combine calendar, service, evidentiary and trial provisions. No full text corpus was harvested here.

Case-specific scheduling orders remain case evidence, not sources to invent or acquire before a case exists. General court calendar instructions should be retained as an operational source alongside PDFs because the calendar sometimes disagrees with a policy's hearing time. The calendar itself is an explicit additional document target, with rendered/manual acquisition required because direct HTTP yields the empty JS shell. Its observed official text is preserved in oc-current-calendar-cache.txt; empty-shell fetch must not count as a successful J2 capture.
