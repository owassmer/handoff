L317: ### EDIBLE FOOD DONATION
L318: 
L319: California law requires businesses classified as commercial edible food generators to donate all leftover or unsold edible food that would otherwise be thrown away by contracting with an cite164†edible food recovery organization or service to reduce food waste and address food insecurity.
L320: Edible food is food intended for people to eat, including food not sold because of surplus, appearance, age, or freshness, and includes prepared food, packaged food, and produce. The city compiled a lcite164†ist of local and County organizations who may be able to assist in recovering or accepting edible food donations. Edible Food Generators are divided into two Tiers.
L321: Tier 1
L322: Supermarkets with an annual revenue of $2 million or greater
L323: Grocery Stores with a total facility size of 10,000 sq. ft. or more
L324: Food Service Providers, Food Distributors, and Wholesale Food Vendors
L325: Tier 1 generators were required to contract with an edible food recovery organization or service and maintain records of donations starting January 1, 2022.
L326: Tier 2
L327: Hotels with on-site food facilities
L328: Restaurants with a total facility size of 5,000 or more square feet or 250+ seats
L329: Health Facilities with on-site food facility and 100+ beds
L330: State Agency Cafeterias with 5,000 or more square feet or 250+ seats
L331: Local Education Agencies (aka school districts) with on-site food facility
L332: Large Venues and Events
L333: 
L334: Tier 2 generators were required to comply starting January 1, 2024.

L387: ### REPORTING ILLEGAL DUMPING
L388: 
L389: To report illegal dumping of items on public space or the public right of way, contact Public Works Operations at (714) 960-8861 or cite111†report the issue through the city website. The City of Huntington Beach DOES NOT pick up bulky or abandoned items from private property.
L390: ### SCAVENGING
L391: Scavenging is the act of unauthorized persons searching through recycling or trash containers located either on public or private property. It is illegal in the City of Huntington Beach to scavenge. If you see a scavenging incident, please report it to Scavenging Enforcement at (714) 374-1739 with a detailed description of the day, time, location, person and vehicle if known.
L392: If you wish to donate your recyclables so that others may collect the redemption value, please separate these items from your container, bag them, and place them where they can be accessed. Do not give permission for individuals to go through your containers. For further information please call Scavenging Enforcement at (714) 374-1739.

L397: ## IMPORTANT LEGISLATION
L398: ### BUSINESS RECYCLING (AB 341)
L399: The “Mandatory Commercial Recycling Law” aims to divert 75% of California’s waste stream away from landfills and instead be reduced, recycled or composted by the year 2020. To accomplish this, businesses and multi-family residential dwellings of five units or more, that generate more than 4 cubic yards of solid waste per week, are required to arrange for recycling services on and after July 1, 2012.
L400: Huntington Beach’s contracted waste hauler, Republic Services, has staff that can assist in implementing the right commercial recycling program for businesses or apartment properties. Contact Republic services at (657) 845-6161 for more information regarding compliance.
L401: For additional information on AB 341 please visit the cite168†CalRecycle website.†www.calrecycle.ca.gov L402: ### CALIFORNIA’S SHORT-LIVED CLIMATE POLLUTANT REDUCTION STRATEGY (SB 1383)
L403: SB 1383 establishes statewide targets to reduce the amount of organic waste disposed of in landfills (50% reduction by 2020 and 75% by 2025). It also sets a goal to rescue at least 20% of currently disposed edible food by 2025 and redirect that food to people in need, by requiring edible food generators to cite169†donate edible food to food recovery organizations. Contact Republic Services at (657) 845-6161 for more information regarding compliance.
L404: For additional information on SB 1383 please visit the CalRecycle cite170†website†www.calrecycle.ca.gov .
L405: ### CUSTOMER ACCESS TO RECYCLING (AB 827)
L406: 
L407: Effective July 1, 2020, certain restaurants and retailers who are subject to Mandatory Commercial Recycling and Organics laws are now required to provide recycling containers at front-of-house to collect food waste and recyclables generated from products purchased and consumed on their premises. The collection containers must be visible, easily accessible to customers, and clearly marked.
L408: ### MANDATORY COMMERCIAL ORGANICS RECYCLING (AB 1826)
L409: This law came into effect on January 1, 2016 and requires businesses and multi-family complexes (with 5 or more units) that generate two or more cubic yards of solid waste and organic waste combined per week to arrange for organics collection services. For more information regarding compliance, contact Republic Services by calling (657) 845-6161 or emailing cite171†[email protected] .
L410: For additional information on AB 1826 please visit the CalRecycle cite172†website†www.calrecycle.ca.gov .
