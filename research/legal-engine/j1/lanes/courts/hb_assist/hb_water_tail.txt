L272: 
L273: ## Water Service Charge
L274: 
L275: The Water Service Charge consists of a basic monthly meter charge and a commodity charge based on water usage, which together support the cost of providing water service to our customers.

L323: ## Food Service Establishments Fats, Oils, Grease Sewer Fees
L324: 
L325: The Fats, Oils, and Grease (FOG) Program supports Food Service Establishments (FSEs) within our service area by reducing the risk of sewer blockages and protecting the City's wastewater system.

L330: ## Payments
L331: 
L332:   * Payments must be received by the due date. Late payments may result in a tag fee, a delinquent status fee and a scheduled shut-off.
L333:   * For same day credit,  payments must be placed in the night drop by 3:00 p.m. , located on the fountain side of the Civic Center near the police station.
L334:   * Huntington Beach Civic Center is open for walk-in payments at 2000 Main Street. Office hours are Monday through Friday,  8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., except holidays.
L335: ## Rates and Charges are effective as of August 15, 2026.
