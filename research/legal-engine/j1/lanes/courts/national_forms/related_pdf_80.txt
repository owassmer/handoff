L0@P0: Instructions, Form 309(A-I)
L1@P0: December 1, 2024
L2@P0: NOTICE OF BANKRUPTCY CASE
L3@P0: General Information
L4@P0: Official Form 309 is used to give notice to creditors, equity security holders, and other
L5@P0: interested parties of the filing of the bankruptcy case, the time, date, and location of the meeting
L6@P0: of creditors, the time for filing various documents in the case, instructions for filing proofs of
L7@P0: claim, and other information concerning the case.
L8@P0: Official Form 309 consists of several variations, numbered 309A through 309I, created to
L9@P0: meet the specialized notice requirements for cases filed under chapters 7, 11, 12, and 13 of the
L10@P0: Bankruptcy Code. The form to be used is determined by the chapter under which the bankruptcy
L11@P0: petition was filed, the type of debtor (Individual or Joint Debtor, or Corporation or Partnership
L12@P0: Debtor) and whether a proof of claim deadline is included. The versions of Official Form 309
L13@P0: are listed below:
L14@P0: 309A (For Individuals or Joint Debtors), Notice of Chapter 7 Bankruptcy Case – No
L15@P0: Proof of Claim Deadline
L16@P0: 309B (For Individuals or Joint Debtors), Notice of Chapter 7 Bankruptcy Case –Proof
L17@P0: of Claim Deadline Set
L18@P0: 309C (For Corporations of Partnerships), Notice of Chapter 7 Bankruptcy Case – No
L19@P0: Proof of Claim Deadline
L20@P0: 309D (For Corporations of Partnerships), Notice of Chapter 7 Bankruptcy Case – Proof
