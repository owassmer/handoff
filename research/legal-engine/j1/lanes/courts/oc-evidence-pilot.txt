L10@P0: C10 – Honorable Shawn S. Nelson
L11@P0: C11 – Honorable Jonathan S. Fish

L13@P0: C21 – Honorable Craig E. Arthur

L17@P0: participating departments. The addition of these five departments expands the Dedicated
L18@P0: Discovery Department Program to 20 Civil Unlimited Panel departments.
L19@P0: Program Overview:
L20@P0: The Dedicated Discovery Department Program is designed to streamline the resolution of
L21@P0: discovery disputes and reduce calendar congestion in the participating departments by
L22@P0: centralizing discovery motions in a single, dedicated department. On an expedited basis, all
L23@P0: discovery motions filed in the participating departments are being set for an informal
L24@P0: discovery conference in the dedicated discovery department with the Honorable Andre De
L25@P0: La Cruz presiding. Only if the conference is unsuccessful, do the motions proceed to full
L26@P0: briefing and a formal hearing in the discovery department. To date, very few motions have
L27@P0: proceeded to a formal hearing.

L12@P0: C25- Judge Gassia Apkarian
L13@P0: C27- Judge Bradley Erdosi
L14@P0: Absent an exemption from judicial officer, counsel shall comply with the following protocol regarding trial
L15@P0: exhibits and otherwise fully comply with Local Rule 317 except as modified by this protocol:
L16@P0: • At the Local Rule 317 issue conference, counsel shall meet and confer regarding exhibits to avoid
L17@P0: duplicate exhibits and agree on the exhibit number range to be used by each party.
L18@P0: • All exhibits are to be uploaded to the Portal no later than 14 days prior to the trial
L19@P0: date. The Portal is accessible through the court’s website at
L20@P0: https://www.occourts.org/online-services/electronic-evidence-portal.
L21@P0: • Counsel will assign exhibit numbers and exhibit descriptions during upload consistent with the
L22@P0: ranges to which they agreed during the Local Rule 317 issue conference. Only numbers shall be
L23@P0: entered, no special characters or letters should be added to the exhibit number. The Portal will
L24@P0: automatically generate digital exhibit tags and exhibit lists
L25@P0: • Impeachment exhibits may be uploaded as “Confidential” with the exhibit description of
L26@P0: “Impeachment.” This allows the exhibit number/description to display on the exhibit list without
L27@P0: disclosing the exhibit description. Please check with the individual courtrooms for additional or
L28@P0: alternative impeachment exhibit procedures.
L29@P0: • With the exception of impeachment exhibits, all exhibits must be shared with opposing counsel via
L30@P0: the Portal’s “Virtual Viewing Room” no later than 14 days prior to the trial date. (Local Rule
L31@P0: 317.A.) Failure to do so may result in sanctions against any non-compliant party.
L32@P0: • The final set of electronic exhibits for the court must be uploaded to the Portal no later than noon
L33@P0: on the Wednesday before trial.
L34@P0: • Counsel will be responsible for displaying the electronic exhibits during trial. Laptops will be made
L35@P0: available on counsel table for use by counsel to access the Portal and will be connected to the
L36@P0: evidence presentation equipment in the courtroom.
