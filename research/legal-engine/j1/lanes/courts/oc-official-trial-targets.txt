L142@P4: 1. Attorneys are to advise their witnesses of rulings that will apply to their testimony.
L143@P4: 2. Do not make speaking objections. Simply state the Objection and the legal grounds.
L144@P4: 3. Except for appropriate transitional comments, do not make any editorial comments prior
L145@P4: to posing a question. (For example, “We have heard about the defendant’s negligent
L146@P4: driving, did you see him change lanes without signaling?”)

L148@P4: to do so on your behalf. If you have concerns about the jurors’ comfort, inform the Court
L149@P4: of those concerns outside the presence of the jury.
L150@P4: 5. Stipulations must be reduced to writing.
L151@P4: 6. Sidebars will be kept to a minimum. Ensure that anticipated objections are dealt with in
L152@P4: limine or otherwise outside the presence of the jury.
L153@P4: 7. Challenges for cause, all motions, and arguments concerning evidentiary objections will
L154@P4: be discussed at sidebar or otherwise outside the presence of the jury. Generally,
L155@P4: sidebars will not be on the record. Counsel can make their record later at a break to
L156@P4: ensure jurors are not kept waiting by lengthy sidebars.
L157@P4: 8. Attorneys are to speak from behind the counsel table or lectern unless the court has
L158@P4: given permission to approach a witness. Permission to approach a particular witness
L159@P4: need only be requested once for that witness.
L160@P4: 9. The Court will conduct a Pretrial Conference on the scheduled trial date, at which the
L161@P4: following issues, among others, will be discussed, jury selection, unusual evidentiary or
L162@P4: instruction issues, scheduling, witness problems, summary of expected facts and
L163@P4: defenses, pretrial motions.
L164@P4: 10. The Court uses a “six-pack” method (18 prospective jurors) to select 14 jurors, including
L165@P4: two designated alternates. Initial voir dire is directed at the panel of 18 prospective
L166@P4: jurors. “For cause” challenges are then made or waived as to the 18 prospective jurors
L167@P4-5: (out of their presence) followed by peremptory challenges directed only toward the 14Rev. 10/30/25 6
L168@P5: prospective jurors “in the box.” Any additional voir dire is directed only toward “new”
L169@P5: prospective jurors who fill seats of those previously excused.
