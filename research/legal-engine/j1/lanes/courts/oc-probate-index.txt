L328: # Electronic Evidence Portal
L329: 
L330: ## The Court has implemented a portal for the submission of electronic evidence in selected courtrooms.
L331: ### Civil Unlimited (Departments C15, C16, C24, C25, and C27) - PILOT PROGRAM
L332: Selected Civil Unlimited departments listed below are participating in a pilot program to submit evidence electronically through the “portal” for use during court and jury trials.
L333: Counsel will be required to upload exhibits to the portal in lieu of trial exhibits notebooks, except for one copy of the notebook.  Please consult the cite316†Civil Unlimited - Electronic Evidence Protocol for details and requirements applicable to all pilot courtrooms, as well as the General Policies and Procedures applicable to each individual courtroom, which are available at cite317†Civil Calendar | Superior Court of California | County of Orange.†voypubapps.occourts.org L334: Counsel shall review the “Electronic Evidence Order and Stipulation” available via the link below and file the signed stipulation by noon the Wednesday before trial. Plaintiff is ordered to serve a copy of this order and stipulation on all parties.

L6@P0: Welcome to Probate. The Court recommends that all counsel and self-represented parties read and
L7@P0: familiarize themselves with Orange County Local Court Rules, Division 6 (Probate Rules) and Titles 3 and
L8@P0: 7 of the California Rules of Court (Civil and Probate Rules, respectively).
L9@P0: To facilitate the progress of your case and assure its timely disposition, this Court has established
L10@P0: the following guidelines:
L11@P0: 1. Unless otherwise ordered by the Court, once contested, your matter will proceed through three
L12@P0: court hearings;
L13@P0: A. Trial Setting Conference (TSC);
L14@P0: B. Mandatory Settlement Conference (MSC);
L15@P0: C. Trial.
L16@P0: 2. The Court expects counsel for all parties to maintain regular communication regarding hearing
L17@P0: dates, progress of the case and settlement possibilities.
L18@P0: 3. The Court believes in cooperative discovery and expects counsel to communicate in order to
L19@P0: reduce the need for discovery motions. The prevailing party on discovery motions will generally
L20@P0: be awarded costs and attorney fees.
L21@P0: 4. The Court requires written notice of all hearing dates unless waived by all counsel or selfrepresented parties.
L22@P0: 5. The Court accepts the standards of liberal amendment of pleadings and expects counsel in most
L23@P0: cases to stipulate to amend pleadings with a minimum response time of 10 days.
L24@P0: 6. Counsel may not stipulate to a continuance of a TSC, MSC or Trial without Court approval. A
L25@P0: stipulation must be submitted with a place for the Court’s signature. If all counsel/parties agree
L26@P0: to the continuance, an ex parte application is not required. However, the stipulation should state
L27@P0: the reason for the requested continuance.
L28@P0: 7. The Court may provide tentative rulings on law and motion matters on the Court website. If the
L29@P0: matter is resolved prior to the hearing date, please call the department where the matter is
L30@P0-1: scheduled.2

L38@P1: 1. A Trial Setting Conference (TSC) may be set by the Court at the first hearing at which the

L41@P1: Statement (see optional local form L-1113) and file it at least five court days before the
L42@P1: hearing.

L44@P1: and when trial should be set.

L47@P1: 1. A Mandatory Settlement Conference (MSC) will be required in all contested matters,

L50@P1: Cal.App.5th 801. The parties should be prepared to discuss the possibility for settlement.

L55@P1: setting of an Order to Show Cause Why Sanctions should not be ordered pursuant to CCP
L56@P1: 177.5 and CCP 128.5
