L6@P0: As most counsel are aware, the Court’s calendars have increased exponentially during
L7@P0: the pandemic. Many of those calendars reflect multiple continuances for notes to be
L8@P0: cleared. In an effort to reduce calendar size and clear the Court’s backlog, the following
L9@P0: policy is now in effect:
L10@P0: “If notes have not been cleared by the third continuance, and depending on the matter
L11@P0: before the Court, the petition may be dismissed without prejudice and the party
L12@P0: bringing the petition may be required to pay the filing fee to re-file or an appointed
L13@P0: personal representative, conservator or guardian may be suspended and an OSC re
L14@P0: removal set.”
L15@P0: This action is not taken lightly. The number of continuances occurring due to failure to
L16@P0: clear notes has begun to interfere with the court’s ability to process, schedule and
L17@P0: approve the matters which otherwise could be more quickly set for hearing. Matters
L18@P0: continued multiple times for failure to clear notes should not prevent the approval of
L19@P0: petitions which have been properly submitted.
L20@P0: We acknowledge that some delays are caused by the significant back log in approving
L21@P0: orders, letters, etc. In those cases, we will try and continue the matter out far enough
L22@P0: that those submitted documents can be reviewed and, hopefully, approved so that
L23@P0: another appearance is not necessary.

L48@P0: and that justice is done. (Id. at p. 427; People v. Williams (2009) 170 Cal. App. 4th 587, 615.)
