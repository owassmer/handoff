L347: 
L348: > An ex parte application is usually brought to court when there is irreparable harm that will occur if the relief requested is not granted before the matter can be heard on regular notice.
L349: 
L350: All ex parte applications are to comply with California Rules of Court 3.1200 et. seq. (and in particular 3.1203 and 3.1204), 7.55, and (if applicable) 7.454.
L351: The court will only consider ex parte applications if the applicant has made an affirmative factual showing in a declaration containing competent testimony based on personal knowledge of irreparable harm, immediate danger, or statutory basis for granting ex parte relief. (CRC 3.1202(c)).
L352: 
L353: When e-filing the ex parte application, please be sure to choose the filing type of ex parte which will assist the clerk’s office in processing your documents expeditiously.
L354: ##### Important Ex Parte notice Information!
L355: 
L356: > All ex parte applications will be non-appearance and submitted on the papers. The outcome of the ex parte application will be sent to the parties involved either electronically or by U.S. mail depending on the initial filing method. Please see the section below regarding required notice language.
L357: ### Filing, Notice, and Service Requirements:
L358: 
L359: • Ex Parte Applications, Declarations, and Proposed Orders may be prepared on pleading paper cite316†(Form L-0930) .
L360: 
L361: • The department listed on the ex parte notice must be “Probate – XP”.
L362: 
L363: • Declaration Re Ex Parte Application (cite317†Form L-3033 ) is required to be submitted at the time of filing pursuant to Orange County Local Rule 601.13.
L364: • Except where the court specifically declares otherwise, all notices must state that the ex parte application will be considered and ruled on in chambers based on the papers, without hearing or oral argument. Parties are not to make an in-person or remote appearance.
L365: • All ex parte applications with supporting documents, declaration regarding notice and orders must be electronically filed and served on all persons entitled to notice no later than 10:00 a.m. the day before the hearing except for Temporary Conservatorships, Temporary Guardianships, and Letters of Special Administration with General Powers or General Powers with IAEA Powers (see below). Please be advised that if your ex parte application is not submitted by the 10:00 a.m.
L366: deadline, it will be scheduled for the next court day in which ex parte applications are reviewed.  Click cite220†here for more information.
L367: • Notice via email alone is insufficient, unless a consent to email notice signed by the receiving party has been filed with the court.
L368: 
L369: • Notice and service requirements are strictly observed by this court.
L370: • Requests for temporary guardianships require personal service of notice at least 5 court days before the hearing date on legal parents of minors, giving them 5 court days to respond to the court. Minors who will come under the guardianship must be personally served with notice if they are age 12 or older. Revised (2/20/26)
L371: 
L372: • Requests for temporary conservatorships require personal service on the proposed conservatee at least 5 court days before the hearing date.
L373: • A petition for Letters of Special Administration with General Powers or General Powers with IAEA Powers requires 15 days’ notice and publication. (Please note that a request for IAEA Powers with Letters of Special Administration also requires a request for General Powers.)
L374: 
L375: cite318†Back to top L376: ### Opposition Filing and Service Requirements:
L377: 
L378: Any written opposition to an ex parte application must be electronically filed and served on all persons entitled to notice by 10:00 a.m. the day of the ex parte hearing.
L379: ### Length Restrictions and Electronic Bookmarking Requirements:
L380: 
L381: An ex parte application or opposition may not exceed 10 pages. The page limit does not include the caption page, exhibits, declarations, attachments, the table of contents, the table of authorities, or any declaration regarding notice.
L382: 
L383: An application that exceeds 5 pages must state in the opening paragraph the relief requested by the order being sought and the grounds for issuance of the order.
L384: A party may apply to the court ex parte for permission to file a longer application. The application must state the reasons why the argument cannot be made within the stated limit.
L385: 
L386: All attachments and exhibits must be electronically bookmarked. Any citation to a specific section or page in an attachment or exhibit must have its own unique bookmark. Filings that do not contain electronic bookmarks may be denied on this basis.
L387: ### Ex Parte Applications for Appointment of Temporary Conservator:
L388:   * The petition for temporary conservatorship must be submitted with or after the filing of a general petition for conservatorship.
L389:   * The ex parte will be set for hearing one week after the date of submission of the ex parte documents.
L390:   * A completed Notice of Hearing (Judicial Council form GC-020) is required and must have been personally served, together with a copy of the petition, on the proposed conservatee at least 5 court days before the date of the ex parte proceedings. (Probate Code sections 2250, 2250(e)).
L391:   * If seeking to shorten or waive the required notice, applicants must follow California Rules of Court, rule 7.1062, and a separate declaration must be submitted detailing the immediate and substantial harm to the proposed person and/or estate that could occur during the notice period if the 5 days’ required notice were to be given.
L393:   * The petition for temporary guardian must be submitted with or after the filing of a general petition for guardianship.
L394:   * The ex parte will be set for hearing one week after the date of submission of the ex parte documents.
L395:   *  A completed Notice of Hearing (Judicial Council form GC-020) is required and must have been personally served together with a copy of the petition, on the minor, if the minor is 12 years or over, at least 5 court days before the date of the ex parte proceeding.
L400:   * Parties must comply with Local Rule 603.01 of the Superior Court of Orange County.
L401:   *  Any ex parte petition for Letters of Special Administration must be submitted with or after the filing of a petition for appointment of a general personal representative.
L402:   *  A completed Notice of Hearing (Judicial Council form DE-120) and an order must be submitted with the ex parte petition for Letters of Administration
L403:   *  An ex parte petition for Letters of Special Administration with General Powers requires 15 days’ notice and publication. (Probate Code sections 8545(a), 8003.)
L404:   *  A request for IAEA Powers with Letters of Special Administration also requires a request for General Powers. (Probate Code section 10405.)

L341: Pursuant to Code of Civil Procedure section 1010.6, California Rules of Court, rules 2.252 and 2.253, and Orange County Superior Court Local Rule 601.01, all documents in Probate Division proceedings other than the original documents specified on the Court’s website (cite316†www.occourts.org†gcc02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com ) must be filed electronically. Original documents must be filed in the Clerk’s Office by the close of business on a court day.
L342: Self-represented parties are exempt from the mandatory electronic filing requirement, but are encouraged to participate in electronic filing and service. If a party with a fee waiver files documents electronically, that party is exempt from the fees and costs associated with electronic filing.  The electronic filing of documents must be effected using the Court’s electronic service providers.
L343: Electronic service provider information is available on the Court’s website (cite317†www.occourts.org†gcc02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com ).
L344: Electronic filing in mental health cases is encouraged but is not required.
