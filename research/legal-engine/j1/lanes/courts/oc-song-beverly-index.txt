IT IS HEREBY STIPULATED BY AND BETWEEN THE PARTIES AS FOLLOWS:

The parties recognize that cases litigating discovery disputes in Song-Beverly Act violations often involve similar or common issues.

The parties further acknowledge that after a discovery dispute arises, there is often a necessary, but unavoidable delay between the filing of a discovery motion and the hearing on that motion due to the high volume of cases on the Court’s inventory and high volume of motions on the Court’s law and motion calendar.
