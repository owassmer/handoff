# Orange County Board of Supervisors Speaker Portal

Welcome to the Orange County Board of Supervisors Speaker Registration Portal! Thank you for your interest in participating in the Board meetings. We appreciate your engagement and look forward to hearing your comments.

The Orange County Board of Supervisors Meetings are held at the County Administration Center, 400 West Civic Center Drive, Santa Ana, California, 92701. Members of the public can pre-register to request an opportunity to speak - either in-person or virtually (by phone) - by using the Orange County Board of Supervisors Speaker Portal. The portal opens one (1) hour prior to the commencement of the meeting. Members of the public have three (3) opportunities to speak. Agendas and supporting documents are available online at https://board.oc.gov/meetings-agendas.

We've created two informational videos covering both in-person and remote participation. 

  * Speaking In Person at a Board of Supervisors Meeting:  https://youtu.be/VIrxh8xLOHQ 
This can delay the submission of your request to speak.
  * SELECT UP TO 3 ITEMS: Choose the agenda item(s) you would like to speak on, or choose Public Comments. Requests to speak on agenda items that have been continued or deleted will not be accepted.
  * INTERPRETER: If you select to attend ‘In Person’, you will be asked if you would like to request an interpreter. Please select your preferred language. Currently, remote interpretation is not available.
  * COMPLETE REGISTRATION: Click ‘Register’.

​​​​​

If you do not want to speak at a Board Meeting, there are other ways you can participate in Board meetings: 

  * To listen to a live Board meeting by telephone, please call (866) 590-5055, Access Code: 4138489.
  * To view the live stream of a Board meeting, visit https://board.ocgov.com/meetings-agendas.
  * To send comments to upcoming Board meetings, email comments@cob.oc.gov. Comments submitted before the start of Board meetings will be part of the public record and distributed to the Board members for their consideration.

 

For more information, please visit the Board of Supervisors Meeting Agendas website at https://board.oc.gov/meetings-agendas or contact the Clerk of the Board's office at (714) 834-2206.

##### Vice Chair Katrina Foley Announces 32% Decrease in Homelessness in District 5 | Fifth District Supervisor Katrina Foley

ORANGE COUNTY, CALIFORNIA – Today, the County of Orange and the Orange County Continuum of Care (CoC) announced the results of the 2026 Point-in-Time (PIT) Count. Compared to the 2024 PIT Count, Orange County recorded a 13.7% decrease in the number of people experiencing homelessness countywide. For the first time, shelters and transitional housing programs housed more individuals than streets, encampments, and other places not intended for human habitation.
