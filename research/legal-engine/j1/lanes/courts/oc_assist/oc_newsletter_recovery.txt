Effective September 29, 2026, at 12:01 a.m., Eastern Time, certain covered products of Canada identified in the Annexes to Presidential Proclamations 11061, 11062, and 11063 are excluded from importation into the United States. Exclusion from importation precludes covered products from admission into a Foreign Trade Zone or bonded warehouse, in-bond transportation, and entry for consumption. As of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on September 29, 2026, any entry containing covered products will be unconditionally rejected by U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP). The attached annexes outline the list of HTSUS classifications subject to exclusion. Covered products imported prior to September 29, 2026, 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time, remain eligible to be entered for consumption. Such covered products, if entered into a bonded warehouse or admitted into a Foreign Trade Zone prior to exclusion from importation going into effect, may be withdrawn for consumption and will be subject to the 50 percent ad valorem duty under Presidential Proclamations 11046, 11047, and 11048, as applicable. The attached annexes outline the list of HTSUS classifications subject to exclusion. As of 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on September 29, 2026, covered products will be unconditionally rejected and denied entry by CBP. Entries filed in ACE which contain covered products that have not been released will be cancelled as of September 29, 2026 at 12:01 a.m. Covered products include certain commodities under Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS) headings 0404, 1702, 1703, 2202, 2203, 2204, 2205, 2206, 2207, 2208, and 8711. Alcoholic Beverages
For alcoholic beverages, the import exclusion applies only where the merchandise meets any applicable scope limitation listed in the Alcohol Annex. Where the scope limitation states “Packaged,” the exclusion applies only to products packaged in bottles, cans, boxes, kegs, or other similar direct-to-consumption containers. Alcoholic beverages that do not meet an applicable scope limitation are not subject to the import exclusion and remain subject to the applicable 50 percent ad valorem duty under Proclamation 11046. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Seattle Office of Labor Standards Reaches Settlement with Instacart for Alleged Violations of App-Based Minimum Payment Law

Seattle, WA (September 24, 2026) – The Office of Labor Standards (OLS) announces a settlement with Maplebear Inc. dba Instacart for alleged violations of the App-Based Worker Minimum Payment (ABWMP) Ordinance. Instacart agreed to settle the allegations for a total of $149,786.22 in back pay, interest, liquidated damages, and civil penalties to 846 affected workers. Under the ABWMP Ordinance, OLS alleged that Instacart failed to provide the mandatory ABWMP minimum payment for orders originating from two restaurants situated within Seattle city limits near the Shoreline boundary. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
