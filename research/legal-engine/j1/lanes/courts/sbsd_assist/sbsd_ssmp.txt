L8@P0: THE SUNSET BEACH SANITARY DISTRICT
L9@P0: Properties within the boundaries of the Sunset Beach Sanitary District (the “District”)
L10@P0: receive services from the District for the collection, treatment and disposal of sewage
L11@P0: and trash. The District is primarily funded by revenue from a share of the County of
L12@P0: Orange property tax basic levy, as well as the service fees charged for each residential and commercial unit which is collected on the annual tax bill. There are three components of this service fee; the trash pick-up fee of $240 per residential and commercial unit, the sewer transport and treatment fee of $200 per each residential and commercial unit, and sewage cleaning fee of $475 per restaurant unit. The total service
L13@P0: fee charged to each parcel is based on the number of units assigned. Commercial
L14@P0: sewer units are calculated based on water consumption and the sewage cleaning fee
L15@P0: is only charged to restaurants.
L16@P0: FEES BEING CONSIDERED FOR INCREASE
L17@P0: The District’s annual per-unit service fees have not been increased since 2016. The
L18@P0: District is proposing to increase the annual trash fee by $60 (from $240 per unit
L19@P0: to $300 per unit), the annual sewer transport and treatment fee by $100 (from
L20@P0: $200 per unit to $300 per unit), and the annual restaurant sewage cleaning fee
L21@P0: by $150 (from $475 per unit to $625 per unit).
L22@P0: The total service fee for a residential or commercial unit is currently $440 ($240 for
L23@P0: trash pick-up and $200 for sewer transport and treatment). If the proposed increase is
L24@P0: approved, the total service fee for a residential or commercial unit is estimated at
L25@P0: $600 ($300 for trash pick-up and $300 for sewer transport and treatment). The total
L26@P0: per-unit service fee for a restaurant is estimated at $1,225 ($300 for trash pickup (if
L27@P0: no dumpster), $300 per unit based on water usage for sewer transport and treatment,
L28@P0: and $625 for sewer cleaning fee).
L29@P0: The District’s cost of service is among the lowest in the area in comparison to other
L30@P0: agencies within Orange County. Properties within the District do not pay the $358
L31@P0: ($371 in 2024-25) service fee levied by the Orange County Sanitation District
L32@P0: (OCSD). In addition, properties within the District do not pay the trash and sewage
L33@P0: charges collected on water bills by the Cities, which average more than $450 per
L34@P0: home per year. The proposed total service fee of $600 for a residential unit within the
L35@P0: District is more than 25% less when compared to fees paid by property owners outside the District (approximately $821), for the same services. Furthermore, the District collects garbage twice a week, while City agencies collect garbage only once per
L36@P0: week.
L37@P0: BASIS UPON WHICH THE AMOUNT OF THE PROPOSED FEE WAS CALCULATED
L38@P0: Nearly all of the sewage facilities were built in 1935-36, and are now more than 80-
L39@P0: years old. The District has made continuous repairs to the facilities to maintain a safe
L40@P0: and reliable system. The majority of the District’s expenses are paid to other agencies for contracted services, such as sewage treatment. These costs increase each
L41@P0: year with inflation and the added cost of new requirements, such as full secondary
L42@P0: treatment for sewage and changes to contractor prevailing wage requirements. The
L43@P0: proposed increase will help provide funding for high-priority sewage facilities replacements and upgrades needed to support the continued provision of safe and reliable
L44@P0: sewer service. Trash Fees are being increased in order to cover per unit fees charged
L45@P0: by the District's contracted trash collector.
