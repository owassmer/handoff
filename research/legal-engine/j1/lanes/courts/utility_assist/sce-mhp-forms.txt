L23@P1: In accordance with California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC or Commission) Decision (D.)
L24@P1: 14-03-021, and subject to the requirements of the Mobilehome Park Utility Upgrade Program Rule
L25@P1: (MHP Program Rule)1, the Commission-regulated electric and natural gas utilities (Utilities) are offering
L26@P1: a Mobilehome Utility Upgrade Park Program (MHP Program) to replace “to-the-meter and beyond-themeter” existing privately owned master-metered/sub-metered electric and/or gas distribution service
L27@P1: within a Mobilehome Park or Manufactured Housing Communities (MHP), to direct distribution and
L28@P1: Utility service to each individual existing MHP space within the MHP.
L29@P1: MHP Owners/Operators who are receiving this Application previously submitted the CPUC’s Form of
L30@P1: Intent during the open application period. After reviewing the information you submitted, the CPUC’s
L31@P1: Safety and Enforcement Division (SED) and/or by the California Department of Housing and
L32@P1: Community Development (HCD) or its local agency designee has pre-selected your MHP to participate
L33@P1: in the MHP Program.
L34@P1: The MHP Owner/Operator must designate below each Utility2 that currently provides electric and/or
L35@P1: natural gas service to the master meter of the MHP. The designated Utilities will be responsible for the
L36@P1: conversion of the existing privately owned master-metered/sub-metered system to direct Utility service,
L37@P1: upon CPUC acceptance of the MHP into the MHP Program. Under the MHP Program, each Utility will
L38@P1: only provide service conversion for the commodity (electricity and/or natural gas) that the Utility
L39@P1: currently provides to the MHP. After the completion of the service conversion, the Utility will provide
L40@P1: direct service to each individual Mobilehome space and the MHP’s common areas. Upon request, the
L41@P1: Utility may provide to the MHP a new electric or gas utility service that is not currently being supplied by
L42@P1: the Utility, provided that (1) the Utility offers the requested electric or natural gas service in that territory
L43@P1: (2) a distribution line is located nearby and can be connected safely and economically to the MHP; and
L44@P1: (3) the request would be governed by the existing Distribution and Service Extension Rules in the
L45@P1: Utility’s Tariff and would not be included in the MHP Program.
