## Statutory successor — AB299

[AB299, 2025 chapter 531](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billTextClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB299), adds CIV 1954.071 as an urgency statute effective October 10, 2025. It makes a disaster-displaced qualifying lodging guest's tenancy threshold 270 days, with written notice, confirmation and vacation-notice conditions and exceptions. The section repeals January 1, 2031. The statute applies by its statewide qualifying-disaster/lodging facts; it is not limited to LA lodging as N23 paragraph 4 was. It does not itself preserve the executive transient-occupancy-tax suspension. No continuous bridge from October 1 to October 10 is inferred. Current code already represents the codified section; `statutory_proposals.json` offers the enactment as a concrete event-time/sunset exception if root chooses to retain the historical transition separately.

## Wildfire statutory crosslink requested by local lane

[SB1370, 2026 chapter 777](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB1370), approved September 29, 2026, is the consolidated/expedited wildfire-review statute identified in the Governor's September 29 legislation announcement. Already selected in the enactment register, it adds PRC4240–4243 and PRC4745–4749. It has no urgency clause; ordinary GOV9600 effectiveness is January 1, 2027. Expedited-authorizations issuance authority becomes inoperative January 1, 2029; its chapter repeals January 1, 2034. Nontimber-program emergency regulations are due July 1, 2028, with later permanent-rule and reporting requirements; that article repeals January 1, 2035. Local and federal compliance remain conditions. The new statute must not be treated as a current universal waiver, or used to erase N38-25's separate project-initiation deadline. The proposal adds these temporal details and the successful actual-text Nav URL to the already selected instrument.
