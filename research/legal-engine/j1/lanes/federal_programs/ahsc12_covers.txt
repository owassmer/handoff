L98@P3: Article I. General
L99@P3: Section 100. Purpose and Scope
L100@P3: (a) The purpose of these Program Guidelines is to implement Division 44, Part 1 of the
L101@P3: Public Resources Code (PRC) (commencing with Section 75200), which establishes the
L102@P3: Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities (AHSC) Program, hereinafter referred
L103@P3: to as the AHSC Program.
L104@P3: (b) The purpose of the AHSC Program is to reduce greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions
L105@P3: through projects that implement land-use, housing, transportation, and agricultural land
L106@P3: preservation practices to support infill and compact development, and that support
L107@P3: related and coordinated public policy objectives, including the following:
L108@P3: (1) reducing air pollution;
L109@P3: (2) improving conditions in disadvantaged communities;
L110@P3: (3) supporting or improving public health and other co-benefits as defined in Section
L111@P3: 39712 of the Health and Safety Code;
L112@P3: (4) improving connectivity and accessibility to jobs, housing, and services;
L113@P3: (5) increasing options for mobility, including the implementation of the Active
L114@P3: Transportation Program established pursuant to Section 2380 of the Streets and
L115@P3: Highway Code;
L116@P3: (6) increasing transit ridership;
L117@P3: (7) preserving and developing affordable housing for lower income households, as
L118@P3: defined in Section 50079.5 of the Health and Safety Code; and
L119@P3: (8) protecting agricultural lands to support infill development.
