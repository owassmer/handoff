L41@P2: Article I. Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities
L42@P2: General Overview
L43@P2: Section 100. Purpose and Scope
L44@P2: The purpose of these Program Guidelines is to implement Division 44, Part 1 Chapter 2 of
L45@P2: the California Public Resources Code (PRC) (commencing with Section 75200), which
L46@P2: establishes the Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities (AHSC) Program, hereinafter referred to as the AHSC Program.
L47@P2: Per the California Public Resources Code, the purpose of the AHSC Program is to reduce
L48@P2: greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions through Projects that implement land use, housing,
L49@P2: transportation, and Agricultural Land preservation practices to support infill and compact
L50@P2: development, and that support related and coordinated public policy objectives, including the
L51@P2: following:
L52@P2: • Reducing air pollution;
L53@P2: • Improving conditions in Disadvantaged Communities (DACs);
L54@P2: • Supporting or improving public health and other co-benefits as defined in Section
L55@P2: 39712 of the Health and Safety Code;
L56@P2: • Improving connectivity and accessibility to jobs, housing, and services;
L57@P2: • Increasing options for mobility, including the implementation of the Active
L58@P2: Transportation Program established pursuant to Section 2380 of the Streets and
L59@P2: Highway Code;
L60@P2: • Increasing transit ridership;
L61@P2: • Preserving and developing affordable housing for lower income households, as
L62@P2: defined in Section 50079.5 of the Health and Safety Code; and
L63@P2: • Protecting Agricultural Lands to support infill development.
