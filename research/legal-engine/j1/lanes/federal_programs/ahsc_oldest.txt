L36@P2: Article I. AHSC General Overview
L37@P2: Section 100. Purpose and Scope
L38@P2: (a) The purpose of these Program Guidelines is to implement Division 44, Part 1 of the
L39@P2: Public Resources Code (PRC) (commencing with Section 75200), which establishes
L40@P2: the Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities (AHSC) Program, hereinafter
L41@P2: referred to as the AHSC Program.
L42@P2: (b) The purpose of the AHSC Program is to reduce greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions
L43@P2: through projects that implement land-use, housing, transportation, and agricultural
L44@P2: land preservation practices to support infill and compact development, and that
L45@P2: support related and coordinated public policy objectives, including the following:
L46@P2: (1) reducing air pollution;
L47@P2: (2) improving conditions in disadvantaged communities;
L48@P2: (3) supporting or improving public health and other co-benefits as defined in Section
L49@P2: 39712 of the Health and Safety Code;
L50@P2: (4) improving connectivity and accessibility to jobs, housing, and services;
L51@P2: (5) increasing options for mobility, including the implementation of the Active
L52@P2: Transportation Program established pursuant to Section 2380 of the Streets and
L53@P2: Highway Code;
L54@P2: (6) increasing transit ridership;
L55@P2: (7) preserving and developing affordable housing for lower income households, as
L56@P2: defined in Section 50079.5 of the Health and Safety Code; and
L57@P2-3: (8) protecting agricultural lands to support infill development.FY 2018-19 AHSC Program Guidelines - 4 - October 31, 2019
L58@P3: Section 101. AHSC Program Overview
L59@P3: The AHSC Program furthers the purposes of AB 32 (Chapter 488, Statues 2006) and
L60@P3: SB 375 (Chapter 728, Statutes, 2008) by investing in projects that reduce GHG emissions
L61@P3: by supporting more compact, infill development patterns, encouraging active
L62@P3: transportation and transit usage, and protecting agricultural land from sprawl
L63@P3: development. The Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund (GGRF), an account established to
L64@P3: receive proceeds from Cap-and-Trade auctions, provides funding for the AHSC
L65@P3: Program. The Cap-and-Trade Program, a key strategy for achieving the GHG emission
L66@P3: reduction goals of AB 32, issues a limited number of GHG emissions permits (called
L67@P3: allowances) each year. A portion of these allowances can be purchased from the State at
L68@P3: quarterly auctions, thereby generating auction proceeds. These State auction proceeds
L69@P3: are then deposited in the GGRF, where they become available for appropriation by the
L70@P3: Legislature to further the purposes of AB 32.
L71@P3: The AHSC Program is administered by the Strategic Growth Council (Council). The
L72@P3: Department of Housing and Community Development (Department) will implement
L73@P3: the transportation, housing and infrastructure components of the AHSC Program. The
L74@P3: Council staff will coordinate efforts with Department staff, working with the California Air
L75@P3: Resources Board (CARB) and the Council to administer the broader AHSC Program,
L76@P3: including developing program guidelines, evaluating applications, preparing agreements,
L77@P3: monitoring agreement implementation, and program reporting.
L78@P3: ▪ The Council will coordinate with CARB to develop and incorporate consistent
L79@P3: guidance in the following areas, which will apply to all GGRF programs, including the
L80@P3: AHSC Program:
L81@P3: ▪ Expenditure records to ensure investments further the goals of AB 32.
L82@P3: ▪ SB 535 (Chapter 830, Statutes 2012) and AB 1550 requirements to maximize benefits
L83@P3: to Disadvantaged Communities, Low-Income Communities, and Low-Income
L84@P3: Households.
L85@P3: ▪ Consistent methodologies for quantifying GHG reductions and other economic,
L86@P3: environmental and public health co-benefits.
L87@P3: ▪ Project tracking and reporting.
L88@P3: The AHSC Program provides grants and/or loans to projects that achieve GHG emission
L89@P3: reductions and benefit Disadvantaged Communities, Low-Income Communities, and
L90@P3: Low-Income Households through increasing accessibility of affordable housing,
L91@P3: employment centers and Key Destinations via low-carbon transportation resulting in
L92@P3: fewer vehicle miles traveled (VMT) through shortened or reduced vehicle trip length or
L93@P3: mode shift to transit, bicycling or walking. Three Project Area types have been identified
L94@P3: to implement this strategy: 1) Transit Oriented Development (TOD) Project Areas, or 2)
L95@P3: Integrated Connectivity Project (ICP) Project Areas, or 3) Rural Innovation Project Areas
L96@P3: (RIPA).
L97@P3: AHSC awards funds will be allocated through a competitive process, based on the merits
L98@P3: of applications submitted and the proposed use of funds within the identified Project
L99@P3: Area. The threshold requirements and application selection criteria focus on the extent to
L100@P3-4: which developments realize the AHSC Program’s objectives of reducing GHG emissions, FY 2018-19 AHSC Program Guidelines - 5 - October 31, 2019
L101@P4: benefiting Disadvantaged Communities, Low-Income Communities, and Low-Income
L102@P4: Households, providing affordable housing, demonstrating project readiness, and meeting
L103@P4: other policy considerations.
L104@P4: Disadvantaged Community Benefits
L105@P4: In June 2018, using the updated results from CalEnviroScreen 3.0, the California
L106@P4: Environmental Protection Agency (CalEPA) identified Disadvantaged Communities to
L107@P4: include census tracts that fall within the top 25 percent of CalEnviroScreen 3.0, plus an
L108@P4: additional 22 census tracts that score in the highest 5 percent of CalEnviroScreen’s
L109@P4: Pollution Burden but do not have an overall CalEnviroScreen score because of unreliable
L110@P4: socioeconomic or health data. AB 1550 has also created investment requirements for
L111@P4: Low-Income Communities and Low-Income Households. In July 2018, CARB
L112@P4: approved the Funding Guidelines for Agencies Administering California Climate
L113@P4: Investments that will provide criteria to evaluate whether a project provides a benefit to a
L114@P4: Disadvantaged Community, Low-Income Community, or Low-Income Household.
L115@P4: These guidelines can be found here: www.arb.ca.gov/ccifundingguidelines.
L116@P4: A Project that is located in and provides benefits to a Disadvantaged Community, LowIncome Community, or Low-Income Households may receive priority for funding in
L117@P4: order to meet the AHSC Program Disadvantaged Community and Low-Income
L118@P4: Community funding requirements. All applicants must evaluate the criteria for
L119@P4: demonstrating a benefit to Priority Populations as part of the application, and, if
L120@P4: applicable, demonstrate in the application how the Capital Project or Program Costs
L121@P4-5: within the Project meets one of the criteria.FY 2018-19 AHSC Program Guidelines - 6 - October 31, 2019
