L10@P1: Audited financial statements are one of the primary tools used by both the
L11@P1: California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD)
L12@P1: and the California Housing Finance Agency (CalHFA) to meet their fiduciary
L13@P1: responsibilities in overseeing housing programs, bond requirements, and assuring
L14@P1: the integrity of their respective multifamily housing portfolios. The areas of
L15@P1: noncompliance and internal control weaknesses noted in these statements will
L16@P1: be addressed by the respective HCD or CalHFA Asset Management staff as
L17@P1: appropriate.
L18@P1: This Audited Financial Statements Handbook for Multifamily Rental Housing
L19@P1: (Audit Handbook) is the result of a joint effort by HCD and CalHFA to coordinate
L20@P1: their audit requirements. The Audit Handbook has been managed and
L21@P1: developed by George Rodine (HCD) and the Asset Management Team at
L22@P1: CalHFA since its initial publication in 2013. The Audit Handbook and submission
L23@P1: requirements detailed herein are for HCD and CalHFA purposes only and the
L24@P1: Independent Certified Public Accountant (auditor) must be aware of and
L25@P1: file appropriate audit submissions as may be required by other governmental
L26@P1: agencies, investors, etc. in their formats. Audits are required to be submitted
L27@P1: to both HCD and CalHFA for jointly funded projects and to the respective
L28@P1: department for individually funded projects.
L29@P1: The Audit Handbook is neither intended to be a complete manual of
L30@P1: procedures, nor is it intended to supplant the auditor’s judgment of audit work
L31@P1: required. Suggested formats contained herein may not cover all circumstances
L32@P1: or conditions encountered in an audit. The auditor must use professional
L33@P1: judgment to add supplementary information and to determine the extent
L34@P1: of testing necessary to support the opinion in the financial statements. All
L35@P1: applicable compliance requirements in this Audit Handbook must be addressed
L36@P1: by the auditor.
L37@P1: The auditor may contact the respective HCD or CalHFA asset management
L38@P1: staff if technical assistance is needed pertaining to HCD or CalHFA programs,
L39@P1: regulations, or operations.
L40@P1: HCD and CalHFA staff continue to be receptive to suggestions and appreciate
L41@P1: the help provided by the accounting profession.

L77@P3: Regulatory Agreement
L78@P3: Each owner participating in various HCD or CalHFA programs is required to sign an HCD or
L79@P3: CalHFA Regulatory Agreement(s) or in some instances an FHA Regulatory Agreement with
L80@P3: CalHFA amendments, addendums, or modifications, as well as other documents relating
L81@P3: to the financing and affordability of the project. The contents of the agreements from
L82@P3: one project to another may vary due to statutory and regulatory changes, specific HCD
L83@P3: or CalHFA program requirements, and/or special project conditions or requirements. The
L84@P3: auditor must obtain a copy of the applicable HCD or CalHFA Regulatory Agreement(s)
L85@P3: and other agreements including, but not limited to, the Capitalized Operating Subsidy
L86@P3: Reserve Agreement (COSRA), promissory note, deed of trust, and other relevant regulatory
L87@P3: information. Once obtained, the auditor must review the terms and conditions, and
L88@P3: document findings of material non-compliance in the accompanying information of the
L89@P3: audited financial statements. Financing arrangements may involve another agency’s
L90@P3: requirements that interact with HCD or CalHFA. Instances of non-compliant financing
L91@P3: arrangements must be disclosed in the audit.

L143@P5: project financial condition without substantial corrections or adjustments.

L146@P5: financial statements within 90 days of the project’s fiscal year ending date. The submitted

L148@P5: accuracy of the financial statements and a signed certification by the management agent,

L157@P5: this Audit Handbook.
