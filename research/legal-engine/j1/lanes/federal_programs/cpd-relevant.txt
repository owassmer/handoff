L55@P1: OVERVIEW
L56@P1: CDBG regulations require grantees to carry out eligible activities that meet a national
L57@P1: objective (24 CFR 570.200(a)(2); 24 CFR 570.483). The CDBG overall benefit requirement
L58@P1: mandates that at least 70 percent of funds over a one-, two-, or three-year period be used for
L59@P1: activities benefiting low- and moderate-income (LMI) persons (24 CFR 570.200(a)(3); 24 CFR
L60@P1: 570.484). Grantees may demonstrate that an activity meets the LMI benefit by: (1) providing
L61@P1: benefits on an area basis; (2) benefiting a limited clientele; (3) providing housing to households;
L62@P1: or (4) creating or retaining jobs. To ensure LMI benefit, CDBG grantees may need to collect and
L63@P1: calculate family or household income data in accordance with the HCDA and applicable
L64@P1: regulations. CDBG regulations provide multiple options for calculating family or household
L65@P1: income data.
L66@P1: On January 20, 2026, Congress amended Section 102(a)(20) of the HCDA to add the
L67@P1: following language: “(C) SERVICE-CONNECTED DISABILITY COMPENSATION. — when
L68@P1: determining whether a person is a person of low and moderate income, a person of low income,
L69@P1: or a person of moderate income under this paragraph, a State, unit of general local government,
L70@P1: or Indian tribe shall exclude any service-connected disability compensation received by such
L71@P1: person from the Department of Veterans Affairs.”
L72@P1: To ensure compliance with this new requirement, HUD recommends that grantees update
L73@P1: program policies and procedures and all documents related to income determinations, train staff,
L74@P1: inform and train subrecipients and non-entitlement units of general local government (for States),
L75@P1: and update all applicable forms.

L81@P1: Section 8 income definition in 24 CFR 5.609; and (3) Census definition. Grantees may select
L82@P1: any of these definitions for a given program or activity. The HCDA now requires grantees to
L83@P1: exclude VA service-connected disability compensation from annual income calculations,
