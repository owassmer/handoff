L25@P0: The Secondary Tenants Policy establishes a petition process to temporarily redesignate a portion of a

L27@P0: Supportive Housing units, as further detailed below.
L28@P0: Background
L29@P0: Assembly Bill 1386 (Chapter 760, Statutes 2023), codified as Military and Veterans Code sections
L30@P0: 987.003 and 987.005, provided for a petition process to temporarily redesignate extremely low-income
L31@P0: Supportive Housing units (Qualified Units) restricted for veterans experiencing homelessness to
L32@P0: Supportive Housing units restricted to veterans experiencing homelessness with incomes up to 60%
L33@P0: AMI. The bill was enacted to resolve issues with matching unhoused veterans with vacant Supportive
L34@P0: Housing units restricted to veterans, while retaining the VHHP program’s original programmatic intent of
L35@P0: serving the highest need veterans experiencing homelessness. Assembly Bill 535 (Chapter 918,
L36@P0: Statutes 2024) clarifies temporary income requirements flexibility for Qualified Units and excludes
L37@P0: service-connected disability benefits from the determination of income eligibility. In accordance with the
L38@P0: requirements of these two bills the Department has established this VHHP Secondary Tenants Policy to

L42@P1: The Veterans Housing and Homeless Prevention Act of 2014 (“the Act”) requires the California Housing
L43@P1: Finance Agency, the California Department of Housing and Community Development, and the
L44@P1: Department of Veterans Affairs (referred to collectively as “the departments”) to establish programs that
L45@P1: focus on veterans at risk of homelessness or experiencing temporary or chronic homelessness, as
L46@P1: specified. The Act requires the departments to ensure at least 50% percent of funds awarded for capital
L47@P1: development are used to provide housing to veterans with adjusted gross incomes at or below 30%
L48@P1: AMI and that at least 60% of those units are Supportive Housing units, as defined.
L49@P1: The Secondary Tenants Policy establishes the petition process that VHHP project sponsors may use to
L50@P1: request authorization from the Department to place homeless veterans with incomes above 30% AMI in
L51@P1: Qualified Units, defined as Supportive Housing units restricted to 30% AMI households, and charge
L52@P1: commensurate rent. These tenants are named Secondary Tenants under the Veterans Housing and
L53@P1: Homelessness Prevention program. Secondary Tenants are defined in statute as veterans
L54@P1: experiencing homelessness with an income of up to 60% AMI. Rents for any redesignated units are
L55@P1: determined by income limits that the correspond to the Secondary Tenants’ household income.
L56@P1: Project sponsors are required to take specified actions to demonstrate a good faith effort to place
L57@P1: Qualified Tenants in Qualified Units and file a petition with the Department before they can place
L58@P1: Secondary Tenants in units otherwise designated for 30% AMI veterans upon approval of the filed
L59@P1: petition. Good faith efforts include: 1) adequate marketing of vacant units spanning at least 90 days
L60@P1: prior to the commencement of initial lease-up or at least 60 days after a unit turns over, 2) partnering
L61@P1: with local homeless and veterans service providers to identify Qualified Tenants, 3) coordination with
L62@P1: the local Continuum of Care to receive prioritized Qualified Tenant referrals, 4) coordination with the
L63@P1: U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs to identify Qualified Tenants, and 5) documented contacts with
L64@P1: Qualified Tenants, along with their case manager(s), who were referred to the project with vacant
L65@P1: Qualified Units and choose not to lease the unit. Project sponsors should provide documentation that
L66@P1: substantiates the timing and frequency of such good faith efforts, including the date that the Qualified
L67@P1: Entity was notified of the vacancy, whenever such documentation exists and provide a descriptive
L68@P1: timeline in the absence of documents to evidence activities such as phone calls and meetings.
