L104@P5: Article I – Program Overview
L105@P5: Section 100. Notice of Funding Availability (NOFA)
L106@P5: The California Department of Housing and Community Development (Department) is
L107@P5: pleased to announce the availability of approximately $736 million in Homekey funding
L108@P5: to sustain and rapidly expand the inventory of housing for people experiencing
L109@P5: homelessness or At Risk of Homelessness and who are, thereby, inherently impacted
L110@P5: by or at increased risk for medical diseases or conditions due to the COVID-19
L111@P5: pandemic or other communicable diseases. Before the COVID-19 pandemic,
L112@P5: homelessness data showed Black, Indigenous, and People of Color (BIPOC) were
L113@P5: overrepresented in the homelessness system. The pandemic made racial disparities
L114@P5: more apparent, and communities are dealing with the additional disproportionate impact
L115@P5: of illness and death among people experiencing homelessness. Homekey recognizes
L116@P5: these impacts and encourages Eligible Applicants to examine disproportionate impacts
L117@P5: in their own communities and to develop strategies to address these impacts.
L118@P5: Homekey is an opportunity for state, regional, and Local Public Entities to develop a
L119@P5: broad range of housing types, including but not limited to hotels, motels, hostels, singlefamily homes and multifamily apartments, adult residential facilities, manufactured
L120@P5: housing, and to convert commercial properties and other existing buildings to Permanent
L121@P5: or Interim Housing for the Target Population.
L122@P5: Of the $736 million in Homekey grant funds, $435 million is derived from the state's
L123@P5: direct allocation of the federal Coronavirus State Fiscal Recovery Fund (CSFRF), which
L124@P5: was established by the American Rescue Plan Act of 2021 (ARPA) (Pub.L. No. 117-2).
L125@P5: In addition, $301 million is derived from the state's General Fund to supplement the
L126@P5: acquisition of, and to provide initial operating subsidies for, Homekey sites.
L127@P5: Section 101. Authorizing Legislation and Applicable Law
L128@P5: Assembly Bill No.140 (2021-2022 Reg. Sess.) provided the statutory basis for Round 3
L129@P5: of the Homekey Program by adding section 50675.1.3 to the Health and Safety Code
L130@P5: (HSC), and it exempted certain Round 3 Homekey Projects from the California
L131@P5: Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) by adding section 50675.1.4 to the HSC. The
L132@P5: statutory scheme includes new construction of dwelling units as an eligible use and
L133@P5: establishes an allocation of funds for Projects serving Homeless Youth and Youth at
L134@P5: Risk of Homelessness.
L135@P5: HSC section 50675.1.3, subdivision (e) states, “The Department of Housing and
L136@P5: Community Development may adopt guidelines for the expenditure of the funds
L137@P5: appropriated to the Department, and for the administration of this program. The
L138@P5: guidelines shall not be subject to the requirements of Chapter 3.5 (commencing with
L139@P5: Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.”
L140@P5: This NOFA serves as the Department’s guidelines for the expenditure of Homekey
L141@P5: funds and the administration of the Homekey Program. As such, this NOFA establishes
L142@P5: the terms, conditions, forms, procedures, and other mechanisms that the Department
L143@P5: deems necessary to exercise its powers and to perform its duties pursuant to the
L144@P5-6: Homekey Program. The matters set forth herein are regulatory mandates and are California Department of Housing and Community Development
L145@P6: 2023 Homekey NOFA 7
L146@P6: adopted as regulations that have the dignity of statutes. (Ramirez v. Yosemite Water
L147@P6: Company, Inc. (1999) 20 Cal. 4th 785, 799 [85 Cal.Rptr.2d 844].)
L148@P6: The Multifamily Housing Program (MHP) (Chapter 6.7 (commencing with Section 50675)
L149@P6: of Part 2 of Division 31 of the HSC), and as subsequently amended, is hereby
L150@P6: incorporated by reference. In accordance with HSC section 50675.1.3, subdivision (d),
L151@P6: in the event of a conflict between this NOFA and the MHP, the provisions of this NOFA
L152@P6: are controlling.
L153@P6: The MHP Final Guidelines (MHP Guidelines), effective March 30, 2022, and as
L154@P6: subsequently amended, are hereby incorporated by reference. In the event of a conflict
L155@P6: between any of this NOFA and the MHP Guidelines, the provisions of this NOFA are
L156@P6: controlling.
L157@P6: The Uniform Multifamily Regulations (UMR) (Cal. Code Regs., tit. 25, § 8300 et seq.),
L158@P6: effective November 15, 2017, and as subsequently amended, are hereby incorporated
L159@P6: by reference, except to the extent that any UMR provision would be inconsistent with
L160@P6: the provisions of this NOFA.
L161@P6: The Department will only amend this NOFA as necessary and in accordance with the
L162@P6: Department's guideline authority pursuant to HSC section 50675.1.3, subdivision (e).
L163@P6: All other criteria and matters set forth within the NOFA shall also govern Tribal Entity
L164@P6: applications submitted under this NOFA, unless and except to the extent expressly
L165@P6: provided to the contrary by terms set forth within this NOFA and subject to any potential
L166@P6: modification or waiver under or pursuant to Assembly Bill No. 1010 (Stats.2019, c. 660),
L167@P6: which is set forth in HSC section 50406, subdivision (p).

L239@P8: Section 201. Eligible Uses
L240@P8: Awarded funds must be used to provide housing for the Target Population of individuals
L241@P8: and families experiencing Homelessness or who are At Risk of Homelessness and who
L242@P8: are inherently impacted by or at increased risk for medical diseases or conditions due to
L243@P8: the COVID-19 pandemic or other communicable diseases. For Grantees utilizing
L244@P8: HOME-ARP funds as match, the Target Population also includes individuals and
L245@P8: families who are “Fleeing, or Attempting to Flee, Domestic Violence, Dating Violence,
L246@P8: Sexual Assault, Stalking, or Human Trafficking” and “Other Populations” as defined in
L247@P8: HUD Community Planning and Development (CPD) Notice 21-10. With respect to the
L248@P8: list of eligible uses below, an Eligible Applicant may choose to target Project Roomkey
L249@P8: properties, or other, non-Project Roomkey properties. The list of eligible uses is as
L250@P8: follows:
L251@P8: i. Acquisition or Rehabilitation, or acquisition and Rehabilitation, of motels, hotels,
L252@P8: hostels, or other sites and assets, including apartments or homes, adult
L253@P8: residential facilities, residential care facilities for the elderly, manufactured
L254@P8: housing, commercial properties, and other buildings with existing uses that could
L255@P8: be converted to Permanent Housing or Interim Housing, subject to any limitations
L256@P8: set forth in this NOFA, including those provided in Section 301.
L257@P8: ii. Master leasing of properties for non-congregate housing.
L258@P8: iii. Conversion of units from nonresidential to residential.
L259@P8-9: iv. New construction of dwelling units.California Department of Housing and Community Development

L261@P9: v. The purchase of affordability covenants and restrictions for units.
L262@P9: vi. Relocation costs for individuals who are being displaced as a result of the
