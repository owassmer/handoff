STAFF REPORT 
Board Meeting of March 18, 2026 
 
 
AGENDA ITEM: 
4-F 
PREPARED BY: 
Sandy Ebersole, Administrative Analyst 
 
 
SUBJECT: 
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities (AHSC) Round 10 Guidelines Approved 
Enclosure: No 
Action: Information and Discussion Only 
 
SUMMARY: 
The California Strategic Growth Council (SGC) approved the Affordable Housing and 
Sustainable Communities (AHSC) Round 10 Guidelines.  
The Guidelines are now posted on the AHSC webpage along with supplemental application 
material and the Notice of Funding Availability (NOFA). The application period will remain 
open for 60 days thereafter.  
Visit SGC’s webpage and the California Housing and Community Development’s AHSC 
webpage for further updates and to access the NOFA. AHSC will also host two in-
person NOFA workshops in the coming weeks and make available a virtual 
recording. Additional information, including workshop dates and details, will be posted 
online. 
The AHSC Round 10 supplemental application materials include required application 
components and instructional resources such as the Narrative Response Rubric, Project Area 
Mapping Guidance and video tutorial, Greenhouse Gas Calculator Tool video tutorial, and 
other reference materials. Applicants may submit questions about the Guidelines, application 
requirements, and the Round 10 process. Responses to submitted inquiries are typically 
provided within four business days.  
 
FISCAL IMPACT: 
No fiscal impact on the approved 2025-26 Overall Work Program and Budget.--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
