L82@P3: ARTICLE I. GENERAL OVERVIEW
L83@P3: Section 100. Purpose and Scope
L84@P3: (a) These CalHome Program Final Guidelines (the “Guidelines”) implement and
L85@P3: interpret, Chapter 6 (commencing with §50650) of Part 2 of Division 31, Health and
L86@P3: Safety Code (H.S.C.), which establishes the CalHome Program administered by
L87@P3: the California Department of Housing and Community Development (the
L88@P3: “Department” or HCD) and Chapter 2 (commencing with §54006) of Part 16 of
L89@P3: Division 31, H.S.C..
L90@P3: (b) These Guidelines implement Assembly Bill (AB) 434 (Chapter 192, Statutes of
L91@P3: 2020), which amends H.S.C. §50517.5. AB 434 requires the Department to
L92@P3: harmonize the Joe Serna, Jr. Farmworker Housing Grant Program (the “Serna
L93@P3: Program”) with the CalHome Program in the following four respects: (1) Serna
L94@P3: Program funds are to be made available at the same time as CalHome Program
L95@P3: funds; (2) Serna Program applications are to be rated and ranked in a manner
L96@P3: consistent with CalHome Program applications; (3) Serna Program funds are to be
L97@P3: administered consistent with the CalHome Program, and; (4) Serna Program Loan
L98@P3: terms are to be consistent with CalHome Program Loan terms to the greatest
L99@P3: extent possible.
L100@P3: (c) These Guidelines establish terms, conditions and procedures for the Award and
L101@P3: disbursement of funds allocated to the CalHome Program pursuant to H.S.C.
L102@P3: §54006(g).
L103@P3: (d) These Guidelines apply only to funds made available through H.S.C. §54006(g) and
L104@P3: do not apply to funds made available to the CalHome Program authorized by Chapter 6
L105@P3: (commencing with §50650) prior to the adoption of H.S.C. §54006(g).
L106@P3: (e) The requirements set forth in these Guidelines are subject to AB 1010 (Stats.2019,
L107@P3: c. 660), which is set forth in H.S.C. §50406, subdivision (p). Accordingly, and
L108@P3: pursuant to H.S.C. §50406, subdivision (p), (a) where the provisions of tribal law,
L109@P3: tribal governance, tribal charter, or difference in Tribal Entity or agency legal
L110@P3: structure would cause a violation or not satisfy the requirements of these
L111@P3: Guidelines, said requirements may be modified as necessary to ensure program
L112@P3: compatibility; and (b) where provisions of tribal law, tribal governance, tribal
L113@P3: charter, or difference in Tribal Entity or agency legal structure create minor
L114@P3: inconsistencies (as determined by the Director of the Department or a duly
L115@P3: authorized designee thereof) with the requirements set forth in these Guidelines,
L116@P3: the Department may waive said requirements, as deemed necessary, to avoid an
L117@P3: unnecessary administrative burden. Matters set forth or otherwise provided for in
L118@P3: these Guidelines that may be modified or waived include, without limitation,
L119@P3: threshold requirements and any other matters set forth in H.S.C. §50406,
L120@P3: subdivision (p)(2). Please note that AB 1010 does not automatically operate to
L121@P3: obviate, or otherwise modify, any provision(s) of these Guidelines. To the
L122@P3: extent that a Tribal Entity applicant believes that AB 1010 is applicable to such
L123@P3: Tribal Entity, their Application, the Project to which their Application relates, or any
L124@P3-4: number of them, it is recommended that such Tribal Entity applicant submit an AB Department of Housing and Community Development Page 2 of 58

L141@P4: To be eligible to apply for funding, the Applicant shall be a Local Public Entity, Local
L142@P4: Public Agency, Tribal Entity, or Nonprofit Corporation. Appendix B of these Guidelines
L143@P4: contains more details on tribal eligibility.
L144@P4: (a) Geographic restrictions
L145@P4: (1) A Local Public Entity that is not a Tribal Entity shall only be eligible to apply
L146@P4: for an Award of CalHome Program funds for a Local Program or project
L147@P4: located within its jurisdictional boundaries. A Tribal Entity shall be eligible to
L148@P4: apply for an award of CalHome program funds for a Local Program or project
L149@P4: located anywhere within the state of California, as long as the Tribal Entity
L150@P4: has provided documentation establishing site control.
L151@P4: (2) A Nonprofit Corporation or Tribally Designated Housing Entity shall only be
L152@P4: eligible to apply for an Award of CalHome Program funds for a program or
L153@P4: project if it has developed a project or operated a housing program in
L154@P4: California within the past two years or if the Nonprofit Corporation or Tribally
L155@P4: Designated Housing Entity has an existing 523 Self-Help Technical
L156@P4: Assistance Grant Agreement with the United States Department of Agriculture
L157@P4: (USDA).
