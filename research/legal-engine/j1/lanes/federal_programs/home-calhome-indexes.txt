L40: 
L41: Background
L42: 
L43: The HOME Investment Partnerships Program (HOME) was established under the Federal HOME Investment Partnerships Act Statutes at Title II of the Cranston-Gonzalez National Affordable Housing Act, as amended, 42 U.S.C. 12701 et seq.
L44: 
L45: Eligible Project Activities
L46: 
L47:   * Rental New Construction
L48:   * Rental Rehabilitation, with or without acquisition
L49:   * Rental Acquisition Only (if no rehabilitation is needed)
L50:   * First-Time Homebuyer (FTHB) subdivision construction projects
L51: Eligible Applicants
L52:   * Cities and counties for projects within their jurisdictional areas, as long as they do not receive HUD HOME funding another way (see NOFA Appendix B for eligible applicants/project areas)
L53:   * Developers, including prospective nonprofit HCD-certified HOME Community Housing Development Organizations (CHDOs) that wish to undertake a project activity in an eligible city or county area found on NOFA Appendix B.
L54:   * Eligible tribal entities as described in the NOFA for a project activity within Native American lands as defined in the NOFA, or in an eligible city or county area found on NOFA Appendix B.
