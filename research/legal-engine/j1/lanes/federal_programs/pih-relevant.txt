L14@P0: Subject: Availability of Tenant Protection Vouchers for Emergency Housing Voucher Families
L15@P0: I. BASIC INFORMATION
L16@P0: A. Purpose
L17@P0: This notice establishes the requirements, funding process, and timeline for awarding
L18@P0: tenant protection vouchers (TPVs) by December 31, 2026, to all families under
L19@P0: emergency housing vouchers (EHVs) housing assistance payments (HAP) contracts
L20@P0: as of September 30, 2026, consistent with the Continuing Appropriations and
L21@P0: Extensions Act, 2027 (section 153 of Pub. L. 119-103) (“2027 CR”).
L22@P0: On September 2, 2026, the President signed the 2027 CR, which provided for
L23@P0: continued government funding through December 11, 2026. As part of the act, HUD
L24@P0: is authorized to provide TPVs for any EHV family under lease as of September 30,
L25@P0: 2026. After implementing this provision, all EHVs will be fully converted to the
L26@P0: Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) program (“EHV-TPVs”).
L27@P0: The 2027 CR directs that these TPVs are not replacement vouchers and, as such, they
L28@P0: cannot be reissued upon turnover.
L29@P0: B. Background
L30@P0: Section 3202 of the American Rescue Plan Act of 2021 (ARP) (Pub. L. 117-2,
L31@P0: approved March 11, 2021) authorized $5 billion for the provision of EHVs,
L32@P0: associated administrative costs and other eligible expenses. ARP made EHV funds
L33@P0: available for obligation by HUD until September 30, 2030, with these funds being set
L34@P0: for cancellation as a matter of law effective September 30, 2035.
L35@P0: On March 6, 2025, HUD sent a letter notifying public housing agencies (PHAs) that
L36@P0-1: the remaining available EHV funds were likely to only cover EHV families through 2
L37@P1: most of calendar year (CY) 2026. HUD then obligated remaining EHV funding to
L38@P1: participating PHAs.
L39@P1: On March 26, 2025, HUD published Notice PIH 2025-07, which directed PHAs to
L40@P1: stop issuing any EHVs to new families and reminded PHAs that any incoming EHV
L41@P1: portability moves must be billed to the initial PHA.
L42@P1: Notice PIH 2026-12 provided that HUD had HAP funding available from returned
L43@P1: EHV services fees to address EHV shortfalls. HUD has proactively provided EHV
L44@P1: shortfall funding with these EHV funds based on need documented by Voucher
L45@P1: Management System (VMS) expenditure data.
L46@P1: In addition, Notice PIH 2026-12 stated that HUD would provide future guidance on
L47@P1: the implementation of the 2026 Appropriations Act provision that allowed TPV
L48@P1: funding to be used for CY 2026 assistance to PHAs that would otherwise be required
L49@P1: to terminate EHVs for families as a result of insufficient funding. As the 2027 CR
L50@P1: repealed this provision and authorized HUD to provide TPVs, that guidance is no
L51@P1: longer necessary.
L52@P1: This notice provides TPVs for all EHV families under lease as of September 30,
L53@P1: 2026, and requires the conversion of these families from EHV to EHV-TPVs by the
L54@P1: end of December 2026.
L55@P1: Once this conversion is complete, the EHV program will end. No later than
L56@P1: December 31, 2026, PHAs will no longer have EHV families under lease. ARP
L57@P1: prohibited PHAs from reissuing EHV vouchers after September 30, 2023.
L58@P1: In early 2027, HUD will begin the process of closing out the EHV program in
L59@P1: accordance with Notice PIH 2023-35, as amended by Notice PIH 2026-02, which
L60@P1: accelerated the services fee reconciliation and closeout timeline. As part of the
L61@P1: closeout process, PHAs must remit all unexpended EHV funds to HUD.

L79@P2: II. FUNDING DETAILS
L80@P2: A. Award Calculation
L81@P2: EHV-TPVs are funded from the $600.6 million made available for TPV under the
L82@P2: 2027 CR. TPV HAP award estimates will be based on the leased EHV HAP costs
L83@P2: reflected in VMS and reported in the Inventory Management System/PIH Information
L84@P2: Center (IMS/PIC). This notice does not provide funding beyond the period covered
L85@P2: by the 2027 CR. The TPV awards will cover 1 month (December 2026), and future
L86@P2: months are planned to be covered as renewals. All funding is subject to future
L87@P2: appropriations.
L88@P2: HUD will calculate funding eligibility for EHV-TPVs based on the average per unit
L89@P2: cost (PUC) in the PHA’s EHV program and the total units estimated to be under lease
L90@P2: as of September 30, 2026, using July 2026 VMS and current IMS/PIC reporting data.
L91@P2: Funding is based on PUC and reported expenses only; no prior year or renewal
L92@P2: amount is carried forward. Unit count estimates are based on the lower of the July
L93@P2: 2026 VMS or IMS/PIC figures, except in those cases of correction.
L94@P2: If a PHA has concerns regarding the sufficiency of the EHV-TPV funding based on
L95@P2: the amounts provided in HUD’s notification, it must contact EHV@hud.gov by
L96@P2: October 15, 2026, and document why HUD’s calculation is incorrect.
L97@P2: PHAs will receive ongoing administrative fees for the converted EHV-TPVs on the
L98@P2: same basis as the rest of their HCV program. Beginning with the effective date of
L99@P2: EHV-TPVs, when the families begin being assisted under the HCV program, these
L100@P2: vouchers are administered under 24 CFR part 982 and are included in the PHA's
L101@P2: HCV unit months under lease (UML) reported in VMS for purposes of the ongoing
L102@P2: administrative fee earned under the annual appropriations act. The EHV-specific
L103@P2: administrative fee and the one-time EHV services fee do not apply to these vouchers
L104@P2: and may not be claimed for this conversion.

L107@P2: PIH 2025-19 to transition EHV families to one of the PHA’s existing HCVs instead
L108@P2: of transitioning the family to an EHV-TPV. Similarly, if the PHA transitions an EHV
L109@P2: family to a unit in the PHA’s PBV portfolio,1 that family may not receive an EHVTPV because it is no longer an EHV family. EHV-TPVs cannot be project-based, as
L110@P2: they are not replacement vouchers. HUD will no longer accept waiver requests that
L111@P2: are focused on transitioning EHV to a PHA’s PBV program after the publication of
L112@P2: this notice.
L113@P2: Families transitioned to the HCV or PBV program are not eligible for EHV-TPVs
L114@P2: under this notice because these families are no longer leasing units under the EHV

L125@P3: III. ELIGIBILITY
L126@P3: A. Eligible Recipients
L127@P3: PHAs currently participating in the EHV program that have EHV families under lease
L128@P3: as of September 30, 2026, are eligible to receive funding under this notice. Non-EHV
L129@P3: PHAs administering an EHV household through portability billing as of September
L130@P3: 30, 2026, are also eligible to receive funding for that family. HUD will use data from
L131@P3: IMS/PIC for families that have moved through portability to non-EHV PHAs and
L132@P3: have not been absorbed to determine potential eligibility for awards.
L133@P3: To determine a PHA’s award, a family is attributed to the PHA administering the
L134@P3: HAP contract for the family as of September 30, 2026. In an EHV portability case,
L135@P3: the family is included in the count of the PHA administering the HAP contract on that
L136@P3: date. This includes PHAs without EHV programs who didn’t absorb families into
L137@P3: their HCV program and instead chose to bill, as the initial PHA still reported those
L138@P3: families in EHV fields for VMS and paid with EHV funds. If the family is under
L139@P3: lease in the EHV program in the initial PHA’s jurisdiction while searching for a unit
L140@P3: in the receiving PHA’s jurisdiction, the family is counted by the initial PHA.
L141@P3: A former EHV family that transitioned to the HCV program or any other housing
L142@P3: assistance program before September 30, 2026, is not eligible for inclusion in any
L143@P3: PHA’s prospective EHV-TPV award. EHV families placed on the HCV waiting list
L144@P3: but not yet transitioned to the HCV program are eligible for EHV-TPVs if they were
L145@P3: still under lease in the EHV program as of September 30, 2026. Such EHV families
L146@P3: would be removed from the HCV waiting list once they have been issued an EHVTPV.
L147@P3: EHV families under lease as of September 30, 2026, are eligible for the EHV-TPV
L148@P3: and are considered continuously assisted. PHAs do not need to add families to the
L149@P3: waiting list or rescreen families, and families can remain in units under lease. The
L150@P3: PHA’s administrative plan regarding payment standard for HCV program participants
L151@P3: is applicable to the EHV-TPV families.
L152@P3: When the EHV-TPV family leaves the program, the PHA must not reissue the
L153@P3: voucher. HUD will reduce the PHA’s unit months authorized to reflect EHV-TPV
L154@P3: families that have left the program.
L155@P3: B. Eligible Activities
L156@P3: Families converted to EHV-TPVs will continue to receive tenant-based assistance
L157@P3-4: under 24 CFR 982 without interruption. EHV fees cannot be used to cover the cost of 5
L158@P4: the conversion, and PHAs are not eligible for a transition fee under Notice PIH 2026-
L159@P4: 12 for converting the EHV voucher to an EHV-TPV. PHAs must track EHV-TPVs
L160@P4: separately from other vouchers as they cannot be reissued upon turnover.
L161@P4: IV. HUD NOTIFICATION
L162@P4: All eligible PHAs will receive an estimate of their EHV-TPV HAP funding intended to
L163@P4: cover December 2026 via email2 on or around October 1, 2026. This document will
L164@P4: consist of your estimated number of EHVs under lease as of September 30, 2026.
L165@P4: V. PHA RESPONSE
L166@P4: PHAs can respond to HUD’s estimate of award by contacting the EHV mailbox at
L167@P4: EHV@hud.gov by 11:59:59 ET on October 15, 2026. Any award given in excess of a
L168@P4: PHA’s families under lease as of September 30, 2026, may be reduced once September
L169@P4: VMS is available.
L170@P4: VI. HUD REVIEW
L171@P4: HUD will review each PHA’s award against IMS/PIC and VMS data to validate the EHV
L172@P4: family count. Where a PHA submits a correction or documented exception, HUD will
L173@P4: reconcile the discrepancy and adjust the award as warranted.
L174@P4: VII. AWARD NOTICE
L175@P4: HUD will send a final notice of award to PHAs several weeks ahead of the December
L176@P4: EHV-TPV HAP disbursements.
L177@P4: VIII. FUNDING REDUCTION AND REALLOCATION
L178@P4: HUD may terminate, reduce, or limit the availability of funding for substantial
L179@P4: noncompliance with program requirements. Because EHV-TPVs are tied to families
L180@P4: under lease as of September 30, 2026, and cannot be reissued upon turnover, HUD will
L181@P4: adjust or recapture funding for any voucher not supported by the confirmed leasing data.
