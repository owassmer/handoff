L14@P0: This Administrative Notice (Notice) sets forth and establishes the California Department of Housing and
L15@P0: Community Development’s (HCD or Department) alternate leasing requirements for multifamily rental
L16@P0: housing projects that are located in Los Angeles County and assisted by the Department as ordered by
L17@P0: Governor Newsom in Executive Order N-23-25 on March 7, 2025 and Executive Order N-37-25 on
L18@P0: November 24, 2025, collectively referred to as the Executive Orders. This Notice suspends specific tenant
L19@P0: selection procedures authorized by state law, including Department regulations, for the purpose of giving
L20@P0: waitlist priority to displaced households who are experiencing homelessness or at imminent risk of
L21@P0: homelessness in Los Angeles County due to the proclaimed emergency, of January 7, 2025, due to
L22@P0: multiple fires.
L23@P0: Purpose
L24@P0: The objective of the Executive Orders and this Notice is to further support residents displaced by the
L25@P0: emergency who are experiencing homelessness or at imminent risk of homelessness by prioritizing them
L26@P0: on waiting lists for affordable rental housing funded by the Department for which they otherwise qualify.
L27@P0: In accordance with the Executive Orders, households displaced from their primary residence in Los
L28@P0: Angeles County as a result of the proclaimed emergency shall be prioritized on the waiting lists for rental
L29@P0: housing in the Department’s housing portfolio upon demonstration that the household (i) was displaced
L30@P0: from a residence in Los Angeles County due to the proclaimed emergency; (ii) is currently experiencing
L31@P0: homelessness or at imminent risk of homelessness; and (iii) meets all HCD program-eligibility
L32@P0: requirements of the vacant dwelling unit.
L33@P0: Applicability
L34@P0: This Notice applies to all multifamily rental housing projects assisted by HCD in the County of Los
L35@P0: Angeles and will remain in effect through July 1, 2027.
L36@P0: Administrative Notice: This Administrative Notice establishes a formal written notification of
L37@P0: administrative guidelines and policies that affect the operation of Department financing
L38@P0: programs. This format is used to identify, clarify, and record administrative guidelines and
L39@P0-1: interpretations of public interest.Policy Requirements
L40@P1: Per the Executive Orders, tenant selection procedures for the Department’s rental housing portfolio
L41@P1: authorized by any state statute, regulation, or guidance document authorized by state law, including,
L42@P1: without limitation, title 25 of the California Code of Regulations, section 8305, subdivision (a)(4)(A), are
L43@P1: suspended to the extent they would prevent a household eligible for priority housing under this Notice from
L44@P1: receiving that priority. This suspension does not apply to federal funding programs administered by the
L45@P1: Department. This Notice establishes alternative tenant selection procedures for all HCD-funded projects
L46@P1: located in Los Angeles County to prioritize households that were displaced as a result of the wildfires and
L47@P1: are currently experiencing homelessness or are at imminent risk of homelessness for any vacant units.
L48@P1: It does not, however, suspend any program eligibility requirements. All other tenant selection and
L49@P1: eligibility requirements for HCD programs remain in effect.

L54@P1: for eligibility before the sponsor reaches out to the CES for additional referrals.

L56@P1: be offered a unit before households not displaced by the fires which gave rise to the proclaimed emergency
L57@P1: are offered a unit. The household must meet the targeting population criteria for all funding programs
L58@P1: restricting a particular unit, inclusive of all exceptions.
