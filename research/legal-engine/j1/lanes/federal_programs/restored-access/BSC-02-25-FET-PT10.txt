ITEM 1 Chapter 3 Provisions for All Compliance Methods, Section 319.2
319.2 Existing conditions. … Adjacent structures or site features that may affect the retrofit design shall be identified. 
The entire load path of the seismic-force-resisting system shall be determined, documented and evaluated. The load path includes encompasses all the horizontal and vertical elements that participate participating in the structural response: such as, including, but not limited to, diaphragms, diaphragm chords, diaphragm collectors, foundations, vertical elements such as walls, frames, and braces; foundations and, as well as the connections between the these components and elements of the load path. Repaired or retrofitted elements and the standards under which the work was constructed shall be identified.
