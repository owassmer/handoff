Local lane's `q4-thpp-edd-delta.json` supplies the final package overlay effective January 1, 2026. It should govern covered licensed THPP/THP-NMD operation against any older mirror text.
- `CA:HCD-TAY-2025-26`, document leaves `TAY-2025-26-Exhibit-A` and `TAY-2025-26-Exhibit-B`, remains a separate HCD funding/agreement source. Exhibit A §§2–3 references HNMP support/training for social workers serving nonminor dependents and directs THPSUP services through THP-Plus. Neither connection establishes that every funded housing arrangement is a CDSS-licensed THPP. The licensing overlay is conditional on the actual provider/placement model and is not a replacement for either HCD exhibit.

These are the statutory parents for the applicability gate; no new duplicate statute instrument is needed.
- `CA:ACT:202520260AB2162` is an already selected county funding/nonminor-dependent enactment. Preserve its separate effective-date/funding changes; the licensing regulation does not itself supersede that enactment.

No selected standalone CDSS THPP interim-licensing-standards manual or `thppman1.pdf` edition was found in the current CA/US instrument registers or this lane's proposals.
