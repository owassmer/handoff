L268:     SEC. 102. <<NOTE: 42 USC 5301 note.>> FEDERAL GUIDELINES FOR
L269:                             POINT-ACCESS BLOCK BUILDINGS.
L270: 
L271:         (a) <<NOTE: Deadline.>>  In General.--Not later than 18 months after
L272:     the date of enactment of this section, the Secretary of Housing and
L273:     Urban Development shall issue guidelines to provide States, territories,
L274:     Tribes, and localities with model code language, best practices,
L275: 
L276:     [[Page 140 STAT. 850]]
L277: 
L278:     and technical guidance that could be used to facilitate the permitting
L279:     of point-access block residential buildings.
L280: 
L281:         (b) Contents.--When developing the guidelines under subsection (a),
L282:     the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development shall consider--
L283:                 (1) fire safety considerations, including sprinkler
L284:             coverage, smoke detection, ventilation, and building egress
L285:             performance;
L286:                 (2) construction costs and potential impacts on housing
L287:             affordability, including the potential for increasing housing
L288:             supply in high-cost jurisdictions;
L289:                 (3) flexibility for diverse consumer needs, including family
L290:             sizes, unit configurations, and accessibility;
L291:                 (4) examples of single-stair codes adopted or considered by
L292:             States and cities in the United States;
L293:                 (5) examples of single-stair codes used in relevant
L294:             international standards;
L295:                 (6) research and model language relating to single-stair
L296:             codes produced by organizations that focus on point-access block
L297:             building design and building-code reform;
L298:                 (7) consulting with experts, including developers,
L299:             architects, fire marshals, researchers, economists, housing
L300:             authorities, and officials in States that have enacted or
L301:             piloted single-stair codes; and
L302:                 (8) alternative methods of safety compliance, including
L303:             options that utilize additional passive or active safety
L304:             features.
L305: 
L306:         (c) Coordination With the International Code Council.--The Secretary
L307:     of Housing and Urban Development shall coordinate with the International
L308:     Code Council to encourage the International Code Council to incorporate
L309:     provisions about point-access block buildings into the International
L310:     Building Code.
L311:         (d) Grants.--
L312:                 (1) In general.--The Secretary of Housing and Urban
L313:             Development may establish a program to award competitive grants
L314:             to eligible entities to implement pilot projects that evaluate,
L315:             demonstrate, or validate the safety, feasibility, or cost-
L316:             effectiveness of point-access block residential buildings.
L317:                 (2) Sunset.--The program established under paragraph (1)
L318:             shall terminate on the date that is 7 years after the date of
L319:             enactment of this subsection.
L320: 
L321:         (e) Treatment of Projects.--Projects assisted under this section
L322:     shall be treated as projects assisted under the Community Development
L323:     Block Grant program under title I of the Housing and Community
L324:     Development Act of 1974 (42 U.S.C. 5301 et seq.).
L325:         (f) Rule of Construction.--Nothing in this section may be construed
L326:     to preempt a State or local building code.
L327:         (g) Definitions.--In this section:
L328:                 (1) Eligible entity.--The term ``eligible entity'' means a
L329:             State, unit of local government, Tribal Government, public
L330:             housing agency, nonprofit housing organization, community
L331:             development organization, private developer, construction firm,
L332:             qualified design firm, engineering firm, academic institution,
L333:             research institution, or any partnership or consortium comprised
L334:             of 2 or more such types of entities.
L335:                 (2) Point-access block building.--The term ``point-access
L336:             block building'' means a Group R-2 occupancy residential
L337:             structure, as such term is defined by the International Building
L338:             Code, in which a single internal stairway provides access and
L339: 
L340:     [[Page 140 STAT. 851]]
L341: 
L342:             egress for all dwelling units in a building that is not greater
L343:             than 6 stories in height.
L344:     SEC. 103. <<NOTE: 42 USC 1471a.>>  EXEMPTION ON CONSTRUCTION OR
L345:                             MODIFICATION OF RESIDENTIAL HOUSING
L346:                             LOCATED ON AN INFILL SITE.
L347: 
L348:         (a) Exemption.--In providing assistance under section 501, 502, 504,
L349:     515, 533, or 538 of the Housing Act of 1949 (42 U.S.C. 1471, 1472, 1474,
L350:     1485, 1490m, or 1490p-2) for the construction or modification of
L351:     residential housing located on an infill site, the Secretary of
L352:     Agriculture shall not be required to carry out any study or report on
L353:     the environmental effects of such assistance.
L354:         (b) Report.--Not later than the date that is 5 years after the date
L355:     of enactment of this section, the Secretary of Agriculture shall submit,
L356:     to the Committee on Financial Services of the House of Representatives
L357:     and the Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs of the Senate,
L358:     a report that--
L359:                 (1) determines whether the implementation of this section--
L360:                         (A) reduced the amount of time it takes to review an
L361:                     application for assistance under the sections of the
L362:                     Housing Act of 1949 identified in subsection (a); and
L363:                         (B) reduced the administrative cost of providing
L364:                     such assistance;
L365:                 (2) describes how the implementation of this section affects
L366:             the affordable housing sector in rural America; and
L367:                 (3) includes any legislative recommendations from the
L368:             Secretary of Agriculture.
L369: 
L370:         (c) Definitions.--In this section:
L371:                 (1) Greenfield.--The term ``greenfield'' means a site that
L372:             has not been developed, including a woodland, farmland, and an
L373:             open field.
L374:                 (2) Infill site.--The term ``infill site''--
L375:                         (A) means a site that is served by existing
L376:                     infrastructure, including water lines, sewer lines, and
L377:                     roads; and
L378:                         (B) does not include--
L379:                               (i) a site that is served by existing
L380:                           infrastructure that only consists of a road;
L381:                               (ii) a site within a census tract designated
L382:                           as very high or relatively high risk for wildfire,
L383:                           coastal flooding, and riverine flooding under the

L385:                           Management Agency pursuant to section 206 of the
L386:                           Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency
L387:                           Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5136); and
L388:                               (iii) a greenfield.
