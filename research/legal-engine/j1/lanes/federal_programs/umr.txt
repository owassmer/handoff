L41@P1: Section 8300. Purpose and Scope.
L42@P1: (a) These regulations provide uniform standards and program rules for
L43@P1: multifamily rental housing developments assisted by the
L44@P1: Department of Housing and Community Development.
L45@P1: (b) When expressly incorporated by reference, some or all of the
L46@P1: provisions of this Chapter shall apply to: the Joe Serna, Jr.
L47@P1: Farmworker Housing Grant (JSJFWHG) Program (Chapter 7,
L48@P1: subchapter 3, commencing with Section 7200); the Multifamily
L49@P1: Housing Program (MHP) (Chapter 7, subchapter 4, commencing
L50@P1: with Section 7300); and the HOME Investment Partnerships
L51@P1: (HOME) Program (Chapter 7, subchapter 17, commencing with
L52@P1: Section 8200). These regulations interpret and make specific the
L53@P1: following statutes applicable to these programs: Health and Safety
L54@P1: Code Division 31, Part 3.2, Chapter 2 (commencing with Section
L55@P1: 50517.5), Chapter 6.7 (commencing with Section 50675), and
L56@P1: Chapter 16 (commencing with Section 50896).
L57@P1: (c) The 2017 adoptions and amendments to subchapter 19 shall be
L58@P1: effective on November 15, 2017 and shall apply prospectively to all
L59@P1: standard agreements between the Department and Sponsors that
L60@P1: are executed or amended on or after the foregoing effective date
L61@P1: and are governed by the authorities set forth in subsection (b).
L62@P1: These agreements are subject to the standard contract format
L63@P1: referenced in the California State Contracting Manual.
