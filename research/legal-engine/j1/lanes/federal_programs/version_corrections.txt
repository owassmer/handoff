L52@P3: Article 1. General.
L53@P3: Section 7300. Purpose and Scope.
L54@P3: (a) These guidelines implement and interpret Chapter 6.7 (commencing with Section
L55@P3: 50675) of Part 2 of Division 31 of the Health and Safety Code (HSC), which establishes
L56@P3: the Multifamily Housing Program (MHP) administered by the California Department of
L57@P3: Housing and Community Development (Department).
L58@P3: (b) These guidelines establish terms, conditions, and procedures for funds awarded after
L59@P3: the effective date of these guidelines, and are authorized by HSC Section 54014,
L60@P3: subdivision (b).
L61@P3: (c) Nothing in these guidelines is intended to be, nor should be, interpreted to amend or
L62@P3: repeal rules, regulations or requirements set forth in prior versions of MHP guidelines
L63@P3: or their amendments; these guidelines shall have no retroactive application. These
L64@P3: guidelines apply to the Multifamily Finance Super NOFA – Round 3 funding offered
L65@P3: subsequent to their publication.
L66@P3: (d) These guidelines implement and interpret AB 434 (Chapter 192, Statutes of 2020),
L67@P3: which amends, repeals, and adds sections of and to the HSC and the Military and
L68@P3: Veterans Code (MVC). AB 434 requests the Department to harmonize the Designated
L69@P3: Programs in four (4) respects:
L70@P3: (1) All Designated Program funds are to be made available at the same time as any
L71@P3: MHP funds are made available;
L72@P3: (2) Designated Program applications are to be rated and ranked in a manner
L73@P3: consistent with MHP applications;
L74@P3: (3) Designated Program funds are to be administered consistent with MHP; and
L75@P3: (4) Designated Program loans, to the extent applicable, are to be consistent with
L76@P3: MHP loan terms.
