California Lead and Copper Rule Improvements (SWRCB-DDW-26-001E)

Information and Documentation Pertaining to this Emergency Regulatory Action

Rulemaking Information

Sections Affected

The proposed emergency regulations would:

  • Repeal California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Chapter 17.5, Sections 64670 – 64691, and create Section 94693, titled “Sunset of Regulations.”
  • Create California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Chapter 17.6, Sections 64700 – 64770.
  • Modify California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Sections 64252, 64254, 64256, 64257, 64258, 64463.1, 64463.4, 64463.7, 64465, 64469, 64481, 64482, 64669.110, 64669.125, and 64814.00.

Rulemaking Status

This emergency rulemaking is in progress.

Inquiries regarding the contents of these regulations may be directed to DDW-LCR@waterboards.ca.gov.

Staff-Led Workshop

State Water Board staff held a virtual staff-led workshop on June 11, 2026, to inform and obtain public input regarding the proposed adoption of the Lead and Copper Rule Improvements (LCRI) regulation and the development of a policy handbook implementing Assembly Bill (AB) 1096. For more information on the AB 1096 Policy Handbook, see the AB 1096 Policy Handbook page.

Workshop information along with the presentation slides and video recording are available below:

  • Public Notice of Staff-led Workshop (English | Spanish)
  • Presentation Slides (English | Spanish)
  • Video Recording (English | Spanish)
  • Frequently Asked Questions (English | Spanish)

Background and Additional Resources

As the latest in a series of regulations to limit exposure to lead and copper through drinking water, the LCRI was finalized by the United States Environmental Protection Agency (US EPA) on October 8, 2024, with a compliance date of November 1, 2027. State Water Board staff propose to adopt the federal LCRI into the California Code of Regulations as a new chapter, chapter 17.6, “Control of Lead and Copper,” through an emergency rulemaking process. The new chapter 17.6 is proposed to become effective on November 1, 2027, with existing chapter 17.5, titled “Lead and Copper,” proposed to be repealed simultaneously.

For more information on the LCRI, please refer to the resources below:
