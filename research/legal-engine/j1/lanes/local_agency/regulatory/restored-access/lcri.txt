California Lead and Copper Rule Improvements (SWRCB-DDW-26-001E)

      
Information and Documentation Pertaining to this Emergency Regulatory Action

      

        

          

            

              
Rulemaking Information

            

            

              
Sections Affected

              
The proposed emergency regulations would:

              

                
Repeal California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Chapter 17.5, Sections 64670 – 64691, and create Section 94693, titled “Sunset of Regulations.”

                
Create California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Chapter 17.6, Sections 64700 – 64770.

                
Modify California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Sections 64252, 64254, 64256, 64257, 64258, 64463.1, 64463.4, 64463.7, 64465, 64469, 64481, 64482, 64669.110, 64669.125, and 64814.00. 

              

              
Rulemaking Status

              
This emergency rulemaking is in progress. 

              
Inquiries regarding the contents of these regulations may be directed to 
DDW-LCR@waterboards.ca.gov
.

Background and Additional Resources

            

            

              
As the latest in a series of regulations to limit exposure to lead and copper through drinking water, the LCRI was finalized by the United States Environmental Protection Agency (US EPA) on October 8, 2024, with a compliance date of November 1, 2027. State Water Board staff propose to adopt the federal LCRI into the California Code  of Regulations as a new chapter, chapter 17.6, “Control of Lead and Copper,”  through an 
emergency rulemaking process
. The new chapter 17.6 is  proposed to become effective on November 1, 2027, with existing chapter 17.5,  titled “Lead and Copper,” proposed to be repealed simultaneously. 


For more information on the LCRI, please refer to the resources below:
