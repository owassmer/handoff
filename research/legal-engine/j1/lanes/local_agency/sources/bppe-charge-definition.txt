L1010@P29: 94874.1. Nonprofit Exemption
L1011@P29: (a) The bureau shall not verify the exemption from this chapter of, or contract for the complaint
L1012@P29: handling for, a nonprofit institution that operated as a for-profit institution during any period on or
L1013@P29: after January 1, 2010, unless the Attorney General verifies all of the following:
L1014@P29: (1) The nonprofit institution acquired the for-profit institution’s assets for no more than the fair
L1015@P29: value of the assets.
L1016@P29: (2) The nonprofit institution has not executed agreements for goods or services exceeding the
L1017@P29: fair value of the goods or services.
L1018@P29: (3) All core functions of the nonprofit institution are conducted by, or under the direction of, the
L1019@P29: nonprofit institution.
L1020@P29: (4) The nonprofit institution has not entered into any contracts, loans, or leases with a term of
L1021@P29: longer than three years with the former for-profit institution’s owners and managers. This
L1022@P29: paragraph does not apply to a nonprofit institution that previously operated as a for-profit
L1023@P29: institution that is owned by or controlled by a public institution of higher learning.
L1024@P29: (b) An action under subdivision (a) may be appealed to the superior court.
L1025@P29: (c) “Fair value” shall be demonstrated through one of the following:
L1026@P29: (1) A third-party appraisal based on comparable assets acquired by, or goods or services
L1027@P29: procured by, nonprofit corporations in similar market conditions.
L1028@P29: (2) Independent financing of the acquisition or procurement based upon the asset acquired or
L1029@P29: goods or services procured.
L1030@P29: (3) Full and open competition in the acquisition of the assets or procurement of the goods or
L1031@P29: services.
