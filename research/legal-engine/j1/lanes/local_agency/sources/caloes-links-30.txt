L2@P0: PROCLAMATION OF A STATE OF EMERGENCY
L3@P0: WHEREAS on December 23 and 24, 2024, the County of Santa Cruz
L4@P0: experienced a major coastal winter storm (“December 2024 Coastal Storm”)
L5@P0: with extreme waves, storm surges, and high tides, resulting in coastal flooding,
L6@P0: wave impacts, and damages along the coastline; and
L7@P0: WHEREAS the December 2024 Coastal Storm caused flooding and debris
L8@P0: damage to East Cliff Drive, the Esplanade in Rio Del Mar, and Beach Drive,
L9@P0: and negatively impacted local residences and business in the County of
L10@P0: Santa Cruz; and
L11@P0: WHEREAS as a result of the December 2024 Coastal Storm, the City of
L12@P0: Santa Cruz experienced damage to its municipal wharf causing portions to
L13@P0: collapse into the ocean, and debris to be distributed along city, county and
L14@P0: state beaches, as well as in marine sanctuary waters; and
L15@P0: WHEREAS as a result of the December 2024 Coastal Storm, the Santa
L16@P0: Cruz Port District also experienced major damage to its pilings and docks; and
L17@P0: WHEREAS under the provisions of Government Code section 8558(b), I
L18@P0: find that conditions of extreme peril to the safety of persons and property exist
L19@P0: due to the December 2024 Coastal Storm in the County of Santa Cruz; and
L20@P0: WHEREAS under the provisions of Government Code section 8558(b), I
L21@P0: find that the conditions caused by the December 2024 Coastal Storm, by
L22@P0: reason of its magnitude, are or are likely to be beyond the control of the
L23@P0: services, personnel, equipment, and facilities of any single local government
L24@P0: and require the combined forces of a mutual aid region or regions to
L25@P0: appropriately respond; and
L26@P0: WHEREAS under the provisions of Government Code section 8625(c), I
L27@P0: find that local authority is inadequate to cope with the magnitude of the
L28@P0: extreme peril posed by the December 2024 Coastal Storm; and
L29@P0: WHEREAS under the provisions of Government Code section 8571, I find
L30@P0: that strict compliance with various statutes and regulations specified in this
L31@P0: Proclamation would prevent, hinder, or delay the mitigation of the effects of
L32@P0: December 2024 Coastal Storm.
L33@P0: NOW, THEREFORE, I, GAVIN NEWSOM, Governor of the State of California,
L34@P0: in accordance with the authority vested in me by the State Constitution and
L35@P0: statutes, including the California Emergency Services Act, and in particular,
L36@P0: Government Code section 8625, HEREBY PROCLAIM A STATE OF EMERGENCY to
L37@P0: exist in the County of Santa Cruz due to the December 2024 Coastal Storm.
L38@P0-1: IT IS HEREBY ORDERED THAT:1. All agencies of the state government shall utilize and employ state
L39@P1: personnel, equipment, and facilities for the performance of any and
L40@P1: all activities consistent with the direction of the Office of Emergency
L41@P1: Services and the State Emergency Plan. Also, all individuals are to
L42@P1: obey the direction of emergency officials with regard to this
L43@P1: emergency in order to protect their safety.
