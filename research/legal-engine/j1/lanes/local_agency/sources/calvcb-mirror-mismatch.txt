L8: (a) Hearings shall be open to public observation, unless otherwise provided by law.
L9: 
L10: (b) A hearing conducted by telephone, television, or other electronic means as provided in section cite3†617.4 complies with subdivision (a) of this section if:
L11: 
L12: (1) members of the public may be physically present at the location where the board or hearing officer is conducting the hearing; and
L13: (2) members of the public may inspect the hearing record and inspect any transcript obtained by the board or hearing officer.
L14: 
L15: (A) A request to inspect the hearing record or any transcript obtained by the board or hearing officer shall be in writing and is governed by the provisions of the Public Records Act, Government Code sections 6250- 6270.
L16: (B) The hearing record and any transcript obtained by the board or hearing officer shall be available for public inspection during regular business hours at the headquarters of the board.
L17: 
L18: (c) This section shall not apply to any prehearing conference or settlement conference.

L67: #  Cal. Code Regs. Tit. 2, § 617.5 - Informal Hearing
L68: 
L69:   * cite49† State Regulations  L70:   * cite50† Compare  L71: 
L72: (a) A party may object to having an informal hearing in writing within five days of receiving the hearing notice.
L73: 
L74: (b) The Executive Officer or hearing officer shall rule on an objection to an informal hearing before evidence is taken at a hearing.
L75: 
L76: (c) An objection to an informal hearing shall include:
L77: 
L78: (1) the specific facts and law upon which the objection is based; and
L79: (2) specific facts and law relevant to the factors contained in subsection (f).
L80: 
L81: (d) An objection to an informal hearing that is limited to written materials shall include the following, to the extent relevant to the basis for the objection, or as required by the Executive Officer or hearing officer:
L82: 
L83: (1) the identity of the witnesses that the party wishes to present;
L84: 
L85: (2) a summary of the testimony that is anticipated from each witness; and
L86: 
L87: (3) the issues to which each witness will testify.
L88: (e) An objection to an informal hearing that requests an opportunity to cross examine witnesses shall identify:
L89: 
L90: (1) the witnesses that the party wishes to cross examine; and
L91: 
L92: (2) the issues that the party wishes to explore during cross examination of each witness.
L93: 
L94: (f) If confidential facts or sources are relevant to the information required under subsections (d) or (e), a party shall:
L95: 
L96: (1) state that confidential facts or sources are involved; and
L97: (2) provide information that can be given without disclosing the confidential facts or sources.
L98: 
L99: (g) The Executive Officer or hearing officer shall consider the following factors when ruling on an objection to an informal hearing:
L100: 
L101: (1) complexity of legal or factual issues;
L102: 
L103: (2) necessity to evaluate credibility of witnesses for a proper determination of issues;
L104: 
L105: (3) parties' representation by legal counsel;
L106: (4) necessity of witnesses being subject to cross examination for the proper determination of issues; and
L107: 
L108: (5) any other factor likely to affect a just and proper determination of issues.
L109: 
L110: (h) If the objection to an informal hearing is sustained, the Executive Officer or hearing officer may permit:
L111: 
L112: (1) testimony from parties;
L113: 
L114: (2) testimony from witnesses who are not parties; or
L115: 
L116: (3) cross examination of witnesses.
L117: (i) If the Executive Officer sustains an objection to an informal hearing over which the board was to preside, the Executive Officer shall assign the hearing to a hearing officer.
