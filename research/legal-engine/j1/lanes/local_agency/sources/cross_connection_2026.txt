L147: ## Policy Handbook Updates
L148: 
L149: The Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook (CCCPH) took effect July 1, 2024. A FAQ dated June 27, 2025 is posted on the Guidance Documents.
L150: 
L151: The State Water Board adopted a revised version of the CCCPH on, April 21,2026, which corrected references to accrediting entities and permits the use of some existing protections for auxiliary water supplies. The following are an updated CCCPH and log of all changes made:

L155: The Cross-Connection Control Committee has released the following guidance document on the fire sprinkler requirements in the CCCPH.
L156: 
L157:   * Guidance Memorandum on Fire Protection Systems, dated September 5, 2025 (cite99†pdf )
L158: The Committee has released four Cross-Connection Control Plan (CCC Plan) templates, one for transient noncommunity water systems, one for nontransient noncommunity water systems, and two for small community water systems with less than 1,000 connections. The Committee has also prepared a training video on the CCCPH for water systems and stakeholders, linked below under Other Guidance Documents.
L159: All Cross-Connection Control Plans, requests for extensions, or questions on Plans from public water systems must be submitted to their assigned DDW District Office or LPA. Comments and inquiries regarding the Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook may be directed to: backflow@waterboards.ca.gov

L1@P0: RESOLUTION NO. 2026-0013
L2@P0: ADOPTING AMENDMENTS TO THE CROSS-CONNECTION CONTROL POLICY
L3@P0: HANDBOOK
L4@P0: WHEREAS:
L5@P0: 1. Health and Safety Code section 116270 et seq., (California Safe Drinking
L6@P0: Water Act (Act)) requires the State Water Resources Control Board (State
L7@P0: Water Board) to administer provisions related to drinking water to protect
L8@P0: public health. The Act allows the State Water Board to adopt regulations that
L9@P0: include requirements for the control of cross-connections.
L10@P0: 2. On October 6, 2017, Assembly Bill 1671 was filed with the Secretary of State,
L11@P0: adding section 116407 to the Health and Safety Code.
L12@P0: 3. Health and Safety Code section 116407 directed the State Water Board to
L13@P0: adopt standards for backflow protection and cross-connection control by
L14@P0: January 1, 2020, and allows those standards to be implemented through a
L15@P0: policy handbook that is not subject to the requirements of Chapter 3.5
L16@P0: (commencing with section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the
L17@P0: Government Code.
L18@P0: 4. The State Water Board adopted the standards for backflow protection and
L19@P0: cross-connection control through a policy handbook titled the CrossConnection Policy Control Policy Handbook on December 19, 2023.
L20@P0: 5. The December 19, 2023, resolution anticipated future revisions to the CrossConnection Control Policy Handbook in response to stakeholder input and
L21@P0: engagement. The resolution also included instructions from the State Water
L22@P0: Board to the Executive Director to make non­substantive changes necessary
L23@P0: for clarity or consistency.
L24@P0: 6. The State Water Board adopted a revised Cross­Connection Control Policy
L25@P0: Handbook on June 17, 2025, that extended certain compliance dates and
L26@P0: made minor corrections.
L27@P0: 7. The State Water Board identified a need to allow accrediting organizations
L28@P0: that are recognized by the American National Standards Institute (ANSI) as
L29@P0: ANSI­equivalent to accredit Tester and Specialist certification organizations to
L30@P0-1: ISO/IEC 17024, and to eliminate the distinction between interconnected and 2
L31@P1: not-interconnected auxiliary water supplies and establish criteria for the
L32@P1: ongoing use of existing double-check valve assemblies.
L33@P1: 8. On January 16, 2026, the State Water Board posted the proposed changes
L34@P1: for public comment to the Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook on the
L35@P1: State Water Board’s website at:
L36@P1: https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/drinking_water/certlic/drinkingwater/cccph.ht
L37@P1: ml and notified public water systems and interested public through the State
L38@P1: Water Board email subscription lists. Written comments were due no later
L39@P1: than February 19, 2026. The State Water Board received fourteen comments
L40@P1: for consideration.
L41@P1: 9. In addition to receiving written comments, the State Water Board held a
L42@P1: virtual public hearing at 2:00 p.m. on February 19, 2026, and received eight
L43@P1: oral comments.
L44@P1: 10.The adoption of these amendments to the Cross-Connection Control Policy
L45@P1: Handbook is exempt from the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA)
L46@P1: pursuant to the following categorical exemptions: (1) Class 1 projects, which
L47@P1: consist of the operation, repair, maintenance, permitting, leasing, licensing or
L48@P1: minor alteration of existing public and private structures, facilities, mechanical
L49@P1: equipment, or topographical features, involving negligible or no expansion of
L50@P1: existing or former use; and (2) Class 2 projects, which consist of replacement
L51@P1: or reconstruction of existing structures and facilities. Additionally, because
L52@P1: prior to the adoption of the Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook, public
L53@P1: water systems were already subject to cross connection and backflow
L54@P1: prevention requirements, it can be seen with certainty that there is no
L55@P1: possibility that the adoption of the revisions to the Cross-Connection Control
L56@P1: Policy Handbook will have a significant effect on the environment.
L57@P1: THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT:
L58@P1: The State Water Board:
L59@P1: 1. Directs State Water Board staff to file a CEQA Notice of Exemption consistent
L60@P1: with paragraph 10, above.
L61@P1: 2. Adopts the revised Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook as appended
L62@P1-2: to this resolution with an effective date of April 21, 2026.3
L63@P2: 3. Authorizes the Executive Director or their delegee to make any nonsubstantive corrections to the Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook
L64@P2: deemed necessary for clarity or consistency, and to effectuate the update of
L65@P2: any existing regulations in Title 22 of the California Code of Regulations to
L66@P2: reference the updated Cross-Connection Control Policy Handbook.
