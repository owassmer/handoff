L6@P0: (VAWA 2013) Policy
L7@P0: On March 7, 2013, President Obama signed a law known as Violence Against Women Reauthorization Act of 2013
L8@P0: (VAWA 2013). Portions of this law provide enhanced protections for those who are assisted with (Section 8) Housing
L9@P0: Choice Vouchers if they become victims of domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault or stalking.
L10@P0: The following is a brief summary of the principal provisions of the law. The information in this handout is intended to
L11@P0: inform participants and landlords of their rights and responsibilities under VAWA.
L12@P0: Protections under VAWA 2013
L13@P0: 1. Denial of Assistance: VAWA 2013 provides that an applicant cannot be denied assistance or admission because
L14@P0: he or she is a victim of domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault or stalking if the applicant is otherwise
L15@P0: qualified for assistance or admission to rental assistance programs.
L16@P0: 2. Termination of Assistance: Criminal activity directly relating to domestic violence, dating violence, sexual
L17@P0: assault or stalking shall not be considered cause for termination of assistance for any participant or affiliated
L18@P0: individual of a participant’s family who is a victim or threatened victim of the domestic violence, dating violence,
L19@P0: sexual assault or stalking.
L20@P0: 3. Lease terms: An incident or incidents of actual or threatened domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault or
L21@P0: stalking will not be considered to be a “serious or repeated” violation of the lease by the victim or threatened
L22@P0: victim of that violence and shall not be good cause for terminating the assistance, tenancy or occupancy rights of
L23@P0: the victim of that violence.
L24@P0: Removal of/Termination of Assistance to Perpetrator
L25@P0: VAWA also creates a new authority under Federal law that allows a Public Housing Agency, or an owner of a Section 8-
L26@P0: assisted property to evict, remove, or terminate assistance to any individual tenant or lawful occupant of the property who
L27@P0: engages in criminal activity directly relating to domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault, or stalking against an
L28@P0: affiliated individual or other individual. This may be done without evicting or taking any other adverse action to the other
L29@P0: occupants.
L30@P0: Limitations
L31@P0: There are some limitations to these protections:
L32@P0:  Assistance and/or tenancy may be terminated if OCHA, the owner, or the manager can demonstrate that an
L33@P0: assisted tenant posed “an actual and imminent threat” to other tenants or to persons employed at or providing
L34@P0: services at a rental property or unit.
L35@P0:  Assistance and/or tenancy may be terminated for lease violations that are not based on an incident or incidents of
L36@P0-1: domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault or stalking for which VAWA provides protections.Orange County Housing Authority

L78@P2: To all Tenants and Applicants
L79@P2: The Violence Against Women Act (VAWA) provides protections for victims of domestic violence, dating
L80@P2: violence, sexual assault, or stalking. VAWA protections are not only available to women, but are
L81@P2: available equally to all individuals regardless of sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation.2
L82@P2:  The U.S.
L83@P2: Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) is the Federal agency that oversees that Orange
L84@P2: County Housing Authority (OCHA) is in compliance with VAWA. This notice explains your rights under
L85@P2: VAWA. A HUD-approved certification form is attached to this notice. You can fill out this form to show
L86@P2: that you are or have been a victim of domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault, or stalking, and
L87@P2: that you wish to use your rights under VAWA.”
L88@P2: Protections for Applicants
L89@P2: If you otherwise qualify for assistance under OCHA, you cannot be denied admission or denied
L90@P2: assistance because you are or have been a victim of domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault,
L91@P2: or stalking.
L92@P2: Protections for Tenants
L93@P2: If you are receiving assistance under OCHA, you may not be denied assistance, terminated from
L94@P2: participation, or be evicted from your rental housing because you are or have been a victim of domestic
L95@P2: violence, dating violence, sexual assault, or stalking.
L96@P2: Also, if you or an affiliated individual of yours is or has been the victim of domestic violence, dating
L97@P2: violence, sexual assault, or stalking by a member of your household or any guest, you may not be denied
L98@P2: rental assistance or occupancy rights under OCHA solely on the basis of criminal activity directly relating
L99@P2: to that domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault, or stalking.
L100@P2: Affiliated individual means your spouse, parent, brother, sister, or child, or a person to whom you stand
L101@P2: in the place of a parent or guardian (for example, the affiliated individual is in your care, custody, or
L102@P2: control); or any individual, tenant, or lawful occupant living in your household.
L103@P2: Removing the Abuser or Perpetrator from the Household
L104@P2: OCHA may divide (bifurcate) your lease in order to evict the individual or terminate the assistance of the
L105@P2: individual who has engaged in criminal activity (the abuser or perpetrator) directly relating to domestic
L106@P2: violence, dating violence, sexual assault, or stalking.
L107@P2: If OCHA chooses to remove the abuser or perpetrator, OCHA may not take away the rights of eligible
L108@P2: tenants to the unit or otherwise punish the remaining tenants. If the evicted abuser or perpetrator was
L109@P2: the sole tenant to have established eligibility for assistance under the program, OCHA must allow the
L110@P2: tenant who is or has been a victim and other household members to remain in the unit for a period of
L111@P2: time, in order to establish eligibility under the program or under another HUD housing program covered
L112@P2: by VAWA, or, find alternative housing.

L115@P2: origin, religion, sex, familial status, disability, or age. HUD-assisted and HUD-insured housing must be made
L116@P2: available to all otherwise eligible individuals regardless of actual or perceived sexual orientation, gender identity, or
