L70@P2: Minimum Requirements for All Field Soil Fumigations
L71@P2: Setback
L72@P2: restrictions
L73@P2: 3 CCR section 6448(b) prohibits 1,3-D field soil fumigations within the
L74@P2: setback distance from occupied structures, including residences, onsite
L75@P2: employee housing, schools, convalescent homes, hospitals, businesses, or
L76@P2: other similar sites identified by the CAC. Non-residential agricultural
L77@P2: buildings, including barns, livestock facilities, sheds, and outhouses, are not
L78@P2: by default considered to be an occupied structure. Additionally, the setback is
L79@P2: measured from the wall of the structure, not lawns or other outdoor areas
L80@P2: associated with a structure. However, a setback is also required for any other
L81@P2: indoor or outdoor site that will be occupied for at least 72 consecutive hours
L82@P2: during and following a 1,3-Dichloropropene application. The CAC has the
L83@P2: discretion to apply the setback to other sites if someone may be at an indoor
L84@P2: or outdoor area for more than 24 consecutive hours during and following
L85@P2: application.
L86@P2: Setback
L87@P2: distance
L88@P2: The minimum setback distance from an occupied structure to the perimeter of
L89@P2: a 1,3-D fumigation is 100 feet. However, the setback tables in this document
L90@P2: specify a longer distance for certain combinations of fumigation method,
L91@P2: application rate, and application block size. The figure shows the setback
L92@P2: from occupied structures represented by the circles to the fumigated area
L93@P2: (application block) represented by the square.

L123@P3: Setbacks for
L124@P3: overlapping
L125@P3: field soil
L126@P3: fumigations
L127@P3: To determine if multiple 1,3-D applications “overlap,” the setback distance is
L128@P3: measured from the 1,3-D application block to other 1,3-D application blocks
L129@P3: rather than occupied structures. Setbacks for overlapping field soil
L130@P3: fumigations are required if the setbacks for two or more application blocks
L131@P3: overlap within 36 hours from the time the earlier field soil fumigation is
L132@P3: complete until the start of the later field soil fumigation. The figure shows
L133@P3: overlapping field soil fumigations occur when the distance between
L134@P3: applications (ΔL) is the combined setback distance (setback 1 + setback 2) or
L135@P3: less.
L136@P3: For overlapping field soil fumigations, the setback from occupied structures is
L137@P3: the same distance for all application blocks and determined using 1) the
L138@P3: combined acreage of all overlapping application blocks, 2) the highest
L139@P3: application rate, and 3) the setback table for the fumigation method with the
L140@P3: largest setback.
L141@P3: Fumigation methods that require the minimum setback restrictions for all
L142@P3: field soil fumigations (page 34) are exempt from the multiple field soil
L143@P3: fumigations requirements.

L149@P4: Maximum
L150@P4: application rate
L151@P4: The maximum application rate is 332 pounds of 1,3-D active ingredient per
L152@P4: broadcast-equivalent acre. However, the setback tables in this document
L153@P4: specify a lower maximum application rate for certain combinations of
L154@P4: fumigation method, setback distance, and application block size. The
L155@P4: broadcast equivalent application rate accounts for alternating treated and
L156@P4: untreated areas when a bed or strip fumigation is conducted. It is determined
L157@P4: by dividing the total amount of 1,3-Dichloropropene (pounds active
L158@P4: ingredient) applied to the application block divided by the total area of the
L159@P4: application block (fumigated acres plus unfumigated acres of the application
L160@P4: block).
L161@P4: Application
L162@P4: block size limit
L163@P4: An application block is a field or the portion of a field treated in a 24-hour
L164@P4: period that typically is identified by visible indicators, maps, or other tangible
L165@P4: means (3 CCR section 6000). The maximum application block size for
L166@P4: applications of 1,3-D is 80 acres. However, the setback tables in this
L167@P4: document specify smaller application block sizes for certain combinations of
L168@P4: fumigation methods, application rates, and setback distances.
L169@P4: Soil moisture
L170@P4: requirements
L171@P4: Except for drip chemigation field soil fumigations, 3 CCR section 6448.2(b)
L172@P4: requires the application block to have a minimum soil moisture of 50% of
L173@P4: field capacity at a depth of three to nine inches below the surface when the
L174@P4: fumigation occurs. One of the following options shall be used to comply with
L175@P4: the soil moisture requirement.
L176@P4: Option 1 – Irrigation: Irrigate with three inches of water 48–72 hours prior
L177@P4: to fumigation with 1,3-D.
L178@P4: Option 2 – Feel and Appearance: Determine if soil moisture content is at
L179@P4: least 50% of field capacity at the time of 1,3-D fumigation using a hand
L180@P4: examination of the soil. The level of soil moisture content is determined
L181@P4: based on several factors, such as the ability to 1) form a ball out of the soil in
L182@P4: your hand, 2) the firmness and surface roughness of the ball, 3) the ability to
L183@P4: form a ribbon with the soil, water glistening on the surface of the sample, 4)
L184@P4: loose soil particles, soil/water staining on fingers, and soil color. The steps are
L185@P4: as follows:
L186@P4: 1. Divide the application block into five or six equal sections.
L187@P4: 2. Obtain a soil sample between three to nine inches deep, using an auger
L188@P4: or shovel, from each section.
L189@P4: 3. Forming a ball: Squeeze the soil sample firmly in your hand several
L190@P4: times to form an irregularly shaped "ball."
L191@P4: 4. Forming a ribbon: Squeeze the soil sample ball out of your hand
L192@P4: between thumb and forefinger to form a “ribbon.”
