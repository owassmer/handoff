L232: ## cite135†Image Language Services at DIR
L233: 
L234: The Department of Industrial Relations (DIR) recognizes the importance of communicating effectively with individuals, including those with limited English proficiency. DIR is making an effort to provide meaningful services for individuals that speak languages other than English.

L248: ## Bilingual Services Complaint Process
L249: 
L250: DIR is committed to providing equal access to all our programs and services for persons with limited English-speaking skills. If you are not satisfied with the bilingual services you have received with DIR, you may file a complaint by sending an email to DIO@dir.ca.gov.
