L34@P1: BACKGROUND INFORMATION:

L45@P1: from Hurricane Marie.

L48@P1: proclaimed a local emergency.

L50@P1: and tidal events caused by Hurricane Marie that have resulted in significant damage to public infrastructure

L52@P1: homes has been lost to such extent that future buffering and berm protective measures may not be possible.
