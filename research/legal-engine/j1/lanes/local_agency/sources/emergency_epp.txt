L6@P0: SUMMARY
L7@P0: This Environmental Protection Plan (EPP) has been developed to fulfill Governor Newsom’s State of
L8@P0: Emergency Proclamation (SOE) signed on March 1, 2025 seeking to expedite fuels reduction projects
L9@P0: that protect communities and reduce severe risks of catastrophic wildfire. This EPP enables critical
L10@P0: wildfire safety projects to proceed expeditiously while protecting public health and the environment. It
L11@P0: does so by identifying the protective measures that will be followed by projects that have received
L12@P0: suspension from state permitting requirements under the SOE.
L13@P0: This EPP provides Best Management Practices (BMPs) and measures to minimize impacts to
L14@P0: environmental resources while completing fuels reduction projects. Specifically, these BMPs have
L15@P0: been designed to protect air quality, water quality, Tribal cultural resources, special-status species,
L16@P0: their habitat, and other habitat resources.
L17@P0: The BMPs were jointly developed by the California Natural Resources Agency (CNRA), California
L18@P0: Environmental Protection Agency (CalEPA), California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection
L19@P0: (CAL FIRE), California Department of Fish and Wildfire (CDFW), California Coastal Commission
L20@P0: (CCC), State Water Resources Control Board (SWRCB) and Nine Regional Water Quality Control
L21@P0: Boards (RWQCB) (collectively, Water Boards), Department of Conservation (DOC), and California
L22@P0: Air Resources Board (CARB).
L23@P0: Please note that the SOE suspensions only apply to State statutes,rules, and regulations, and that
L24@P0: compliance with all local and federal statutes, rules, and regulations is still required.

L26@P0: a. Inspection Access. Reasonable access to the property shall be provided whenever

L33@P0: recommendations into the project unlessthe project proponent presents a
L34@P0-1: similarly protective alternative approved by the requesting agency.2

L38@P1: operations.

L52@P1: resources identified in project scoping/planning prior to the start of project activities
L53@P1: by the certified orlicensed responsible party. For help identifying potential habitats
L54@P1: and species,resourcessuch asthe California Vegetation Treatment Program,
L55@P1: California Natural Diversity Database, Areas of Conservation Emphasis, etc., as well
L56@P1: as your local CDFW, DOC, Water Boards, and Coastal Commission staff can be used.
