L11@P0: improve program outcomes. If you sign this consent form, you agree to allow information gathered by a participating service

L13@P0: participating service providers/organizations to view and use your data to provide services to you. Additionally, the service
L14@P0: provider/organization will also be able to see what kind of services you have received in the past in Orange County. Signing this
