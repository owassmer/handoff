L2@P0: § 18327. Voting Requirements for Formal Action by the Commission

L9@P0: testimony; to convene, adjourn or recess a meeting; or to conduct discussions.

L10@P0: advice is not an Opinion of the Commission under Section 83114(a), nor a declaration of policy
L11@P0: by the Commission. It may be subject to further review and action by the Commission.
L12@P0: (b) Formal Written Advice. Formal written advice is provided pursuant to Section
L13@P0: 83114(b) as follows:
L14@P0: (1) Request. Any person whose duties under the Act are in question, or that person's
L15@P0: authorized representative, may request formal written advice. A request for advice must include:

L17@P0: question and, where applicable, a statement of authorization and contact information for the
L18@P0: authorized representative; and
L19@P0: (B) A statement of the material facts. If additional facts are needed, Commission staff
L20@P0: will contact the requestor. If a requestor fails to respond to a request for additional information

L22@P0: (b)(6)(C). The time period for formal written advice begins upon receipt of the additional
L23@P0-1: information.2

L25@P1: written acknowledgement of receipt of the request, and any additional written information.

L29@P1: a determination of good cause.

L31@P1: identified as the subject(s) of the request, and to the specific facts set forth in the formal written

L33@P1: (5) Withdrawal. A requestor may withdraw the request at any time prior to receiving
L34@P1: formal written advice. Commission staff will provide written confirmation of the withdrawal.
L35@P1: (6) Decline to give advice. A formal advice request will be declined in writing where the
L36@P1: request:
L37@P1: (A) Relates to past conduct;

L41@P1: (D) Presents a question outside the jurisdiction of the Act;
L42@P1: (E) Regards an unsettled area of law, or an area of the law susceptible to multiple
L43@P1: reasonable interpretations, and thus presents one or more issues that require an interpretation best
L44@P1: considered through the Commission Opinion process set forth in Regulation 18320, the
L45@P1-2: regulatory process, or a legislative action;3
L46@P2: (i) When appropriate, Commission staff will provide the requestor with guidance

L48@P2: (ii) Where advice may be provided without construing the unsettled law or interpretation
L49@P2: issue, Commission staff may provide advice or assistance to the requestor to facilitate timely
L50@P2: compliance with the Act pending further action by the Commission. Advice or assistance

L65@P2: seeking a Commission Opinion pursuant to Regulation 18320.

L70@P3: actions under the Act, or by any agency whose members or employees are subject to the
L71@P3: provisions of the Act.
L72@P3: (2) Response. Commission staff may respond to a request for informal assistance either
L73@P3: orally or in writing.
L74@P3: (3) No immunity. Informal assistance does not provide the requestor with the immunity
L75@P3: set forth in Section 83114.
L76@P3: (4) Decline assistance. Informal assistance will be declined or limited where the request:
L77@P3: (A) Regards past conduct, unless the request regards corrective action to satisfy

L4@P0: (a) A gift of a rebate or discount is both “received” and “accepted” under Section 89503.5

L6@P0: course of business to members of the public without regard to official status. An official who
L7@P0: receives a rebate or discount has the burden of showing that the rebate or discount was made in
L8@P0: the regular course of business to members of the public without regard to official status.
L9@P0: (b) Promise of a Gift. For the purposes of Sections 87100 and 87103(e), a gift is
L10@P0: “promised” on the date it is offered to the official provided the official thereafter obtains actual
L11@P0: possession of the gift or takes any action exercising direction or control over the gift or, with
L12@P0: respect to family members under Regulation 18943, on the date the official becomes aware of a
L13@P0: promise of a gift to a family member provided that the family member obtains actual possession
L14@P0: of the gift or takes any action exercising direction or control over the gift.
L15@P0: (c) General Rule for Return, Donation, or Reimbursement of a Gift. Notwithstanding
L16@P0: Section 89503.5, a gift is neither accepted nor received if, within 30 days:
L17@P0: (1) The gift is returned to the donor, the donor's agent, or the donor's intermediary from
L18@P0: whom the item was received, unused and without receiving anything of value in exchange for the
L19@P0: returned payment or;
L20@P0: (2) The gift is donated, unused, to a 501(c)(3) charitable organization with which the
L21@P0: official, or a member of the official's immediate family, holds no position, or to a state, local, or
L22@P0-1: federal government agency, without being claimed as a deduction for tax purposes or;2
L23@P1: (3) The official reimburses the donor, donor's agent, or the donor's intermediary from
L24@P1: whom the payment was received, in full, or for a portion thereof. If the donor is not reimbursed
L25@P1: for the full value of the payment, the value of the gift the official has received is reduced by the
L26@P1: amount of the reimbursement.
L27@P1: (d) Relief from Disqualification. In order to relieve the official of an otherwise
L28@P1: disqualifying financial interest under Section 87100 the return, donation, or reimbursement of the
L29@P1: gift pursuant to subdivision (c) above:
L30@P1: (1) Must occur within 30 days of receipt and before the date the official makes,
L31@P1: participates in making, or uses official position to influence the governmental decision in
L32@P1: question; or
L33@P1: (2) If the return, donation, or reimbursement has not been made before the decision, and
L34@P1: the gift would otherwise cause the official to be disqualified from participating in a governmental
L35@P1: decision, the official must publicly disclose the receipt of the gift on the public record, disclose
L36@P1: its value, and declare that the return, donation, or reimbursement will occur within two working
L37@P1: days following the decision. The subsequent return, donation, or reimbursement of the gift must
L38@P1: be made within two working days, and within 30 days after receipt or acceptance, and it must be
L39@P1: documented in the public record.
