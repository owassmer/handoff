L90:   * cite37†GoGreen Home Energy Financing Program Regulations (effective October 10, 2025) L91: ##### 7/20/2026 - Notice of Regular Rulemaking
L92: 
L93: The California Alternative Energy and Advanced Transportation Financing Authority (CAEATFA) is issuing a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking with proposed regulation text for the regular rulemaking process for the GoGreen Home Energy Financing Program. The notice is being published on July 31, 2026.
L94: This rulemaking will complete the rulemaking process initiated by the emergency regulation modifications approved by the Office of Administrative Law (OAL) in October 2025. The public comment period for the Notice of Proposed Rulemaking will end on September 14, 2026.
L95: These regulations are comprised of changes made during the emergency regulations process that are proposed to be made permanent. Examples include modifications to clarify eligibility requirements for residential homes to include properties adversely affected by natural disasters and an establishment of a 180-calendar day timeline for project submittals into GoGreen Home.
L96: •    cite38†View the Notice of Proposed Rulemaking L97: •    cite39†View the Initial Statement of Reasons L98: •    cite40†View the Proposed modified Regulations L99: The Authority has not scheduled a public hearing regarding the regulations. However, the Authority will hold a hearing if it receives a written request for a public hearing from any interested person, or his or her authorized representative, no later than 15 days prior to the close of the written comment period. A hearing may be requested by making such a request in writing addressed to dly@treasurer.ca.gov or alingenfelter@treasurer.ca.gov.
L100: Any public hearings will be publicized via the Authority’s email contact list and on this webpage.
L101: 
L102: Any interested parties, or their authorized representatives, may submit written comments relevant to the regulations to the Authority at cheef@treasurer.ca.gov or at a public hearing should one be held. The written comment period on the regulations ends on Monday, September 14, 2024.
L103: If you prefer to receive a hard copy of the Notice of Proposed Regulations or if you have any questions regarding this action, please contact Davey Ly at dly@treasurer.ca.gov or (916) 653-3032.
L104: ##### 6/25/2026 - Emergency Regulations 2^{nd} Readoption
L105: 
L106: On 6/24/2026, the Office of Administrative Law (OAL) approved the 2^{nd}readoption of emergency regulations for the GoGreen Home Program. These regulations will become effective on 7/21/2026, and are effective until 10/20/2026.
L107: 
L108:   * cite41†Approved Form 400 L109:   * cite42†Notice and Finding of Emergency L110: The proposed emergency regulations were publicly noticed, considered, and approved by the CAEATFA Board at a public meeting held on August 19, 2025.
L111: Since its launch, GoGreen Home financing has been available to borrowers retrofitting and/or upgrading existing structures.
L112: However, following the destructive fires in the Los Angeles area that took place in January 2025, there was considerable interest amongst various stakeholders to assist with the rebuilding efforts by utilizing the GoGreen Home program.  As such, CAEATFA would like to clarify that that the Program eligibility includes “reconstruction” specifically allowing victims of natural disasters to access the Program.  The amendments clarify program eligibility for reconstruction of commercial and multifamily buildings following a natural disaster.
L113: Thereby making improved financing rates and terms, expanded creditworthiness, lower energy bills, and a reduced climate impact available to natural disaster victims.  Since the initial emergency action was approved by OAL in file number 2025-1008-01E, CAEATFA has worked diligently to implement these amendments. Given the time sensitive nature of the amendments, it is necessary to readopt the emergency regulations, with no additional modifications from the initial emergency action, to allow CAEATFA enough time to complete the Certificate of Compliance.  CAEATFA is simultaneously working through the Regular Rulemaking process to make these changes permanent in 2026.

L111: #### 6/18/2026 – Notice of Proposed Rulemaking
L112: 
L113: The California Alternative Energy and Advanced Transportation Financing Authority (CAEATFA) is issuing a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking with proposed regulation text for the regular rulemaking process for the Commercial Energy Efficiency Financing (“GoGreen Business”) Program. The Notice is planned to be published in the California Regulatory Notice Register on June 26, 2026.
L114: These regulations will complete the rulemaking process initiated by the emergency regulations adopted on September 29, 2025, and a subsequent emergency rulemaking adopted on November 6, 2025. This regular rulemaking will make permanent the amendments from both emergency rulemaking actions. The public comment period for the Notice of Proposed Rulemaking will end on August 10, 2026.
L115: The regulations include changes made during the emergency process proposed to be made permanent. Some of the changes include expanding eligibility to reconstruction of properties damaged by natural disasters. Changes also include modifications to better meet the needs of the multifamily market including broadened types of security, accelerated loss reserve contributions, and simplified measure eligibility for multifamily properties. Other clarifications and minor modifications are proposed.
