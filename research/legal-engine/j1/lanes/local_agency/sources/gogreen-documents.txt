#### 9/29/2025 – Notice of Adopted Proposed Emergency Regulations: Amendments to the GoGreen Business Program Regulations

On September 29, 2025 the Office of Administrative Law approved regulations to modify the Commercial Energy Efficiency Financing (“GoGreen Business”) Program. These modifications are intended to clarify program eligibility for reconstruction of commercial and multifamily buildings following a natural disaster.

CAEATFA has considered all comments, objections, and recommendations regarding the proposed action. You may review the regulatory documents below:

  * View the approved and endorsed Form 400 Certificate of Compliance filed with the Secretary of State
  * View the approved regulations filed with the Secretary of State

The modified regulations were publicly noticed, considered, and approved by the CAEATFA Board at a public meeting held on August 19, 2025, at 10:30am.

#### 9/10/2025 – Notice of Proposed Emergency Regulations: Amendments to the GoGreen Business Program Regulations

CAEATFA proposes to adopt emergency regulations to modify the Commercial Energy Efficiency Financing (“GoGreen Business”) Program. The proposed modifications are intended to clarify program eligibility for reconstruction of commercial and multifamily buildings following a natural disaster. CAEATFA has considered all comments, objections, and recommendations regarding the proposed action. You may review the regulatory documents below:

  * Notice and Finding of Emergency
  * Proposed Regulatory Text

The proposed regulations were publicly noticed, considered, and approved by the CAEATFA Board at a public meeting held on August 19, 2025, at 10:30am.

Government Code Section 11346.1(a)(2) requires that, at least five working days prior to submission of the proposed adoption of emergency regulations to the Office of Administrative Law (OAL), the adopting agency provide a notice of the proposed emergency action to every person who has filed a request for notice of regulatory action with the agency. CAEATFA sent notice on 9/10/2025. After submission of the proposed emergency regulations to OAL, OAL shall allow interested parties five (5) calendar days to submit comments on the proposed emergency regulations as set forth in Government Code Section 11349.6. Upon filing, OAL will have ten (10) calendar days within which to review and make a decision on the proposed emergency rulemaking package.

#### 9/3/2025 – Workshop slide deck and revised proposed modifications to program regulations.

CAEATFA held a public workshop at 10:30 AM PDT on Monday, August 25, 2025, to propose modifications to the existing regulations for the GoGreen Business Energy Financing Program (GoGreen Business).

CAEATFA invites public comments during the workshop and by email to cheef@treasurer.ca.gov until Monday, September 1, 2025 at 5:00PM PDT.

The revised proposed modifications to program regulations include, among other things, the simplification of the loss reserve contribution calculation for Affordable Multifamily projects and expanded eligibility for multifamily retrofits. These proposed amendments are planned to be heard at the September 16, 2025 board meeting.  Following receiving board approval, CAEATFA will utilize the emergency rulemaking process and submit the proposed regulations to the Office of Administrative Law, with their expected approval in October 2025.
