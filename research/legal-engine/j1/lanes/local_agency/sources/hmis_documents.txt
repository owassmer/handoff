L60@P2: In order to be eligible for housing restricted to chronically homeless
L61@P2: individuals or families under the CoC program, participants must
L62@P2: meet the definition of chronically homeless. The definition of
L63@P2: chronically homeless is:
L64@P2:  A homeless individual with a disability as defined in section
L65@P2: 401(9) of the McKinney-Vento Assistance Act (42 U.S.C.
L66@P2: 11360(9)), who:
L67@P2: o Lives in a place not meant for human habitation, a
L68@P2: safe haven, or in an emergency shelter, and
L69@P2: o Has been homeless and living as described for at
L70@P2: least 12 months* or on at least 4 separate
L71@P2: occasions in the last 3 years, as long as the
L72@P2: combined occasions equal at least 12 months and
L73@P2: each break in homelessness separating the
L74@P2: occasions included at least 7 consecutive nights of
L75@P2: not living as described.
L76@P2:  An individual who has been residing in an institutional care
L77@P2: facility for less, including jail, substance abuse or mental
L78@P2: health treatment facility, hospital, or other similar facility,
L79@P2: for fewer than 90 days and met all of the criteria of this
L80@P2: definition before entering that facility**; or
L81@P2:  A family with an adult head of household (or, if there is no

L96@P3: Continuum of Care CoC
L97@P3: The Continuum of Care is a collection of nonprofits, agencies, and
L98@P3: people with lived experience of homelessness that come together to
L99@P3: promote community wide commitment to the goal of ending
L100@P3: homelessness; promote access to and affect utilization of
L101@P3: mainstream programs by individuals and families experiencing
L102@P3: homelessness; and optimize self-sufficiency among individuals and
L103@P3: families experiencing homelessness.

L120@P3: Continuum of Care
L121@P3: Program CoC Program
L122@P3: The CoC Program is designed to assist individuals (including
L123@P3: unaccompanied youth) and families experiencing homelessness and
L124@P3: to provide the services needed to help such individuals move into
L125@P3: transitional and permanent housing, with the goal of long-term
L126@P3: stability. More broadly, the program is designed to promote
L127@P3: community-wide planning and strategic use of resources to address
L128@P3: homelessness; improve coordination and integration with
L129@P3: mainstream resources and other programs targeted to people
L130@P3: experiencing homelessness; improve data collection and
L131@P3: performance measurement; and allow each community to tailor its
L132@P3: program to the particular strengths and challenges within that
L133@P3: community.

L147@P4: Coverage Rate
L148@P4: Coverage rate refers to the percentage of beds targeted to serve
L149@P4: people experiencing homelessness in a geographic area that are
L150@P4: captured in the HMIS divided by the total number of beds targeted
L151@P4: to serve people experiencing homelessness in the geographic area
L152@P4: (excluding beds targeted to serve domestic violence clients).
L153@P4: Coverage rate estimates are used to project a total homeless count if
L154@P4: there are homeless service providers in the jurisdiction that do not
L155@P4: participate in HMIS.
