# [Archives] CalRecycle Informal Hearing Rules Regulations

CalRecycle maintains archived information on its website as a record of its activities for use by the public and CalRecycle staff.

## Overview

On May 12, 2026, the Office of Administrative Law (OAL) approved the permanent regulations. The effective date of these regulations is July 1, 2026.

The Department of Resources Recycling and Recovery (CalRecycle)  proposed permanent regulations to implement a unified set of hearing procedures applicable to CalRecycle’s informal hearings. These standardized procedures aim to ensure consistency across all CalRecycle informal hearings.

These proposed regulations conform to the Administrative Procedure Act and aim to promote consistency, transparency, and accessibility in the informal hearing process.

L52: ## Overview
L53: 
L54: On June 24, 2026, the Office of Administrative Law (OAL) approved the Covered Electronic Waste Recycling Program Enforcement Emergency Regulations. The regulations became effective on June 24, 2026, and will expire on June 24, 2028.
L55: The Department of Resources Recycling and Recovery (CalRecycle) proposed to adopt emergency regulations in response to cite36†CalRecycle’s Informal Hearing Rules Regulations . The rulemaking expressly exempts the Covered Electronic Waste (CEW) Recycling Program from the regulations and procedures instituted in CalRecycle’s newly adopted Informal Hearing Rules Regulations and specifies the burden of proof on an appeal of a payment claim adjustment or denial.
L56: These emergency regulations are necessary to maintain existing program practices with respect to appeals and administrative hearings and to provide clarification with respect to the burden of proof on an appeal of a decision by CalRecycle to adjust or deny a payment claim.

L58: 
L59: CalRecycle proposed emergency regulations to amend Title 14, California Code of Regulations (CCR), Articles 2.1, 2.3, and 6, sections 18660.19, 18660.31, 18660.44, 18660.45, and 18660.46.
