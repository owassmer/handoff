L2383@P32: The Department of Fish and Wildlife (Department)
L2384@P32: received a proposal from Mason Holmes, senior biologist with the Santa Clara Valley Water District, requesting authorization to take California Ridgway’s
L2385@P32: rail (Rallus obsoletus obsoletus), formerly known
L2386@P32: as California clapper rail, and California black rail
L2387@P32: (Laterallus jamaicensis coturniculus) (‘rails’), Fully
L2388@P32: Protected birds, for scientific research purposes consistent with conservation and recovery of the species.
L2389@P32: The California Ridgway’s rail is listed as Endangered
L2390@P32: under the California Endangered Species Act and Endangered under the federal Endangered Species Act,
L2391@P32: and the California black rail is listed as Threatened
L2392@P32: under the California Endangered Species Act.
L2393@P32: Mason Holmes is planning to conduct surveys
L2394@P32: throughout the range of the rails in the Bay Area,
L2395@P32: in accordance with a standard protocol approved
L2396@P32: by the Department and the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (Service). The proposed research activities consist of searching for vocalizing individuals
L2397@P32: of the rails and employing broadcasts of recorded,
L2398@P32: species–specific vocalizations to determine distribution and status of local populations. Mason Holmes
L2399@P32: and other qualified researchers would collect data by
L2400@P32: interpreting calls received from marsh birds that respond to broadcast vocalizations and by observing individual rails. There would be no attempt to capture
L2401@P32: individual rails or to approach nests of the rails, unless
L2402@P32: specifically approved by the Department. If any rails
L2403@P32: are found dead, they will be salvaged and donated to a
L2404@P32: scientific institution open to the public, as designated
L2405@P32: by the Department and the Service. No adverse effects
L2406@P32: on individual rails or rail populations are expected.
L2407@P32: The Department intends to issue a Memorandum of
L2408@P32: Understanding to the Santa Clara Valley Water District that would authorize Mason Holmes and other qualified professional wildlife researchers, under
L2409@P32: specified conditions, to carry out the proposed activities. The applicants are also required to have a valid
L2410@P32: federal recovery permit for the Ridgway’s rail.
