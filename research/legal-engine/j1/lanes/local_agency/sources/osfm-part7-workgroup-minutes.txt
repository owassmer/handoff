L302@P6: that the system draw is comparable to domestic usage and would not run
L303@P6: all zones simultaneously.
L304@P6: iv. Kevin Scott also raised an independent but related concept: requiring an

L306@P6: areas, whether or not sprinklers are installed. The rationale is that when
