L116@P2: § 3591.33. Emerald Ash Borer Eradication Area.
L117@P2: (a) Proclamation of Eradication Area. The area where the insect emerald ash
L118@P2: borer (Agrilus planipennis, Family Buprestidae, Order Coleoptera)
L119@P2: is known to exist or there is an immediate threat of introduction is hereby proclaimed to
L120@P2: be an eradication area with respectto said pest:
L121@P2: The entire State of California.
L122@P2: (b) Articles, Commodities, Hosts, and Carriers Covered .
L123@P2: .1. The emerald ash borer, any living life stage.
L124@P2: b Firewood of ash trees (Fraxinus species) and mixed firewood containing
L125@P2: hardwood (non-coniferous) species from an infested area. Infested area means
L126@P2: a nation, state, or part of state where the Department has determined more than
L127@P2: six trees contain emerald ash borer adults or larvae. Part of a state shall only be
L128@P2: designated an infested area if survey data provided by a state plant regulatory
L129@P2: agency or the United States Department of Agriculture is detailed enough to
L130@P2: indicate six or more trees containing emerald ash borer adults or larvae are
L131@P2: confined to a particular part of the state.
L132@P2: ~ Nursery stock of ash, and other ash tree material, including green lumber
L133@P2: and other woody material living, dead, cut, or fallen, including logs, stumps,
L134@P2: roots, branches, and composted and uncomposted chips.
L135@P2: (c) Means and Methods. The following means and methods may be used in the
L136@P2: eradication, control or suppression of said pest within said area.
L137@P2: ill The repeated application of contact insecticide spray treatments, deployment of
L138@P2: bait stations, insecticide soil drenches, release of sterile insects, insecticide
L139@P2: treatments of host trees and any other articles or things which are infested or
L140@P2: exposed to infestation and capable of harboring or spreading emerald ash borer.
L141@P2: m The removal and destruction of any and all possible carriers, including host plant
L142@P2: nursery stock or trees and shrubs if such action is the only practical way of
L143@P2: eliminating the infestation of a host or possible carrier to prevent the spread or
L144@P2: reinfestation of emerald ash borer .
L145@P2: .Q} The searching for all stages of emerald ash borer by visual inspection, the use of
L146@P2: traps to detect or delimit, or any other means anywhere within said area.
L147@P2: 11} The removal and destruction of abandoned or unwanted hosts and possible
L148@P2-3: carriers. .(fil The importation, rearing, liberation, and fostering of parasites, predators, or
L149@P3: pathogens that attack emerald ash borer in any life stage.
L150@P3: NOTE: Authority cited: Sections 407 and 5322, Food and Agricultural Code.
L151@P3: Reference: Sections 5761 , 5762, and 5763, Food and Agricultural Code.
