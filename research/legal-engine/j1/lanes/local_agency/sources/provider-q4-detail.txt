## 

§ 828. Review of Application for Home Improvement Salesperson Registration.

Currentness

(a) The Board shall inform, in writing, an applicant for registration as home improvement salesperson within 30 days of receipt whether the application is deficient and what specific information is required or whether the registration has been issued.

(b) When an application is returned which was previously rejected for deficiencies, the Board shall decide whether the applicant meets the requirements for registration within 5 days after return of the completed application. A “completed application” means that an acceptable application form together with all required information, documentation and fee has been filed by the applicant.

(c) The time periods outlined in (a) and (b) may be extended by 5 weeks if the fee is in the form of a personal or company check, or by 60 days if an application requires investigation to determine if a statement of issues must be filed.

L3883@P143: perform debris removal during a declared disaster. Out of necessity, CSLB has made
L3884@P143: this determination on an ad hoc basis during recent declared disasters, but a permanent
L3885@P143: solution is needed. By specifying who is authorized to perform debris removal during a
L3886@P143: declared disaster, including muck-out and ash-out operations, and requiring that
L3887@P143: contractors possess sufficient training and certification to conduct debris removal,
L3888@P143: worker safety is enhanced and the risk of additional health and environmental hazards
L3889@P143: resulting from improper debris removal and disposal practices can be mitigated.
L3890@P143: In the wake of unprecedented natural disasters in California over the past several years,
L3891@P143: residential rebuilding efforts have commenced across the state in areas devastated by
L3892@P143: floods, fires, and earthquakes. CSLB has received inquiries from concerned parties
L3893@P143: about whether contractors digging to remove contaminated materials from these
L3894@P143: devasted areas are trained or have the qualifications to do the work safely. This
L3895@P143: proposal does not alter existing law as it relates to debris removal on sites unrelated to
L3896@P143: a disaster declaration. Limiting this proposal to the removal of debris by certain
L3897@P143: classifications who hold Hazardous Substance Removal Certification in a disaster area
L3898@P143: only is intended to address the safe disposal of dangerous contaminants and chemicals
L3899@P143: left behind after property is damaged or destroyed by a disaster.
