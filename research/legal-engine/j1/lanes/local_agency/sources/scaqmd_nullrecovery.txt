L17@P4: Monitoring Computer. The RTU shall collect data, perform calculations, generate the
L18@P4: appropriate data files, and transmit the data to the Central Station. The Facility Permit
L19@P4: holder of a large source or process unit may elect to use an RTU. Alternatively, the
L20@P4: Facility Permit holder of a large source or process unit shall compile the required rule

L24@P4: Central Station emissions from all RECLAIM SOx sources. Alternative to transmitting
L25@P4: data to the District Central Station, the Facility Permit holder may use the District

L28@P4: A. GENERAL
L29@P4: 1. General
L30@P4: The Facility Permit holder of a major source shall telecommunicate rule
L31@P4: compliance data to the District via Remote Terminal Units (RTU). This
L32@P4: form of reporting may also be used for large sources or process units. The
L33@P4: RTU shall collect data from a CEMS, a CPMS, or other equipment
L34@P4: specified in the Facility Permit and send data periodically to the Central
L35@P4: Station Emissions Monitoring Computer (Central Station).
L36@P4: This chapter specifies the tasks and characteristics required of the RTU
L37@P4: and shall be used as a guide for providing the required software/hardware
L38@P4: for the RTU. Emissions Data Collection System conformity as well as
L39@P4: establishing and maintaining communications with the emission
L40@P4: monitoring system and the Central Station shall be the responsibility of the
L41@P4: Facility Permit holder. This chapter also serves as a functional guideline
L42@P4: for operating requirements of the RTU, and provides information
L43@P4: concerning RTU hardware/software procurement, configuration,
L44@P4: installation, maintenance, and compatibility with the emission monitoring
L45@P4: system and the Central Station.
L46@P4: In lieu of using a modem, the Facility Permit holder of a major source may
L47@P4: elect to use an alternative electronic reporting system approved by the
L48@P4: Executive Officer to telecommunicate rule compliance data to the South
L49@P4: Coast AQMD Central Station Emissions Monitoring Computer. Any use
L50@P4: of an approved alternative electronic reporting system shall be in
L51@P4: accordance with specifications approved by the Executive Officer.
