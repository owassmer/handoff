### New Residential Construction

Submit an application for residential new construction if you need to establish natural gas service by installing new pipeline and/ or meter(s) to a residential construction site that did not have prior service, or to re-establish natural gas service by installing pipeline and/or meter(s) to a structure that was demolished and will be rebuilt.

You may also submit this application if your project involves replacing a SoCalGas meter with a larger capacity meter (meter upsize) due to the installation of additional natural gas appliances on your residential site.

Permits for construction must be secured before completing this application.

Start Application > Get Estimate
