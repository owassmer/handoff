L387: The Water Boards develop and issue general permits to cover multiple facilities within a specific category. The use of general permits allows the Water Boards to allocate resources in a more efficient manner and provide timely permit coverage for large numbers of facilities in the same category. In addition, the use of a general permit ensures consistency of permit conditions for similar facilities.
L388: According to cite202†40 CFR section 122.28†www.gpo.gov , general permits may be written to cover categories of point sources having common elements, such as:
L389:   * Stormwater point sources
L390:   * Facilities that involve the same or substantially similar types of operations
L391:   * Facilities that discharge the same types of wastewater
L392:   * Facilities that require the same effluent limitations or operating conditions
L393:   * Facilities that require the same monitoring where tiered conditions may be used for minor differences within class (e.g., size or seasonal activity)
L394:   * Facilities that are more appropriately regulated by a general permit
L395: However, general permits may only be issued to dischargers within a specific geographical area such as the following:
L396: 
L397:   * City, County, or State boundary
L398:   * Designated planning area
L399:   * Sewer district
L400:   * Standard metropolitan statistical area
L401:   * State highway system
L402:   * Urbanized area
L403: 
L404: Each general permit typically includes the following information:
L405:   * How to apply for coverage under a general permit - see the "Notice of Intent" (NOI) form
L406:   * How to request rescission from coverage - see the "Notice of Termination" (NOT) form
