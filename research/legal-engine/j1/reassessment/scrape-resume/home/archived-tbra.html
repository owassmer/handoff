Tenant Based Rental Assistance

The City of Huntington Beach allocates HOME funds to support three tenant-based rental assistance programs. Families Forward and Mercy House currently operates two of these programs, each aimed at assisting households comprising homeless individuals, veterans, and victims of domestic violence. The third program, operated by GRC Associates, is specifically designed to aid senior residents of mobile home communities facing escalating space rent costs. If you are a mobile home owner, please click here to learn more.
