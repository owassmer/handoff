                           Division 4. Environmental Health
          Chapter 15. Domestic Water Quality and Monitoring Regulations
                 Article 19. Records, Reporting and Recordkeeping


Amend Section 64469 to read as follows:
§ 64469. Reporting Requirements.
(a) Analytical results of all sample analyses completed in a calendar month shall be
reported to the State Board no later than the tenth day of the following month.
(b) Analytical results of all sample analyses completed by water wholesalers in a
calendar month shall be reported to retail customers and the State Board no later than
the tenth day of the following month.
(c) Except for the results of field tests not conducted by a laboratory, each public water
system shall use a laboratory duly accredited by the State Board for performing analysis
of drinking water for regulatory purposes that complies with the reporting requirements
specified in this section, including the requirements for submitting data, as prescribed
below:
   (1) A laboratory performing chemical and radiological analyses on drinking water
   samples shall report analytical results to the State Board electronically through the
   California Laboratory Intake Portal (CLIP), using the
   CA_SDWIS_Lab_Analytical_Data Format. If the State Board is unable to accept
   results for these specific analytes electronically using CLIP, the results shall be
   submitted via mail or email.
   (2) A laboratory performing microbiological analyses on drinking water samples shall
   report analytical results to the State Board via mail or email.
Analytical results shall be reported to the State Board electronically using the Electronic
Deliverable Format as defined in The Electronic Deliverable Format [EDF] Version 1.2i
Guidelines & Restrictions dated April 2001 and Data Dictionary dated April 2001.
