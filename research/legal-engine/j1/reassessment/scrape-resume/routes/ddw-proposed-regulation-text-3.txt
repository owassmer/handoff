                     TITLE 22, CALIFORNIA CODE OF REGULATIONS

                            Division 4. Environmental Health
          Chapter 15. Domestic Water Quality and Monitoring Regulations
                  Article 19. Records, Reporting and Recordkeeping


Amend Section 64469 to read as follows:
§ 64469. Reporting Requirements.
(a) Analytical results of all sample analyses completed in a calendar month shall be
reported to the State Board no later than the tenth day of the following month.
(b) Analytical results of all sample analyses completed by water wholesalers in a
calendar month shall be reported to retail customers and the State Board no later than
the tenth day of the following month.
(c) Analytical results shall be reported to the State Board electronically by a laboratory
duly certified by the State Board in the form and format as specified in this section.
using the Electronic Deliverable Format as defined in The Electronic Deliverable Format
[EDF] Version 1.2i Guidelines & Restrictions dated April 2001 and Data Dictionary dated
April 2001.
(d) Within 10 days of giving initial or repeat public notice pursuant to Article 18 of this
Chapter, except for notice given under section 64463.7(d), each water system shall
submit a certification to the State Board that it has done so, along with a representative
copy of each type of public notice given.
(e) Each public water system shall only use a laboratory for performing analysis of
drinking water for regulatory purposes that complies with the reporting requirements
specified in this section, including the requirements for submitting data, as prescribed
below:
   (1) A laboratory performing chemical and radiological analyses on drinking water
   samples shall report analytical results to the State Board electronically through the
   California Laboratory Intake Portal (CLIP). If the State Board is unable to accept
   results for these specific analytes electronically using CLIP, the results shall be
   submitted via mail or email.

   (2) A laboratory performing microbiological analyses on drinking water samples shall
   report analytical results to the State Board via mail or email. Once the State Board

   used for microbiological analyses, the laboratory will have three (3) months from the
   date of notification to fully implement the reporting under that paragraph.
