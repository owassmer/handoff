Board Meeting Media Archive 2007 to Present

Board Meeting Media Archive 2007 to Present

Records of the County of Orange Board of Supervisors’ from 01/09/2007 to 04/14/2026 may be searched below. To view the minutes for a Board Meeting, click on the minutes link corresponding to the meeting date. In the minutes, you will find individual agenda items with hyperlinks to access the Agenda Staff Report and any supporting documents related to the item. To view a Board meeting video, click on video link corresponding to the meeting date.

 If you require additional assistance, please contact the Board Services Division of the Clerk of the Board at 714-834-2206.
