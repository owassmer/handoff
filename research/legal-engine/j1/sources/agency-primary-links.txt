L459@P12: SECTION I – INTRODUCTION
L460@P12: Part 1.1 Background of the CTCAC Program
L461@P12: In 1986, Congress enacted the Low-Income Housing Tax Credit Program (LIHTC). This
L462@P12: program provides incentives for the investment of private equity capital in the development of
L463@P12: affordable rental housing. The LIHTC reduces the federal tax liability of project owners in
L464@P12: exchange for the acquisition, rehabilitation, or construction of affordable rental housing units that
L465@P12: will remain income and rent restricted over a long period. The amount of tax credit allocated is
L466@P12: based on the number of qualified low-income units that meet federal rent and income targeting
L467@P12: requirements.
L468@P12: The LIHTC is authorized and governed by Section 42 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as
L469@P12: amended (the “Code”). The California Tax Credit Allocation Committee (CTCAC) is the
L470@P12: designated “housing credit agency” to allocate and administer tax credits for the entire state.
L471@P12: Each state develops a Qualified Allocation Plan (QAP), which establishes the guidelines and
L472@P12: procedures for the acceptance, scoring, and competitive ranking of applications and for the
L473@P12: administration of the LIHTC Program. The QAP and California’s Program Regulations are
