L18@P2: I. BACKGROUND
L19@P2: Under the Stewart B. McKinney Homeless Assistance Act, in 1996, the Orange County Housing
L20@P2: Authority (OCHA) and the Orange County Health Care Agency (HCA) established a partnership to
L21@P2: compete in the U.S Department of Housing and Urban Development’s annual SuperNOFA
L22@P2: competition for funds to implement the Shelter Plus Care Program. Shelter Plus Care Program
L23@P2: linked rental assistance with supportive services for hard-to-serve homeless persons with
L24@P2: disabilities.
L25@P2: As a result of the aforementioned, OCHA was awarded its initial funding for Shelter Plus Care
L26@P2: Program in 1997 and implemented the program the same year. Over the course of the following
L27@P2: 16 years, OCHA, HCA, and subsequently additional supportive services partners successfully
L28@P2: administered Shelter Plus Care Program in compliance with U.S. Department of Housing and
L29@P2: Urban Development (HUD) requirements and consistently exceeded HUD’s performance
L30@P2: expectations.
L31@P2: In 2009, the Homeless Emergency Assistance and Rapid Transition to Housing (HEARTH) Act,
L32@P2: was enacted amending the McKinney-Vento Homeless Assistance Act (formerly referred to as the
L33@P2: Stewart B. McKinney Homeless Assistance Act) to:
L34@P2: 1. Promote countywide commitment to the goal of ending homelessness
L35@P2: 2. Provide funding for efforts by nonprofit providers, States, and local governments to quickly
L36@P2: rehouse the homeless, while minimizing the trauma and dislocation caused by
L37@P2: homelessness
L38@P2: 3. Promote access to and effective utilization of mainstream programs by homeless
L39@P2: 4. Optimize self-sufficiency among those experiencing homelessness.
L40@P2: The HEARTH Act consolidated the three separate McKinney-Vento Homeless Assistance Act
L41@P2: programs, including the Supportive Housing Program, Shelter Plus Care Program, and Section 8
L42@P2: Moderate Rehabilitation Single Room Occupancy Program into a single grant program known as
L43@P2: the Continuum of Care (CoC) Program.
L44@P2: In response to the aforementioned amendment, in 2012, HUD published the Continuum of Care
L45@P2: (CoC) Program Interim Rule establishing the “Continuum of Care Program”. Via the annual
L46@P2: Continuum of Care Program competition, the CoC Program awards funds to eligible organizations
L47@P2: to provide housing and/or services to assist the homeless with the ultimate goal of long-term
L48@P2: stability.
L49@P2: Shelter Plus Care Program projects that received renewal funding under a Continuum of Care
L50@P2: Program competition are now referred to as Continuum of Care Program Permanent Supportive
L51@P2: Housing (PSH) projects.
L52@P2: II. PURPOSE
L53@P2: The purpose of this guidebook is to document OCHA’s administration of its CoC Program PSH
L54@P2: projects.
