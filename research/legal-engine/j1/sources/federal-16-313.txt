L114: 
L115: cite23†65 FR 33677†www.federalregister.gov , May 24, 2000, unless otherwise noted.
L116: #### § 313.1 Purpose and scope.
L117: 
L118: (a) Purpose. This part governs the treatment of nonpublic personal information about consumers by the financial institutions listed in cite24†paragraph (b) of this section. This part:
L119: 
L120: (1) Requires a financial institution in specified circumstances to provide notice to customers about its privacy policies and practices;
L121: (2) Describes the conditions under which a financial institution may disclose nonpublic personal information about consumers to nonaffiliated third parties; and
L122: 
L123: (3) Provides a method for consumers to prevent a financial institution from disclosing that information to most nonaffiliated third parties by “opting out” of that disclosure, subject to the exceptions in cite25†§§ 313.13 , cite26†313.14 , and cite27†313.15 .
L124: (b) Scope. This part applies only to nonpublic personal information about individuals who obtain financial products or services primarily for personal, family or household purposes from the institutions listed below. This part does not apply to information about companies or about individuals who obtain financial products or services for business, commercial, or agricultural purposes.
L125: This part applies to those “financial institutions” over which the Federal Trade Commission (“Commission”) has rulemaking authority pursuant to section 504(a)(1)(C) of the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act. An entity is a “financial institution” if its business is engaging in an activity that is financial in nature or incidental to such financial activities as described in section 4(k) of the Bank Holding Company Act of 1956, cite28†12 U.S.C.
L126: 1843(k)†www.govinfo.gov , which incorporates activities enumerated by the Federal Reserve Board in cite29†12 CFR 225.28 and cite30†225.86 . The “financial institutions” subject to the Commission's rulemaking authority are any persons described in cite22†12 U.S.C. 5519†www.govinfo.gov that are predominantly engaged in the sale and servicing of motor vehicles, the leasing and servicing of motor vehicles, or both.
L127: They are referred to in this part as “You.” Excluded from the coverage of this part are motor vehicle dealers described in cite22†12 U.S.C. 5519(b)†www.govinfo.gov that directly extend to consumers retail credit or retail leases involving motor vehicles in which the contract governing such extension of retail credit or retail leases is not routinely assigned to an unaffiliated third party finance or leasing source.
L128: [cite23†65 FR 33677†www.federalregister.gov , May 24, 2000, as amended at cite31†86 FR 70025†www.federalregister.gov , Dec. 9, 2021]
L129: #### § 313.2 Model privacy form and examples.
L130: 
L131: (a) Model privacy form. Use of the model privacy form in cite32†appendix A of this part , consistent with the instructions in appendix A, constitutes compliance with the notice content requirements of cite33†§§ 313.6 and cite34†313.7 of this part , although use of the model privacy form is not required.
L132: 
L133: (b) Examples. The examples in this part are not exclusive. Compliance with an example, to the extent applicable, constitutes compliance with this part.
L134: [cite35†74 FR 62965†www.federalregister.gov , Dec. 1, 2009]
L135: #### § 313.3 Definitions.
L136: 
L137: As used in this part, unless the context requires otherwise:
L138: 
L139: (a) Affiliate means any company that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with another company.
L140: 
L141: (b)
L142: 
L143: (1) Clear and conspicuous means that a notice is reasonably understandable and designed to call attention to the nature and significance of the information in the notice.
L144: 
L145: (2) Examples —
L146: 
L147: (i) Reasonably understandable. You make your notice reasonably understandable if you:
L148: (A) Present the information in the notice in clear, concise sentences, paragraphs, and sections;
L149: 
L150: (B) Use short explanatory sentences or bullet lists whenever possible;
L151: 
L152: (C) Use definite, concrete, everyday words and active voice whenever possible;
L153: 
L154: (D) Avoid multiple negatives;
L155: 
L156: (E) Avoid legal and highly technical business terminology whenever possible; and
L157: 
L158: (F) Avoid explanations that are imprecise and readily subject to different interpretations.
L159: (ii) Designed to call attention. You design your notice to call attention to the nature and significance of the information in it if you:
L160: 
L161: (A) Use a plain-language heading to call attention to the notice;
L162: 
L163: (B) Use a typeface and type size that are easy to read;
L164: 
L165: (C) Provide wide margins and ample line spacing;
L166: 
L167: (D) Use boldface or italics for key words; and
L168: (E) In a form that combines your notice with other information, use distinctive type size, style, and graphic devices, such as shading or sidebars, when you combine your notice with other information.
L169: (iii) Notices on web sites. If you provide a notice on a web page, you design your notice to call attention to the nature and significance of the information in it if you use text or visual cues to encourage scrolling down the page if necessary to view the entire notice and ensure that other elements on the web site (such as text, graphics, hyperlinks, or sound) do not distract attention from the notice, and you either:
L170: (A) Place the notice on a screen that consumers frequently access, such as a page on which transactions are conducted; or
L171: 
L172: (B) Place a link on a screen that consumers frequently access, such as a page on which transactions are conducted, that connects directly to the notice and is labeled appropriately to convey the importance, nature and relevance of the notice.
L173: (c) Collect means to obtain information that you organize or can retrieve by the name of an individual or by identifying number, symbol, or other identifying particular assigned to the individual, irrespective of the source of the underlying information.
L174: 
L175: (d) Company means any corporation, limited liability company, business trust, general or limited partnership, association, or similar organization.
L176: 
L177: (e)
L178: (1) Consumer means an individual who obtains or has obtained a financial product or service from you that is to be used primarily for personal, family, or household purposes, or that individual's legal representative.
L179: 
L180: (2) For example:
L181: 
L182: (i) An individual who applies to you for credit for personal, family, or household purposes is a consumer of a financial service, regardless of whether the credit is extended.
L183: (ii) An individual who provides nonpublic personal information to you in order to obtain a determination about whether he or she may qualify for a loan to be used primarily for personal, family, or household purposes is a consumer of a financial service, regardless of whether the loan is extended.
L184: (iii) If you hold ownership or servicing rights to an individual's loan that is used primarily for personal, family, or household purposes, the individual is your consumer, even if you hold those rights in conjunction with one or more other institutions.
L185: (The individual is also a consumer with respect to the other financial institutions involved.) An individual who has a loan in which you have ownership or servicing rights is your consumer, even if you, or another institution with those rights, hire an agent to collect on the loan.
L186: (iv) An individual who is a consumer of another financial institution is not your consumer solely because you act as agent for, or provide processing or other services to, that financial institution.
L187: 
L188: (v) An individual is not your consumer solely because he or she is a participant or a beneficiary of an employee benefit plan that you sponsor or for which you act as a trustee or fiduciary.
L189: (f) Consumer reporting agency has the same meaning as in section 603(f) of the Fair Credit Reporting Act (cite36†15 U.S.C. 1681a(f)†www.govinfo.gov ).
L190: 
L191: (g) Control of a company means:
L192: 
L193: (1) Ownership, control, or power to vote 25 percent or more of the outstanding shares of any class of voting security of the company, directly or indirectly, or acting through one or more other persons;
L194: (2) Control in any manner over the election of a majority of the directors, trustees, or general partners (or individuals exercising similar functions) of the company; or
L195: 
L196: (3) The power to exercise, directly or indirectly, a controlling influence over the management or policies of the company.
L197: 
L198: (h) Customer means a consumer who has a customer relationship with you.
L199: 
L200: (i)
L201: (1) Customer relationship means a continuing relationship between a consumer and you under which you provide one or more financial products or services to the consumer that are to be used primarily for personal, family, or household purposes.
L202: 
L203: (2) For example:
L204: 
L205: (i) Continuing relationship. A consumer has a continuing relationship with you if the consumer:
L206: 
L207: (A) Has a credit or investment account with you;
L208: 
L209: (B) Obtains a loan from you;
L210: 
L211: (C) Purchases an insurance product from you;
L212: (D) Enters into an agreement or understanding with you whereby you undertake to arrange or broker a home mortgage loan, or credit to purchase a vehicle, for the consumer;
L213: 
L214: (E) Enters into a lease of personal property on a non-operating basis with you; or
L215: 
L216: (F) Has a loan for which you own the servicing rights.
L217: 
L218: (ii) No continuing relationship. A consumer does not, however, have a continuing relationship with you if:
L219: (A) The consumer obtains a financial product or service from you only in isolated transactions, such as cashing a check with you or making a wire transfer through you;
L220: 
L221: (B) You sell the consumer's loan and do not retain the rights to service that loan; or
L222: 
L223: (C) The consumer obtains one-time personal appraisal services from you.
L224: 
L225: (j) Federal functional regulator means:
L226: 
L227: (1) The Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System;
L228: 
L229: (2) The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency;
L230: (3) The Board of Directors of the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation;
L231:
