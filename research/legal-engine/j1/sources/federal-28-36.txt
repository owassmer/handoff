L284: ## Subpart A—General
L285: #### § 36.101 Purpose and broad coverage.
L286: (a) Purpose. The purpose of this part is to implement subtitle A of title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 (cite97†42 U.S.C. 12181-12189†www.govinfo.gov ), as amended by the ADA Amendments Act of 2008 (ADA Amendments Act) (cite98†Pub. L. 110-325†www.govinfo.gov , 122 Stat.
L287: 3553 (2008)), which prohibits discrimination on the basis of disability by covered public accommodations and requires places of public accommodation and commercial facilities to be designed, constructed, and altered in compliance with the accessibility standards established by this part.
