L115: ## Subpart A—General
L116: #### § 262.1 Terms used in this part.
L117: 
L118: As used in this part:
L119: Condition for exemption means any requirement in cite27†§ 262.14 , cite28†§ 262.15 , cite29†§ 262.16 , cite30†§ 262.17 , cite31†§ 262.70 , or cite32†subpart K or cite33†L of this part that states an event, action, or standard that must occur or be met in order to obtain an exemption from any applicable requirement in cite34†parts 124 , cite35†264 through cite36†268 , and cite37†270 of this chapter , or from any requirement for notification under section 3010 of RCRA for treatment storage, and disposal facilities.
L120: Independent requirement means a requirement of part 262 that states an event, action, or standard that must occur or be met; and that applies without relation to, or irrespective of, the purpose of obtaining a conditional exemption from storage facility permit, interim status, and operating requirements under cite27†§ 262.14 , cite28†§ 262.15 , cite29†§ 262.16 , cite30†§ 262.17 , or subpart K or cite33†subpart L of this part .
L121: [cite38†81 FR 85806†www.federalregister.gov , Nov. 28, 2016, as amended at cite39†88 FR 54108†www.federalregister.gov , Aug. 9, 2023]
L122: #### § 262.10 Purpose, scope, and applicability.
L123: 
L124: (a) The regulations in this part establish standards for generators of hazardous waste as defined by cite40†40 CFR 260.10 .
L125: 
L126: (1) A person who generates a hazardous waste as defined by cite41†40 CFR part 261 is subject to all the applicable independent requirements in the subparts and sections listed below:
L127: 
L128: (i) Independent requirements of a very small quantity generator.
L129: 
L130: (A) Section 262.11(a) through (d) Hazardous waste determination and recordkeeping; and
L131: (B) Section 262.13 Generator category determination.
L132: 
L133: (ii) Independent requirements of a small quantity generator.
L134: 
L135: (A) Section 262.11 Hazardous waste determination and recordkeeping;
L136: 
L137: (B) Section 262.13 Generator category determination;
L138: 
L139: (C) Section 262.18 EPA identification numbers and re-notification for small quantity generators and large quantity generators;
L140: 
L141: (D) Part 262 subpart B—Manifest requirements applicable to small and large quantity generators;
L142: (E) Part 262 subpart C—Pre-transport requirements applicable to small and large quantity generators;
L143: 
L144: (F) Section 262.40 Recordkeeping;
L145: 
L146: (G) Section 262.44 Recordkeeping for small quantity generators; and
L147: 
L148: (H) Part 262 subpart H—Transboundary movements of hazardous waste for recovery or disposal.
L149: 
L150: (iii) Independent requirements of a large quantity generator.
L151: 
L152: (A) Section 262.11 Hazardous waste determination and recordkeeping;
L153: 
L154: (B) Section 262.13 Generator category determination;
L155: (C) Section 262.18 EPA identification numbers and re-notification for small quantity generators and large quantity generators;
L156: 
L157: (D) Part 262 subpart B—Manifest requirements applicable to small and large quantity generators;
L158: 
L159: (E) Part 262 subpart C—Pre-transport requirements applicable to small and large quantity generators;
L160: 
L161: (F) Part 262 subpart D—Recordkeeping and reporting applicable to small and large quantity generators, except cite42†§ 262.44 ; and
L162: (G) Part 262 subpart H—Transboundary movements of hazardous waste for recovery or disposal.
L163: 
L164: (2) A generator that accumulates hazardous waste on site is a person that stores hazardous waste; such generator is subject to the applicable requirements of cite34†parts 124 , cite35†264 through cite43†267 , and cite37†270 of this chapter and section 3010 of RCRA for treatment, storage, and disposal facilities, unless it is one of the following:
L165: (i) A very small quantity generator that meets the conditions for exemption in cite27†§ 262.14 ;
L166: 
L167: (ii) A small quantity generator that meets the conditions for exemption in cite28†§§ 262.15 and cite29†262.16 ; or
L168: 
L169: (iii) A large quantity generator that meets the conditions for exemption in cite28†§§ 262.15 and cite30†262.17 .
L170: (3) A generator shall not transport, offer its hazardous waste for transport, or otherwise cause its hazardous waste to be sent to a facility that is not a designated facility, as defined in cite40†§ 260.10 of this chapter , or not otherwise authorized to receive the generator's hazardous waste.
L171: (b) Determining generator category. A generator must use cite44†§ 262.13 to determine which provisions of this part are applicable to the generator based on the quantity of hazardous waste generated per calendar month.
L172: 
L173: (c) [Reserved]
L174: 
L175: (d) Any person who exports or imports hazardous wastes must comply with cite45†§ 262.18 and cite46†subpart H of this part .
L176: 
L177: (e) Any person who imports hazardous waste into the United States must comply with the standards applicable to generators established in this part.
L178: (f) A farmer who generates waste pesticides which are hazardous waste and who complies with all of the requirements of cite31†§ 262.70 is not required to comply with other standards in this part or cite37†40 CFR parts 270 , cite35†264 , cite47†265 , cite43†267 , or cite36†268 with respect to such pesticides.
L179: 
L180: (g)
L181: 
L182: (1) A generator's violation of an independent requirement is subject to penalty and injunctive relief under section 3008 of RCRA.
L183: (2) A generator's noncompliance with a condition for exemption in this part is not subject to penalty or injunctive relief under section 3008 of RCRA as a violation of a cite48†40 CFR part 262 condition for exemption.
L184: Noncompliance by any generator with an applicable condition for exemption from storage permit and operations requirements means that the facility is a storage facility operating without an exemption from the permit, interim status, and operations requirements in cite34†40 CFR parts 124 , cite35†264 through cite43†267 , and cite37†270 of this chapter, and the notification requirements of section 3010 of RCRA.
L185: Without an exemption, any violations of such storage requirements are subject to penalty and injunctive relief under section 3008 of RCRA.
L186: (h) An owner or operator who initiates a shipment of hazardous waste from a treatment, storage, or disposal facility must comply with the generator standards established in this part.
L187: 
L188: (i) Persons responding to an explosives or munitions emergency in accordance with cite49†40 CFR 264.1(g)(8)(i)(D) or cite50†(iv) or cite51†265.1(c)(11)(i)(D) or cite52†(iv) , and cite53†270.1(c)(3)(i)(D) or cite54†(iii) are not required to comply with the standards of this part.
L189: 
L190: (j) [Reserved]
L191: (k) Generators in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts may comply with the State regulations regarding Class A recyclable materials in 310 C.M.R.
L192: 30.200, when authorized by the EPA under cite55†40 CFR part 271 , with respect to those recyclable materials and matters covered by the authorization, instead of complying with the hazardous waste accumulation conditions for exemption in cite28†§§ 262.15 through cite30†262.17 , the reporting requirements of cite56†§ 262.41 , the storage facility operator requirements of cite35†40 CFR parts 264 , cite47†265 , and cite43†267 , and the permitting requirements of cite37†40 CFR part 270 .
L193: Such generators must also comply with any other applicable requirements, including any applicable authorized State regulations governing hazardous wastes not being recycled and any applicable Federal requirements which are being directly implemented by the EPA within Massachusetts pursuant to the Hazardous and Solid Waste Amendments of 1984.
L194: (l) The laboratories owned by an eligible academic entity that chooses to be subject to the requirements of cite32†subpart K of this part are not subject to (for purposes of this paragraph, the terms “laboratory” and “eligible academic entity” shall have the meaning as defined in cite57†§ 262.200 ):
L195: 
L196: (1) The independent requirements of cite58†§ 262.11 or the regulations in cite28†§ 262.15 for large quantity generators and small quantity generators, except as provided in subpart K, and
L197: (2) The conditions of cite27†§ 262.14 , for very small quantity generators, except as provided in subpart K.
L198: 
L199: (m) All reverse distributors (as defined in cite59†§ 266.500 ) are subject to cite60†40 CFR part 266 subpart P for the management of hazardous waste pharmaceuticals in lieu of this part.
L200: (n) Each healthcare facility (as defined in cite59†§ 266.500 ) must determine whether it is subject to cite60†40 CFR part 266 subpart P for the management of hazardous waste pharmaceuticals, based on the total hazardous waste it generates per calendar month (including both hazardous waste pharmaceuticals and non-pharmaceutical hazardous waste).
