L115: ## Subpart A—General
L116: #### § 273.1 Scope.
L117: 
L118: (a) This part establishes requirements for managing the following:
L119: 
L120: (1) Batteries as described in cite27†40 CFR 273.2 ;
L121: 
L122: (2) Pesticides as described in cite28†§ 273.3 ;
L123: 
L124: (3) Mercury-containing equipment as described in cite29†§ 273.4 ;
L125: 
L126: (4) Lamps as described in cite30†§ 273.5 ; and
L127: 
L128: (5) Aerosol cans as described in cite31†§ 273.6 .
L129: 
L130: (b) This part provides an alternative set of management standards in lieu of regulation under cite32†40 CFR parts 260 through cite33†272 .
L131: [cite26†60 FR 25542†www.federalregister.gov , May 11, 1995, as amended at cite34†64 FR 36488†www.federalregister.gov , July 6, 1999; cite35†70 FR 45520†www.federalregister.gov , Aug. 5, 2005; cite36†84 FR 67217†www.federalregister.gov , Dec. 9, 2019]
L132: #### § 273.2 Applicability—batteries.
L133: 
L134: (a) Batteries covered under cite37†40 CFR part 273 .
L135: 
L136: (1) The requirements of this part apply to persons managing batteries, as described in cite38†§ 273.9 , except those listed in cite39†paragraph (b) of this section.
L137: 
L138: (2) Spent lead-acid batteries which are not managed under cite40†40 CFR part 266, subpart G , are subject to management under this part.
L139: (b) Batteries not covered under cite37†40 CFR part 273 . The requirements of this part do not apply to persons managing the following batteries:
L140: 
L141: (1) Spent lead-acid batteries that are managed under cite40†40 CFR part 266, subpart G .
L142: 
L143: (2) Batteries, as described in cite38†§ 273.9 , that are not yet wastes under cite41†part 261 of this chapter , including those that do not meet the criteria for waste generation in cite42†paragraph (c) of this section.
L144: (3) Batteries, as described in cite38†§ 273.9 , that are not hazardous waste. A battery is a hazardous waste if it exhibits one or more of the characteristics identified in cite43†part 261, subpart C of this chapter .
L145: 
L146: (c) Generation of waste batteries.
L147: 
L148: (1) A used battery becomes a waste on the date it is discarded (e.g., when sent for reclamation).
L149: 
L150: (2) An unused battery becomes a waste on the date the handler decides to discard it.
L151: [cite26†60 FR 25542†www.federalregister.gov , May 11, 1995, as amended at cite34†64 FR 36488†www.federalregister.gov , July 6, 1999]
L152: #### § 273.3 Applicability—pesticides.
L153: 
L154: (a) Pesticides covered under this part 273. The requirements of this part apply to persons managing pesticides, as described in cite38†§ 273.9 , meeting the following conditions, except those listed in cite44†paragraph (b) of this section:
L155: 
L156: (1) Recalled pesticides that are:
L157: (i) Stocks of a suspended and canceled pesticide that are part of a voluntary or mandatory recall under FIFRA Section 19(b), including, but not limited to those owned by the registrant responsible for conducting the recall; or
L158: 
L159: (ii) Stocks of a suspended or cancelled pesticide, or a pesticide that is not in compliance with FIFRA, that are part of a voluntary recall by the registrant.
L160: (2) Stocks of other unused pesticide products that are collected and managed as part of a waste pesticide collection program.
L161: 
L162: (b) Pesticides not covered under cite37†40 CFR part 273 . The requirements of this part do not apply to persons managing the following pesticides:
L163: (1) Recalled pesticides described in cite45†paragraph (a)(1) of this section, and unused pesticide products described in cite46†paragraph (a)(2) of this section, that are managed by farmers in compliance with cite47†40 CFR 262.70 . (cite47†40 CFR 262.70 addresses pesticides disposed of on the farmer's own farm in a manner consistent with the disposal instructions on the pesticide label, providing the container is triple rinsed in accordance with cite48†40 CFR 261.7(b)(3) );
L164: (2) Pesticides not meeting the conditions set forth in cite49†paragraph (a) of this section. These pesticides must be managed in compliance with the hazardous waste regulations in cite32†40 CFR parts 260 through cite33†272 , except that aerosol cans as defined in cite38†§ 273.9 that contain pesticides may be managed as aerosol can universal waste under cite50†§ 273.13(e) or cite51†§ 273.33(e) ;
L165: (3) Pesticides that are not wastes under cite41†part 261 of this chapter , including those that do not meet the criteria for waste generation in cite52†paragraph (c) of this section or those that are not wastes as described in cite53†paragraph (d) of this section; and
L166: 
L167: (4) Pesticides that are not hazardous waste. A pesticide is a hazardous waste if it is listed in cite54†40 CFR part 261, subpart D or if it exhibits one or more of the characteristics identified in cite43†40 CFR part 261, subpart C .
L168: (c) When a pesticide becomes a waste.
L169: 
L170: (1) A recalled pesticide described in cite45†paragraph (a)(1) of this section becomes a waste on the first date on which both of the following conditions apply:
L171: 
L172: (i) The generator of the recalled pesticide agrees to participate in the recall; and
L173: 
L174: (ii) The person conducting the recall decides to discard (e.g., burn the pesticide for energy recovery).
L175: (2) An unused pesticide product described in cite46†paragraph (a)(2) of this section becomes a waste on the date the generator decides to discard it.
L176: 
L177: (d) Pesticides that are not wastes. The following pesticides are not wastes:
L178: 
L179: (1) Recalled pesticides described in cite45†paragraph (a)(1) of this section, provided that the person conducting the recall:
L180: (i) Has not made a decision to discard (e.g., burn for energy recovery) the pesticide. Until such a decision is made, the pesticide does not meet the definition of “solid waste” under cite55†40 CFR 261.2 ; thus the pesticide is not a hazardous waste and is not subject to hazardous waste requirements, including this part 273. This pesticide remains subject to the requirements of FIFRA; or
L181: (ii) Has made a decision to use a management option that, under cite55†40 CFR 261.2 , does not cause the pesticide to be a solid waste (i.e., the selected option is use (other than use constituting disposal) or reuse (other than burning for energy recovery), or reclamation). Such a pesticide is not a solid waste and therefore is not a hazardous waste, and is not subject to the hazardous waste requirements including this part 273.
L182: This pesticide, including a recalled pesticide that is exported to a foreign destination for use or reuse, remains subject to the requirements of FIFRA.
L183: (2) Unused pesticide products described in cite46†paragraph (a)(2) of this section, if the generator of the unused pesticide product has not decided to discard (e.g., burn for energy recovery) them. These pesticides remain subject to the requirements of FIFRA.
L184: 
L185: [cite26†60 FR 25542†www.federalregister.gov , May 11, 1995, as amended at cite34†64 FR 36488†www.federalregister.gov , July 6, 1999; cite56†84 FR 67218†www.federalregister.gov , Dec. 9, 2019]
L186: #### § 273.4 Applicability—Mercury-containing equipment.
L187: 
L188: (a) Mercury-containing equipment covered under this part 273. The requirements of this part apply to persons managing mercury-containing equipment, as described in cite38†§ 273.9 , except those listed in cite57†paragraph (b) of this section.
L189: 
L190: (b) Mercury-containing equipment not covered under this part 273. The requirements of this part do not apply to persons managing the following mercury-containing equipment:
L191: (1) Mercury-containing equipment that is not yet a waste under cite41†part 261 of this chapter . cite58†Paragraph (c) of this section describes when mercury-containing equipment becomes a waste;
L192: 
L193: (2) Mercury-containing equipment that is not a hazardous waste. Mercury-containing equipment is a hazardous waste if it exhibits one or more of the characteristics identified in cite43†part 261, subpart C of this chapter or is listed in cite54†part 261, subpart D of this chapter ; and
L194: (3) Equipment and devices from which the mercury-containing components have been removed.
L195: 
L196: (c) Generation of waste mercury-containing equipment.
L197: 
L198: (1) Used mercury-containing equipment becomes a waste on the date it is discarded.
L199: 
L200: (2) Unused mercury-containing equipment becomes a waste on the date the handler decides to discard it.
L201: 
L202: [cite35†70 FR 45520†www.federalregister.gov , Aug. 5, 2005]
L203: #### § 273.5 Applicability—lamps.
L204: 
L205: (a) Lamps covered under this part 273. The requirements of this part apply to persons managing lamps as described in cite38†§ 273.9 , except those listed in cite59†paragraph (b) of this section.
L206: 
L207: (b) Lamps not covered under this part 273. The requirements of this part do not apply to persons managing the following lamps:
L208:
