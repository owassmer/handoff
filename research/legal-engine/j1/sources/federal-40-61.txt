L183: #### § 61.02 Definitions.
L184: 
L185: The terms used in this part are defined in the Act or in this section as follows:
L186: 
L187: Act means the Clean Air Act (cite19†42 U.S.C. 7401†www.govinfo.gov et seq.).
L188: 
L189: Administrator means the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency or his authorized representative.
L190: Alternative method means any method of sampling and analyzing for an air pollutant which is not a reference method but which has been demonstrated to the Administrator's satisfaction to produce results adequate for the Administrator's determination of compliance.
L191: Approved permit program means a State permit program approved by the Administrator as meeting the requirements of cite53†part 70 of this chapter or a Federal permit program established in this chapter pursuant to title V of the Act (cite52†42 U.S.C. 7661†www.govinfo.gov ).
L192: Capital expenditure means an expenditure for a physical or operational change to a stationary source which exceeds the product of the applicable “annual asset guideline repair allowance percentage” specified in the latest edition of Internal Revenue Service (IRS) Publication 534 and the stationary source's basis, as defined by section 1012 of the Internal Revenue Code.
L193: However, the total expenditure for a physical or operational change to a stationary source must not be reduced by any “excluded additions” as defined for stationary sources constructed after December 31, 1981, in IRS Publication 534, as would be done for tax purposes. In addition, “annual asset guideline repair allowance” may be used even though it is excluded for tax purposes in IRS Publication 534.
L194: Commenced means, with respect to the definition of “new source” in section 111(a)(2) of the Act, that an owner or operator has undertaken a continuous program of construction or modification or that an owner or operator has entered into a contractual obligation to undertake and complete, within a reasonable time, a continuous program of construction or modification.
L195: Compliance schedule means the date or dates by which a source or category of sources is required to comply with the standards of this part and with any steps toward such compliance which are set forth in a waiver of compliance under cite60†§ 61.11 .
L196: 
L197: Construction means fabrication, erection, or installation of an affected facility.
L198: 
L199: Effective date is the date of promulgation in the Federal Register of an applicable standard or other regulation under this part.
L200: Existing source means any stationary source which is not a new source.
L201: Force majeure means, for purposes of cite61†§ 61.13 , an event that will be or has been caused by circumstances beyond the control of the affected facility, its contractors, or any entity controlled by the affected facility that prevents the owner or operator from complying with the regulatory requirement to conduct performance tests within the specified timeframe despite the affected facility's best efforts to fulfill the obligation.
L202: Examples of such events are acts of nature, acts of war or terrorism, or equipment failure or safety hazard beyond the control of the affected facility.
L203: Issuance of a part 70 permit will occur, if the State is the permitting authority, in accordance with the requirements of cite53†part 70 of this chapter and the applicable, approved State permit program. When the EPA is the permitting authority, issuance of a title V permit occurs immediately after the EPA takes final action on the final permit.
L204: Monitoring system means any system, required under the monitoring sections in applicable subparts, used to sample and condition (if applicable), to analyze, and to provide a record of emissions or process parameters.
L205: 
L206: New source means any stationary source, the construction or modification of which is commenced after the publication in the Federal Register of proposed national emission standards for hazardous air pollutants which will be applicable to such source.
L207: Owner or operator means any person who owns, leases, operates, controls, or supervises a stationary source.
L208: 
L209: Part 70 permit means any permit issued, renewed, or revised pursuant to cite53†part 70 of this chapter .
