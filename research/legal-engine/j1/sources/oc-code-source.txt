Sec. 7-9-21.3. - General terms.

(a)

County means the County of Orange including any special district governed by the Board of Supervisors.

(b)

Days included within a specific time period do not include the day action was taken but include all subsequent days unless the last day falls upon a Saturday, Sunday, or upon a legal County holiday, in which case the next business day shall be the last day of the time period.

(c)

Director or Director, EMA means the Director of the Planning and Development Services Department, County of Orange, or his authorized agent or representative.

(d)

General Plan means the Orange County General Plan.

(e)

Government Code means the California Government Code.

(f)

State means the State of California.

(g)

Zoning Code means the Comprehensive Zoning Code of the County of Orange, including zoning district maps and planned community or specific plan development plan maps and texts adopted pursuant to or as an amendment to sections 7-9-48, 7-9-103, 7-9-155, and 7-9-156.

Sec. 1-2-63. - Position established.

Share Link to section Print section Download (Docx) of sections Email section

There is hereby created in and for the County of Orange a position to be known and designated as "County Executive Officer." The County Executive Officer shall be appointed by the Board of Supervisors on the basis of educational qualifications and executive ability as demonstrated by responsible administrative experience. Upon appointment, the County Executive Officer becomes an employee of the County. His or her salary and other terms and conditions of employment shall be specially set by the Board of Supervisors.

(Code 1961, § 12.061; Ord. No. 2650, § 1, 2-6-73; Ord. No. 3515, § 1, 4-23-85; Ord. No. 3955, § 1, 12-19-95; Ord. No. 98-15, § 4, 12-8-98)

PREAMBLE

Share Link to section Print section Download (Docx) of sections Email section

We, the citizens of Orange County, with a desire for self-determination in selecting our county elected officials and to initiate the process to govern our county by charter government, do hereby adopt this charter.

ARTICLE I. - BOARD OF SUPERVISORS

Share Link to section Print section Download (Docx) of sections Email section

  * #### 

101. - Governing Body.

Share Link to section Print section Download (Docx) of sections Email section

The governing body of the county is a Board of Supervisors of five (5) members elected by and from designated supervisorial districts.

Except as expressly set forth in this charter, the general law set forth in the Constitution of the State of California and the laws of the State of California shall govern the operations of the County of Orange.

  * #### 

203. - County Ordinances Enacted by the Voters Remain in Effect.

Share Link to section Print section Download (Docx) of sections Email section

Ordinances of the County of Orange adopted by the voters prior to the enactment of this charter shall remain in full force and effect and may only be modified or repealed by a vote of the people.

(a)

The Board of Supervisors shall not take any action, by ordinance, resolution, or otherwise, which increases the retirement benefits of any employee, legislative officer or elected official of the County of Orange in the Orange County Employees Retirement System or any successor retirement system ("retirement system"), with the exception of statutorily-established cost of living adjustments, salary increases, and annual leave or compensatory time cash-outs, without first obtaining the approval of a majority of those qualified electors voting on the matter.
