L2776@P84: Action Item 3. Official Form 106C (Schedule C: The Property You Claim as Exempt)
L2777@P84: The Advisory Committee received a suggestion from a chapter 12/chapter 13 trustee to
L2778@P84: amend Official Form 106C to include a total value of claimed exemptions. Section 589b(d)(3) of
L2779@P84: title 28 requires the uniform final report submitted by trustees to total the “assets exempted.”
L2780@P84: Without the amount totaled on the form, trustees must manually add up the amounts on each form
L2781@P84: to prepare the required final report.
L2782@P84: Complicating the decision of how to respond to the suggestion was the fact that Form
L2783@P84: 106C—in response to a statement by the Supreme Court in Schwab v. Reilly, 560 U.S. 770
L2784@P84: (2010)—provides two options for claiming an exemption. The debtor can either list a specific
L2785@P84: dollar amount or can claim as exempt “100% of fair market value, up to any applicable statutory
L2786@P84: limit.” If the latter option is chosen, there is no dollar amount that can be added.
L2787@P84: The Advisory Committee resolved this problem by proposing an amendment to Form 106C
L2788@P84: to require the debtor to “[a]dd the dollar value of all entries [in Column C] with a specific amount.”
L2789@P84: It also approved for publication the addition of a space on the form for the total value of the debtor’s
L2790@P84: interest in property for which exemptions are claimed (in Column B).
L2791@P84: The form was published last August, and one comment was filed in response. The National
L2792@P84: Association of Consumer Bankruptcy Attorneys (“NACBA”) expressed concern about the
L2793@P84: wording of the directive to add the total value of the debtor’s interest in property for which
L2794@P84: exemptions are claimed. NACBA urged the Advisory Committee to revise the form to “add the
L2795@P84: language ‘with a specific amount’ to the section calling for the total for Column B.” (NACBA also
L2796@P84: suggested revisiting the approach taken on the form in response to Schwab v. Reilly.)
L2797@P84: The suggestion that prompted the proposed amendments to Form 106C only sought a total
L2798@P84: value of the claimed exemptions and not a total of the property values. The addition to the form of
L2799@P84: space for the latter was really an afterthought—for the sake of symmetry. The Consumer
L2800@P84: Subcommittee considered whether there is a need for a total of Column B. It decided that there is
L2801@P84: not because trustees do not include that total in their final report and that figure has no significance
L2802@P84: in the bankruptcy case. The subcommittee therefore recommended that item 2.1 be deleted from
L2803@P84: the form as published and that, with that deletion and the renumbering of item 2.2, the amendment
L2804@P84: to Official Form 106C be given final approval.
L2805@P84: The Advisory Committee recommended final approval of the amendments to Form 106C
L2806@P84: with the deletion of item 2.1 and the renumbering of item 2.2 and agreed with the subcommittee
L2807@P84: not to revisit Schwab.
