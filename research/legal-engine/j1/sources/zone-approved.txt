L58: MISSION & GOALS
L59: 
L60: The Zone Zero Regulatory Advisory Committee is a special advisory committee of the Board of Forestry and Fire Protection. Established in 2025, the Committee is tasked with developing defensible space regulations establishing a zero to five‑foot ember‑resistant zone, as directed by Public Resources Code Section 4291 and Executive Order No. N‑18‑25.

L81: ## [Button: Zone 0 (0-5 ft) ]
L82: In response to the increased frequency and intensity of wildfires in California over the last decade, the Legislature passed cite34†AB 3074 (2020)†leginfo.legislature.ca.gov and cite35†SB 504 (2024)†leginfo.legislature.ca.gov , making changes to cite36†Public Resource Code 4291†leginfo.legislature.ca.gov .
L83: These changes require the Board of Forestry and Fire Protection (Board) to establish an ember-resistant defensible zone the first 5 feet around a structure, referred to as Zone 0.  Governor Newsom’s cite37†Executive Order N-18-25†34c031f8-c9fd-4018-8c5a-4159cdff6b0d-cdn-endpoint.azureedge.net requires that the Board complete rulemaking for Zone 0 no later than December 31, 2025.

L116: ## [Button: Zone 1 (5-30 ft) ]
L117: 
L118: Zone 1 extends from Zone 0 (first 5 feet around a structure) to 30 feet around a structure and deck or to the property line if less than 30 feet. Zone 1 is designed to reduce wildfire risk by minimizing flammable materials and maintaining vegetation in a way that slows fire spread.
L119: 
L120: Zone 1 Requirements:
L121:   * Remove dead leaves, pine needles, and debris from your yard, roof, and gutters.
L122:   * Trim overhanging branches to at least 10 feet away from chimneys.
L123:   * Keep trees pruned to maintain spacing.
L124:   * Clear flammable vegetation and items from under decks, balconies, and stairs.
L125:   * Maintain space between trees and shrubs.
L126:   * Ensure outbuildings and propane tanks have 10 feet of clearance with no flammable vegetation nearby.
L127: Creating and maintaining defensible space is crucial for wildfire safety, helping protect homes and firefighters.
L128: ## [Button: Zone 2 (30-100 ft) ]
L129: 
L130: Zone 2 extends 30 to 100 feet around a structure or to the property line if less than 100 feet. Zone 2 is designed to slow the spread of wildfire by reducing the amount of vegetation that could fuel flames.
L131: 
L132: Zone 2 Requirements:
L133:   * Mow grass to a maximum height of 4 inches.
L134:   * Create horizontal spacing between shrubs and trees.
L135:   * Remove dead vegetation and fallen leaves.
L136:   * Prune lower branches of trees.
L137:   * Ensure outbuildings and propane tanks have 10 feet of clearance with no flammable vegetation nearby.
L138: 
L139: This zone helps create a buffer that reduces fire intensity before it reaches a structure.
