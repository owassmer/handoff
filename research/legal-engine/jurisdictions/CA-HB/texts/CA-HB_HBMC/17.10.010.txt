§ 17.10.010 Nuisance Defined.
As used in this chapter, a nuisance shall mean activities or conditions which affect the social and economic stability of neighborhoods, impair property values and which are injurious or detrimental to the health, safety and general welfare of the citizens of Huntington Beach.
