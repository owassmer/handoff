§ 17.10.040 Abatement of Nuisance Related Activities or Condition.
Any activity, condition or premise(s) maintained as described herein is declared to be a public nuisance and shall be abated by cessation of the activity, rehabilitation, demolition, removal, repair or other appropriate remedy pursuant to the procedures set forth in this chapter.

(3386-3/98, 3398-7/98)
