§ 17.10.070 Alternatives.

Nothing in the foregoing sections shall prevent the City Attorney from commencing a civil or criminal proceeding to abate a public nuisance under applicable provisions of the California Civil Code or Penal Code as an alternative to the proceedings set forth herein.

(3386-3/98, 3398-7/98)
