§ 17.10.080 Reward.
The City Council may provide a reward, by resolution, for information leading to the identification and apprehension of any person who applies graffiti within the City. In cases where multiple persons are identified and apprehended, based on a single graffiti incident, only one reward is authorized.

(3765-4/07, 3777-10/07)
