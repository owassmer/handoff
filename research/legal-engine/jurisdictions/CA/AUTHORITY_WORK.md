## Deposit accounting and independent recovery

The [June 11, 2024 Senate Judiciary analysis of AB2801](https://sjud.senate.ca.gov/system/files/2024-06/ab-2801-friedman-sjud-analysis.pdf), discussing the April 25 bill version, distinguishes bad-faith documentation failure from good-faith noncompliance. Printed page 11 describes the proposed bar on claiming the deposit and separately discusses a landlord offsetting a tenant's damages with otherwise proper deductions after a good-faith violation. It cites *250 L.L.C. v. PhotoPoint Corp.*, 131 Cal.App.4th 702, 727 (2005). This gives a concrete legislative-history path for the current-amendment question. It does not decide the effect of the final enacted text or establish the bad-faith landlord's independent rights. The retained web capture contains the relevant passages and version metadata.

Next: retrieve the cited opinion in full, inspect its commercial/residential context and treatment of Granberry, compare the final AB2801 language with the analyzed version and current section 1950.5, and search subsequent controlling treatment. Do not convert the committee's description of good faith into a rule that a manager's assertion of honest error proves compliance.
