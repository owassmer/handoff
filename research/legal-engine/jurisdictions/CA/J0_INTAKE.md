The engine covers California residential physical work and outgoing-account resolution: lawful access and occupancy, necessary repairs, safe and permitted work, vendor commitments, tenant responsibility, deposits, refunds, recovery, disputes, litigation and close-out. Earlier tenancy facts and protections remain included wherever they change these decisions. The legal functions determine source selection. Other cities need their own local layer before a city-complete answer.

## 2. Which law and courts

The current geographic chain is **US → California → Orange County → Huntington Beach**. County coverage is conditional: incorporated-city location does not automatically import unincorporated-only ordinances. City adoption and each provision's territorial reach must be read. The county's [enforcement description](https://pwds.oc.gov/service-areas/oc-development-services/neighborhood-preservation/community-resources) illustrates that distinction. Programs attach according to their authority, terms and covered units. The US legacy research remains subject to reuse/currentness review.

The court map includes California's Supreme Court, all six appellate districts, state trial courts, the Orange County Superior Court and appellate division, all four California federal district and bankruptcy courts, the Ninth Circuit/BAP and US Supreme Court. The current local application forums are Orange County and the federal Central District, with jurisdiction and venue determined for the actual proceeding.

Orange County's ordinary unlimited-civil appeals go to Fourth District, Division Three; limited-civil appeals go to the superior court appellate division; eligible small-claims appeals are new trials. These routes do not limit precedent research to local appellate decisions. [Orange County appellate manual](https://appellate.courts.ca.gov/district-courts/4dca/publication/self-help-manual-orange-county-civil-appeals), [civil appeal routing](https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/appeals/which-court), [small-claims appeals](https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/small-claims/after-trial/small-claims-appeals).

California Supreme Court authority and published appellate holdings govern according to hierarchy, conflicts and subsequent history; all appellate districts must be searched. [Auto Equity Sales](https://law.justia.com/cases/california/supreme-court/2d/57/450.html). Publication and review status require the actual opinion and orders under [Rule 8.1115](https://courts.ca.gov/cms/rules/index/eight/rule8_1115), including its conflict exception. Federal and California authority are separated by issue: Ninth Circuit holdings govern federal courts, not state courts; federal courts applying California law must examine the California authorities. [Moore](https://law.justia.com/cases/california/court-of-appeal/4th/50/1202.html), [Ryman](https://openjurist.org/505/f3d/993/ryman-v-sears-roebuck-co).

The profile's `binds` graph is a search aid. Its accompanying issue and publication rules are necessary to decide what a holding controls. The current J5 script cannot make that substantive decision. Appellate-division, BAP and district opinions remain in the research plan without inventing universal binding relationships.

## 3. What facts change applicability

Each profile now records the fact, the legal question it changes, the evidence to obtain and source references. The inputs cover:

- Geography and actual tenancy type.
- Certificate-of-occupancy date, building/property/portfolio unit counts, ADU status and owner occupancy.
- Ownership/entity structure, separate title, transfers and statutory exemption notices.
- Recorded affordability restrictions, covered units and exact assistance/financing programs.
- Lease and occupant history, plus the separate dates of legally significant events.
- Household protections, condition/work requirements, and account/collector/payment configurations.

The distinctions have practical consequences. Rent and termination exceptions do not erase deposit duties. The small-landlord deposit exception uses portfolio counts and owner type, not just the size of one building. A program label does not establish which agreement governs a unit. These distinctions follow the separate application provisions in [CIV 1946.2](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1946.2.), [1947.12](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1947.12.) and [1950.5](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1950.5.). J1 onward must derive their full rules and dependencies; these intake entries are not executable substitutes.
