# Federal atoms for a California apartment account (proposal)

Source: `research/legal-engine/stage-a/US.json` (359 atoms, scoped to New York and Virginia, resolution pass 2026-09-28).

This is a proposed classification only. Nothing has been migrated. Atom ids below omit the `US:` prefix.

California's federal forum is the Ninth Circuit; the coordinating Orange County court is C.D. Cal. Wherever an atom's "controlling authority" is Second or Fourth Circuit law, its legal effect must be re-grounded on Ninth Circuit or Supreme Court authority before it is used for California.

Federal law applies by its own terms. California changes three things:
- which state deadline or rule a federal provision defers to;
- how far Rosenthal (Civ 1788.17) incorporates the FDCPA;
- which forum authority controls.
