# Outgoing-account collection: investigated operating branches

Reopened synthesis, October 1, 2026. Updated legal conclusions passed final independent integration review. These branches support Handoff's investigation, recommendations and authorized execution. They do not constitute a deployed ruleset or permission to bypass other applicable account, tenancy, reporting or litigation requirements. Exact source texts and source locations are retained in the three research directories.

## Operating purpose

These findings support the financial side of Handoff's complete tenancy-ending workflow. Physical work can continue while an account dispute is resolved; account deadlines can require action before repairs finish. Evidence produced by inspections and repairs informs the account, but a vendor cost does not itself establish tenant responsibility. The agent must recommend a practical course using the supported balance, evidence, recovery prospects, expense, delay and accepted instructions, then carry authorized work to completion.

The decision is not simply whether collection is permitted. It is what Handoff should do next to resolve the account economically and defensibly: obtain evidence, correct a charge, explain a supported claim, refund, negotiate, recover or recommend stopping. Applicable legal duties shape that work. They do not prescribe an organizational chart or replace the agent's reasoning with a checklist.

## Start with the account and intended action

Before recommending a demand, transfer, settlement or collection restart, reconcile the balance and proposed action. Separate rent, agreed services/utilities, damage claims, fees, interest, deposit applications, concessions and payments. A disputed or incorrectly asserted amount is still an alleged debt for applicable collection protections. Missing agreement, date or authority evidence is an investigation task; it is not zero liability, a valid charge, or an automatic legal exemption.

## Apply holds together

Maintain each hold with its trigger, debt/portion, affected action, completion evidence and legal basis. Supplying a document packet clears that documentation hold only. It does not clear identity-theft review, a cease-communication request, bankruptcy, missing authority, settled balance or an independently applicable reporting restriction. Conversely, a communication restriction is not automatically debt forgiveness. The agent should recommend the next useful allowed action—retrieve evidence, correct a charge, send a permitted response, propose settlement, or stop recovery—rather than treating every restriction as a request for operator instructions.
