## The two regimes must remain separate

CIV1788.14.5 addresses a debt collector assigned qualifying delinquent covered debt: nonmortgage, at least90days past due, not charged off (g). Consumer-debt sale/assignment event must be on/after July1,2022 (h); covered-commercial event on/after July1,2025 (i). SB531 chapter455(2021) originally added the consumer rule; SB1286 chapter522(2024), effectiveJan1,2025, broadened the covered-debt terminology and added commercial-event treatment. SB531 proposals containing30-day delinquency or a separate creditor transfer notice are not the enacted rule.

CIV1788.50 defines the debt buyer as a person/entity regularly purchasing charged-off consumer debt for collection; use of another collector or lawyer does not change buyer status. Charge-off means removal from creditor asset books and treatment as loss/expense, not mere nonpayment. An incidental charged-off acquisition in a predominantly uncharged-off consumer portfolio is excluded. Returned-check acquisition by the specified check-services company is not a purchase under this title. The title applies to consumer debt sold/resold on/after Jan1,2014; old origination alone does not exclude a later resale. Rosenthal definitions carry into the title under1788.50(c), so a tenancy balance must first satisfy applicable consumer-debt meaning. Commercial expansion of Rosenthal does not automatically rewrite the debt-buyer title's consumer-only wording.

1788.14.5(f) gives a qualifying debt buyer that complies with1788.52 deemed compliance with1788.14.5. This is not a choice to use whichever deadline is longer, nor permission to treat every assignment as a debt purchase. Preserve the predicate facts and apply the correct branch.

## Assigned uncharged-off debt: source1788.14.5(a)–(e)

Before a written collection statement, collector must have access to the contract or other document evidencing the debtor's agreement. If no signed contract/agreement exists, the substitute must be a document provided while the account was active demonstrating that debtor incurred the debt; a newly invented post-dispute ledger is not automatically sufficient. The special revolving-credit monthly-statement alternative is not a general lease exception. Access is the statutory standard here, not necessarily original-paper possession.

A written debtor request for debt information or proof triggers free provision of both(a)information and(b)documents within30calendar days of receipt. Required information: collection authority; balance separated from total interest/fees with amount/nature/reason explanations; delinquency date or last-payment date; identifiable creditor name/address/account number; debtor name/last known address before assignment; identifiable names/addresses of every other assignee; collector California license number if applicable. This paragraph does not itself authorize the charged amounts.

If the information/documents cannot be supplied within30calendar days, cease all collection until supplied. This provision alone does not impose an immediate stop on receipt; a concurrent1692g dispute or identity-theft trigger may. The section has no express30-day-from-initial-notice window limiting the debtor's request. Do not import the debt-buyer section's express1692g consistency clause into this section.

Provide an active postal request address to contacted debtors; email is optional and requires parties' agreement for the stated request/delivery arrangement. The first written communication must carry the exact separate prominent statutory notice in at least12-point type. If initial oral contact principally uses another language, provide that notice in that language within5business days. The source keeps that translation clock distinct from the30calendar-day document-response clock.

## Purchased charged-off consumer debt: source1788.52(a)–(g)

Before any written collection statement the buyer must possess the(a)information as well as have access to the(b)agreement document. Required information differs from the assigned branch: sole ownership/authority for all owners; balance at charge-off and separately identified aggregate post-charge-off interest/fees with nature/reason explanation; default or last payment; charge-off creditor name/address/account; debtor identity/address from charge-off creditor before sale (with the express pre2014 alternative); every post-charge-off purchaser including the current buyer; California buyer license number. No specific itemization is required by(a)(2), but the three financial categories must remain separate.

Free information/document response is due within15calendar days of receipt of the debtor's written request; inability to provide it within that period requires all collection to cease until supplied. Unlike1788.14.5, this paragraph expressly makes the request consistent with1692g validation requirements except as otherwise provided by the title. The completed authority and federal investigations leave the late-request interpretation limit explained below; do not publish a blanket rule that all requests indefinitely trigger this deadline or that all later disputes may be ignored.

Active postal address; optional agreed email arrangement. First written communication requires the exact12-point separate prominent records notice. Time-barred debts require the appropriate(d)(2)or(d)(3)notice selected by federal reporting obsolescence, rather than a generic notice. Initial oral non-English contact requires the(d)notice in that language within5working days. Hospital-origin debts have an additional(f)notice dependency; that branch is outside a residential-tenancy account, but must not be lost from the source map. Under(g), federal law prevails only in the stated impracticable conflict with(d); it does not globally erase state requirements.

## Direct follow-on operating consequences

1788.54 requires settlement agreements documented in open court or reduced to writing and a copy to debtor; payments require receipt/statement with the specified account/payment/balance information within30calendar days, for a partial payment; a full/final settlement instead requires a final statement within the same 30 calendar days, incorporating subsection (b)’s required contents. It prohibits selling an interest in a resolved debt or personal/financial information relating to it. This matters to settlement closure and prevents successor recovery of a settled balance.

1788.56 bars a buyer's collection suit/arbitration after limitations expiration.1788.58requires specified complaint allegations and the contract/document attachment.1788.60 requires authenticated business records for the specified allegations and authenticated agreement documentation before default/other judgment, with consequences for insufficient proof. A collection-ready communication packet is not automatically a litigation-ready evidentiary packet.

1788.62 supplies actual/statutory damages, fee rules, defenses and one-year-from-last-violation action limit; same-act recovery under Rosenthal/FDCPA precludes duplicate recovery under this title.1788.64 makes waiver void and unenforceable.1788.66 separately prohibits sale/assignment of specified unpaid COVID-19 rental debt, conditioned on the statutory dates, rental-assistance eligibility and household income; its explicit rental-debt scope requires an independent check even where ordinary rent is outside the Rosenthal consumer-credit definition. Its incorporated eligibility definitions and historical effect are examined below using the retrieved primary sources. Capture these as exposure/remedy dependencies rather than promising that document delivery erases prior violations.

## Facts Handoff must obtain

- Basis and timing of each component of the account, actual payment agreement, lease/other agreement, statements furnished while active, ledger and all credits/deposit applications.
- Creditor and collector's actual collection/ownership authority and assignment chain, each transfer date, delinquency age at relevant events, mortgage/nonmortgage and charge-off accounting status.
- Whether buyer regularly purchases charged-off consumer debt and whether a statutory acquisition exclusion applies.
- Initial communication dates, channels and principal oral language; exact notice sent, type size and delivery record.
- Each request's contents, written/oral status, receipt time, proof of notice receipt, agreed delivery channel and completeness of response.
- Other holds: identity theft, timely federal validation dispute, representation, bankruptcy/discharge, limitation period, payment or settlement. No documentation rule authorizes overriding those holds.

## Independent interpretation review and resulting decisions

See [review_assigned/REVIEW.md](../review_assigned/REVIEW.md) for directly checked official court, enforcement and legislative sources. All seven SB233 analyses were retrieved; final SB531 analyses and focused judicial searches were also examined.

**Agreement evidence:** Published *Velocity Investments, LLC v. Nguyen*, C102846 (August 28, 2026), pages 6–10, allows adequate alternative evidence under section 1788.52(b) even where a signed agreement exists. The borrower-linked loan summary, Truth in Lending disclosure and payment history together sufficed; a generic agreement unlinked to the borrower/debt did not. This is a direct holding on 1788.52(b), relevant reasoning rather than a direct holding on differently drafted 1788.14.5(b). Pages 10–14 distinguish access from complaint attachment and show that later correction does not erase potential earlier liability. Handoff should assess the actual record and outgoing packet rather than unnecessarily abandoning justified recovery for want of an original signed agreement.

**Late buyer requests:** Section 1788.52(c)’s federal-consistency clause supports a 30-day-window argument; its records notice and independent document obligations support caution about automatic expiration. No located controlling opinion or the available legislative analysis resolves that precise issue. The narrow *Aragon* screening order does not adjudicate a proper but late standalone request. Recommended operating standard: supply required records free within 15 calendar days even when a timing-based exemption is uncertain. Label this an operational service standard when it exceeds established legal duties. If records are incomplete, pause for evidence/interpretation review; identify whether a concurrent hold independently compels stopping. Do not silently classify a request as late without notice-receipt evidence. Continuing without response on an asserted exemption requires resolving that specific legal position.

**Earlier assignment followed by aging:** The clear covered case is assignment of already qualifying delinquent debt after the statutory event date. Text and final legislative analyses do not conclusively resolve assignment at day 60 followed by collection at day 100. Collection placement can qualify even when creditor retains ownership; it is not limited to outright sale. Obtain actual placement/authority and transfer dates. For the uncertain aging sequence, provide records access, response and additional disclosure as an explicitly identified precaution while resolving applicability. Do not invent a new assignment date or retroactively label a later notice as the initial notice. An original-creditor account with no assignment remains a separate branch, subject to other independently applicable duties.

**Currentness:** Live official section footers and chaptered SB531/SB1286 text were reconciled; targeted 2025–2026 amendment searches located no later amendment of these three sections. This is a dated bounded check, not a guarantee of exhaustive future enactment monitoring. The tenancy investigation separately recovered the January 2026 amendment to the governing 1788.2 definitions, so the older SB1286 definitions are not substituted for current law.

## Recovered COVID rental-debt assignment branch

Current1788.66 is an explicit prohibition on a person selling/assigning qualifying unpaid COVID rental debt; it does not depend on the debt-buyer label. The official currentCCP1179.02 page returned a navigation shell with no operative section. AB832's chaptered text supplies the referred definition in section14: unpaid rent **or another unpaid financial obligation under the tenancy** that came due March1,2020–September30,2021; tenant is a natural person with the listed commercial/transient-occupancy exclusions. Section19 set the chapter's repeal forOctober1,2025. Keep that repeal separate from current1788.66, which remains displayed as law without its own sunset; the separate [incorporation analysis](../federal/INCORPORATION_REPEAL.md) supports continued use of the historical definition based on current text, same-enactment contrast, and controlling intent-based interpretation principles. No direct post-sunset appellate holding on this specific provision was located.

The additional1788.66 predicates require that the person would have qualified under ERA1 or ERA2 and household income at/below80%AMI for2020or2021. Actual receipt of assistance is not the statutory wording. Saved2021federal code15USC9058a(k)(3) and9058c(f)(2) trace the named enactments. ERA1 eligibility includes rental obligation, unemployment qualification or COVID-related financial hardship, housing-instability/homelessness risk, and<=80%AMI; ERA2 differs by hardship during or due to the pandemic and its referenced low-income-family definition. The state provision adds its own explicit2020/2021income condition. Do not substitute present income or today's assistance availability. Obtain historical eligibility evidence, household/area facts and the actual unpaid line-item due dates before assigning; income alone is insufficient to prove all predicates.

The current official Treasury program guidance says ERA2 can no longer provide financial assistance after its performance period. That program closure is not itself repeal of a California prohibition using historical eligibility. Historical eligibility source text is retained separately from current administrative guidance.42USC1437a(b)'s2021low-income definition was retrieved for theERA2cross-reference. CIV1940(b) was retrieved for transient/other excluded occupancies. These are case-fact branches, not a demand to prove every household fact for accounts with no2020–2021debt.

## Timing preparation

Maintain distinct receipt date, first-written-contact date, initial-oral-contact date/language, and delivery evidence.30calendar days in1788.14.5(c),15calendar days in1788.52(c),5business days in1788.14.5(e)(2), and5working days in1788.52(e) are not interchangeable. CIV7,9,10,11andGOV6700/6701were retrieved: the general Civil Code rule excludes the first day and excludes a holiday last day, with performance on the next business day; business days are days other than the specified holidays, not automatically a Monday–Friday calendar. Saturday is not categorically a holiday under those sources. GOV6707's specific Saturday extension concerns filing with a state agency, not these document deliveries. Do not apply a court-filing or bank calendar by analogy without establishing applicability. A production deadline calculation still needs the event-date holiday version, locality-specific applicable holidays/proclamations and the specific act; no production calculator or case-specific due date is asserted here.
