## Substantive challenge and result

1. **Calendar units and last-day extension.** Civil Code 7/9 support intermediate Saturdays in the cited Civil Code business-day periods. The distinct working-day interpretation is defended as statutory construction supported by Puni Pa'u, not misrepresented as a controlling collection decision. Purifoy's contextual construction does not automatically rewrite these Civil Code periods. Parsons and Ystrom establish that CCP 12a reaches private statutory duties; Gans's contractual option is distinguishable. CIV 10/11 and CCP 12a/135 must both be respected rather than substituting the judicial calendar for all general holidays. CCP 12b is limited to the affected public-office business. Checked the October examples arithmetically and against the stated calendars.
2. **Partial holiday resolved.** Requested a concrete Good Friday conclusion, not an unresolved partial-day flag. [Lancel v Postlethwaite, 172 Cal. 326, 330–331](https://law.justia.com/cases/california/supreme-court/1916/172-cal-326.html) interprets the last-day holiday language to require a whole-day holiday. Purifoy footnote 9 preserves the distinction. The audit correctly applies it to Good Friday's noon–3 period and correctly avoids resurrecting Lancel's historical Saturday result against today's express Saturday rule. March 4 plus 30 days is April 3, 2026, without a Good Friday end-of-day extension. The payroll agency's different holiday calendar is not authority for changing these collection periods.
3. **Federal clocks.** The initial five-calendar-day notice period, five-business-day assumed-receipt calculation, and thirty-calendar-day validation period are distinct. Current federal submission timeliness and collection cessation on receipt are correctly separated. A state extension does not rewrite the direct federal extrajudicial validation period; independent historical California protection remains relevant.
4. **License and function.** DCLA's on-credit coverage, actual regulated person's exemption, and real-estate licensing predicates are separately evaluated. A client license does not license a vendor. The limited employee/resident-manager exceptions do not authorize every independent collection function. The 2026 DRE supervision text and branch-office provision support the stated factual gates. Prefiling DFPI text is not treated as adopted law. No unsupported categorical rent or software exemption is introduced.
5. **First notice.** Required correction from an imprecise debtor-residence formulation to 1812.700's California-address test; corrected. The translated-notice clock and type-size rule are accurately distinguished from other 12-point requirements. The rights disclosure is independent of the federal and records-request notices.
6. **Electronic delivery.** Required correction of the agreed buyer-response citation to 1788.52(c); corrected. UETA alone preserves specified methods. Full E-SIGN substitution for the ordinary historical mailed-verification duty is supported by 7001 and 7002(c), with the interstate-transaction and consumer-consent conditions. The added 85 FR 76851 explanation supports this reading of preexisting law without silently incorporating later Reg F. [Lavallee v Med-1 Solutions, No. 17-3244](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca7/17-3244/17-3244-2019-08-08.html) does not supply a contrary blanket E-SIGN holding: its disposition rests on deficient communications/link delivery and declines the separately raised E-SIGN issue. Residential rental default/eviction notices retain the 7003 exception and their actual service requirements. Possessing an email address is insufficient consent for every disclosure.
7. **Federal action overlay and concurrent duties.** The action table correctly distinguishes coverage, rebuttable call presumptions, duplicate-dispute prerequisites, debt-parking delivery/waiting, transfer exceptions, payment allocation, venue, limitations and retention. It does not treat one completed response as clearing identity-theft or other independent holds. Requested correction of the buyer-records row so federal validation expiry does not imply an unlimited state request right or erase the separately resolved state window; corrected. Packet delivery remains separate from substantive fee entitlement and retrospective liability.
