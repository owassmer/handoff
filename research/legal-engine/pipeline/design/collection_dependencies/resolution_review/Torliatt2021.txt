                                  21          “A district court has discretionary power to stay proceedings in its own court under Landis

                                  22   v. North American Co., 299 U.S. 248, 254 (1936).” Lockyer v. Mirant Corp., 398 F.3d 1098, 1109

                                  24   Landis stay is proper:
                                                      Where it is proposed that a pending proceeding be stayed, the

                                                      to grant a stay must be weighed. Among those competing interests are
