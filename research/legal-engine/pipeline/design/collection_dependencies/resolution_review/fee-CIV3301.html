Civil Code - CIV

DIVISION 4. GENERAL PROVISIONS [3274 - 9566]

  ( Heading of Division 4 amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 160, Sec. 16. )
  

PART 1. RELIEF [3274 - 3428]

  ( Part 1 enacted 1872. )
  

TITLE 2. COMPENSATORY RELIEF [3281 - 3361]

  ( Title 2 enacted 1872. )
  

CHAPTER 2. Measure of Damages [[3300.] - 3361]

  ( Chapter 2 enacted 1872. )
  

ARTICLE 1. Damages for Breach of Contract [[3300.] - 3322]
  ( Article 1 enacted 1872. )

  
3301.  

No damages can be recovered for a breach of contract which are not clearly ascertainable in both their nature and origin.

(Enacted 1872.)
