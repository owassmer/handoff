Civil Code - CIV

DIVISION 3. OBLIGATIONS [1427 - 3273.91]

  ( Heading of Division 3 amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 160, Sec. 14. )
  

PART 1. OBLIGATIONS IN GENERAL [1427 - 1543]

  ( Part 1 enacted 1872. )
  

TITLE 4. EXTINCTION OF OBLIGATIONS [1473 - 1543]

  ( Title 4 enacted 1872. )
  

CHAPTER 5. Novation [1530 - [1533.]]
  ( Chapter 5 enacted 1872. )

  
1532.  

Novation is made by contract, and is subject to all the rules concerning contracts in general.

(Enacted 1872.)
