                                                                           3                                             Legal Background
                                                                           4          A.      The Commissioner has jurisdiction over the licensing and regulation of persons
                                                                           5   engaged in the business of debt collection in California under the Debt Collection Licensing Act
                                                                           6   (DCLA) (Cal. Fin. Code §§ 100000 – 100025.)
                                                                           7          B.      The Commissioner also has jurisdiction over the regulation of persons who engage,




State of California – Department of Financial Protection and Innovation
                                                                           8   have engaged, or propose to engage in offering or providing a consumer financial product or service
                                                                           9   in California and affiliated service providers under the California Consumer Financial Protection
                                                                          10   Law (CCFPL) (Cal. Fin. Code §§ 90000 – 90019). Collecting debt related to a consumer financial
                                                                          11   product or service is conduct covered by the CCFPL (Cal. Fin. Code § 90005(k)(10).)
                                                                          12                                          Applicable Law – DCLA
                                                                          13          C.      Under the DCLA, “[n]o person shall engage in the business of debt collection in this
                                                                          14   state without first obtaining a license.” (Cal. Fin. Code § 100001(a).)
                                                                          15          D.      The DCLA provides that “[t]he commissioner shall allow any debt collector that
                                                                          16   submits an application before January 1, 2023, to operate pending the approval or denial of the
                                                                          17   application.” (Cal. Fin. Code § 100000.5(a).)
                                                                          18          E.      “Debt” is defined as “money, property, or their equivalent that is due or owing or
                                                                          19   alleged to be due or owing from a natural person to another person.” (Cal. Fin. Code § 100002(h).)
                                                                          20          F.      “Consumer debt” or “consumer credit” is defined as “money, property, or their
                                                                          21   equivalent, due or owing, or alleged to be due or owing, from a natural person by reason of a
                                                                          22   consumer credit transaction. The term “consumer debt” includes “charged-off consumer debt” as
                                                                          23   defined in Section 1788.50 of the Civil Code.” (Cal. Fin. Code § 100002(f).)
                                                                          24          G.      The DCLA defines “debt collection” as “any act or practice in connection with the
                                                                          25   collection of consumer debt.” (Cal. Fin. Code § 100002(i).)
                                                                          26          H.       “Debt collector” means “any person who, in the ordinary course of business,
                                                                          27   regularly, on the person’s own behalf or on behalf of others, engages in debt collection. The term
                                                                          28   includes any person who composes and sells, or offers to compose and sell, forms, letters and other

                                                                           1   collection media used or intended to be used for debt collection. The term “debt collector” includes
                                                                           2   “debt buyer” as defined in Section 1788.50 of the Civil Code.” (Cal. Fin. Code § 100002(j).)
                                                                           3                                         Applicable Law – CCFPL
                                                                           4          I.      Under the CCFPL, it is unlawful for a “covered person” to do any of the following:
                                                                           5   “(1) Engage, have engaged, or propose to engage in any unlawful, unfair, deceptive, or abusive act
                                                                           6   or practice with respect to consumer financial products or services. (2) Offer or provide to a
                                                                           7   consumer any financial product or service not in conformity with any consumer financial law or




State of California – Department of Financial Protection and Innovation
                                                                           8   otherwise commit any act or omission in violation of a consumer financial law. . .” (Cal. Fin. Code
                                                                           9   § 90003(a)(1)-(2).)
                                                                          10          J.      A “covered person” includes “[a]ny person that engages in offering or providing a
                                                                          11   consumer financial product or service to a resident of this state.” (Cal. Fin. Code § 90005(f)(1).)
                                                                          12          K.      A “consumer financial product or service” is generally a “financial product or service
                                                                          13   that is delivered, offered, or provided for use by consumers primarily for personal, family, or
                                                                          14   household purposes.” (Cal. Fin. Code § 90005(e)(1).)
                                                                          15          L.      Under California Financial Code section 90015(d), if, in the opinion of the
                                                                          16   Commissioner, any person engages, has engaged, or proposes to engage in any activity prohibited by
                                                                          17   section 90003 or 90004, the Commissioner “may issue an order directing the person to desist and
                                                                          18   refrain from engaging in the activity, act, practice, or course of business.” (Cal. Fin. Code §
                                                                          19   90015(d).)
                                                                          20          M.      Under California Financial Code section 90012(c), in any administrative action
                                                                          21   brought pursuant to the CCFPL, any person that violates, through any act or omission, any provision
                                                                          22   of the CCFPL shall forfeit and pay a penalty not exceeding $2,500.00 for each act or omission in
                                                                          23   violation of the CCFPL. (Cal. Fin. Code § 90012(c)(1)(A)(i).)
                                                                          24       Applicable Law – Rosenthal Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (Cal. Civ. Code §§ 1788 –
                                                                          25                                            1788.33) (Rosenthal)

                                                                          27   collector to which delinquent debt has been assigned shall include in its first written communication

                                                                          13                                           Commissioner’s Findings
                                                                          14           O.       At all relevant times, Saturn Systems is and was a Delaware Corporation with a
                                                                          15   principal place of business at 633 Rustlers Road, Bailey, Colorado, 80421.
                                                                          16           P.       Saturn Systems is a debt collector within the meaning of California Financial Code
                                                                          17   section 100002(j) of the DCLA.
                                                                          18           Q.       On or about September 17, 2024, Saturn Systems filed an application for a debt
                                                                          19   collection license pursuant to Cal. Fin. Code § 100001(a). As of January 23, 2025, the application is
                                                                          20   still pending.
                                                                          21           R.       Before September 17, 2024, Saturn Systems had never applied nor obtained a debt
                                                                          22   collection license in California. Since January 1, 2022, Saturn Systems has engaged in debt
                                                                          23   collection activities with 572 California consumers, in violation of California Financial Code section
                                                                          24   100001(a).
                                                                          25           S.       In or around November 2022, Saturn Systems mailed a letter to a California consumer
                                                                          26   containing the following statement: “Our client may claim this as a deduction on their Federal
                                                                          27   Income Tax. The Internal Revenue Service may consider this as income to you!! You will then be
                                                                          28   required to report this amount on your income tax return and pay federal taxes on this amount.”



                                                                                                                                    4
                                                                                                                          CONSENT ORDER
                                                                           1          T.      Saturn Systems confirmed the above statement refers to filing a Form 1099-C. Form
                                                                           2   1099-C is filed for debts of $600 or more. Saturn Systems sent this letter to a California consumer
                                                                           3   with an alleged debt of less than $600. Moreover, the letter did not explicitly refer to Form 1099-C,
                                                                           4   so the consumer had no way to deduce that the statement did not apply to them. This statement is
                                                                           5   material and likely to mislead a consumer acting reasonably under the circumstances, constituting
                                                                           6   deceptive acts or practices, in violation of Financial Code section 90003, subdivision (a)(1).
                                                                           7          U.      After July 2022, Saturn Systems mailed letters to two California consumers. Saturn

                                                                          11   balance, including an explanation of any interest charges and additional fees; (3) the date the debt
                                                                          12   became delinquent or the date of the last payment; (4) the name of the creditor and the account
