Code of Civil Procedure - CCP
PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS  ( Preliminary Provisions enacted 1872. )
12b.
If any city, county, state, or public office, other than a branch office, is closed for the whole of any day, insofar as the business of that office is concerned, that day shall be considered as a holiday for the purposes of computing time under Sections 12 and 12a.(Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 655.)
