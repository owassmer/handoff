Civil Code - CIV
PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS  ( Preliminary Provisions enacted 1872. )
11.
Whenever any act of a secular nature, other than a work of necessity or mercy, is appointed by law or contract to be performed upon a particular day, which day falls upon a holiday, it may be performed upon the next business day, with the same effect as if it had been performed upon the day appointed.(Enacted 1872.)
