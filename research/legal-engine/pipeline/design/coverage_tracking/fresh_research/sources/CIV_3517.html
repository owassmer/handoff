Civil Code - CIV

DIVISION 4. GENERAL PROVISIONS [3274 - 9566]

  ( Heading of Division 4 amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 160, Sec. 16. )
  

PART 4. MAXIMS OF JURISPRUDENCE [3509 - 3548]

  ( Part 4 enacted 1872. )
  
3517.  

No one can take advantage of their own wrong.

(Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 812, Sec. 4. (AB 1899) Effective January 1, 2025.)
