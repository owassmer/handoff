## Operating purpose and boundary

Help the research agent notice legal pointers worth inspecting, retain their context, and decide what to read next. Missing a definition, exception, enclosing scope rule, or incorporated source can change Handoff's repair/account recommendations. Detection is one atomic contribution to that investigation, not proof of applicability, a resolved dependency, or complete section review.

In scope: a precise contract over supplied English legal passages; explicit self-references (including a whole-section focus referring to itself), enclosing-unit references, other provisions/instruments, relative/unresolved references, ranges/subdivisions, mixed passages, and ordinary-language lookalikes. Establish how generic legal qualifications, private-document references, quoted text, source headings, and incomplete input are handled. Every boundary receives a reason and test evidence. Distinguish detection from locating a reference, classifying its relationship, resolving its target/version, and interpreting its legal effect. Use the agent and code where those tasks are better performed there.

Not a claim of completion of statewide source discovery, statutory applicability, target retrieval, legal interpretation, general semantic dependency discovery, or production exclusion routing. Those remain separately tracked work; a NO here cannot close them. Existing pilots, labels, prompts, responses, and scores remain intact.

## Observable acceptance criteria, declared before new model execution

1. **Defined contract:** independent review resolves material ambiguities for every boundary above; answer meanings are mutually distinguishable, including uncertainty and invalid/missing input. Each supported answer maps to a concrete agent action. Multiple references are not forced into one exclusive relationship category.
2. **Source-grounded development:** real saved legal passages plus explicitly identified adversarial constructions exercise every boundary. Exact excerpts/source identities are checked. Independent reviewer labels/reasons are recorded before model output inspection; disagreements are resolved against the contract and source rather than model preference.
3. **Separate evaluation:** after development, freeze versioned questions/input policy and an independently prepared evaluation set, separate from development source families or explicitly synthetic new contrast groups. Record and justify all overlap. Expected answers never enter Jev input. Evaluate through the configured OpenRouter Jev integration with pinned build, request hashes, all attempts/results, and cost/time. Preserve failed/invalid/unanswered cases in totals.
4. **Useful behavior:** demonstrate handling of self/enclosing/cross references, lookalike negatives, uncertainty, and mixed passages; show what the agent actually does with representative outputs. Independently inspect every disagreement, every missed consequential pointer, and adversarial cases. Correct material contract/implementation defects and recheck affected work. If any evaluation inputs inform revision, retire that set to development and use a separately frozen replacement for the revised version.
5. **Failure accounting:** publish per-boundary counts and failure cases, including distinctions between model error, ambiguous contract, missing context, and preparation/transport failure. Do not infer probabilistic calibration or broad accuracy from a small set or move failures out of historical totals. Known model errors may be retained only with an explicit demonstrated agent-assisted handling boundary; no unsupported reliance on silent exclusion or confidence thresholds.
6. **Working artifacts:** provide versioned question(s), reproducible preparation/evaluation commands, meaningful regression tests, and agent instructions sufficient to use answers without inventing stronger conclusions. Keep changes focused on this goal; main-client cache repair and broad pipeline integration remain subsequent goals.
7. **Completion audit:** independent final review and a requirement-by-requirement evidence table prove criteria 1–6, old-pilot preservation, and reconciliation of every declared coverage item. Correct and recheck material findings. Update PLAN.md only for achievements backed by this evidence. If a necessary dependency is unavailable, leave this goal incomplete with precise remaining work.
