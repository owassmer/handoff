Civil Code - CIV

DIVISION 3. OBLIGATIONS [1427 - 3273.91]

  ( Heading of Division 3 amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 160, Sec. 14. )
  

PART 4. OBLIGATIONS ARISING FROM PARTICULAR TRANSACTIONS [1738 - 3273.91]

  ( Part 4 enacted 1872. )
  

TITLE 5. HIRING [1925 - 1997.270]

  ( Title 5 enacted 1872. )
  

CHAPTER 1. Hiring in General [1925 - 1938]
  ( Chapter 1 enacted 1872. )

  
1929.  

The hirer of a thing must repair all deteriorations or injuries thereto occasioned by his want of ordinary care.

(Amended by Stats. 1905, Ch. 454.)
