# Section-to-record design for independent challenge

Status: proposal for implementation and evaluation, not acceptance.

## Unit of work and output

One work unit has captured source sections plus recovered dependencies, a candidate inventory, focused semantic jobs and explicit composition rules. One section can produce multiple records and relationships. A job is one meaningful question, not one arbitrary sentence fragment. Independent jobs can share evidence. Dependent jobs wait for the judgments or recovered context they actually need.

Outputs are a graph of identified source-bound things and relationships:

- Entities/concepts: source expressions for parties, actions, objects, events and defined terms.
- Operative records: duty, prohibition, permission, definition, temporal rule and consequence.
- Relationships: actor/action/object, applies_under, defined_as, exception_to, limits_scope_of, alternative_to, triggered_by, due_under, consequence_of, references and qualifies.
- Conditions preserve logical grouping (all/any/not) and their source qualifiers. Facts about a current operating case remain separate from legal conditions.
- Timing stores duration/unit, anchor event, counting convention, tolling or adjustment and the affected action; uncertainty is explicit. Code computes dates only from complete validated rule and factual inputs.

Every semantic assertion must identify the Jev job/result establishing it and the exact evidence selections. Candidate records exist before inference, but are not asserted facts. Code promotes only outputs licensed by validated judgments under the frozen decision policy. An answer to one proposition cannot assert neighboring fields that were never judged.

## Preparation without pre-answering

The agent reads the unit and selects source expressions, candidate actors/actions and possible relationships. It prepares concrete neutral questions and necessary evidence. It does not put completed legal conclusions into an accepted record before dispatch.

Candidates may contain proposed structured values—for example a selected phrase for an action or a possible duration—because Jev's typed primitives choose/judge supplied alternatives rather than generate arbitrary legal prose. Those values must remain candidates until corresponding jobs establish them. Their source spans and competing possibilities are reviewable. Atom completeness is independently assessed against the whole operative unit, so a preparer cannot produce a high score by omitting difficult candidates.

Use semantic jobs for things the consumer needs to know. Avoid a broad gate asking whether anything matters before asking the useful questions. A presence/discovery signal may prioritize retrieval but cannot silently suppress other source components.

## Questions, configuration and dependencies

Each job declares its evidence, concrete instructions, native primitive/criteria, required predecessors, and exactly which output fields or relations its answers can establish. Structured question-specific instructions can place the selected subject next to its question, while shared source text appears once in state. IDs and source bookkeeping do not substitute for semantic instructions.

Noul is for a proposition; Choice is for genuinely exclusive alternatives. Coexisting effects receive independent jobs. Score requires a demonstrated ordered judgment need; no current section output calls for a score. Preserve full returned distributions and unanswered states. Consumer thresholds must be evaluated and versioned, not inherited from the old routing cutoff.

Dependencies are an acyclic graph. A predecessor may supply a candidate selection or determine whether another question is meaningful. Conditional nonexecution is recorded as not-applicable only when the required predecessor answer establishes that condition; missing, invalid or uncertain predecessors remain unresolved. Independent jobs must not rely on another question in the same request being answered first.

## Worked structural example: entry notice

Source-driven candidate jobs separately ask whether the selected provisions establish:

1. Advance notice for ordinary entry, its minimum interval and the applicable conditions.
2. Emergency entry permission, waiver of advance notice and waiver of consent.
3. Notice after emergency entry in the tenant's absence, its interval and required content.
4. Connections between each condition, action and timing provision.

Code composes distinct records for the ordinary-entry duty, emergency permission and post-entry notice duty. An operating consumer receives those records and evaluates supplied case facts against the conditions. It should not reread the section to discover the post-entry duty after seeing an emergency-exemption label. Independence and dependency are specified per concrete job, not assumed from this numbered description.

## Integration and evaluation

The new store must be consumed by actual research queues/rule assembly and a determinate operating-case consumer. Broad legacy scores may remain historical evidence, but cannot be the new semantic output or proof of exclusion. Work left to the agent after consumption must be new investigation or synthesis, not repeating an already answered actor/action/condition relation.

Independent expectations cover all required semantic components, connections and downstream results for each worked section. Evaluate omitted assertions as well as wrong ones, negative candidates, ambiguous/failed execution, dependency recovery and state changes. Freeze atom contracts and composition rules before held-out sections and case variations. Candidate proposal and source-family/jurisdiction variation must be part of generalization evidence.

The initial 120 unused definition/support cases can contribute atom tests after independent review. They cannot alone establish complete section output, dependent execution or downstream usefulness. Section-level evaluation needs separate full-unit expectations and operating cases.
