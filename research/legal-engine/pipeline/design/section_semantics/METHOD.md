# Working method for connected semantic judgments

Use this method when preparing legal material for reusable Handoff engine outputs. Read PLAN.md for demonstrated capabilities and unresolved work; the implementation is still undergoing complete-section and consumer validation. Tests of request integrity are not evidence of legal completeness.

## Prepare from the operating decision and complete source unit

Identify the operator action, financial consequence or protection the outputs must support. Record jurisdiction, source family, effective context and case facts separately. Preserve a complete operative source unit and an inventory of its meaningful components, including scope, definitions, qualifications, exceptions, remedies and referenced rules needed to use it. Recover the necessary meaning behind a reference; storing the pointer alone does not complete that dependency. State why unrelated branches are outside the declared operating scope.

Supply operative evidence to Jev. Remove navigation, page controls, source identifiers and editorial bookkeeping from semantic passages. Keep their exact paths, hashes and offsets outside the judged text. Include enclosing clauses, definitions and exceptions when they affect a question. Atomicity means one useful determination with sufficient context, not the shortest possible excerpt.

## Prepare jobs that produce usable meaning

Select source expressions and candidate meanings; keep them proposed until answered. Use separate judgments for simultaneous effects. Give each question a self-contained subject and concrete action. A predicate such as “the claimant named above” or a relationship such as “qualifies the rule” leaves semantic work unfinished: identify the actor, affected field, condition and effect needed downstream.

Use Noul for a meaningful proposition and Choice for distinct alternatives. Avoid overlapping candidates such as a specific dispatch event versus an alternative that includes that event. Do not transplant another case's actor menu or assume an actor is required for a consequence arising by operation of law. Use native structured state and instructions where they clarify the selected object. Share identical evidence across independent questions; supply actual predecessor selections to dependent questions. A batch does not create an answering order within the request.

Declare which answers license each field, logical operator, qualification and relationship. Preserve negation, all/any grouping, necessary versus sufficient conditions, timing anchors, thresholds and cumulative effects. Specify executable relationship effects rather than relying on suggestive edge names. Candidate propositions do not prove discovery completeness; assess omissions independently against the whole unit.

When a dependent job consumes an established condition group, supply its child predicate meanings as well as its operator. `semantics.context.expand_condition_context` expands declared condition-child paths into actual predecessor selections and dependency edges without creating new semantic content. Apply it to a new preparation version, inspect its change log, then compile to check the full dependency graph. Missing field producers require preparation or recovery; child identifiers are not a substitute for meaning. The helper covers declared condition groups, not every definition, notice, timing or scope dependency, and does not prove context completeness.

## Execute and compose

The proposal adapter is `pipeline.semantics.prepare.compile_proposal`. `plan.py` validates source selections and jobs; `templates.py` supplies evidence and actual predecessor fields. `run.py` uses the existing native Jev transport and captures serialized bodies and raw responses. `replay.py` derives outcomes from those records. `compose.py` promotes only answer-licensed fields and retains unresolved or conflicting findings.

Use the current registry for provider, model and pinned build. Supply explicit per-job decision policies with an evaluation rationale; synthetic test cutoffs are not calibrated production thresholds. Validate policies before dispatch. Preserve original results and corrections. Resume only from validated history, and reconcile a dispatched call with an unknown outcome before resending. Changes to preparation, source, configuration, policy or implementation require the appropriate new version and dependency revalidation.

## Evaluate independently

Establish source-grounded component, relationship and operating-case expectations before responses. Map expected meaning to exact output checks without rewriting it to fit the proposed schema. Missing or inexpressible mappings remain findings. Freeze preparation, expectations, mappings, sources, configuration, policies and composition before held-out execution. `freeze.py` preserves these artifacts; its checks verify recorded bindings, not the quality of the independent judgment.

Use `evaluate.py` to report incorrect, missing and unmapped requirements separately. Inspect raw answer distributions and component failures alongside coverage and actual consumer results. Keep source-unit and instrument clustering visible; hundreds of questions over one section are not hundreds of independent tests. Development corrections need fresh validation before generalization claims.

When a judgment fails, examine selection, question, context, native configuration and composition first. Do not infer Jev incapability from an inadequately prepared or compound question. Agent-authored final conclusions with Jev merely corroborating them are not the default replacement. Any alternative must be justified by properly prepared evidence and demonstrate the same required capability.

## Consume and complete

Consumers use established semantic fields and explicit relationships; case facts are separate inputs. New factual interpretation can itself be decomposed into Jev jobs. Code computes determinate conditions, dates and amounts, retaining missing facts as unknown. Verify that consumers need no source rereading to reconstruct already judged meaning, while keeping evidence inspectable.

Current research integration can attach raw-replayed semantic fields to newly planned review batches through source/plan-bound `semantic_runs.json` registrations. It does not itself migrate historical batches, replace broad routing, resolve all interacting rule effects or prove whole-section coverage. Complete those affected paths and demonstrate real changed-case behavior before claiming integration. The operator-facing result remains completed physical and financial work, avoided unnecessary cost and disputes, and preserved justified recovery.
