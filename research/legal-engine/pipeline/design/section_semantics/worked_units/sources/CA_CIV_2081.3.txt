DIVISION 3. OBLIGATIONS [1427 - 3273.91]
( Heading of Division 3 amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 160, Sec. 14. )
PART 4. OBLIGATIONS ARISING FROM PARTICULAR TRANSACTIONS [1738 - 3273.91]
( Part 4 enacted 1872. )
TITLE 6. SERVICE [2019 - 2082]
( Title 6 enacted 1872. )
CHAPTER 4. Lost and Unclaimed Property [2080 - 2082]
( Chapter 4 added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 656. )
ARTICLE 2. Unclaimed Property [2081 - 2081.6]
( Article 2 added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 656. )
2081.3.
If the owner or his agent fails to demand the surplus within 60 days after the sale, it shall be paid into the county treasury, subject to the order of the owner.
(Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 656.)
