Food and Agricultural Code - FAC
DIVISION 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY [16301 - 19700]
( Division 9 enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15. )
PART 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE [16301 - 17153]
( Part 1 enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15. )
CHAPTER 7. Estrays [17001 - 17128]
( Chapter 7 enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15. )
ARTICLE 1. Definitions and General Provisions [17001 - 17006]
( Article 1 enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15. )
17004.
The director may take up estray animals whose owners are unknown and cannot be located.
(Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 630.)
