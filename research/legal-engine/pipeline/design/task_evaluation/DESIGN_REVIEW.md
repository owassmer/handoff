# Independent initial design review — Goal 5

Reviewed GOAL.md, OBJECTIVE.txt, prepared_tasks/questions.json and prepared_tasks/CONFIGURATION.md on October 1, 2026. This is an independent design challenge, not acceptance of an implementation or capability. No inference was run and no earlier artifacts were changed.

## Conclusion

The objective and agent–Jev–code division are appropriate. Six capabilities require separate contracts and evidence. The existing five relevant Noul questions are prototypes; candidate-meaning selection has no question contract yet. Resolve the distinctions below before preparing labels or freezing requests. Do not silently turn Goal 5 into candidate discovery recall or the Goal 6 whole-process comparison. Within Goal 5, however, a prepared task with missing necessary context must actually be repaired and completed.

## Contracts that must be explicit

| Capability | Required input and truth boundary | Permitted consequence; prohibited shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Definition recognition | Choose whether the object is one selected expression/term or whether any term is defined anywhere in a selected passage. Prefer one selected term occurrence with complete defining clause. Distinguish operative definition from examples, descriptive discussion, a quotation of someone else's proposed definition, and a cross-reference that imports a definition. Decide whether incorporation counts as a definition relation or a separate retrieval prerequisite. | YES supports recording a candidate definition relation. It does not establish the definition's governing scope or discover every definition. NO does not justify dropping a source. |
| Same-sense use | Bind exact selected occurrences, the full candidate definition, and context resolving homonyms, negation and contrast. Semantic equivalence is different from whether the definition legally governs a use. A court discussing the same concept while rejecting its applicability can still use the same sense. | YES supports a candidate meaning link. Agent establishes applicability/scope; explicit containment is subsequently code work. |
| Exception to requirement | One atomic requirement and one candidate clause, including qualifying conditions and referents. Define whether exception means narrowing/removing the duty under stated circumstances; distinguish alternative satisfaction, replacement, independent duties, remedies and factual non-triggering. Broad wording must not hide several independently contestable relations. | YES means investigate and represent the exception and its conditions with that requirement. It does not establish that an actual case meets those conditions. |
| Deadline modification | One requirement, its temporal rule and candidate modifying provision. Explicitly include/exclude changed duration, tolling, extension, holiday adjustment, trigger replacement, service additions and historical amendments. Distinguish another duty's deadline and a remedy available after a deadline. If pure computation is excluded, state the semantic relation that Jev judges before code calculates. | YES links a relevant temporal modifier; agent resolves applicability and code computes a date from established inputs. NO cannot remove unresolved cross-references or unresolved context. |
| Candidate meaning | Selected expression in context plus independently prepared, mutually exclusive alternatives. Distinguish no offered meaning fits from insufficient supplied context; these need not be the same output. Alternatives must not mix word meaning, legal applicability and ultimate outcome. Define deterministic treatment of semantic duplicate or overlapping options before inference. | Choice ranks/selects only among these candidates. It does not establish completeness of candidate discovery, and relative probability is not the probability that the legal result is correct. |
| Atomic claim support | One proposition with its actor, modality, conditions and temporal/scope limitations; complete supporting or opposing text. Choose a support standard: supplied evidence warrants this specific proposition with its qualifications, rather than merely mentioning its subject or failing to contradict it. Distinguish a passage reporting an argument from adopting it, and a holding from background facts. Separate material inference requiring other authority from direct or necessarily implied support. | YES supports that claim against those passages only. It does not certify a whole rule, all missing conditions, current validity or real-world factual truth. |

For every contract, define handling of conditional statements: “the exception modifies this duty if condition X holds” can be true as a textual relation without proof that X holds in the operator's case. Do not encode insufficient preparation as semantic NO. Noul is appropriate only after prerequisite retrieval/interpretation yields an independently labelable binary proposition. If the distinction remains genuinely contested after investigation, record the competing readings and demonstrate the completing agent method; do not force a binary gold label or silently exclude the difficult case.

## Defensible sample plan

Recommend **24 development cases and 60 fresh held-out cases per capability**: 144 development and 360 held-out judgments before configuration comparisons or corrective rounds. This is a feasible evidence floor, not a reliability certification. Freeze these counts and inclusion rules before held-out inference; increase them if development reveals distinct material branches that the planned cells omit.

For each capability, allocate the 60 held-out cases to 20 statutory, 20 regulatory and 20 interpretive source clusters. Within interpretive sources use at least 10 judicial and 10 administrative examples. Administrative cases must use actual interpretation/adjudication/guidance relevant to the judged relation, not relabel regulatory text as independent interpretation. Prefer distinct provisions or decision issue units; repeated quotations, sibling excerpts applying one definition and paired edits of one clause share a cluster. Record larger instrument/authority lineage as well. If independent clusters are unavailable, report the reduced effective coverage rather than padding the count with variants. Statutes from one chapter do not alone establish statutory-family breadth.

Cover positive and consequential negative relations in each family (for binary tasks target approximately balanced classes, while reporting that this is a challenge set rather than deployment prevalence). For Choice, balance correct-option positions and include real no-fit/insufficient-context situations under the frozen contract. Use at least several distinct instruments or decisions per family and more than one legal subject. Include the actual California property-operation setting plus transfer cases; do not claim cross-jurisdiction dependability from California-only sources.

Across each capability, explicitly cover local clauses; cross-section incorporation requiring completed context recovery; negative/qualified phrasing; scope and actor changes; operative versus historical/quoted/proposed text; and near-miss relations. Use paired hard negatives where useful, but count the pair as one source cluster. The family totals are not enough if an important contract branch has no example. Publish a task-by-family-by-edge-case matrix and explain any inapplicable cell.

With zero observed errors in 60 independent Bernoulli cases, a one-sided 95% exact upper error bound is about **4.87%** (1 − 0.05^(1/60)); for 20 it is about **13.91%**. These are too weak to claim safe unattended consequential legal use. The design is purposive and clustered, so even these bounds are conditional descriptive benchmarks, not a guarantee on unknown legal material. Report per-task and per-family counts, errors and exact binomial intervals with those assumptions; separately report the number of source/instrument clusters and cluster-level any-error results. Do not present 360 pooled cases as the effective sample for every capability. If a later policy requires a numerical error ceiling, choose its sample size beforehand from that ceiling and the appropriate independence assumptions; no such justified ceiling exists yet.

Reserve genuinely untouched replacement material for correction: a failed held-out case becomes development evidence permanently. Following a material contract/configuration change, validate the affected capability on a new frozen held-out set, not merely a retest of the failure. A narrow preparation repair may support a narrower retest only if independent review establishes its affected branch and the unchanged remaining contract; preserve both denominators and do not merge old and new results into a single clean score.

## Blindness and freezing controls

1. An expectation reviewer reads captured primary evidence and necessary surrounding context before any response is available. Save answer, reason, exact supporting/contrary spans, required context and materiality rationale in a separate access path never included in requests. Independence here means a distinct reviewer agent/process with disclosed shared model family, not a claim of blinded human legal validation.
2. Source selection follows a predeclared matrix, not model confidence or prior success. Split by source/instrument lineage where practical; never place a paraphrase or a sibling excerpt of a development fact pattern into held-out and call it fresh. Disclose any unavoidable cross-capability reuse and avoid treating it as independent pooled evidence.
3. Resolve preparation defects and expectation disputes before inference. Keep an exclusion/replacement ledger with reasons and original materials so difficult cases cannot disappear. Unresolved material cases require completed agent investigation, not silent denominator reduction.
4. Freeze source captures, exact spans, prepared semantic payload, question/configuration version, model/native SDK version, option order policy, transport serializer and scoring/consumer policy. Hash the manifest and label artifact separately before execution. Verify captured serialized request bytes against the frozen expectation about native fields; assembly-level JSON alone is insufficient.
5. Evaluate instruction/criteria/structured-context alternatives on development only, preferably matched inputs with factors changed separately. Shared-context batching must preserve question independence and must not leak another question's expected answer. Compare separate and batched behavior on a prespecified development subset before choosing the final mode.
6. Held-out response inspection begins only after freeze and independent label completion. Preserve first attempts, raw distributions, invalid replies, transport failures, retry reasons and all submitted serialized requests. Execution failures are neither NO nor removed successful cases. Majority answers are diagnostic classifications, not calibrated operating permissions.
7. Publish a full case ledger. Classify preparation defects, ambiguous expectations, judgment errors and execution failures separately; record the operational consequence and investigation for each. Revisit a gold label only through documented source reasoning independent of wishing to improve a score.

## Consequences and acceptance

Before inference, each case needs a concrete downstream failure description. Examples: false definition imports an inapplicable meaning; false same-sense joins a homonym; missed exception leaves an unlawful charge; false temporal link alters the wrong deadline; wrong meaning selects the wrong actor/obligation; unsupported YES authorizes a proposition the source never warrants. False negatives may conceal relevant duties, so do not assume YES is the only costly direction.

For each task, acceptance requires a working versioned runner, verified requests, independently labeled development and fresh tests across the declared branches, resolution of every material finding and precise consumer instructions. If Jev remains unsuitable, complete and demonstrate the agent/code method on that capability's cases, with independently reviewed conclusions and deterministic computation where appropriate. “Send to agent” without performing and verifying the work does not satisfy the objective. A fallback can be accepted as the completing method without claiming Jev itself dependable.

This review recommends the sample plan and contract decisions; it does not settle their substantive truth conventions by implication. The implementing author should publish explicit contracts and the final matrix for independent recheck before held-out execution. Goal 6 process comparisons and Goal 7 routine integration remain separate.
