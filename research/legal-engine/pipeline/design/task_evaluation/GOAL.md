## Evaluation design constraints

Development cases are never relabeled as held-out. Expected answers and reasons live outside requests. Source metadata stays outside the semantic payload unless needed to interpret the provision. Exact selected text must match captured source bytes. A missing passage requires completed retrieval and a new prepared judgment; an unsent or failed request cannot be a semantic negative.

Sample size will be fixed before held-out inference after independent design review. Report uncertainty by capability, not only pooled accuracy; related examples do not count as independent source samples. Numerical bounds describe the sampling assumptions and cannot establish universal legal reliability. All material defects require resolution regardless of aggregate score.

Score has no presently justified task: the scoped judgments are binary relations or alternatives, not ordered ratings. Evaluate optional criteria, structured instructions and shared-context batching where they fit. Verify the actual transport payload, not only the pre-SDK object. Retain raw probabilities; a majority answer is a diagnostic classification, not a calibrated authorization threshold.
