## Implementable contract

The agent prepares a small record with exact source links and one selected actor/action or term occurrence:

- **Sense:** occurrence, candidate definition, governing use meaning, material inclusion/exclusion differences, same operative meaning YES/NO, and separate applicability conclusion. A broad shared concept is not enough for YES when legal inclusion tests differ.
- **Exception:** selected base obligation, its already-stated conditions, candidate conditions, and effect: removes duty, reduces required scope, alternative satisfaction without reduction, independent duty/remedy, or no relation. Keep the reasoned effect, not merely its category.
- **Timing:** actor/action, duration, unit, trigger, conditions, and candidate effect: duration change, trigger/legal-date definition, computation adjustment, tolling, waiver/alternative branch, another actor's clock, or no temporal effect. Distinguish a qualification of the base regime from amendment of an already-conditioned branch.

These are structured outputs of completed agent interpretation, not categories the source is forced to fit. If several effects coexist, prepare separate atomic records and preserve their connections.
