§ 20-117 Licensee disclosure of breach of security, notification requirements.

Any person who is required to be licensed pursuant to chapter two of this title or pursuant to provisions of state law enforced by the department, and who is also required to make a notification pursuant to subdivision 2 or 3 of section 899-aa of the general business law, shall promptly submit a copy of such notification to the department. Such notice shall be made without delaying notice to any individual whose private information was, or is reasonably believed to have been, accessed, acquired, disclosed, or used by an unauthorized person.
