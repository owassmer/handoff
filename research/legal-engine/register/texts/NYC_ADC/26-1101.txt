§ 26-1101 Definitions.

For purposes of this chapter, the following terms are defined as follows: [ALP S-010]

"Department" means the department of housing preservation and development. [ALP S-010]

"Dwelling unit" means a dwelling unit as defined in paragraph thirteen of subdivision a of section 27-2004 of the code. [ALP S-010]

"Multiple dwelling" means a dwelling as defined in paragraph seven of subdivision a of section 27-2004 of the code. [ALP S-010]

"Owner" means an owner as defined in paragraph forty-five of subdivision a of section 27-2004 of the code. [ALP S-010]

"Tenant" means any lawful occupant of a dwelling unit in a multiple dwelling. [ALP S-010]
