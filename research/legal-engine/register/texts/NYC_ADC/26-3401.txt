§ 26-3401 Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the term "duty to mitigate damages" means the duty of a landlord to mitigate damages pursuant to section 227-e of the real property law. [ALP S-068]

(L.L. 2021/169, 12/24/2021, eff. 6/22/2022; Renumbered L.L. 2023/030, 2/21/2023, eff. 10/19/2023) [ALP S-090]
