§ 26-3402 Limitation of fees.

Where a landlord has a duty to mitigate damages, such landlord may not recover from a tenant any amount in excess of the fair market cost necessary to prepare the physical conditions of the premises for rental. In seeking such amount from a tenant, such landlord must provide an itemized list to such tenant demonstrating the calculation of such amount. [ALP S-068]
