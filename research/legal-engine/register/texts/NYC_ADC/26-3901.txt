§ 26-3901 Definitions.

As used in this chapter, the following terms have the following meanings: [ALP S-124]

Department. The term “department” means the department of housing preservation and development. [ALP S-124]

Household. The term “household” means an individual or family. [ALP S-124]

Income-eligible. The term “income-eligible” means an applicant whose total gross income does not exceed 50 percent of the area median income, as defined by the United States department of housing and urban development. [ALP S-124]

Regulatory agreement. The term “regulatory agreement” means a legally binding agreement between the owner of a building and a federal, state, or local agency or instrumentality or a restrictive declaration that requires all or some of the units in such building to be income-restricted or occupied by households meeting specified requirements set forth in such agreement. [ALP S-124]

Shelter. The term “shelter” means temporary emergency housing provided to homeless adults, adult families, families with children, and runaway and homeless youth by the city or a provider under contract or similar agreement with the city. [ALP S-124]

Unit. The term “unit” means an apartment, room, or single room occupancy. [ALP S-124]

(L.L. 2026/126, 7/30/2026, eff. 1/26/2027) [ALP S-124]
