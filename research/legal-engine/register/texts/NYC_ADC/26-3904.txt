§ 26-3904 Maximum monthly rents.

Maximum rental allowances shall be set in accordance with section 982.503 of title 24 of the code of federal regulations, regarding voucher payment standard amounts, or a successor provision, at the payment standard used for the housing choice voucher program administered by the department pursuant to section 1437f of title 42 of the United States code.
