§ 26-3908 Report.

Beginning on August 1, 2027, and annually thereafter, the department shall submit to the speaker of the council and post on its website a report that includes the following information: [ALP S-124]

1. the total number of households enrolled in the rental assistance voucher program established pursuant to this chapter, disaggregated by the eligibility categories established by subdivision a of section 26-3903; [ALP S-124]

2. the total number of households newly enrolled in the fiscal year prior to the submission of such report, disaggregated by borough of residence, number of people in such households, and primary language spoken in such households; [ALP S-124]

3. the average amount of rental assistance provided to households; [ALP S-124]

4. the total amount of rental assistance provided during the prior fiscal year, disaggregated by administrative costs and the total amount paid as rental assistance; and [ALP S-124]

5. the total number of households on the waitlist as of the end of the prior fiscal year. [ALP S-124]

(L.L. 2026/126, 7/30/2026, eff. 1/26/2027) [ALP S-124]
