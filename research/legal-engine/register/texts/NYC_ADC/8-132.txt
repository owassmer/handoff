§ 8-132 Sexual harassment information.

a. The commission shall post conspicuously on the commission's website online resources about sexual harassment, including but not limited to: [ALP S-025]

1. Information that sets forth in simple and understandable terms: [ALP S-025]

(a) An explanation that sexual harassment is a form of unlawful discrimination under local law; [ALP S-025]

(b) Specific descriptions and examples of activities which may be sexual harassment; [ALP S-025]

(c) A description of the commission's complaint process, and how to contact the commission; [ALP S-025]

(d) A list of alternate and additional government agencies for filing complaints about sexual harassment, and the websites for such agencies, to the extent available; [ALP S-025]

(e) An explanation that retaliation, including but not limited to retaliation for complaints concerning allegations of sexual harassment, is prohibited by subdivision 7 of section 8-107, and examples of activities which may be retaliation for such complaints; and [ALP S-025]

(f) Bystander intervention education and the importance of taking action to prevent workplace sexual harassment. [ALP S-025]

2. An interactive tool describing each step of the complaint process available through the commission, from when a complaint is filed to when a determination is made on such complaint. [ALP S-025]

(L.L. 2018/094, 5/9/2018, eff. 8/7/2018) [ALP S-025]
