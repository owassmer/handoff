§ 11-02 Owner's Responsibility to Remediate.

An owner must remediate all lead-based paint hazards and underlying defects in a dwelling unit and in the common areas of a dwelling where a child of applicable age resides in accordance with the applicable work practices set forth in 28 RCNY § 11-06. [ALP S-107]

(Amended City Record 2/14/2025, eff. 3/16/2025) [ALP S-107]
