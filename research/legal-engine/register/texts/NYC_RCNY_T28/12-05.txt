§ 12-05 Definitions.

§ 12-05 Definitions.

For the purposes of this chapter

(a)
CO means carbon monoxide; [ALP S-118]

(b)
CO alarm means a "carbon monoxide alarm" as defined in section 202 of the Building Code and shall also mean a "carbon monoxide detecting device" as such term is used in Article 7 of Subchapter 17 of Chapter 1 and Subchapter 2 of Chapter 2 of Title 27 of the Administrative Code of the City of New York, and defined in section 202 of the Building Code; and [ALP S-118]

(c)
Private Dwelling means a dwelling unit in a one-family or two-family home that is occupied by a person or persons other than the owner of such unit or the owner's family. [ALP S-118]

(Amended City Record 1/2/2026, eff. 2/1/2026) [ALP S-118]
