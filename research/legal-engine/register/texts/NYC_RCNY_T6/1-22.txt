§ 1-22 Reservation of Rights.

Nothing in this chapter shall impair, diminish, or otherwise affect any other authority granted to the Department by any general, special or local law or any rule promulgated pursuant thereto to deny an application for a license, permit or registration, or suspend, terminate or revoke a license, permit or registration.
