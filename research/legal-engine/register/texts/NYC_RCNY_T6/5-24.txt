§ 5-24 Credit Card Limitations.

§ 5-24 Credit Card Limitations.

(a) A seller who accepts credit cards must conspicuously disclose every limitation the seller imposes on their use and must comply with all provisions of New York General Business Law § 518. [ALP S-112]

(b) Disclosures required by this section must appear at or near every entrance to the seller's business premises and in all advertising that indicates credit cards are accepted. Where a disclosure is required by this section to be consistent with the notice requirements of New York General Business Law § 518, such disclosure must be clearly and conspicuously posted in compliance with such section. [ALP S-112]

(Amended City Record 6/6/2025, eff. 8/5/2025) [ALP S-112]
