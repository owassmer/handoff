§ 5-41 Unlawful Sales Tax.

It is a deceptive trade practice for any seller to collect sales tax on the sale of any good or service that is not subject to such tax under Article 28 of the New York State Tax Law or the rules and regulations promulgated thereunder. [ALP S-003]

(Added City Record 7/7/2016, eff. 8/6/2016) [ALP S-003]
