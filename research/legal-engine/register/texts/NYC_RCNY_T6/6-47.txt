§ 6-47 Consumer Protection Law Penalty Schedule.

All citations are to Title 20 of the Administrative Code of the City of New York or Title 6 of the Rules of the City of New York. [ALP S-005]

Unless otherwise specified, the penalties set forth for each section of law or rule shall also apply to all subdivisions, paragraphs, subparagraphs, clauses, items, or any other provision contained therein. Each subdivision, paragraph, subparagraph, clause, item, or other provision charged in the Notice of Violation shall constitute a separate violation of the law or rule. [ALP S-005]

For the fine amounts marked by a single asterisk, if the respondent timely submits the appropriate proof of having cured a first-time violation, the respondent will not be subject to a civil penalty pursuant to Local Law 153 of 2013. [ALP S-005]

Pursuant to Section 20-703(d) of the Administrative Code of the City of New York, the knowing violation of any provision of Subchapter 1 of Chapter 5 of Title 20 of the Administrative Code of the City of New York or of any rule promulgated thereunder is subject to a penalty of $3,500. [ALP S-069]

Pursuant to Section 20-703(b) of the Administrative Code of the City of New York, each individual statement, description or other representation or omission that constitutes a deceptive trade practice shall give rise to a distinct and independent violation. [ALP S-069]

The number of violations of any provision listed below shall be calculated pursuant to § 20-703(c) of the Administrative Code of the City of New York. [ALP S-069]

Unless otherwise specified by law, a second or third or subsequent violation means a violation by the same respondent, whether by pleading guilty, being found guilty in a decision, or entering into a settlement agreement for violating the same provision of law or rule, within three years of the prior violation(s). [ALP S-069]
