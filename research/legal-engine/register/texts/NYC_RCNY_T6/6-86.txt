§ 6-86 Powered Mobility Devices Penalty Schedule.

All citations are to Title 20 of the Administrative Code of the City of New York. [ALP S-088]

Unless otherwise specified, the penalties set forth for each section of law or rule shall also apply to all subdivisions, paragraphs, subparagraphs, clauses, items, or any other provision contained therein. Each subdivision, paragraph, subparagraph, clause, item, or other provision charged in the Notice of Violation shall constitute a separate violation of the law or rule. [ALP S-088]

Unless otherwise specified by law, a second or third or subsequent violation means a violation by the same respondent, whether by admitting to the violation, being found in violation in a decision, or entering into a settlement agreement for violating the same provision of law or rule, on a different day within two years of the prior violation(s). [ALP S-088]

Each failure to comply with respect to any one stock keeping unit gives rise to a separate violation of subdivision a, b, or c of § 20-610 of the Administrative Code. [ALP S-088]

Each day on which a violation of § 20-610.1 of the Administrative Code continues constitutes a separate violation. [ALP S-102]
