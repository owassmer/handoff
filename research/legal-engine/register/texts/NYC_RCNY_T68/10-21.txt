§ 10-21 Moves and Referrals to CityFHEPS Subchapter A.

For good cause, HRA may grant approval for a move from one Subchapter B unit to another or a referral for a Subchapter A shopping letter. For purposes of this section, good cause includes when a Subchapter B unit has lost or will lose its designation as a Subchapter B unit, or when the household's size has decreased resulting in a difference between the rent and the CityFHEPS rental assistance amount that is greater than 40 percent of the household's total monthly gross income.
