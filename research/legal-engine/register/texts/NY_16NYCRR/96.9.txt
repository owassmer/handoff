N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 16 § 96.9 - Severability

If any provision of this Part is adjudicated invalid by a
court of competent jurisdiction, such invalidity shall not affect the other
provisions which can be given full effect without the invalid
provision(s).
