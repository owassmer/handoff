N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 18 § 352.10 - Payment of life insurance premiums

Life insurance premiums shall be met under the following
circumstances and conditions:
(a) when
the recipient is aged, his life expectancy is short, or he is deemed
uninsurable; or
(b) by vendor
payment when the policy is assigned to the agency.
