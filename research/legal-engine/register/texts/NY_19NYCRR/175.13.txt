N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 19 § 175.13 - Accepting services of another broker's salesman or employee

A real estate broker shall not accept the services of any
salesman or employee in the organization of another real estate broker without
the knowledge of the broker and no real estate broker should give or permit to
be given or directly offer to give anything of value for the purpose of
influencing or rewarding the actions of any salesman or employee of another
real estate broker in relation to the business of such broker or the client of
such broker without the knowledge of such broker.
