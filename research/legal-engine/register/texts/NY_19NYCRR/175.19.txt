N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 19 § 175.19 - Net listing agreements

(a) The
term net listing as used herein shall mean an agency or other agreement whereby
a prospective seller of real property or an interest therein, lists such
property or interest for sale with a licensed real estate broker authorizing
the sale thereof at a specified net amount to be paid to the seller and
authorizing the broker to retain as commission, compensation, or otherwise, the
difference between the price at which the property or interest is sold and the
specified net amount to be received by the seller.
(b) No real estate broker shall make or enter
into a "net listing" contract for the sale of real property or any interest
therein.
