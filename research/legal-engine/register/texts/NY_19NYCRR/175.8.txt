N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 19 § 175.8 - Negotiating with party to exclusive listing contract

No real estate broker shall negotiate the sale, exchange or
lease of any property directly with an owner or lessor if he knows that such
owner, or lessor, has an existing written contract granting exclusive authority
in connection with such property with another broker.
