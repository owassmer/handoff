N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 19 § 175.9 - Inducing breach of contract of sale or lease

No real estate broker shall induce any party to a contract
of sale or lease to break such contract for the purpose of substituting in lieu
thereof a new contract with another principal.
