N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 22 § 202.10 - Appearance at Conferences

(a) Any
party may request to appear at a conference by electronic means. Where feasible
and appropriate, the court is encouraged to grant such requests.
(b) Adjournments of conferences shall be
granted upon a showing of good cause. An adjournment of a conference will not
change any date in any court order, including but not limited to the
preliminary conference order, unless otherwise directed by the court.
