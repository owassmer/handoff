N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 22 § 202.32 - Engagement of counsel

No adjournment shall be granted on the ground
of engagement of counsel except in accordance with Part 125 of the Rules of the
Chief Administrator of the Courts (22 NYCRR Part 125).
