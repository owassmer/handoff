N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 22 § 202.53 - Trust accountings; procedure

(a) Applications by trustees for
interlocutory or final judgments or final orders in trust accountings or to
terminate trusts shall be by notice of petition or order to show cause after
the account has been filed in the county clerk's office.
(b) In all actions involving an
accounting of a testamentary trustee or a trustee under a deed, notice must be
given to the State Tax Commission before the accounts of such trustees may be
approved.
(c) Where
all parties file a written consent to the entry of a judgment or order, it may
be presented at a motion part for consideration by the court.
