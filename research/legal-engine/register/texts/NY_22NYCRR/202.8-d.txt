N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 22 § 202.8-d - Orders to Show Cause

Motions shall be brought on by order to show cause only
when there is genuine urgency (e.g., applications for provisional relief), a
stay is required or a statute mandates so proceeding. See Section
202.8 -e. Absent advance
permission of the court, reply papers shall not be submitted on orders to show
cause.
