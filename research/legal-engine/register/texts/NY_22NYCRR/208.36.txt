N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 22 § 208.36 - Infants' and incapacitated persons' claims and proceedings

The settlement of an action by an infant or
judicially declared incapacitated person (including an incompetent or
conservatee) shall comply with CPLR 1207 and 1208, section
202.67 of the Rules of the Chief
Administrator (22 NYCRR 202.67) and, in the case of an infant, with section
474 of the
Judiciary Law.
