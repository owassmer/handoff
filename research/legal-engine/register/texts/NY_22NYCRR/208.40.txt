N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 22 § 208.40 - Arbitration

(a)
Alternative method of dispute resolution by arbitration. Where the Chief
Administrator of the Courts has established this arbitration program, Part 28
of the Rules of the Chief Judge (22 NYCRR Part 28) shall control the
proceedings.
(b) Where
the parties agree to arbitrate a claim under NYCCCA 206, arbitration
proceedings shall be conducted in accordance with CPLR article 75.
