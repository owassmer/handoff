N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 9 § 2520.2 - Amendment of codes

Sections 1 through 66, inclusive, of the code of the Rent
Stabilization Association of New York City, Inc., and sections 1 through 64,
inclusive, of the code of the Metropolitan Hotel Industry Stabilization
Association, Inc., as last amended, are deleted in their entirety, and sections
2520.1 through
2531.9 of this Subchapter,
inclusive, are adopted, and this Code is known as the Rent Stabilization Code.
Chapter VIII of this Subtitle is known as Rent Stabilization Regulations, and
divided into Subchapter A - Emergency Tenant Protection Regulations, consisting
of Parts 2500-2511; and Subchapter B - Rent Stabilization Code, consisting of
new Parts 2520-2531.
