N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 9 § 2520.3 - Construction and implementation

This Code shall be construed so as to carry out the
intent of the Rent Stabilization Law to ensure that such statute shall not be
subverted or rendered ineffective, directly or indirectly, and to prevent the
exaction of unjust, unreasonable and oppressive rents and rental agreements,
and to forestall profiteering, speculation and other disruptive practices
tending to produce threats to the public health, safety and general welfare;
and that the policy herein expressed shall be implemented with due regard for
the preservation of regulated rental housing.
