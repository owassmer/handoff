N.Y. Comp. Codes R. & Regs. Tit. 9 § 2520.9 - Filing of amendments

Such amendment or revocation shall be filed with the
Secretary of State and shall take effect upon the date of publication of the
notice of adoption in the State Register unless otherwise specified therein or
as otherwise provided by the State Administrative Procedure Act, or otherwise
required by law.
