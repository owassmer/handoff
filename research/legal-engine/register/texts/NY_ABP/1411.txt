N.Y.

Abandoned Property Law Section 1411

Waiver of publication

The state comptroller may waive the publication of any notice required by this chapter, except a notice required by section fourteen hundred two, whenever in his opinion the cost of publishing such notice would be unreasonable in relation to the amount of abandoned property.
