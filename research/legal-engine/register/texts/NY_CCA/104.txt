SECTION 104. Expenses of court

§ 104. Expenses of court. All salaries of both judicial and
non-judicial personnel of the court and all other expenses of the court
whatsoever shall be a charge upon the city of New York. As provided in
subdivision d of section twenty-nine of article six of the constitution,
the governing body of the city shall annually include in its final
estimate such sums as may be necessary to pay such salaries and
expenses.
