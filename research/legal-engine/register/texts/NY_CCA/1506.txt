SECTION 1506. Execution where order of attachment issued

§ 1506. Execution where order of attachment issued. Where the real
property of the judgment-debtor has been duly attached under an order of
attachment that has not been vacated, the execution may not issue out of
this court. In such a case, a transcript of the judgment must be filed
and docketed with the county clerk and the execution issued out of
supreme court.
