SECTION 1507. Limitation on execution against property of tenant

§ 1507. Limitation on execution against property of tenant. No levy
shall be made on the property of a tenant dispossessed for non-payment
of rent under any execution within twenty-four hours of the time of
dispossess, if the property of which the tenant is being dispossessed
was his residence.
