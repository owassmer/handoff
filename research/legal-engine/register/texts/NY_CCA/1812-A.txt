SECTION 1812-A. Enforcement of commercial claims judgments

§ 1812-A. Enforcement of commercial claims judgments. Where a judgment
has been entered in a commercial claims part and remains unsatisfied,
the commercial claims clerk shall, upon request, issue information
subpoenas, at nominal cost, for the judgment creditor and provide the
creditor with assistance on their preparation and use.
