SECTION 1906. Costs allowed by court

§ 1906. Costs allowed by court. The court may in its discretion impose
costs not exceeding fifty dollars in the following cases:

(a) Upon granting or denying a motion.

(b) Upon allowing an amendment of a pleading.

(c) Upon adjournment of a trial.
