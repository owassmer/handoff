SECTION 2104. References to predecessor courts

§ 2104. References to predecessor courts. A reference in any general
or special statute, law, local law, ordinance, resolution, rule,
regulation or order to the city court of the city of New York or to the
municipal court of the city of New York shall be deemed a reference to
this court if such substituted reference be consistent with this act and
with all other provisions of law applicable to this court.
