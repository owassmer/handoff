SECTION 403. Summons; method and place of service

§ 403. Summons; method and place of service. Service of summons shall
be made in the manner prescribed in supreme court practice, including
the optional method of service by mail authorized by CPLR 312-a, but it
shall be made only within the city of New York unless service beyond the
city be authorized by this act or by such other provision of law, other
than the CPLR, as expressly applies to courts of limited jurisdiction or
to all courts of the state.
