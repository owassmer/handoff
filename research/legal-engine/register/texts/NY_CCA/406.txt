SECTION 406. Summons; service by publication authorized

§ 406. Summons; service by publication authorized. The court, upon
motion without notice, shall order service of a summons by publication
in an action described in § 405 if service cannot be made by another
method with due diligence. Practice and procedure on service by
publication shall be governed by the CPLR, except insofar as this act
otherwise provides.
