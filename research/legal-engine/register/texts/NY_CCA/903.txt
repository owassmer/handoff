SECTION 903. Pleadings; requirements of formal pleading inapplicable to indorsement pleading

§ 903. Pleadings; requirements of formal pleading inapplicable to
indorsement pleading. The requirements of this act or of the CPLR
applicable to a formal pleading shall not be applicable to an
indorsement pleading.
