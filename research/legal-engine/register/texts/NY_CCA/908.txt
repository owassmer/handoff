SECTION 908. Pleadings; verification

§ 908. Pleadings; verification. Verification of pleadings shall be
governed by the CPLR, except that if a pleading be not formal it need
not be verified. The court in such instance may require a formal
pleading as provided in subdivision (e) of § 902 and order its
verification.
