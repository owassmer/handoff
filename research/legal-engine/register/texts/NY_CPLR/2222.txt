N.Y.

Civil Practice Law & Rules Section 2222

Docketing order as judgment

At the request of any party the clerk shall docket as a judgment an order directing the payment of money, including motion costs, or affecting the title to, or the possession, use or enjoyment of, real property, provided, however, that where the clerk maintains a section and block index, an order affecting the title to, or the possession, use or enjoyment of, real property may be entered in such index in lieu thereof.
