Civil Practice Law & Rules Section 4525

Copies of statements under article nine of the uniform commercial code

Rule 4525. Copies of statements under article nine of the uniform commercial code. A copy of a statement which is noted or certified by a filing officer pursuant to section 9--523 of the uniform commercial code and which states that the copy is a true copy is prima facie evidence of the facts stated in the notation or certification and that the copy is a true copy of a statement filed in the office of the filing officer.
