N.Y.

Civil Practice Law & Rules Section 4529

Inspection certificate issued by United States department of agriculture

Rule 4529. Inspection certificate issued by United States department of agriculture. An inspection certificate issued by the authorized agents of the United States department of agriculture on file with the United States secretary of agriculture is prima facie evidence of the facts stated.
