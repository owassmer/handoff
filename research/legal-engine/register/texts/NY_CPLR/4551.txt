N.Y.

Civil Practice Law & Rules Section 4551

New York voting and elections database

The data, information, and/or estimates maintained by the New York voting and elections database within the state board of elections or copies of such data, information and/or estimates provided to the attorney general pursuant to subdivision three of section 3-112 of the election law shall be granted a rebuttable presumption of validity by any court concerning any claim brought.
