N.Y.

Civil Practice Law & Rules Section 6205

Order of attachment in certain cases

An order of attachment may be granted in aid of execution to a party that has been awarded a money judgment against a foreign state, as defined in 28 United States Code Section 1603, in accordance with and subject to the limitations of 28 United States Code Section 1610 and other applicable law.
