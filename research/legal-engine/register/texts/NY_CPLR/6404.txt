N.Y.

Civil Practice Law & Rules Section 6404

Accounts

A temporary receiver shall keep written accounts itemizing receipts and expenditures, and describing the property and naming the depository of receivership funds, which shall be open to inspection by any person having an apparent interest in the property. Upon motion of the receiver or of any person having an apparent interest in the property, the court may require the keeping of particular records or direct or limit inspection or require presentation of a temporary receiver’s accounts. Notice of a motion for the presentation of a temporary receiver’s accounts shall be served upon the sureties on his undertaking as well as upon each party.
