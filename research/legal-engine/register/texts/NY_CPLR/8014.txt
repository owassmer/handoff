N.Y.

Civil Practice Law & Rules Section 8014

Collection of sheriff’s fees on execution

The fees of a sheriff, upon an execution against property, which are not required by statute to be paid by a particular person and which are not included in the bill of costs of the party in whose favor the execution is issued, shall be collected by virtue of the execution in the same manner as the sum therein directed to be collected.
