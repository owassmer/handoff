N.Y.

Civil Practice Law & Rules Section 8110

Costs against a fiduciary

Where costs are awarded against a fiduciary, they shall be chargeable only upon the estate, fund or person he represents, unless the court directs them to be paid personally for mismanagement or bad faith in the prosecution or defense of the action.
