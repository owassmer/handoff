N.Y.

Civil Practice Law & Rules Section 8402

Taxation with notice

Costs may be taxed upon at least five days’ notice to each adverse party interested in reducing the amount thereof except one against whom judgment was entered on default in appearance. A copy of the bill of costs, specifying the items in detail, and a copy of any supporting affidavits shall be served with the notice.
