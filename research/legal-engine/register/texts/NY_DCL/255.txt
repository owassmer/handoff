N.Y.

Debtor & Creditor Law Section 255

Proceedings on return of citation

Upon the return of such citation properly served, such court shall have the powers in relation to such estates, claims, property and committee, which devolve on courts, by virtue of section twenty-one, in relation to assignments and assignees for benefit of creditors and such assigned estates.
