N.Y.

Debtor & Creditor Law Section 284

Exclusivity of exemptions

In accordance with the provisions of section five hundred twenty-two (b) of title eleven of the United States Code, debtors domiciled in this state are not authorized to exempt from the estate property that is specified under subsection (d) of such section.
