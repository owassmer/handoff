N.Y.

Estates, Powers & Trusts Law Section 1-1.4

Severability

§ 1-1.4 Severability If any provision of this chapter or application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, such invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications of this chapter which can be given effect without regard to the invalid provision or application, and to this end the provisions of this chapter are declared to be severable.
