N.Y.

Estates, Powers & Trusts Law Section 1-2.1

Codicil

§ 1-2.1 Codicil A codicil is a supplement to a will, either adding to, taking from or altering its provisions or confirming it in whole or in part by republication, but not totally revoking such will.
