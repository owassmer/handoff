N.Y.

Estates, Powers & Trusts Law Section 1-2.10

Issue

§ 1-2.10 Issue (a) Unless a contrary intention is indicated:

(1)
Issue are the descendants in any degree from a common ancestor.

(2)
The terms “issue” and “descendants”, in subparagraph (1), include adopted children.
