N.Y.

Estates, Powers & Trusts Law Section 1-2.7

Fiduciary

§ 1-2.7 Fiduciary A fiduciary is a person who meets the description, in this part, of a “personal representative” or who is designated by the creator or by the court to act as an assignee for the benefit of creditors, or a committee, conservator, curator, custodian, guardian, trustee or donee of a power during minority.
