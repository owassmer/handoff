N.Y.

Estates, Powers & Trusts Law Section 1-2.9-A

Infant or minor

§ 1-2.9-a Infant or minor As used in this chapter, the term “infant” or “minor” means a person who has not attained the age of eighteen years, provided, however, that such definition shall not be applicable to any provision relating to the New York Uniform Transfers to Minors Act, nor to section 13-3.4.
