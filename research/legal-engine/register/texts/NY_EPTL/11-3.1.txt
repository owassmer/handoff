N.Y.

Estates, Powers & Trusts Law Section 11-3.1

Actions

§ 11-3.1 Actions Any action, other than an action for injury to person or property, may be maintained by and against a personal representative in all cases and in such manner as such action might have been maintained by or against his decedent.
