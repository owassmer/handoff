N.Y.

Estates, Powers & Trusts Law Section 11-4.2

When personal and representative causes of action may be joined

§ 11-4.2 When personal and representative causes of action may be joined Actions or proceedings brought against a personal representative personally and in his representative capacity may be joined. In such case a judgment for the plaintiff must clearly indicate whether it is awarded against the defendant personally or in his representative capacity.
