N.Y.

Estates, Powers & Trusts Law Section 11-4.5

Want of assets not to be pleaded by personal representative

§ 11-4.5 Want of assets not to be pleaded by personal representative In an action or proceeding against a personal representative, in his representative capacity, in which the complaint demands judgment for a sum of money, the non-existence or insufficiency of assets may not be pleaded and the plaintiff’s right of recovery is not affected thereby.
