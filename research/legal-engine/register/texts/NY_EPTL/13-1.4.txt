Estates, Powers & Trusts Law Section 13-1.4

Action in supreme court to compromise controversies between

§ 13-1.4 Action in supreme court to compromise controversies between claimants to estate assets An action may be maintained in the supreme court to compromise controversies between claimants to the property of an estate in accordance with and subject to the provisions of SCPA 2106.
