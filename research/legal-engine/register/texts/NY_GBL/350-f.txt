N.Y.

General Business Law Section 350-F

Exceptions

Nothing in this article shall apply to any television or sound radio broadcasting station or to any publisher or printer of a newspaper, magazine, or other form of printed advertising, who broadcasts, publishes, or prints such advertisement.
