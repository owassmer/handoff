N.Y.

General Business Law Section 380-P

Unauthorized disclosures by officers or employees

penalty

Any officer or employee of a consumer reporting agency who knowingly and willfully provides information concerning an individual from the agency’s files to a person not authorized to receive that information shall, upon conviction, be fined not more than five thousand dollars or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
