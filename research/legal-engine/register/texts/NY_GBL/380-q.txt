N.Y.

General Business Law Section 380-Q

Disclosure of medical information

Whenever any provision of this article requires disclosure of medical information, or the disclosure of a reason for adverse action which involves medical information, such information or reason shall be disclosed only to a physician designated by the consumer for such purpose.
