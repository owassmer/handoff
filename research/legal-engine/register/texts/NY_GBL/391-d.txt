N.Y.

General Business Law Section 391-D

Sale of matchbooks

All one piece matchbooks sold or distributed within the state must have the striking area on the side other than the side which the matchbook opens. Any person in the ordinary course of their business who violates the provisions of this act shall be subject to a fine of not more than two hundred and fifty dollars.
