N.Y.

General Business Law Section 391-P

Prohibit the rental of clothing articles previously worn

1.
No person shall rent articles of clothing which have been previously worn unless such articles of clothing are cleaned prior to such rental.

2.
A knowing violation of this section shall be punishable by a civil penalty not to exceed two hundred fifty dollars for the first violation and not to exceed one thousand dollars for each subsequent violation.
