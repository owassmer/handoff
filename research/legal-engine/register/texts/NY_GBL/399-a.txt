N.Y.

General Business Law Section 399-A

Pay toilets

prohibition

1.
On and after September first, nineteen hundred seventy-five, no owner, lessee or other occupant of any real property or any other person, copartnership or corporation shall operate or permit to be operated pay toilet facilities upon such real property.

2.
A violation of the provisions of this section shall constitute a violation.
