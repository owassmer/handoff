N.Y.

General Business Law Section 399-DD

Sale of alcohol vaporizing devices prohibited

1.
For purposes of this section, “alcohol vaporizing device” means any device, machine or process which mixes spirits, liquor or other alcohol product with pure oxygen or other gas to produce a vaporized product for the purpose of consumption by inhalation.

2.
No person, corporation, partnership or limited liability company shall knowingly sell or offer for sale an alcohol vaporizing device.

3.
A violation of the provisions of this section shall be an offense punishable by a fine of not more than five thousand dollars. A violation of the provisions of this section after having been previously convicted of such an offense within the previous five years shall be a class B misdemeanor punishable by a fine of not more than ten thousand dollars. * NB There are 3 § 399-dd’s
