N.Y.

General Business Law Section 399-Y

Child safety devices for hot water dispensers

Upon the retail sale, lease, or rental of any water dispenser, water cooler, or other non-plumbed device or system which dispenses hot water instantly, written notice shall be provided to the customer informing such customer of the availability, at no extra charge to the customer, of a child resistant safety device or similar protective feature for the hot water dispenser which is designed to prevent accidental injury to children. Upon customer request, such device shall be promptly provided at no charge. * NB There are 2 § 399-y’s
