N.Y.

General Business Law Section 604-GG

Scope

1.
The provisions of this article shall apply only to a creditor to whom a debt is owed, due, or asserted to be due or owed, where such debt is asserted to be coerced debt.

2.
The provisions of this article shall not be construed to impose any duty or liability not expressly provided herein.

3.
Nothing in this article shall prevent a creditor from seeking recourse for fraudulent claims of coerced debt.
