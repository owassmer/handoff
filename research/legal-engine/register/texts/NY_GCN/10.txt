N.Y.

General Construction Law Section 10

Acknowledge and acknowledgment

The terms acknowledge and acknowledgment, when used with reference to the execution of an instrument or writing other than a deed of real property, include a compliance with the provisions of the next section by either such proof or acknowledgment.
