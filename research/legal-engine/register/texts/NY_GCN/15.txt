N.Y.

General Construction Law Section 15

Chattels

The term chattels includes goods and chattels; and, where the term appears in any statute or rule pertaining to an action to recover the same, it also includes all specific personal property, such as, but not limited to, certificates of stock, bonds, notes, or other securities or obligations.
