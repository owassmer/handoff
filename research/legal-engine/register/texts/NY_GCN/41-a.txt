N.Y.

General Construction Law Section 41-A

Recital in record of meeting as evidence

A recital in any order, resolution or other record of any proceeding of a meeting referred to in section forty-one of this chapter that such meeting had been held or adjourned as provided in said section, or that it had been held upon notice to the members, as therein provided, is prima facie evidence thereof.
