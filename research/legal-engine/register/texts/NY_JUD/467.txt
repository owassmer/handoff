N.Y.

Judiciary Law Section 467

Registration of attorneys

The clerks of each appellate division shall forward to the clerk of the court of appeals and the chief administrator of the courts a list of attorneys newly admitted to practice. Such list shall be forwarded at such times and in such form as the clerk of the court of appeals and the chief administrator shall provide.
