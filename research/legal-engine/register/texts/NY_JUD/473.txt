N.Y.

Judiciary Law Section 473

Constables, coroners, criers and attendants prohibited from practicing during term of office

A constable, coroner, crier, or attendant of a court, shall not, during his continuance in office, practice as an attorney or counsellor in any court, nor shall a sheriff, under-sheriff, deputy-sheriff, or sheriff’s clerk so practice in the county in which he is elected or appointed.
