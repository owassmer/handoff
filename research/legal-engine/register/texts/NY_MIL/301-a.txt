N.Y.

Military Law Section 301-A

Notice of benefits to persons in and persons enlisting in the state organized militia

The adjutant general shall ensure the giving of notice of the benefits accorded by this article and accorded by the laws of this state to persons in and to persons enlisting in the state organized militia.
