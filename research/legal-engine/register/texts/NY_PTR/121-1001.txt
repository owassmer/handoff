N.Y.

Partnership Law Section 121-1001

Parties to actions

A limited partner, unless he is also a general partner, is not a proper party to proceedings by or against a partnership, except where the object is to enforce a limited partner’s right against or liability to the partnership and except in cases provided for in section 121-1002 of this article.
