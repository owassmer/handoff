N.Y.

Partnership Law Section 121-606

Right to distribution

Subject to sections 121-607 and 121-804 of this article, at the time a partner becomes entitled to receive a distribution, he has the status of, and is entitled to all remedies available to, a creditor of the limited partnership with respect to the distribution.
