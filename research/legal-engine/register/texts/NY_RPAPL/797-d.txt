N.Y.

Real Property Actions & Proceedings Law Section 797-D

Time of service

order to show cause

1.
The notice of petition and petition shall be served at least ten and not more than seventeen days before the time at which the petition is to be heard.

2.
The court may grant an order to show cause to be served in lieu of a notice of petition at a time and in a manner specified therein.
