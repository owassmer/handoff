N.Y.

Real Property Actions & Proceedings Law Section 797-J

Judgment

1.
The court shall direct that final judgment be entered determining the rights of the parties.

2.
The judgment may include:

a.
An order to repair conditions constituting violation of applicable local and state housing standards or section two hundred thirty-five-b of the real property law; and

b.
Any other relief that the court may deem just.
