N.Y.

Real Property Law Section 2

Definitions

1.
The terms “real property” and “lands” as used in the first eight articles of this chapter are co-extensive in meaning with lands, tenements and hereditaments.

2.
The term “infant” or “minor” as used in this chapter means a person who has not attained the age of eighteen years.
