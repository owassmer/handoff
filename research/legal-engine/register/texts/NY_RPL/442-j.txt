N.Y.

Real Property Law Section 442-J

Effect of invalid provision

Should the courts of this state declare any provision of this article unconstitutional, or unauthorized, or in conflict with any other section or provision of this article, then such decision shall affect only the section or provision so declared to be unconstitutional or unauthorized and shall not affect any other section or part of this article.
