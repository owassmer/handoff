§ 1006. Failure to qualify

The court must upon application of any person interested, a public
administrator, the chief fiscal officer of the county, or creditor
require an administrator designated by it to receive letters to qualify
within a time specified and direct that in default thereof he be deemed
to have renounced his appointment.
