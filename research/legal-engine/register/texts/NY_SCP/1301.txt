§ 1301. Definitions

In this article:

1. A small estate is the estate of a domiciliary or a non-domiciliary
who dies leaving personal property having a gross value of $50,000 or
less exclusive of property required to be set off under EPTL 5-3.1 (a).

2. A voluntary administrator is a person who qualifies and undertakes
to settle the estate of the decedent without the formality of court
administration as hereinafter provided.
