SECTION 723. Copy of letters issued to chief fiscal officer of county to be transmitted to comptroller

§ 723. Copy of letters issued to chief fiscal officer of   county to be

       transmitted to comptroller.

Within ten days after granting letters to the chief fiscal officer of
the county the court must transmit to the state comptroller a certified
copy thereof.
