N.Y.

Uniform Commercial Code Law Section 1-104

Construction Against Implied Repeal

Section 1--104. Construction Against Implied Repeal. This act being a general act intended as a unified coverage of its subject matter, no part of it shall be deemed to be impliedly repealed by subsequent legislation if such construction can reasonably be avoided.
