N.Y.

Uniform Commercial Code Law Section 1-106

Use of Singular and Plural

Gender

Section 1--106. Use of Singular and Plural; Gender. In this act, unless the statutory context otherwise requires:

(1)
words in the singular number include the plural, and those in the plural include the singular; and

(2)
words of any gender also refer to any other gender.
