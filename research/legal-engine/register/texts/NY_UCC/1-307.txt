N.Y.

Uniform Commercial Code Law Section 1-307

Prima Facie evidence by Third-party Documents

Section 1--307. Prima Facie evidence by Third-party Documents. A document in due form purporting to be a bill of lading, policy or certificate of insurance, official weigher’s or inspector’s certificate, consular invoice, or any other document authorized or required by the contract to be issued by a third party is prima facie evidence of its own authenticity and genuineness and of the facts stated in the document by the third party.
