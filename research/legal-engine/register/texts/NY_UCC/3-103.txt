N.Y.

Uniform Commercial Code Law Section 3-103

Limitations on Scope of Article

Section 3--103. Limitations on Scope of Article.

(1)
This Article does not apply to money, documents of title or investment securities.

(2)
The provisions of this Article are subject to the provisions of the Article on Bank Deposits and Collections (Article 4) and Secured Transactions (Article 9).
