N.Y.

Uniform Commercial Code Law Section 3-120

Instruments “Payable Through” Bank

Section 3--120. Instruments “Payable Through” Bank. An instrument which states that it is “payable through” a bank or the like designates that bank as a collecting bank to make presentment but does not of itself authorize the bank to pay the instrument.
