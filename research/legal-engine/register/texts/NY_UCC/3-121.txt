N.Y.

Uniform Commercial Code Law Section 3-121

Instruments Payable at Bank

Section 3--121. Instruments Payable at Bank. A note or acceptance which states that it is payable at a bank is the equivalent of a draft drawn on the bank payable when it falls due out of any funds of the maker or acceptor in current account or otherwise available for such payment.
