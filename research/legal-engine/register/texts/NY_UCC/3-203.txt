N.Y.

Uniform Commercial Code Law Section 3-203

Wrong or Misspelled Name

Section 3--203. Wrong or Misspelled Name. Where an instrument is made payable to a person under a misspelled name or one other than his own he may indorse in that name or his own or both; but signature in both names may be required by a person paying or giving value for the instrument.
