Uniform Commercial Code Law Section 3-207

Negotiation Effective Although It May Be Rescinded

Section 3--207. Negotiation Effective Although It May Be Rescinded.

(1)
Negotiation is effective to transfer the instrument although the negotiation is (a) made by an infant, a corporation exceeding its powers, or any other person without capacity; or

(b)
obtained by fraud, duress or mistake of any kind; or

(c)
part of an illegal transaction; or

(d)
made in breach of duty.

(2)
Except as against a subsequent holder in due course such negotiation is in an appropriate case subject to rescission, the declaration of a constructive trust or any other remedy permitted by law.
