Uniform Commercial Code Law Section 3-411

Certification of a Check

Section 3--411. Certification of a Check.

(1)
Certification of a check is acceptance. Where a holder procures certification the drawer and all prior indorsers are discharged.

(2)
Unless otherwise agreed a bank has no obligation to certify a check.

(3)
A bank may certify a check before returning it for lack of proper indorsement. If it does so the drawer is discharged.
