1182. Definitions

§ 1182.
Definitions
In this subchapter:
(1)
Debtor.—
The term “debtor” means a small business debtor.
(2)
Debtor in possession.—
The term “debtor in possession” means the debtor, unless removed as debtor in possession under section 1185(a) of this title.
