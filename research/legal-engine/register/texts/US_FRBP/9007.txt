9007. Authority to Regulate Notices

Rule 9007.
Authority to Regulate Notices
(a)
In General.
Unless these rules provide otherwise, when notice is to be given, the court must designate:
(1)
the deadline for giving it;
(2)
the entities to whom it must be given; and
(3)
the form and manner of giving it.
(b)
Combined Notices.
When feasible, the court may order notices under these rules to be combined.
