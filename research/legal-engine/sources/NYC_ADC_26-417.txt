§ 26-417 Surcharges for tenant-installed air conditioner units; prohibited.

The imposition of any surcharge for the installation and use of a tenant-installed air conditioner unit is prohibited where the tenant pays for electric utility service.

(2022 N.Y. Laws Ch. 619, 11/21/2022, eff. 11/21/2022) [ALP S-079]
