               New York City Department of Consumer and Worker Protection

                        NOTICE OF CHANGE OF EFFECTIVE DATE

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the final rules of the Department of Consumer and Worker
Protection relating to debt collectors published in the City Record on February 26, 2026 will go
into effect on January 1, 2027, not September 1, 2026, the date contained in the publication.
