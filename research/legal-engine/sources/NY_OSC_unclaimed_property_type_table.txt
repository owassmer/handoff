When determining the applicable reporting property type codes, dates and requirements, you should: 

• 
Review the Calendar of Events to determine the reporting schedule for your organization. 
• 
Review the related property type(s) on the tables below regarding the associated article/section 
pursuant to which you are reporting and determine the dormancy period for the property type(s) you 
are holding. 
• 
Review the Abandoned Property Law to determine if any additional statutory requirements apply to 
your organization. 

Consult the Industry-specific handbook to verify the relationship between property type and reporting 
schedule. Each industry-specific handbook is linked to the title of its corresponding property table below.
