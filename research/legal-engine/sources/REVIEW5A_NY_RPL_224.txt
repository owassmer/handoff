N.Y.

Real Property Law Section 224

Attornment by tenant

 The attornment of a tenant to a stranger is absolutely void and does not in any way affect the possession of the landlord unless made either:

1.
 With the consent of the landlord; or, 2. Pursuant to or in consequence of a judgment, order, or decree of a court of competent jurisdiction; or

3.
 To a purchaser at foreclosure sale.
