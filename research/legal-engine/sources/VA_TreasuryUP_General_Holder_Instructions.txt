              Unclaimed Property Division
  General Holder Reporting & Payment
              Instructions
1. Determine if you should file and have any unclaimed property to report

    • If your legal entity is a business association (corporation, banking or financial organization,
       life insurance corporation, utility company, etc.), governmental agency, court or public
       authority, or nonprofit you are likely required to file annual reports of unclaimed property.

              • Reporting Deadline is November 1
              • Insurance Reporting Deadline is May 1

    • Any report of unclaimed property containing 25 or more properties must be reported
       electronically utilizing the NAUPA format. Negative reports are not required.

    • To identify the accounts meeting the criteria to be considered abandoned property reference
       the Property Type Codes and Dormancy Periods and Date Chart on our Reporting Guidelines
       page under the Notable Information tab.

       •   The Property Type Chart provides information of what types of account are involved and
           the Dormancy Chart shows you the maximum length of time an account may remain on
           your books without activity before it is considered abandoned (You may voluntarily report
           accounts early if you have determined the whereabouts of the owner is unknown).

       •   The Date Chart assists you in using the correct date range when considering the date of
           last activity on a particular type of account.

2. Perform Due Diligence

    • For items $100 and greater in value, the law requires that written notice be sent by first class
       mail to the owner's last known address on the holder's records at least 60 days prior to
       reporting.
