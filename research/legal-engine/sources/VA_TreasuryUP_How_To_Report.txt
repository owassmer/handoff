         Why Must Unclaimed
        Property be Reported?
1.   It is the law. This consumer protection act has been in effect since
     1961.

2.   The property does not belong to the entity holding it; it belongs to the
     owner. If the owner cannot be located, then upon reaching a set
     holding period it is to be reported as unclaimed property.

3.   The Division of Unclaimed Property acts as custodian of the property
     until the rightful owner can be located and the property returned to
     them.

 How Does Property Become
      “Unclaimed”?

In accordance with the Code of Virginia, Disposition of Unclaimed Property
Act (Chapter 25 §55.1-2500 – 55.1-2545), property becomes unclaimed
when the holder has not had contact with the owner of the property for a
specified period of time. After the passage of the dormancy period, if
there has been no activity generated by the owner, the property is then
presumed abandoned.

        Reporting Guidelines
•   Reporting Deadlines
-   November 1st for corporations, public entities, etc.
-   May 1st for insurance companies

•   Naupa Software
-   This is required to prepare your NAUPA formatted reporting
    file. Reports can be created directly through our website or by
    using third party software.

•   Notable information
-   Owner Type and Relationship Codes, Property Type Codes,
    Dormancy Periods, and a Date Chart are all available on our
    website to assist in determining what property is due and when.




             VIRGINIA TREASURY'S UNCLAIMED PROPERTY PROGRAM
        Reporting Guidelines
•   Securities
-   Two days prior to delivery, you must email an intent to deliver
    to our custodian. All securities must be delivered per
    instructions, the same time your report is submitted.

•   Tangible Property
-   Property must be pre-approved prior to remitting to The
    Commonwealth.
-   Tangible property reports must be performed separately from
    intangible reports.

•   Reciprocal Reporting
-   Virginia will accept property due to another state but there are
    limitations on the number of items and amounts.
-   Virginia will not accept reciprocal reporting of securities.

           Perform Due Diligence
 For items $100 or greater in value, the law requires that written notice be
  sent by first class mail to the owner’s last know address at least 60 days
  prior to reporting.

 Penalties for the failure to perform due diligence are specified in
  §55.1-2540. The fine, not to exceed $50, is applied to each account
  upon which due diligence was not performed.

 Notice should be mailed no later than September 1st if reporting by
  November 1st and no later than March 1st if reporting by May 1st.

 Due to this correspondence, if the owner contacts you before submitting
  your report, you may return the property directly and will not have
  to report it as unclaimed property.

       Important Information
•   There is no minimum reportable amount. By law, any and all
    property that meets the guidelines should be reported, no matter
    how small.

•   Negative reports are not required to be submitted.

•   Extensions may be granted upon request for either 30 or 60
    additional days.

•   No CD’s, diskettes, or flash drives will be accepted.




           VIRGINIA TREASURY'S UNCLAIMED PROPERTY PROGRAM
             Submitting a Report
•   Visit our website at www.vamoneysearch.gov and click Submit a Report to
    file an unclaimed property report to Virginia.

•   If you have a NAUPA formatted file ready to submit, select Upload a
    Report.

•   If you do not already have a NAUPA formatted file, then selecting Enter a
    Manual Report will allow one to be created without the use of additional
    software or forms. Here, you will enter your information as the holder,
    then create records for each property and its respective owner.

•   Upon submission, a confirmation email will be sent to the email address
    given along with payment instructions.




               VIRGINIA TREASURY'S UNCLAIMED PROPERTY PROGRAM
          Submitting a Payment
•   Once your report has been submitted, payment may be made using the
    payment portal on our website by selecting Submit a Payment. *Note that
    the portal uses ACH debit and you will need to make sure your bank
    account is setup to do so prior to proceeding.

•   Payment details for wires and ACH credit may also be found under
    the Reporting Guidelines tab on our website. *Note that these types of
    payments must be initiated by your financial institution.




               VIRGINIA TREASURY'S UNCLAIMED PROPERTY PROGRAM
   Failure to Report and Remit

Pursuant to §55.1-2540, the holder shall pay interest and a civil penalty up to
$1,000 for each day the report and remittance is withheld up to a maximum of
the lesser of $50,000 or 100 percent of the value of the property which should
have been paid or delivered and shall be required to pay the administrator
interest at the same annual rate as is applicable to delinquent taxes under
§58.1-1812.
