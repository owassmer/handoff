## Retrieval notes

- vacourts.gov opinion PDFs are at `https://www.vacourts.gov/static/opinions/opnscvwp/1<record no>.pdf` (Supreme Court of Virginia) and `.../opncavwp/<record no without dashes>.pdf` (Court of Appeals). The URL without `/static/` returns a 302.
- The CourtListener v4 search API works without a key but is limited to 5 requests a minute. Opinion pages return nothing to curl; web_extract works but caps at 50,000 characters, so use the vacourts.gov PDF for long opinions.
- law.lis.virginia.gov: the section body is inside `span#va_code`. The full-title pages (`vacodefull/titleN/`) are the fastest way to search a whole title for a defined term.
